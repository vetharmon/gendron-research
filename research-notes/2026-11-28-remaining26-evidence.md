# Remaining-candidate evidence continuation — 28 November 2026

The candidate grades are unchanged. This note records new sources and bounded searches; original manuscripts, later archival copies, finding aids and unverified comparisons remain distinguished.

## C181

A complete **later archival typed copy** records the burial of **Joseph**, aged **one and a half**, on **26 January 1828**, son of **Joseph Gendron, cultivateur, and Marguerite Daigneau**. He had died **about three weeks earlier**. The witnesses are François and Antoine Hyneault, who could not sign. The contemporary burial manuscript has not yet been inspected. [Full copied act and English translation](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c181/#document-burial-joseph-gendron-1828-01-26)

The matching parents and timing reopen the earlier compiled **5 January death / 26 January burial** claim. The copy does not supply an exact 5 January death date. It names **Joseph**, whereas the newly read baptism names **Étienne**, born and baptized **2 April 1826**. Étienne would have been about **twenty-one months** old in early January 1828, compared with the burial's eighteen months. Neither discrepancy is silently resolved. This is an **unresolved childhood-death lead**, not an established burial of C181 or an elimination. [Baptism and complete English translation](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c181/#document-act-4253604)

The new discovery screen covered **C-2889 images 356–434**, 79 typed frames after the April 1826 baptism through the start of 7 March 1828. It found this burial but no separate same-parent Joseph baptism. OCR was used to find possible entries, followed by selected visual readings; this does **not** establish an exhaustive absence. The next checks are the contemporary 26 January 1828 burial manuscript, births before April 1826 and the complete sibling sequence. **Needs work remains unchanged.**

Contemporary printed Kennebec voters lists provide exact tracts for the **unassigned Ontario lead**. They repeatedly place Joseph on lots **14–15, concession 5**, and a separate John B./Baptiste on lot **13, concession 4**. These are working correlations among Ontario records; no list identifies their parents or states a family relationship.

The next land searches are the abstract indexes and instruments for **concession 5, lots 14 and 15**, and **concession 4, lot 13**, Kennebec. The OnLand historical-books route was located, but no deed or abstract page was read. These records do not connect either man to the **1829 Châteauguay baptism**. The original **1860 Shandrow/Clark marriage return**, with its unresolved Andrew/Antoine comparison, remains the parentage priority. **Needs work remains unchanged.**
