# C109 and C194 — original family records, 18 November 2026

## Decision

C109 Joseph Gendreau moves from likely to solid elimination. The identification of the child born 24 January 1823 with Célina Lavimodière’s husband rests on matching paternal identity and maternal surname, compatible geography and age, the later family associations, and the exclusion of the younger same-family Joseph. The 1823 reading Lisette Allard is preserved; no original has been represented as an explicit Lisette/Louise alias statement.

C194 Godefroy Gendreau remains solid, with documented Québec life extended from his 1852 marriage to his remarriage on 23 June 1885.

## Identity checks and limits

The original 1823 baptism reads Lisette, not Louise. The 1863 marriage reads Louise and gives no numerical age. Both name Laurent Gendreau. The 28-act couple-index sweep already recorded on 16 November returned the 1823 baptism as the only Lisette entry; the other maternal forms were Marie Louise, Louise or Marie. This pattern is indexed corroboration rather than a substitute for the original readings.

The younger Joseph Antoine Julien is C248, already eliminated by the original 1831 burial, aged two and a half, naming Laurent Gendreau and Marie Louise Allard. That established reading was reviewed, not newly performed. The compiled 1828 birth and age 35 in NosOrigines do not come from a cited baptism; its cited 1863 marriage gives only majeur. They do not establish another surviving son.

The family relationships are inferred from the combination of records. Neither Joseph’s daughter’s baptism nor Joseph’s burial expressly calls Godefroy a brother. Signature consistency is supporting evidence, not a formal handwriting identification.
