# Family reconstruction and the Sainte-Brigide household — 28 November 2026

This continuation strengthens the family comparisons for [C080](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c080/), [C111](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c111/), [C211](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c211/), [C266](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c266/) and [C275](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c275/). It adds **fourteen complete selected acts from later archival typed copies**, an **original 1881 census household**, and specific compiled record targets. **All 26 Needs work assessments remain unchanged; C275 retains its qualified likely grade.**

## C080 and C111: two Claire Rollins distinguished

The parent-naming marriages separate the two women encountered in the earlier Lucie baptism comparisons:

| Marriage at Saint-Clément | Bride’s parents in the typed copy |
| --- | --- |
| **Toussaint Gendron and Claire Rolin dite Macouais, 1 August 1820** | **Joseph Rolin dit Macouais and Françoise Bergevin dite Langevin**. [Complete two-page act](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c080/#document-c2889-63-64-marriage-1820) |
| **Louis Roy and Claire Rollin, 8 October 1832** | **Amant Rollin and the late Louise Gendron**. [Complete two-page act](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c080/#document-c2889-610-611-roy-rollin-1832) |

Toussaint is described as acting as father to the 1832 bride. That role does not establish biological fatherhood or another exact relationship. The June and December 1842 Lucie entries remain separate.

The [2 June 1840 remarriage](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/973), already known as an event, now has a fully read public typed copy. Jean Baptiste Gendron is explicitly the widower of **Marguerite Gervais**, and Claire Rollin the widow of **Toussaint Gendron**. Witnesses **Charles Gendron and Jean Baptiste Gendron** have no stated parents, ages or kinship; neither is assigned to C111 or C080.

The stepfather’s [1817 marriage](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2892/24) names **Jean-Baptiste Gendron and Jossephte Roussel** as his parents. A comparison [11 October 1790 baptism](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2891/383) names **J. Bte. Jeandron and Josette Roussel**; its abbreviated year is supplied from the surrounding page. The spellings are retained, and the marriage does not explicitly identify that baptism. Marguerite Gervais died **23 January 1839**, aged about **39**, and was [buried 25 January](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2893/138) as Jean-Baptiste’s wife.

Five selected child acts supply four observations for this first union:

| Child | What the later typed copy records |
| --- | --- |
| Unnamed child | [Buried 8 April 1824](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2892/459); father written **Jean-Baptiste Jandron**. Sex, age, birth/death dates and baptismal status are unstated. |
| Marguerite | [Born and conditionally baptized 20 February 1828](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c080/#document-c2892-765-766-marguerite-baptism-1828); sponsors **Paul Daigneau and Marie Gervais**. The compilation instead gives **24 February**. |
| Jean-Baptiste | [Born 16 June, baptized 17 June 1831](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2892/995), strongly correlated with the same-named child who [died 17 April and was buried 19 April 1833](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2892/1178). Recorded age **two** is retained beside the calculated **about 22 months**. |
| Unnamed child | [Buried 11 March 1835](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c080/#document-c2892-1311-1312-anonymous-child-1835), after home baptism and immediate death. Sex and exact birth/death dates are unstated; this is not described as a stillbirth. |

The deceased Jean-Baptiste cannot be Lucie’s **1842 godfather described as brother**. The incomplete reconstruction does not exclude another surviving son of that name, so the godfather remains unassigned.

Three further typed acts refine the comparison:

- [Zoé Gendron’s marriage to Louis Ranger, 27 November 1849](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2890/615), names **Toussaint Gendron and Claire Rollin** as her parents, but no Gendron brother as witness. The groom’s father is called deceased and then named as father-witness: both statements remain pending manuscript comparison.
- [Zoé Primeau’s baptism, 14 November 1845](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2890/294), born on 12 November to **Jean Baptiste Primeau and Ursule Rollin**, names **Charles Gendron** as godfather. This repeats the name in the family’s 1844 sponsorship, without proving the sponsors identical or identifying C111.
- The competing [Charles Gendron–Angèle Gendron marriage](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2890/315) names the groom’s parents **Charles Gendron and Catherine Hinse**. Its typed date is **17 February 1846**, versus **1845** in the earlier locator and public index. **Hinse/Hains** and the year conflict are preserved; the parental distinction from C111 stands.

The next precise collateral target is the [compiled **17 February 1852 Saint-Clément marriage of Marguerite Gendron and Édouard Primeau**](https://www.nosorigines.qc.ca/GenealogieQuebec.aspx?genealogie=Gendron_Marguerite&pid=1334110). It reportedly joins the Gendron–Gervais and Primeau–Rollin families. The original and witnesses remain unread, beyond the screened Saint-Clément dates.

## C211: three exact Saint-Pie targets

The [Claude Jutras compilation, family F167904](https://sgdrummond.quebec/cjutras/CJ_GENDREAU-Pie.html#F167904), places three useful locators under **Pierre Gendreau, cultivateur, and Marie Dussault**:

| Person | Reported Saint-Pie event |
| --- | --- |
| **Paul-Léon Gendreau** | Born **25 February 1838**, baptized **27 February**; sponsors **Toussaint Forcier and Elmire Dussault**. |
| **Charles-Joseph Gendreau** | Born and baptized **14 September 1835**; sponsors **Charles-Joseph Drolet and Antoinette Lefebvre**. |
| **Marie Dussault** | Died **17 October 1873**, buried **19 October**, aged **73**. |

These are factual abstracts of a compilation without event-specific archival citations or original images. Paul-Léon is a plausible comparison for [census Paul](https://data2.collectionscanada.gc.ca/e/e093/e002317782.jpg), **13 at next birthday in 1851/1852**, with an approximately one-year discrepancy. Marie’s compiled age also differs from the census **54 at next birthday**. Neither is silently reconciled, and C211 is not named at a later event.

A [modern Milton history](https://www.miltonqc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/175e-capsule-historique-6C-2-La-tannerie.pdf) lists **Paul-Léon Gendreau** in the tannery-land ownership chain in **1879**. The [companion account](https://www.miltonqc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/175e-capsule-historique-6C-1-La-tannerie.pdf) identifies former southern **lot 19, 3e Rang Ouest**, and the Granby registry, but supplies no identifying family details or 1879 instrument number. The owner remains unassigned. Marie’s original **19 October 1873 burial** is the strongest immediate family-witness target. [Selected abstracts and translations](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c211/#document-findingaid-marie-dussault-burial-1873-10-19)

## C266 and C275: the household followed to 1881

The [original 1881 Sainte-Brigide census](https://data2.collectionscanada.gc.ca/e/e326/e008149251.jpg), district 64 Iberville, division 1, page 20, house 91, family 98, lines 14–22, records **Joseph Gendron, 50**, farmer; **Dorothée, 36**; **Césarie, 17**; **Audena, 15**; **Napoléon, 8**; **Sylvia, 6**; **Arthur, 4**; **Rose Anna, 3**; and **Adélina, 1**. The adults are marked married. Ditto marks carry Québec birthplace, Catholic religion and French origin.

Joseph, Césarie and Odéna–Audena strongly support continuation of [1871 family 197](https://data2.collectionscanada.gc.ca/1871/jpg/4395448_00122.jpg) in the same parish. **Audena, 15**, remains distinct as a reading from **Odéna, 4**, ten years earlier. Neither census states relationships or Joseph’s parents, so neither conclusively assigns the household to C266 or C275.

A [public Geneanet compilation](https://gw.geneanet.org/klc?lang=en&n=gendron&oc=4&p=joseph) supplies **17 October 1871, Sainte-Brigide**, for Joseph’s marriage to **Dorothée Bédard**, and names **Joseph Gendron/Marie Comeau** as his parents. The original is needed. Its earlier-wife name, **Cécile Délima Bonvouloir**, does not replace the [preserved 1861 reading](https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/candidates/c275/#evidence-notes) **Marie Adélaïde [Bonvouloir?]** or the groom’s uncertain maternal surname **Marie [M…neau?]**. A separate [public index](https://www.mesaieux.com/genealogie/henri_mercure) lists **Henri Mercure/Audina Gendron, Sainte-Brigide, 1886**, without an exact day or parents; Audina is not yet identified as either census girl.

## Scope and assessment

The new OCR screen covered **547 C-2892 frames: 24–508, 750–780 and 965–995**, with additional bounded cached material re-screened. The Saint-Clément screen processed **C-2890 frames 200–700**, reaching 1850. Selected acts were visually read in full and independently checked; OCR coverage is not an exhaustive register or family search. The 1881 screen covered **e008149241–253 and 263–282**, plus full image **283**; downloaded images **254–262** were not substantively screened. Focused compilation checks likewise supply no general absence finding.

The update adds **45 source rows representing 21 unique document groups** across five candidate pages. Complete selected French texts have full English translations; compilations are labelled abstracts. Nine new public log entries preserve all 672 previous entries, while eleven additional working-log entries retain search limits and comparisons. The sources narrow the next tests without establishing a new candidate identity, death or incompatible later life. **No grade or tier changes.**
