# C003 — census birthday and assessment

14 November 2026. Assessment changed from possible to **likely eliminated**. This is a circumstantial identification, not a solid elimination.

## New record found

The 1901 LAC search for first name Em*, surname Gend*, Ontario returned four entries and missed the elder. A first-name-only Lochiel search also missed him. An age-based search (75–95, Lochiel, Ontario, 1901) returned **Gandron, Millan**, item 35665825. This is a different person from the younger Millen Gendron previously located on page 13.

The row gives a male widower, head, born in Quebec, **8 February 1814**, aged **87**. The indexed name is Millan Gandron. The following row was not fully transcribed and is not used for the identification.

## Baptism rechecked

The existing original scan, C003_GENDREAU_Emilien_1816-02-09_Baptism_LAcadie_LAFRANCE-2819955.jpg, was retrieved and visually re-read. Left page, third entry, B36: dated **9 February 1816**, Emilien **né hier**, son of **Jacques Gendro**, laboureur, and **Genevieve St Yves**. His birth date is therefore **8 February 1816**, independently of the compiled tree.

The baptism and census agree on **day and month**, but **differ by two years**. The year difference is not silently corrected.

## Earlier household rechecked

The enlarged original reads **Millen**, male, **75**, married; next row **Sophronie**, female, **65**, wife. The earlier research note's original reading was confirmed. Age 75 implies about 1816. The next rows name John and Sophronie; their relationships do not establish the elder's parents.

## Assessment

The matching 8 February birthday adds a specific correspondence that the earlier census work lacked. Together with the Emilien/Millen/Millan name forms, approximate birth years, and the Lochiel household with Sophronie, it supports identifying the Ontario man with C003 as **likely**. His presence in 1891 and 1901 would exclude him from being Franklin, who died in 1886.

The adult records do not name Jacques Gendreau and Genevieve St Yves. The two-year discrepancy remains, and no exhaustive search for rival Emilien baptisms has been completed. These limitations prevent a solid assessment. A record naming his parents, or a sufficiently distinctive contemporary family link, remains the next target.

The Hogansburg 1853 marriage and Ontario 1903 death remain prior supporting leads. The marriage names Emilian Jandreau and Sophronia Fontaine but no parents; the death-index age 89 implies about 1813/14. Those originals were not newly re-read in this pass. NosOrigines supplies the compiled association but is not proof of parentage.
