# C266: a probable 1851 family appearance — 28 November 2026

[C266 Joseph Gendron](../candidates/c266/) moves from **Needs work to Likely eliminated**. The change applies an explicit brother-witness identification in a previously saved original to the candidate whose page had continued to say no later record was known.

## Evidence

At **Saint-Antoine-sur-Richelieu on 30 September 1851**, Antoine Gendron married Hermine Cormier. The original, folio 272, names **François and Joseph Gendron as brothers of the groom**. Antoine’s parents are François Gendron and deceased Marie-[Anne?] Élisabeth Dupont; the compressed middle name is retained as uncertain. Antoine’s 1826 baptism names François Geandron and Marie Anne Dupont. [1851 marriage](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400860) · [1826 baptism](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4594379)

The known same-parent Josephs were baptized in **September 1828 (C237)** and **December 1829 (C266)**. The one-month-old Joseph buried 13 October 1828 probably is C237, but his mother is recorded as **Isabelle Dussault**, whereas his baptism says **Marianne Dupont**. Both originals were re-read; the conflict remains. This makes C266 the probable, rather than certain, later Joseph. [Older baptism](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4594528) · [Proposed infant burial](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4596550)

## Limits

The 1851 act states Joseph’s relationship, but gives no age, spouse or separate residence. One wedding appearance does not prove continuous Canadian residence or rule out travel. The later family has not been exhaustively reconstructed. These limits are retained in the **Likely** grade. The Sainte-Brigide census household remains unassigned.

LAC lists both Contrecoeur and St. Antoine in Verchères as **missing in 1851**. Their index non-hits carry no evidence of absence; the previous explanation of simple under-indexing is superseded. [Official coverage](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/genealogy-family-history/censuses/pre-confederation/1851/districts/canada-east-quebec.html)

The next specific originals are father François’s reported **9 July 1852 burial** and Julie Gendron/Félix Viau’s **31 March 1856 marriage**. Their public compiled locators were checked; the originals remain unread.
