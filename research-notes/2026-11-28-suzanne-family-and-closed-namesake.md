# Suzanne’s family records and the closed Île-d’Orléans comparison

Research continuation, 28 November 2026. Candidate assessments are unchanged: **25 remain Needs work**. This update adds fuller collateral evidence for [Pierre C010](../candidates/c010/) and [Narcisse C212](../candidates/c212/), reconciles an already resolved namesake comparison for [Jean Baptiste C006](../candidates/c006/), and brings [Théophile C184](../candidates/c184/)’s summaries into agreement with the newly published original audit.

## Two complete Suzanne Gendron acts

The later typed Saint-Clément de Beauharnois register copy supplies two complete selected acts, with full French text and English translations on both candidate pages:

- **19 November 1835 marriage**, MG8 G3, volume 2, typed p. 744, [C-2889 image 756](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/756). Suzanne Gendron is the minor daughter of Ignace Gendron and deceased Josephte Gendron. Ignace is explicitly the bride’s father among the witnesses. The groom is written Vincent **Augé**, and his mother **Jeannes Gendron**.
- **11 February 1841 marriage revalidation**, volume 3, typed p. 992, [C-2889 image 1006](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/1006). It explicitly refers to the 1835 marriage and acknowledges Marie Virginie, born 17 October 1836, and Charles, born 30 August 1838, as the couple’s legitimate children.

The first act records fourth-to-fourth-degree consanguinity with a 17 November 1835 dispensation. The second describes a third-to-third-degree diriment impediment, a missing required earlier dispensation and a new 9 February 1841 dispensation. These differences are preserved. Neither act names Pierre or Narcisse, and no kinship is inferred for the other witnesses. The typed copies were visually inspected and independently checked; the underlying parish manuscripts were not newly read.

## A fuller reading of the known census page

The previously cited 1851 Saint-Urbain page was newly retrieved through [LAC’s public original image](https://data2.collectionscanada.gc.ca/e/e092/e002296024.jpg). Printed p. 17, nominal lines 47–50, gives:

| Line | Name | Birthplace | Age at next birthday |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 47 | Ignace Gendron, farmer | Beauharnois | 70 |
| 48 | Amable Lamadeleine | Châteauguay | 61 |
| 49 | Adeline Bertrand | St Philom. [Saint-Philomène] | 17 |
| 50 | [Marianne?] Lamadeleine, female | Lachine | 51 |

The fuller reading supports the withdrawal, already recorded on 15 November, of the old “Narcisse Gendron, 51” interpretation. It is not a new discovery of that error. The public transcription covers nominal columns 1–8 only. Corresponding marks on the [p. 18 continuation](https://data2.collectionscanada.gc.ca/e/e092/e002296025.jpg) sit one printed line lower and require editorial alignment. No explicit spouse or parent relationship or fixed household size is inferred.

The [compiled Ignace profile](https://gw.geneanet.org/ra41dr07?lang=en&n=gendron&p=ignace) and [Amable profile](https://gw.geneanet.org/ra41dr07?lang=en&n=lamadeleine&p=amable) give a 30 July 1844 Sainte-Martine remarriage. The Ignace profile also supplies a 9 August 1856 death and 11 August burial at Beauharnois. Those originals remain unread. The matching adjacent names make the census comparison worth pursuing, but Ignace’s age and birthplace differ from the compiled May 1786 Châteauguay birth. Amable’s age also differs from her compiled 24 November 1792 birth. The census man is not established as the candidates’ father.

For C212, the LAC image and existing FamilySearch link remain in the same **source-004** document group, with the newly checked public image primary. The continuation is a secondary link in that group.

## Reconcile the C006 namesake comparison

The expanded parental names and birth date in the separate compiled profile are not attributed to the original-reading report. No original image was newly inspected for this reconciliation, and existing private Log publication flags are unchanged.

C006 remains established through his sister’s 6 October 1840 Chambly marriage.
