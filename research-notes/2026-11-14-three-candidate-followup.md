## C039 — Michel Archange Gendron, 1818

Source: https://electriccanadian.com/history/ontario/pioneerpapers.pdf#page=351

Visually read printed pp. 143–144 (PDF pp. 351–352), A. C. Osborne, Old Penetanguishene, Pioneer Papers No. 6, Simcoe County Pioneer and Historical Society, Barrie, **1917**. Verified the title page at PDF p. 285. The host index describes No. 1 (1908), but this bound scan contains later numbers; do not cite these biographies as 1908.

P. 143 identifies Charles Gendron, father of Carolus (born Newmarket 1827), as half-brother of Michel the tanner. P. 144 gives Michel’s birthplace as St-Martin, Québec; settlement at Smith’s Falls; training at Lyn near Brockville; arrival Penetanguishene about 1835; wife Miss Judson of Brockville; death September 1874; wife’s death 1896. Names children C. G., Sidney, Edmond, Michel Jr., Vincent, Anthony, Mrs. W. J. Martin and Mrs. S. Whalen. No parents named.

This strengthens identification of the Ontario household but weakens the proposed connection to C039’s Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade baptism. The source is retrospective, and the 1835 move versus family chronology needs checking. It is not an elimination. Next: original death/marriage records and reconstruction of the reported half-brother Charles. Do not merge tree parents without those records.
