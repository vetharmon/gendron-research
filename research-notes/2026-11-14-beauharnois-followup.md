## Public register access

https://www.canada.ca/fr/bibliotheque-archives/collection/aide-recherche/genealogie-histoire-famille/documents-naissances-mariages-deces/paroisses.html

Canadiana C-2889 contains Public Archives typed parish transcriptions. These are derivative copies, not photographs of the handwritten acts. Relevant pages were visually inspected. Images 310–1080 were also screened by OCR for Gendron and related strings; some downloads failed, OCR omitted text, and this was not a complete manual reading. No absence from that screen is treated as proof of absence from the register.

## C240 Toussaint

- [Image 452, page 441](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/452): baptized 21 September 1828, born same day, son of Toussaint Gendron and Claire **Rollin**. Earlier candidate data said Nolin. The derivative spelling is now disclosed rather than silently equated.
- [Image 455, page 444](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/455): anonymous-child burial on 28 October 1828 names Léon Gendron and Catherine Cadot. This corrects the earlier page's description of that different family as Charles Gendron / Catherine Hébert.
- [Image 696, page 684](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/696): Jérémie, age one year, son of Toussaint Gendron / Claire Rollin, buried 16 August 1834. Not C240.
- [Image 776, page 763](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/776): Toussaint, fourteen months old, died the preceding day, buried 20 May 1836, parents Toussaint Gendron / Claire Lorin. This belongs to the later birth, not C240.
- [C-2892 image 810, page 2614](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2892/810): spot reading around 28 October–1 November 1828 found no matching Toussaint burial. This is not a full-register negative search.

The claimed Châteauguay death of 30 October 1828 remains unverified. Neither the anonymous burial nor the 1836 namesake supports his elimination.

## Other rejected matches and leads

[C-2889 image 887, page 874](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/887): Pierre Gendron, thirteen, died 25 July and buried 27 July 1838, son of Pierre Gendron / Angélique Hébert. Not C172, whose parents are Jean-Baptiste Gendron / Claire Chatigny.

[Image 678, page 666](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/678): Régis, five, buried 12 March 1834, son of Jean-Baptiste Gendron / Marie Poirier. Not C226, whose mother is Claire Chatigny.

[Image 317, page 308](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/317): Charles baptized 6 June 1825, born the day before, parents François Gendron / Joseph Bougie (as typed). This is a possible retrieval lead for C164, whose stored date is 10 June 1825, but the date and maternal transcription need comparison with the handwritten baptism before adoption. [Images 324–325](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/324) instead contain a Charles baptized 28 July 1825, parents Ignace Gendron / Josephte Gendron; do not merge these children.

Public searches for C003, C004, C015, C028, C033, C039, C054, C172, C212, C220 and C274 did not provide a new contemporary bridge to an adult record. Compiled trees and namesake results were used as leads only.
