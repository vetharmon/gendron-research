Ferdinand the child's age reads 5 in the original, although the Ancestry index displays 3. The image is the authority for the transcription.

## Identification

C192’s original baptism, previously inspected on 14 November 2026, records birth at St-Michel on 16 December 1826 to Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denis. C119’s original baptism identifies the same parents; his 19 October 1847 Lauzon marriage identifies his wife as Victoire Lecours dite Barras. The census has both this named married couple and Catherine Denis, whose recorded age fits her 1799 baptism. Narcisse’s name, birthplace and approximate age complete the identification.

Kinship between Narcisse, Catherine and Ferdinand is reconstructed from the original baptismal and marriage records; it is not explicitly stated in the census. François and Joseph are transcribed here without asserting a baptismal identification for either.

Narcisse’s unmarried presence with the family in Québec after Franklin’s 1850 Missouri family census supports a solid elimination. The 1861 Ancestry index hit under Narcipe Geudron at St-Michel (record 793520067) has not been inspected in the original and is not used for this conclusion.
