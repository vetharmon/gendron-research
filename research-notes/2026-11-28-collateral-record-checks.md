## Olivier C110: the father-Thomas burial hit names another maternal identity

The complete later typed copy of **Félicité Gendron’s burial at Saint-Clément de Beauharnois on 5 July 1847** names **Thomas Gendron and Françoise Rufiange** as her parents. Félicité was **40** and had died **two days earlier**; 3 July is calculated from that phrase. Witnesses are **Jean Ginestet and Didier Montmarquet**, with **F. Caisse** as priest. Two readers checked the selected act, and the complete French and English texts are included on [C110’s page](../candidates/c110/#document-beauharnois-felicite-gendron-burial-1847-07-05-copy). [Archival copy: C-2890, image 427, MG 8 G 3, vol. 4, p. 1477](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2890/427)

This is the full copied text behind a previously recorded father-only index hit. The maternal name differs from **Isabelle Texier/Tessier** in Olivier’s baptism. The burial does not mention Olivier or a sibling relationship. It therefore does not establish Félicité as his sister, although it also does not prove that the two fathers named Thomas were different men. The contemporary handwritten register remains unread.

## C086/C120: a Sorel résumé supports Provençal but omits the Louis witness

The **later typed résumé** on **C-7203, image 963**, MG 8 G 59, volume 3, records the **11 October 1842 marriage of Cyrille Gendreau and Marie Anne Viens**. Under October 1842, entry **445 M11** gives:

| Person | Parent columns as copied |
| --- | --- |
| Cyrille Gendreau | François; Geneviève Beaucage |
| Marie Anne Viens | f Joseph; Geneviève Provençal |

The copied *f* is preserved and explained as *feu*, “late.” **M11 is the day code within October**, not the original act number M47. The full selected French and English are displayed in [C086’s dated evidence note](../candidates/c086/) and in [C120’s conditional-comparison source card](../candidates/c120/#document-act-13400800). [Typed résumé](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c7203/963)

The résumé agrees with **Provençal** in the retained greffe continuation and the known Vermont repertoire. It does not certify the wording of either original register copy or resolve the alternative unverified **Roussel** reading. It contains **no witnesses, sibling clauses or signatures**. The original continuation’s claimed **Louis Gendreau** relationship therefore remains unverified, and the résumé does not connect C120’s mother **Geneviève Pagé** with **Bocage/Beaucage**.

On C086, the new copy is grouped with the existing marriage as **act-13400800**, with the earlier selected text and its uncertainty notes retained. The verified résumé text is also reproduced in the narrative. On C120, the same résumé is the displayed text for an explicitly unassigned collateral comparison. Selecting a displayed text does not make a derivative source an original record.

## C275: an exact Napoléon marriage target

The cached text of the Maine Franco-American Genealogical Society’s *American/Canadian Marriages 1599–1984* reports **Napoléon Gendron and Émélie Fréchette**, married **20 November 1893 at Saint-Césaire, Rouville**. The groom’s parent field gives **Joseph Gendron and Dorothée Bédard**, followed by **Ste Brig.** The bride’s parents are **François Fréchette and Elmire Tetreau**. [Compiled table](https://usgenwebsites.org/MEAndroscoggin/marriages_American-Canadian-Marriages-1599-1984.pdf) · [C275 source card](../candidates/c275/#document-mfgs-1893-napoleon-gendron-emelie-frechette)

The selected row was independently checked in cached extracted text. It occurs in the table ending with the extracted **Page 1990** footer; neither that scanned page nor the parish original was inspected. This is a new exact locator for the present household comparison, not a new original marriage reading or a claim of American residence.

The groom may be **Napoléon, aged 8**, enumerated with Joseph and Dorothée at Sainte-Brigide in 1881. That identity remains unverified. The compiled entry does not name Joseph’s parents and cannot assign the household to C275 or C266. The **17 October 1871 Joseph/Dorothée marriage**, the **1886 Henri Mercure/Audina marriage**, and now the **1893 Napoléon/Émélie marriage** remain distinct original-record targets.
