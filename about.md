# About this research

This site documents the historical identification research into Franklin Jondro of Missouri and the proposed connection to François Gendron baptized 6 September 1823 at Châteauguay, Québec.

The register contains **281 stable rows**, including **210 recorded exclusions**, **69 unexcluded alternative identities**, **one proposed identity (C127)**, and **one indexing error (C270)**. The underlying register was consolidated 10 November 2026.

A recorded exclusion is a research assessment, not independent recertification of every scan. Qualified leads are generally **not** counted as exclusions. The four qualified François candidates are the exception: they are counted as eliminated, with the uncertainty in each stated on its page and in the register. The proposed identity remains a hypothesis pending the evidence bridging Québec to Missouri.

## Archives and licensing

No register images are hosted or reproduced here. Source links lead to providers including BAnQ, FamilySearch, PRDH-IGD and Généalogie Québec, where recorded in the research notes. Some services require sign-in or subscription; URLs have not all been live tested. The filenames shown on candidate pages reference a private evidence archive and are not downloadable images.

## Citation caution

The present source pages are working research notes. Verify the historical act, its location and the image before relying upon it in an official submission. The earliest records shown are indexed baptism dates unless otherwise stated.

## How to use the research assessments

The narrative on each candidate page preserves the project's existing interpretation and any original-record reading notes. Distinguish those working readings from a letter-for-letter transcription: names, dates, parental identifiers, and conflicting entries should be checked against the original scan. In particular, a burial lead with unresolved parental conflict is **not** a conclusive exclusion.

The evidence entries on candidate pages are **readable source abstracts**, prepared from existing research metadata — not verbatim transcriptions of manuscript images. Exact wording must be checked against the original before quoting.

## Research method

Every candidate retains its original C-series ID so the pages can be reconciled with the master elimination register. Evidence is assessed through childhood death records, adult marriages and continuing families, census records, and source-parent matching. A record that cannot currently be found is not treated as proof of emigration or death.
