FROM THE FAMILY COLLECTION

The Johndrow family Bible

A family history kept between the covers.

A keepsake worth sharing

Alongside the census returns and parish registers, there is this: a family Bible, its cover worn and its decorated record pages filled in by hand.

The pages preserve marriages, births, deaths, and later family memoranda. They are part of the story of Franklin Jondro's American family, whose name appears in these records as Johndrow.

These copies and the photograph of the cover were shared with Tim Harmon in June 2026. The original Bible remains in family custody.

The introductory page carries an 1870 copyright notice. The dates written in a family Bible describe the events being remembered; they do not, by themselves, establish when each entry was written.
