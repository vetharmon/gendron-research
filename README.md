# Gendron · Gendreau Research Atlas

Jekyll/GitHub Pages site documenting 281 Gendron/Gendreau candidate records as of 10 November 2026. Source images are **not hosted in this repository**; the candidate pages link externally to the record providers where direct URLs were preserved in the research register.

- 212 recorded exclusions (research assessments)
- 67 unexcluded alternatives (48 unresolved, 19 qualified)
- C127 proposed identity; C270 index error
- 30 François-name entries, available with the **François only** checkbox

The site is built from the consolidated project audit and is not an independent verification of every historical claim or external link. Candidate URLs follow /candidates/c001/ etc.

## Publish
In Settings → Pages choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch `main`, folder `/(root)`. Then the site should publish at https://vetharmon.github.io/gendron-research/.

Candidate sidebars show parents’ names where recorded and direct source links. PRDH references are stored in `_data/prdh_links.json` as labels, URLs and uncertainty notes only; reconstructed cards and their bulk data are not published. A retired-index notice preserves old bookmarks.

The family Bible gallery includes the cover and six historical pages. No affidavit or paid-provider register scans are hosted.
