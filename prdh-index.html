How complete is this collection?

Recovery of a card does not mean it is complete or verified. Saved transcriptions retain database information; OCR can be cropped or contain reading errors. Most recovered family child tables still need structured transcription. Missing records are shown explicitly on candidate pages.

The initial recovery linked saved individual cards to 141 of 280 actual candidates, baptism certificates to 143, burial certificates to 106, and parental family cards to 27. The current C138/C226 family dossier adds a documented family link and all twelve children. The remaining reference-only entries include relatives outside the candidate list.

The initial conservative acquisition allowance was up to 529 hits for the core individual/baptism/parental-family index, or 703 including burial certificates. Shared families and additional saved material reduce that allowance. Candidates' own married families, spouses, and further generations require a separate estimate.
