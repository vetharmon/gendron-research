## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2903622
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 13 August 1817 St-Jean-Port-Joli, B57 right middle, names Louis born that day, son of Pierre Gendron and Madeleine Hudon. Original marriage 9 April 1839 St-David, M4 lower right with following-page continuation, names Louis, minor son of Pierre Abraham Gendron and deceased Madeleine Hudon, and Marianne Allard, daughter of Joseph Allard and Marianne Niquet. Burial 11 February 1859 St-David, S14 bottom left with following-page continuation, names infant Jean Baptiste, son of Louis Gendron, journalier, and Marianne Allard, died previous day aged nine months. Marriage of their son Louis, 22 October 1861 St-Hugues, M23 left page, again names Louis Gendron and Marianne Allard, then of Ste-Hélène. This linked continuing family supports exclusion. A separate saved baptism of Marie Hermine, 5 December 1859, gives mother Magdelaine Allard rather than Marianne; that conflicting entry is retained as a qualified lead and is not needed for this exclusion. PRDH family 205124 corroborates the family sequence.
