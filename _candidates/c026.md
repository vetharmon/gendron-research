## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 13 August 1817 and baptized the same day at St-Jean-Port-Joli (L'Islet), the son of Pierre Gendron and Madeleine Hudon. He married Marianne Allard, daughter of Joseph Allard and Marianne Niquet, on 9 April 1839 at St-David (St-David-d'Yamaska); the act calls him the minor son of Pierre Abraham Gendron and the late Madeleine Hudon. Their infant son Jean Baptiste died on 10 February 1859, aged nine months, and was buried the next day at St-David, the act naming Louis, a journalier (day labourer), and Marianne Allard as his parents. A daughter, Marie Hermine, was baptized at St-David on 5 December 1859, though that act names her mother as Magdelaine Allard rather than Marianne — a conflicting entry kept as a qualified lead. On 22 October 1861 his son Louis married at St-Hugues, the act naming Louis Gendron and Marianne Allard, by then of Ste-Hélène, as the groom's parents. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 22 October 1861.
