## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Joseph Gendron was born and baptized on 22 June 1819 at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Michel Gendron and Claire Apolline Labossiere. [Baptism](#document-act-2631411)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 27 August 1819, aged two months, and was buried at St-Ours the following day. [Burial](#document-act-2633151)

- **[Burial, 28 August 1819 — LAFRANCE act 2633151](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2633151)** — His burial at St-Ours (middle right): he had died the day before, aged two months, and the act names the same parents as his baptism.
