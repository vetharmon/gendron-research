## Biography

Joseph Gendron was born on 21 June 1819 and baptized the next day at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Michel Gendron and Claire Apolline Labossiere. He died on 27 August 1819, aged two months, and was buried at St-Ours the following day.
