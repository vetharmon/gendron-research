## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2925154
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Three original scans downloaded and inspected 9 November. Baptism 8 September 1823, bottom right, Louis born avant hier (6 September), legitimate son of Francois Gendro, journalier, and Marguerite Martel. Marriage M18 upper left, 18 July 1843 Mascouche, names Louis Gendreau, fils mineur (majeur crossed out), son of Francois Gendreau and Marguerite Martel, to Louise Paille, adult daughter of Jean Baptiste Paille and deceased Marie Brien dite Desroches. The minor status is compatible with the baptism. Child baptism B73 upper left, 1856 St-Roch-de-l'Achigan, names George, born avant hier, legitimate son of Louis Gendreau, cultivateur du lieu, and Louise Paille; the act says the father signed, and Louis Gendreau appears as a signature below the priest's closing. IMPORTANT DATE QUALIFICATION: PRDH/LAFRANCE place this child at 1 September birth / 3 September baptism, and following entries are September. The original month in the George act appears to read Aout, with an alteration/overwriting visible; it is not represented as an unambiguous September transcription. The exclusion rests on the 1856 year, same marital pair, direct paternal participation and matching parental bridge from the 1823 baptism to the 1843 marriage, not on deciding August versus September. PRDH profile 808770 and family 224884 corroborate this distinct adult family. A later 1858 child lead (act 5581758) had no linked original available and is not relied upon. This supports exclusion from Franklin's Missouri identity.
