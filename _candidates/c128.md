## Biography

Louis Gendreau was born on 6 September 1823 and baptized two days later at St-Roch-de-l'Achigan (L'Assomption), the son of François Gendron, journalier, and Marguerite Martel. He married Louise Paille on 18 July 1843 at Mascouche; the act names him Louis Gendreau, a minor, son of François Gendreau and Marguerite Martel. In 1856 he appears at St-Roch-de-l'Achigan as the father of George, whose baptism names Louis Gendreau, cultivateur of that place, and Louise Paille as the parents, and records that the father signed the act. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 1856.

The indexes place the birth on 1 September and the baptism on 3 September 1856, but the month written in the original appears altered and may read August; the year, the parents' names and the father's participation are what the record establishes.
