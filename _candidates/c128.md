## Biography

Louis Gendreau was born on 6 September 1823 and baptized two days later at St-Roch-de-l'Achigan (L'Assomption), the son of François Gendron, journalier, and Marguerite Martel. A likely continuation is Louis Gendreau’s marriage to Louise Paille on 18 July 1843 at Mascouche. The inspected marriage names the minor groom’s father as deceased Jean Gendreau, not François, and his mother as Marguerite Martel. The matching mother, locality and compatible age support the association but do not resolve the father’s given-name conflict. The married couple appears again in 1856 at St-Roch-de-l'Achigan in the baptism of George, whose baptism names Louis Gendreau, cultivateur of that place, and Louise Paille as the parents, and records that the father could not sign.

The inspected copy names deceased Jean Gendreau and Marguerite Martel as the groom’s parents. His minority fits the baptism date, but Jean versus François prevents an exact parental match. The reported François reading in the separate FamilySearch copy remains unverified. Also at [Généalogie Québec (image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400833).
- **[Baptism of the couple’s son, 1856 — LAFRANCE act 5604534](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5604534)** — George, son of Louis Gendreau and Louise Paille, baptized at St-Roch-de-l'Achigan (entry B73, upper left), with a closing statement that the father could not sign; the closing signature is the priest’s. The indexes place the birth on 1 September and the baptism on 3 September 1856, but the month written in the original appears altered and may read August; the year, the parents' names and the father's participation are what the record establishes.
