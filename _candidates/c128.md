## Biography

Louis Gendreau was born on 6 September 1823 and baptized two days later at St-Roch-de-l'Achigan (L'Assomption), the son of François Gendron, journalier, and Marguerite Martel. A likely continuation is Louis Gendreau’s marriage to Louise Paille on 18 July 1843 at Mascouche. Both inspected copies of the 1843 marriage name the minor groom’s father as deceased Jean Gendreau and his mother as Marguerite Martel. Direct inspection of the supplied FamilySearch image on 23 November 2026 corrected the earlier transcription of that copy’s father as François. The matching mother and compatible age support the proposed association but do not resolve the conflict between Jean in the marriage and François in the baptism. The married couple appears again in 1856 at St-Roch-de-l'Achigan in the baptism of George, whose baptism names Louis Gendreau, cultivateur of that place, and Louise Paille as the parents, and records that the father could not sign.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 18 July 1843 — LAFRANCE act 13400833](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400833)** — The proposed marriage to Louise Paillé at Mascouche (entry M18, upper left). The saved greffe image and the [FamilySearch copy](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L99Q-SGHN), directly inspected from the supplied JPEG on 23 November 2026, both name deceased Jean Gendreau and Marguerite Martel as the groom’s parents. The earlier François reading of the FamilySearch marriage was a transcription error, not a difference between copies. The groom is a minor and the bride an adult; the greffe copy corrects majeur to mineur by an endorsed interlineation. His minority fits the baptism date, but Jean versus François still prevents an exact parental match. Witnesses are described as friends, and the closing clause says they and the spouses could not sign. Tentative witness and dit-surname readings are bracketed in the source transcriptions. Also at [Généalogie Québec (image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400833).
- **[Baptism of the couple’s son, 1856 — LAFRANCE act 5604534](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5604534)** — George, son of Louis Gendreau and Louise Paille, baptized at St-Roch-de-l'Achigan (entry B73, upper left), with a closing statement that the father could not sign; the closing signature is the priest’s. The indexes place the birth on 1 September and the baptism on 3 September 1856, but the month written in the original appears altered and may read August; the year, the parents' names and the father's participation are what the record establishes.
- **[Baptism, 8 September 1823 — LAFRANCE act 2925154](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2925154)** — His baptism at St-Roch-de-l'Achigan (bottom right of the page), born 6 September, son of François Gendron and Marguerite Martel. Also at [Généalogie Québec (image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/2925154).
