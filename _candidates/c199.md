## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Louis Octave Gendron was born on 19 April 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Ours, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron, a menuisier, and Archange Poulin. The original baptism was read on 16 November 2026. [Baptism](#document-act-4653068)
- **Probable later appearance:** He is probably the unmarried 34-year-old man indexed as **Itan Jondro** in the 1861 Barford census, among Jean Baptiste Gendron's family. The given name remains difficult to read. [Census](#document-source-007)

## Family continuation

The original 1861 census, Barford, Stanstead, page 21, records the following people on lines 40–50:

The man on line 44 is unmarried. His age fits Louis Octave’s 1827 birth. The father and younger family members fit the family reconstructed in the Gunnis tree. Alfred’s original 1864 marriage independently names Jean Baptiste Gendron and Lucie Dudevoir of Barford as his parents.

Jean Baptiste’s original 1834 remarriage names him as widower of **Lucie Poulin**, marrying **Lucie Dudevoir**. Louis Octave’s baptism and the parents’ original 1826 marriage call his mother **Archange Poulin**. That given-name difference remains to be reconciled directly; it is not silently normalized.

A notarial repertory records a **quittance et ratification** on **20 April 1848**, Jean-Baptiste Maranda, act **1291**, by **Louis Octave Gendron** to **Jean Baptiste Gendron**. This is one day after Louis Octave’s twenty-first birthday. The repertory does not expressly state their relationship. The full deed has not been inspected.

## Assessment

**Likely elimination.** The 1861 age and surrounding family support Louis Octave’s identification as an unmarried man in Québec after Franklin had established his Missouri family. The census given name is difficult to read, and the maternal given-name difference and full notarial deed remain to be checked. These limits keep the assessment below solid.

The Gunnis tree also attaches a married Octave Gendron, 45, at McNider in 1871, with Vitaline and children and near Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard. That index was inspected, but no parentage bridge connects that man to C199; it is not used here. The tree’s 1881 attachment was not inspected. The previously rejected St-Pierre-les-Becquets Louis remains a separate namesake.

## Further original-record checks — 16 November 2026

The original **21 March 1831 St-Ours burial** calls the deceased **Lucie Poulin**, aged **24**, wife of **Jean Baptiste Gendron, menuisier of this parish**. She died two days earlier. The husband’s name, occupation, parish and her age fit the family, strengthening the connection to the Lucie Poulin named in the 1834 remarriage. The entry does not explicitly identify her as Archange.

A closer reading of the original **11 April 1826 marriage** corrects Archange Poulin’s parents to **the late Joseph Poulin and Marie Louise Chicoine**. The earlier transcription of those grandparents was wrong and has been corrected in the log and notes.

The 1848 repertory was re-read: it names *sieur Ls Octave Gendron* and *sieur Jean Bte Gendron*, without expressly stating their relationship. The 1861 census name remains a difficult reading. **Likely elimination remains unchanged.**
