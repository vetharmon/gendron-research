## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4566321
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism B86, 28 December 1826, names Nazaire born the previous day, Pierre Gendron and Angele Vallee. Original burial 27 February 1830 names Nazaire, son of Pierre Gendron and Angele Vallee, died 24 February, age four years. Calculated age is three years one month twenty-eight days; the original age difference remains unresolved literally. Newly inspected parental marriage M12, 24 June 1823, begins bottom right and continues top left on the following image: Pierre Gendron, son of Michel Gendron and Madeleine Leduc, to Marie Angele Vallee, daughter of Michel Vallee and deceased Therese Morand Douville. Original sister’s baptism B44, 19 May 1825, names Marie Efrasie born that morning, same Pierre Gendron and Angele Vallee. PRDH family 147688 was freshly checked: Marie born February 1824, Marie Euphrasie May 1825, one Nazaire December 1826, then Marie Tilibee March 1828. This reconstruction corroborates the family sequence but is not proof that no unrecorded child existed. On the combined exact child name, both matching original parents, same parish, childhood chronology and corroborating family sequence, the 1830 burial is assessed as this candidate. Counted exclusion after additional original family-context verification and reassessment of the age-only conflict. Four in the burial is not silently corrected to three; a fourth-year age convention or clerical error is possible but not proved.
