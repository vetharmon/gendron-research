## Biography

Nazaire Gendron was born on 27 December 1826 and baptized the next day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, the son of Pierre Gendron and Angele Vallee. He died on 24 February 1830 and was buried on 27 February 1830 at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade. The burial gives his age as four years, though the interval from his baptism makes him just over three; the research notes leave that difference unresolved.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 27 February 1830 — LAFRANCE act 4569580](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4569580)** — His burial at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade: Nazaire, son of Pierre Gendron and Angele Vallee, died 24 February, aged four years by the act (just over three by his baptism). The name, both parents and the parish match his baptism.
- **[Baptism, 28 December 1826 — LAFRANCE act 4566321](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4566321)** — His baptism at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (entry B86): Nazaire, born the previous day, son of Pierre Gendron and Angele Vallee.
- **[Parents' marriage, 24 June 1823 — LAFRANCE act 5694355](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5694355)** — Pierre Gendron, son of Michel Gendron and Madeleine Leduc, marries Marie Angele Vallee, daughter of Michel Vallee and the deceased Therese Morand Douville (entry M12, beginning bottom right and continuing on the following image). It documents the couple named as his parents.
- **[Baptism of his sister, 19 May 1825 — LAFRANCE act 4566165](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4566165)** — Marie Efrasie, born that morning at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (entry B44), daughter of the same Pierre Gendron and Angele Vallee — a sibling in the same parish register a year and a half before Nazaire.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 2707342](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/2707342)** — PRDH's compiled record for Nazaire.
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 147688](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/147688)** — PRDH's compiled family record, which lists the children in sequence — Marie (February 1824), Marie Euphrasie (May 1825), Nazaire (December 1826) and Marie Tilibee (March 1828) — corroborating the household around him.
