## Biography

Charles Gendron was born on 27 July 1825 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Ignace Gendron and Josephte Gendron. He died on 15 September 1847, aged twenty-two, and was buried at Ste-Martine on 17 September 1847.
