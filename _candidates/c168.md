## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Charles Gendron was born on 27 July 1825 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Ignace Gendron and Josephte Gendron. [Baptism](#document-act-4253539)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 15 September 1847, aged twenty-two, and was buried at Ste-Martine on 17 September 1847. [Burial](#document-act-4155825)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 17 September 1847 — LAFRANCE act 4155825](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4155825)** — His burial at Ste-Martine (entry S59, upper left): Charles died on 15 September, aged twenty-two, son of Ignace Gendron and the late Josephte Gendron. The age and both parents tie the burial to the 1825 baptism.
