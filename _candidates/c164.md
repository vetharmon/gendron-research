## Biography

Charles Gendron was baptized on 10 June 1825 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of François Gendron and Josephte Bougie. The baptism index gives his birth as 9 June. No later record has been securely connected to Charles.

His father died on 11 August 1844 and was buried two days later, aged 54. The original burial names Josephte Bougie as his wife. His brother Paul’s marriage index, dated 17 September 1844, names the same parents and records François as deceased.

## Assessment

Charles remains uneliminated. These family records do not establish his whereabouts after baptism. The 1851 census search should follow his widowed mother and siblings; his father had already died. An unsuccessful census-index search does not establish absence from Canada.
