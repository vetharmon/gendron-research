## Biography


- **Baptism:** Charles Gendron was baptized on 10 June 1825 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of François Gendron and Josephte Bougie. The baptism index gives his birth as 9 June. [Baptism](#document-act-4253516)
- **Father's death:** His father died on 11 August 1844 and was buried two days later, aged 54. The original burial names Josephte Bougie as his wife. [Father's burial](#document-act-4258503)
- **Brother's marriage:** Paul's original marriage of 17 September 1844 names the same parents and records François as deceased. It identifies Jean Gendron as his brother; Charles is not named. [Paul's marriage](#document-act-13401048)
- **Possible later appearance:** A record names Charles Gendron as godfather in his brother's family in 1861; the identification remains unproved. [Joseph Emery's baptism](#document-act-6103932)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Father’s burial, 13 August 1844 — act 4258503](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4258503)** — Original, folio 22, S43: François Gendron, 54, husband of Josephte Bougie, died 11 August.
- **[Brother Paul’s marriage, 17 September 1844 — act 13401048](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401048)** — Both pages of the original were inspected on 23 November 2026. Paul Gendron married Josephte Brossoie; his parents are deceased François Gendron and Josephte Bougie. Jean Gendron is identified as his brother, and Luc Mallette as his brother-in-law. Charles is not named.

- **[Joseph Emery Gendron’s baptism, 15 December 1861 — act 6103932](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/6103932)** — Original B191, folio 50: Charles Gendron is godfather and signs; Marcelline Allard is godmother. The father is written Firmin Gendron and the mother Françoise Hébert. The existing [C070 reconstruction](../c070/) identifies the father as Charles’s brother François; the first-name discrepancy is retained there.

## Assessment

**Possible elimination.** Charles Gendron’s presence as a signing godfather in his brother’s family at Beauharnois in December 1861 is a concrete lead for a separate life in Québec. The record does not identify the godfather as the child’s uncle, give his age, or name his parents. It therefore does not prove that he is C164, and the signature cannot yet be attributed to C164.

Follow Charles through further records of his brothers François (C070) and Paul (C116), and his widowed mother Josephte Bougie. His father had died in 1844. A record explicitly naming Charles’s parents or kinship is needed to strengthen this identification.

## Further record check

Paul’s burial of 6 September 1854 records him as the thirty-one-year-old husband of Josephte Brossois, accidentally drowned according to a coroner’s certificate. The burial does not give a specific death date. Its named witnesses are Joseph [Prévot?] and Louis Hébert, followed by unnamed others; Charles is not named, so this record supplies no additional identification. [Paul’s burial](#document-act-6104366)

## Family follow-up

Josephte Bougie’s original burial confirms her death on 28 July 1867 and burial at Beauharnois on 30 July (S62, folio 28 verso). The act names François Gendron as her husband. François Gendron and François Primeau witnessed the burial but are not assigned relationships. Charles is not named.
