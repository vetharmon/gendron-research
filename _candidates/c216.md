Original marriage 24 October 1847 at Granby identifies Louis Narcisse Gendron, minor son of Charles Gendron and Angelique Letellier, marrying Marguerite Gagnon. Original baptism 12 July 1850 at Stukely-Nord names Adeline, born 8 July, child of Narcisse Gendron and Marguerite Gagnon. This matched parental and marital chain supports a separate continuing Quebec family from Franklin and Phebe’s Missouri household. The child act explicitly says the father was absent.
