## Biography

- Louis Narcisse Gendron was born on 6 December 1827 and baptized the same day at Notre-Dame de Québec, the son of Charles Gendron and Angelique Letellier. [Baptism](#document-act-3038286)

- He married Marguerite Gagnon on 24 October 1847 at Granby, the act naming him as the minor son of Charles Gendron and Angelique Letellier. [Marriage](#document-act-13401020)

- Their daughter Adeline, born on 8 July 1850, was baptized on 12 July 1850 at Stukely-Nord, the act naming Narcisse Gendron and Marguerite Gagnon as her parents. [Adeline baptism](#document-act-5989442)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 24 October 1847 — LAFRANCE act 13401020](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401020)** — His marriage to Marguerite Gagnon at Granby. It names his parents, Charles Gendron and Angelique Letellier, tying the groom to the 1827 baptism. His daughter Adeline’s [baptism at Stukely-Nord on 12 July 1850](#document-act-5989442) names Narcisse and Marguerite as the child’s parents. Its closing says that the godparents and father did not know how to sign; it does not state that the father was absent.
- **[Baptism, 6 December 1827 — LAFRANCE act 3038286](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3038286)** — His baptism at Notre-Dame de Québec: baptized as Louis Narcisse, born the same day, son of Charles Gendron and Angelique Letellier.
