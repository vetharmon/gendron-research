## Biography

Louis Narcisse Gendron was born on 5 December 1827 and baptized the next day at Notre-Dame de Québec, the son of Charles Gendron and Angelique Letellier. He married Marguerite Gagnon on 24 October 1847 at Granby, the act naming him as the minor son of Charles Gendron and Angelique Letellier. Their daughter Adeline, born on 8 July 1850, was baptized on 12 July 1850 at Stukely-Nord, the act naming Narcisse Gendron and Marguerite Gagnon as her parents. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 12 July 1850.
