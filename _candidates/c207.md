## Biography

- Cyrille Gendron was born on 23 August 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets, the son of Damase Gendron and Sophie Baril. [Baptism](#document-act-3367429)

- He married Marguerite Paris on 25 September 1849 at Deschaillons, the act naming the same parents. [Marriage](#document-act-13400885)

- Their son Cyrille was born on 23 February 1861 and baptized the next day at Deschaillons, the act naming Cyrille Gendron and Marguerite Paris as his parents. [Cyrille baptism](#document-act-5613834)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 25 September 1849 — LAFRANCE act 13400885](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400885)** — His marriage to Marguerite Paris at Deschaillons. It names his parents, Damase Gendron and Sophie Baril, tying the groom to the 1827 baptism. A further record in the file, his son Cyrille's baptism at Deschaillons on 24 February 1861 ([LAFRANCE act 5613834](#document-act-5613834)), names Cyrille and Marguerite as the child's parents, though the act does not state that the father was present.
- **[Baptism, 24 August 1827 — LAFRANCE act 3367429](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3367429)** — His baptism at St-Pierre-les-Becquets: born the previous day, son of Damase Gendron and Sophie Baril.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 869384](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/869384)** — PRDH's compiled record for Cyrille Gendron; it gives his mother as Marie Sophie Baril and repeats the 1849 marriage to Marguerite Paris.
