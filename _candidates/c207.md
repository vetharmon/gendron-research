## Biography

Cyrille Gendron was born on 23 August 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets, the son of Damase Gendron and Sophie Baril. He married Marguerite Paris on 25 September 1849 at Deschaillons, the act naming the same parents. Their son Cyrille was born on 23 February 1861 and baptized the next day at Deschaillons, the act naming Cyrille Gendron and Marguerite Paris as his parents. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 24 February 1861.
