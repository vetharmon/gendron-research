## Biography

Olivier Gendron was baptized on 29 April 1819 at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Joseph Gendron and Marie Mathieu. An Olivier Gendron married Justine Roberge on 2 February 1841 at Sorel. The marriage describes him as a journalier (day labourer), son of François Gendron and Louise Mathieu, whereas this baptism names Joseph Gendron and Marie Mathieu. Both parental given names therefore differ. The adult husband of Justine Roberge died of cholera on 22 July 1849 and was buried the next day at Lauzon, described as an ingénieur (engineer), aged about thirty-two. That burial names no parents and does not resolve his identification with the child baptized in 1819.
