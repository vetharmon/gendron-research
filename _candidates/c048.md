Marriage 2 February 1841 at Sorel identifies Olivier Gendron, navigateur, son of Joseph Gendron and Louise Mathieu, marrying Justine Roberge. Burial 23 July 1849 at Lauzon identifies Olivier Gendron, navigateur and husband of Justine Roberge, deceased previous day of cholera. PRDH family 104020 corroborates the Mathieu family and Olivier’s placement.
