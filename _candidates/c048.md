## Biography

Olivier Gendron was baptized on 29 April 1819 at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Joseph Gendron and Marie Mathieu. He married Justine Roberge on 2 February 1841 at Sorel; the marriage act calls him a navigateur (sailor) and names his mother as Louise Mathieu. He died of cholera on 22 July 1849 and was buried the next day at Lauzon, the act naming him as navigateur and husband of Justine Roberge and giving his age as about thirty-two, though he was thirty by his baptism.
