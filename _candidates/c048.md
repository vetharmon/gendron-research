Baptism 29 April 1819 names Olivier, Joseph Gendron and Marie Mathieu. Marriage 2 February 1841 at Sorel identifies Olivier Gendron, navigateur, son of Joseph Gendron and Louise Mathieu, marrying Justine Roberge. Burial 23 July 1849 at Lauzon identifies Olivier Gendron, navigateur and husband of Justine Roberge, deceased previous day of cholera. PRDH family 104020 corroborates the Mathieu family and Olivier’s placement. The original marriage supplies the missing spouse-to-parents bridge; burial age about 32 differs from the baptism interval of 30 years. Maternal Marie/Louise variation is retained explicitly.
