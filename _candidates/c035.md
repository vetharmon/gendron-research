## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 11 February 1818 and baptized the next day at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu (St-Antoine-de-Padoue), the son of Louis Gendron and Marie Gaudette. He died on 25 May 1818 and was buried the next day at Contrecoeur, aged three months; the burial names the same parents.
