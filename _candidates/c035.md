## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Louis Gendron was born on 11 February 1818 and baptized the next day at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu (St-Antoine-de-Padoue), the son of Louis Gendron and Marie Gaudette. [Baptism](#document-act-2853701)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 25 May 1818 and was buried the next day at Contrecoeur, aged three months; the burial names the same parents. [Burial](#document-act-2737536)
