## Biography

Fabien Gendreau was baptized on 29 January 1818 at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies (L'Islet). No later Québec record has been connected to him. A Fabien Jandrew appears in Ontario with his wife, Catherine Jones, in the saved 1881 and 1891 censuses, and a later family account names a twin, Marie Marcelline; Methodist ministers named Howard and Leduc were identified on 10 November 2026 as specific leads for a marriage search. But no contemporary record naming his parents or spouse bridges that Ontario man to this baptism, so the identification remains unproven.

## Ontario follow-up

Two [family worksheets](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/53052d10e4b0b111f005ebe2/t/5fe250cf421188466b4b79d4/1608667445932/Family%2Bwork%2Bsheets.pdf) give a narrower search route. PDF page 3 records Fabian Jandrew’s death in **1896 at Stockdale, Ontario**, with Catherine Jones as his wife; her death is entered as **13 May 1897**. His parents are blank. The worksheet gives his birth as 1816 in France, which conflicts with this candidate’s 1818 Québec baptism. These entries have no cited supporting acts.

PDF page 8 identifies Margaret Jandrew as a daughter of Fabian Jandrew and Catherine Jones, and records her marriage to Robert Jones on **17 January 1872 at Madoc/Rawdon**. A [transcription of her obituary](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/53052d10e4b0b111f005ebe2/t/5f617effc295d97626e6ba2e/1600225031321/Obituaries%2BVol.%2B2%2BJohns%2Bto%2BJulian.pdf#page=25), attributed to the *Marmora Herald*, 1 July 1909, page 1, names brothers W. J. Jandrew of Ottawa and Charles of Frankford, and sister Mrs. John Stacy of North Bay. It describes Margaret as born in Rawdon in 1851. The obituary does not name her parents.

The next records to obtain are Fabian’s 1896 Ontario death registration and Margaret’s 1872 marriage registration. The Ontario family remains unconnected to the St-Roch-des-Aulnaies baptism.
