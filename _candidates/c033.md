## Biography

Fabien Gendreau was born on **28 January 1818** and baptized the next day at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies, son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau, a shoemaker, and Claire Caron. The adjoining act baptizes his twin Marie Marcelline, also born the previous day.

He is likely the Fabien Jandrew who lived in Murray Township, Ontario, with Catherine Jones. The original 1881 census records a Québec-born French shoemaker named Fabien Jandrew, aged 64, with Catherine, 51, William, 25, and Charles, 11. The saved 1891 census image records Fabien, **76**, Catherine, 61, and Charles, 21, in the same township. Survival to 1891 would distinguish this man from Franklin, who died in 1886.

## Assessment

**Likely eliminated.** The identification rests on the combination of the census household and a later family account naming Fabien’s St-Roch origin and twin Marie Marcelline. The original baptism confirms that distinctive twin detail; the Québec origin, approximate ages, name and shoemaking background also fit. This is a reasoned correlation of the records and account, rather than acceptance of a tree attachment.

The family account was contributed by Joan H. King and contains a reference to 1996. Its original author and documentary basis have not been established, and it may draw on parish research rather than independent family memory. Neither census names Fabien’s parents. The recorded ages 64 and 76 imply earlier births than 1818; 76 is the corrected reading of the saved 1891 image, previously reported as 75. A separate compiled worksheet gives 1816 and France, contrary to the Québec census birthplace. These limits prevent a solid elimination.

## Ontario follow-up

Two [family worksheets](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/53052d10e4b0b111f005ebe2/t/5fe250cf421188466b4b79d4/1608667445932/Family%2Bwork%2Bsheets.pdf) give a narrower search route. PDF page 3 records Fabian Jandrew’s death in **1896 at Stockdale, Ontario**, with Catherine Jones as his wife; her death is entered as **13 May 1897**. His parents are blank. The worksheet gives his birth as 1816 in France, which conflicts with this candidate’s 1818 Québec baptism. These entries have no cited supporting acts.

PDF page 8 identifies Margaret Jandrew as a daughter of Fabian Jandrew and Catherine Jones, and records her marriage to Robert Jones on **17 January 1872 at Madoc/Rawdon**. A [transcription of her obituary](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/53052d10e4b0b111f005ebe2/t/5f617effc295d97626e6ba2e/1600225031321/Obituaries%2BVol.%2B2%2BJohns%2Bto%2BJulian.pdf#page=25), attributed to the *Marmora Herald*, 1 July 1909, page 1, names brothers W. J. Jandrew of Ottawa and Charles of Frankford, and sister Mrs. John Stacy of North Bay. It describes Margaret as born in Rawdon in 1851. The obituary does not name her parents.

The next records to obtain are Fabian’s 1896 Ontario death registration and Margaret’s 1872 marriage registration. A contemporary parentage record is still needed to make the likely identification solid.

## Family follow-up, 16 November 2026

The [marriage of Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Modeste Ouellet, 31 January 1826](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400815), names the groom as the minor son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau, a shoemaker of St-Pierre-les-Becquets, and the late Marie Claire Caron. The original was manually read, including its continuation. Matching these parents to Fabien's baptism identifies the groom as Fabien's brother. He is also the father of [François Séverin C221](../c221/).

This supplies another family branch to trace for a contemporary connection to the Ontario Fabien. The marriage does not name Fabien or place him in Ontario, so his assessment remains **likely eliminated**.

### Twin sister Marcelline

The [24 November 1846 marriage at St-André](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13447976) names Marcelline as daughter of Jean Baptiste and the late Marie Claire Caron of St-Roch-des-Aulnaies. She married Germain Michaud. The original, manually read on 16 November 2026, includes her signature as **Marcelline Gendreau**, although the act is indexed under **Gaudreau**. It does not name Fabien. This record was missed by the Gendreau parental search and found after removing the bride's surname from the couple search.

Two Fabien Gaudreau marriage leads were checked in the detailed indexes and belong to different families: Henryville, 7 November 1837 (act 13404792), names François Godreau and Françoise Fortin; Sainte-Luce, 27 February 1843 (act 13398953), names Germain Gaudreau and Louise Fournier. Neither supplies an identification for C033.

Margaret's marriage index (FMKH-KWL), 17 January 1872, names her parents as John and Catharine. Her death index (JF89-8B3), 28 June 1909, names John Gandrew, born France, and Catharine Jones. The attached Find a Grave index gives 1816–1896; the nine-source list does not include Fabian's actual 1896 death registration.

These records add to the Ontario family history but do not establish the connection to the 1818 baptism. **Likely eliminated remains unchanged.**
