## Biography

Fabien Gendreau was baptized on 29 January 1818 at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies (L'Islet). No later Québec record has been connected to him. A Fabien Jandrew appears in Ontario with his wife, Catherine Jones, in the saved 1881 and 1891 censuses, and a later family account names a twin, Marie Marcelline; Methodist ministers named Howard and Leduc were identified on 10 November 2026 as specific leads for a marriage search. But no contemporary record naming his parents or spouse bridges that Ontario man to this baptism, so the identification remains unproven.
