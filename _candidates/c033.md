## Biography

Fabien Gendreau was born on **28 January 1818** and baptized the next day at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies, son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau, a shoemaker, and Claire Caron. The adjoining act baptizes his twin Marie Marcelline, also born the previous day.

He is likely the Fabien Jandrew who lived in Murray Township, Ontario, with Catherine Jones. The original 1881 census records a Québec-born French shoemaker named Fabien Jandrew, aged 64, with Catherine, 51, William, 25, and Charles, 11. The saved 1891 census image records Fabien, **76**, Catherine, 61, and Charles, 21, in the same township. Survival to 1891 would distinguish this man from Franklin, who died in 1886.

## Assessment

**Likely eliminated.** The identification rests on the combination of the census household and a later family account naming Fabien’s St-Roch origin and twin Marie Marcelline. The original baptism confirms that distinctive twin detail; the Québec origin, approximate ages, name and shoemaking background also fit. This is a reasoned correlation of the records and account, rather than acceptance of a tree attachment.

The family account was contributed by Joan H. King and contains a reference to 1996. Its original author and documentary basis have not been established, and it may draw on parish research rather than independent family memory. Neither census names Fabien’s parents. The recorded ages 64 and 76 imply earlier births than 1818; 76 is the corrected reading of the saved 1891 image, previously reported as 75. A separate compiled worksheet gives 1816 and France, contrary to the Québec census birthplace. These limits prevent a solid elimination.

## Ontario follow-up

Two [family worksheets](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/53052d10e4b0b111f005ebe2/t/5fe250cf421188466b4b79d4/1608667445932/Family%2Bwork%2Bsheets.pdf) give a narrower search route. PDF page 3 records Fabian Jandrew’s death in **1896 at Stockdale, Ontario**, with Catherine Jones as his wife; her death is entered as **13 May 1897**. His parents are blank. The worksheet gives his birth as 1816 in France, which conflicts with this candidate’s 1818 Québec baptism. These entries have no cited supporting acts.

PDF page 8 identifies Margaret Jandrew as a daughter of Fabian Jandrew and Catherine Jones, and records her marriage to Robert Jones on **17 January 1872 at Madoc/Rawdon**. A [transcription of her obituary](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/53052d10e4b0b111f005ebe2/t/5f617effc295d97626e6ba2e/1600225031321/Obituaries%2BVol.%2B2%2BJohns%2Bto%2BJulian.pdf#page=25), attributed to the *Marmora Herald*, 1 July 1909, page 1, names brothers W. Fabian’s 1896 Ontario death registration has now been recovered (see below). Margaret’s 1872 marriage registration has also been inspected (see below). A contemporary parentage record is still needed to make the likely identification solid.

### Twin sister Marcelline

The [24 November 1846 marriage at St-André](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13447976) names Marcelline as daughter of Jean Baptiste and the late Marie Claire Caron of St-Roch-des-Aulnaies. She married Germain Michaud. The original, manually read on 16 November 2026, includes her signature as **Marcelline Gendreau**, although the act is indexed under **Gaudreau**. It does not name Fabien. This record was missed by the Gendreau parental search and found after removing the bride's surname from the couple search.

Two Fabien Gaudreau marriage leads were checked in the detailed indexes and belong to different families: Henryville, 7 November 1837 (act 13404792), names François Godreau and Françoise Fortin; Sainte-Luce, 27 February 1843 (act 13398953), names Germain Gaudreau and Louise Fournier. Neither supplies an identification for C033.

### Marriage and migration leads in the family account

The [full family narrative](https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/memories/L5DD-DB1) was reopened. It says an unnamed brother accompanied Fabien to the Kingston–Belleville area, then moved on to an unknown destination. It names the two marriage ministers as **Mr Howard and Mr Leduc**, and places the couple near Stirling or Hubbell Hill before Stockdale. These details offer searches in Methodist marriage records and property records. The account names Pierre and Jean Baptiste as brothers, but does not identify which brother travelled with Fabien. Its authorship and documentary basis remain unestablished.

### Marriage search follow-up, 16 November 2026

A [biographical compilation for Nathan H. Howard](https://krassoc.wordpress.com/2013/01/05/nathan-h-howard-methodist-episcopal-minister/) places this Methodist Episcopal minister at Stirling/Rawdon in 1847. He is a possible identification for the narrative’s “Mr Howard”; neither his participation in this marriage nor its date is established. “Mr Leduc” remains unidentified.

The [United Church Archives microfilm inventory](https://www.unitedchurcharchives.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/FORM.043-LCM-SHELF-copyright.pdf) lists St. Paul’s, Stirling, fonds 1573, series 1, accession 91.192L, item 2-2, marriage register **1906–1917**. This listed register is too late for the couple. Its dates do not establish that earlier records never existed or are absent from other holdings.

The Eastern Upper Canada marriage index returned no text matches for Jand* or Gendr*. Its stated county coverage does not include Hastings or Northumberland, so this is a limited finding, not evidence against an Ontario marriage. FamilySearch searches with spouse Cath*/Jones and subject Fab*/Jandr* recovered the already-known William Cyrus death; Gendr* variants added no marriage result.

A published study of the Massena Jandrew surname concerns Louis Jandrew’s line, not a documented connection to this Fabien. It supplies no parentage bridge for C033. Assessment unchanged.

### Margaret’s original marriage inspected, 16 November 2026

The [original 17 January 1872 marriage entry](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:9Q97-Y395-NBZ?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AFMKH-KWL&cc=1784216&lang=en&groupId=M9S4-8BP), DGS **004529102**, image **133 of 508**, right-hand column, was manually read. A witness is John, also with a difficult surname, resident at Rawdon. This entry does not name Fabian or supply his parents.

The original marriage therefore remains an Ontario family record, not a new parentage bridge for C033. **Likely unchanged.**
