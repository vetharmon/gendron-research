## Biography

Gabriel Gendron was baptized on 27 November 1823 at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Charbonault. He may be the Gilbert Gendron who was buried on 7 February 1826 at St-Jude, aged about two, the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Chardonais. The age and the father fit, but the burial changes both the child's name, from Gabriel to Gilbert, and the mother's surname, from Charbonault to Chardonais, and neither difference has been resolved, so it is not certain the buried child is this one.

The same entry appears in the parish copy on FamilySearch (film 005471045, image 221). Both the child's name and the mother's surname differ from the baptism, which is why this record remains a lead rather than a certainty.
