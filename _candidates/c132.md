## Biography

Gabriel Gendron was born and baptized on 27 November 1823 at St-Ours, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Charbonault. He was probably the child recorded as Gilbert, about two years old, buried at St-Jude on 7 February 1826 after dying two days earlier. The burial names Pierre Gendron and Marie Chardonais as his parents.

## Assessment

**Likely eliminated.** The burial is stronger when considered with Paul’s records. Paul was born on 7 January 1826 and baptized the next day at St-Jude to Pierre Gendron and Marie Charbonneau. A Paul of the same father and Marie Chardonais was buried there on 9 February, aged about one month, after dying the previous day. The two burials occur two days apart and repeat the same maternal surname. Their stated ages fit the two baptismal children: Gabriel was about two years two months old and Paul about one month.

This paired chronology supports identifying both deaths with the baptismal family. It does not prove that Charbonault/Charbonneau and Chardonais identify the same woman, or resolve Gabriel versus Gilbert. A separate Pierre Gendron/Marie Chardonais household has not been ruled out through a complete reconstruction. Those gaps keep C132 at likely, with no claim of a solid elimination. Paul’s likely status alone is not used as proof of Gabriel’s identity; the underlying records are compared together.
