## Biography

Césaire Gendron was baptized on 7 June 1825 at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville, the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Sabourin. He married Julie Bonin on 27 November 1849; the marriage act names his parents as Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Choinière. The two forms of his mother's name are resolved by his parents' marriage at Beloeil on 2 October 1809, which names the bride as Marie Louise Choinière dit Sabourin, daughter of the late Jacques Choinière dit Sabourin and Désanges Véronneau, and the groom Pierre Gendron as the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Hélène Giard. Césaire's daughter Marie Adeline, born on 20 April, was baptized on 21 April 1861, the child of Césaire Gendron and Julie Bonin. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 21 April 1861.
