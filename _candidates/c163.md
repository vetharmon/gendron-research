## Biography


- **Baptism:** Césaire Gendron was baptized on 7 June 1825 at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville, the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Sabourin. [Baptism](#document-act-4187194)
- **Marriage:** He married Julie Bonin on 27 November 1849; the act names his parents as Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Choinière. [Marriage](#document-act-13554397)
- **Mother's name:** His parents' marriage at Beloeil on 2 October 1809 resolves the two forms: the bride is Marie Louise Choinière dit Sabourin, daughter of the late Jacques Choinière dit Sabourin and Désanges Véronneau; Pierre Gendron is the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Hélène Giard. [Parents' marriage](#document-act-5740318) · [Baptism](#document-act-4187194) · [Marriage](#document-act-13554397)
- **Later family:** Césaire's daughter Marie Adeline, born on 20 April, was baptized on 21 April 1861, the child of Césaire Gendron and Julie Bonin. [Marie Adeline's baptism](#document-act-5413242)
