## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4187194
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original 1825 baptism names Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Sabourin. His November 1849 marriage names Pierre and Marie Louise Choiniere. The original parental marriage M19 of 2 October 1809 Beloeil explicitly says Marie Louise Choiniere dit Sabourin, daughter of late Jacques Choiniere dit Sabourin and Desanges Veronneau, and names Pierre's parents Jean Baptiste and Helene Giard. This original resolves the maternal surname variation that had kept C163 qualified. His daughter Marie Adeline's original baptism 21 April 1861, born 20 April, names Cesaire Gendron and Julie Bonin, confirming the continuing separate Quebec family.
