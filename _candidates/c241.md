## Biography

Jacques Pascal Gendron was born on 21 September 1828 and baptized the next day at St-Hugues (Bagot), the son of Jacques Pascal Gendron and Adelaide Hibert. He died on 31 March 1832 and was buried at St-Hugues on 1 April 1832, aged three years and six months.
