## Biography

Civiel Gendron — written Ciril in the register itself — was born on 2 April 1829 and baptized the next day at La Durantaye (St-Michel), the son of Jacques Gendron, a farmer (agriculteur), and Catherine Denis dite Lapierre. His godparents were Simon Mercier and Rosalie Paquet, and his father could not sign. He died on 23 April 1836 and was buried at La Durantaye on 25 April 1836, aged seven years; the burial names him Cyrille, legitimate son of Jacques Gendron, cultivateur, and Catherine Denys, of the same parish.
