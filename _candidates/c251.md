## Biography

- Civiel Gendron — written Ciril in the register itself — was born on 2 April 1829 and baptized the next day at La Durantaye (St-Michel), the son of Jacques Gendron, a farmer (agriculteur), and Catherine Denis dite Lapierre. [Baptism](#document-act-4173307)
- His godparents were Simon Mercier and Rosalie [Paquet?], and his father could not sign. [Baptism](#document-act-4173307)
- He died on 23 April 1836 and was buried at La Durantaye on 25 April 1836, aged seven years; the burial names him Cyrille, legitimate son of Jacques Gendron, cultivateur, and Catherine Denys dite Lapierre, of the same parish. [Burial, 25 April 1836](#document-act-4175485)
## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 25 April 1836 — LAFRANCE act 4175485](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4175485)** — His burial at La Durantaye (entry S18, left middle of the page): Cyrille, son of Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denys dite Lapierre of that parish, died two days earlier aged seven years — seven years and twenty-one days after his birth. Both parents, the parish, and the age agree with the baptism, under the spelling Cyrille.
- **[Baptism, 3 April 1829 — LAFRANCE act 4173307](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4173307)** — His baptism at La Durantaye (entry B22, beginning bottom right and continuing at top left of the next spread): the manuscript names him Ciril — Civiel is the index form, kept for traceability — born the previous day, son of Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denis dite Lapierre, with sponsors Simon Mercier and Rosalie [Paquet?]. PRDH keeps the baptism and the burial as two separate, unmerged profiles (2765739 and 2981218); the original acts match on parents, parish, and age.
