## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4173307
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism B22, 3 April 1829 St-Michel (La Durantaye), begins bottom right and continues top left on the next spread. The manuscript names Ciril (not the index form Civiel), born yesterday, son of Jacques Gendron, agriculteur, and Catherine Denis dite Lapierre. Continuation completes Lapierre and gives sponsors Simon Mercier and Rosalie Paquet; the father could not sign. Original burial S18, 25 April 1836, left middle, names Cyrille, legitimate son of Jacques Gendron, cultivateur, and Catherine Denys, of this parish, died avant-hier aged seven years. From 2 April 1829 to 23 April 1836 is seven years twenty-one days. Original child name spelling Ciril/Cyrille, both parents, locality and age agree. This supports exclusion through childhood death. PRDH maintains separate birth-only Civiel profile 2765739 and burial profile 2981218; the independent original comparison, not a merged database profile, establishes this linkage. Stable identifier and original indexed name Civiel are retained for traceability.
