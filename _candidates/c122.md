## Biography

- Francois Xavier Gendron was born on 25 July 1823 and baptized the next day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Francois Gendron, a journalier, and Marie Josephe Macon. [Baptism](#document-act-2375704)
- He died on 9 April 1824 and was buried at Berthierville the next day. [Burial, 10 April 1824](#document-act-2378083)

## Research status

The burial's stated age was read as seven months, which differs from the eight and a half months between his birth and death; the identification rests on the matching compound name, both parents and the parish, and PRDH's family record lists a single Francois Xavier among the couple's children, between Catherine, born in 1821, and Pierre, born in 1825. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
