## Biography

Francois Xavier Gendron was born on 25 July 1823 and baptized the next day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Francois Gendron, a journalier, and Marie Josephe Macon. He died on 9 April 1824 and was buried at Berthierville the next day. The burial's stated age was read as seven months, which sits oddly against the eight and a half months between his birth and death; the identification rests on the matching compound name, both parents and the parish, and PRDH's family record lists a single Francois Xavier among the couple's children, between Catherine, born in 1821, and Pierre, born in 1825.
