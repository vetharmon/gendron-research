## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 3382505
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 28 November 1826 St-Roch-des-Aulnaies, act 3382505, image d1p_10601334.jpg, right middle/lower, names Félix Lazare, born previous day, son of Jean Baptiste Jeandreau and Modeste Ouellet. Original marriage 11 January 1848 Rivière-Ouelle, act 13400804, image d1p_18220661.jpg, middle left, names Félix Gendreau, cordonnier, adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau, cordonnier, and deceased Modeste Ouellet, marrying Clarisse Lebrun, minor daughter of Jean Lebrun and Constance Miville dite Deschênes. Original baptism of Elzéar Napoléon, 16 January 1851 Québec Notre-Dame, act 5565149, image d1p_1600a0664.jpg, B40 bottom right, names Félix Gendreau, cordonnier, and Clarisse Lebrun as parents; child born previous day. It explicitly states that the father signed, with Félix Gendreau's signature visible. This provides direct later-life participation in a separate family, supporting exclusion from Franklin's Missouri identity. Original baptism of Virginie Euphémie 13 March 1857 Québec Notre-Dame, act 5572792, image d1p_1601a0961.jpg, B156 right middle, also names Félix Gendreau, cordonnier, but mother Claire Lebrun and explicitly says father absent. Claire/Clarisse is disclosed; this 1857 record is supplementary, while the 1851 original independently matches Clarisse and documents the father's signature. Lazare is omitted in adult acts. PRDH family 248699 corroborates the family sequence.
