## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Félix Lazare Gendreau was born on 27 November 1826 and baptized the next day at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Modeste Ouellet. [Baptism](#document-act-3382505)
- **Marriage:** A cordonnier, he married Clarisse Lebrun on 11 January 1848 at Rivière-Ouelle; the act names him as the adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau, also a cordonnier, and the deceased Modeste Ouellet, and Clarisse as the minor daughter of Jean Lebrun and Constance Miville dite Deschênes. [Marriage](#document-act-13400804)
- **Son's baptism:** Elzéar Napoléon, born 15 January 1851, was baptized the next day at Québec (Notre-Dame), and Félix signed the act. [Elzéar Napoléon's baptism](#document-act-5565149)
- **Daughter's baptism:** Virginie Euphémie was born and baptized on 13 March 1857 at Québec (Notre-Dame), with Félix named as father and recorded as unable to sign, and the mother named as Claire Lebrun. [Virginie Euphémie's baptism](#document-act-5572792)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 11 January 1848 — Généalogie Québec act 13400804](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400804)** — His marriage to Clarisse Lebrun at Rivière-Ouelle (middle left), naming his parents and his trade of cordonnier, which ties the groom to the 1826 baptism. PRDH's compiled family record (family 248699) corroborates the family sequence; it is not among the linked sources.
- **[Baptism of his son, 16 January 1851 — Généalogie Québec act 5565149](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5565149)** — Elzéar Napoléon, born the previous day, baptized at Québec (Notre-Dame) (entry B49, bottom right), son of Félix Gendreau, cordonnier, and Clarisse Lebrun. The act states that the father signed, and his signature is visible.
- **[Baptism of his daughter, 13 March 1857 — Généalogie Québec act 5572792](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5572792)** — Virginie Euphémie, baptized at Québec (Notre-Dame) (entry B136, right middle), naming Félix Gendreau, cordonnier, as father and Claire Lebrun as mother, and stating that the godmother and father could not sign. The Claire/Clarisse variation is left as it stands in the act; the 1851 baptism above independently names Clarisse.
- **[Baptism, 28 November 1826 — LAFRANCE act 3382505](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3382505)** — His baptism at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies (right middle/lower): Félix Lazare, born the previous day, son of Jean Baptiste Jeandreau and Modeste Ouellet. The second given name, Lazare, is dropped in his adult acts. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/3382505).

- **Manuscript correction, 23 November 2026:** The 1857 phrase is “qui n’a su signer non plus que le père,” not a statement that Félix was absent. The 1851 act explicitly records his signature, so the inconsistency in the two signing statements remains visible. The checked margin numbers are B49 (1851) and B136 (1857).
