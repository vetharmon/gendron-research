## Biography

Félix Lazare Gendreau was born on 27 November 1826 and baptized the next day at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Modeste Ouellet. A cordonnier, he married Clarisse Lebrun on 11 January 1848 at Rivière-Ouelle; the act names him as the adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau, also a cordonnier, and the deceased Modeste Ouellet, and Clarisse as the minor daughter of Jean Lebrun and Constance Miville dite Deschênes. Their son Elzéar Napoléon, born 15 January 1851, was baptized the next day at Québec (Notre-Dame), and Félix signed the act. A daughter, Virginie Euphémie, was baptized on 13 March 1857 at Québec (Notre-Dame) with Félix named as father but recorded as absent, and the mother named there as Claire Lebrun. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 13 March 1857.
