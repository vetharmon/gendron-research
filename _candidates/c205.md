## Biography

- Cyprien Gendron was born on 3 August 1827 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Cyprien Gendron and Catherine Dugas. [Baptism](#document-act-4588555)

- He died a small child, on 19 January 1830, and was buried at Châteauguay on 21 January 1830, aged two. [Burial](#document-act-4592707)
