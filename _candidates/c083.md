## Biography

- Francois Xavier Gendron was baptized on 3 August 1821 at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Michel Gendron and Claire Apolline Labossière. [Baptism](#document-act-2631779)

- He died aged twenty-four hours and was buried at St-Ours on 6 August 1821. [Burial](#document-act-2633329)
