## Biography

Francois Xavier Gendron was baptized on 3 August 1821 at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Michel Gendron and Claire Apolline Labossière. He died aged twenty-four hours and was buried at St-Ours on 6 August 1821.
