## Biography

- Julien Gendreau was born on 8 November 1827 and baptized the same day at St-Pierre, Île d'Orléans, the son of Julien Gendreau and Marie Pouliot. [Baptism](#document-act-4078600)

- He married Eugenie Leclerc on 18 July 1854 at St-Laurent, Île d'Orléans, the act naming him as the adult son of Julien Gendreau and Marie Pouliot. [Marriage](#document-act-13400819)
