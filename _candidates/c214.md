## Biography

- Julien Gendreau was born on 8 November 1827 and baptized the same day at St-Pierre, Île d'Orléans, the son of Julien Gendreau and Marie Pouliot. [Baptism](#document-act-4078600)

- He married Eugenie Leclerc on 18 July 1854 at St-Laurent, Île d'Orléans, the act naming him as the adult son of Julien Gendreau and Marie Pouliot. [Marriage](#document-act-13400819)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 18 July 1854 — LAFRANCE act 13400819](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400819)** — His marriage to Eugenie Leclerc at St-Laurent, Île d'Orléans. It names his parents, Julien Gendreau and Marie Pouliot, tying the groom to the 1827 baptism; the entry is heavily inked, though legible, and should be read with care.
