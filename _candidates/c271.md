## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4371790
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 20 June 1830 names twins Jean Baptiste and Marguerite born the previous day to Francois Gendron; the newly recovered continuation names their mother Archange Chagnon. Burial 3 February 1832 identifies Jean Baptiste, deceased two days earlier, son of Francois Gendron and deceased Archange Chagnon. Marguerite's burial immediately above also names those parents. Both burials give eighteen months, an approximate age versus about nineteen months from baptism; this discrepancy is disclosed. The matching twins and parents support the identity. Earlier reading Arthamize is corrected to Archange. Childhood death excludes this candidate.
