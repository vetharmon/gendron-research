## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 19 June 1830 and baptized the next day at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), a twin with his sister Marguerite, the children of Francois Gendron and Archange Chagnon. He died on 1 February 1832 and was buried at St-Damase on 3 February 1832; the burial act gives his age as eighteen months (about nineteen months from his baptism) and names his mother, by then deceased, as Archange Chagnon. His twin Marguerite's burial stands immediately above his in the register and names the same parents.

## Documents

The parents and the twin sister buried just above in the register tie the burial to the 1830 baptism.
- **[Baptism, 20 June 1830 — LAFRANCE act 4371790](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4371790)** — His baptism at St-Damase, jointly with his twin sister Marguerite: born the previous day; the act's continuation names their mother, Archange Chagnon.
