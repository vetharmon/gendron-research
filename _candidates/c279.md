## Biography

- Joseph Hubert Gendron was born on 6 November 1830 and baptized the next day at St-Hugues (Bagot), the son of Hubert Gendron and Josephte Magnan. [Baptism](#document-act-4376291)
- He died on 3 February 1831, about three months old, and was buried at St-Hugues on 6 February 1831. [Burial, 6 February 1831](#document-act-4378229)
