## Biography

Joseph Hubert Gendron was born on 6 November 1830 and baptized the next day at St-Hugues (Bagot), the son of Hubert Gendron and Josephte Magnan. He died on 3 February 1831, about three months old, and was buried at St-Hugues on 6 February 1831.
