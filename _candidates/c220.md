## Biography

- George Gendron was baptized on 1 February 1828 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Joseph Gendron and Marie-Anne Guy. [Baptism](#document-act-4253799)
- **February 1838 — collateral marriage:** Marie Domitilde Gendron married Joseph Roy at Saint-Clément. The later typed copy names her as a minor daughter of Joseph Gendron, a day labourer expressly consenting to the marriage, and deceased Marie Anne Guy. Joseph also witnesses as the bride’s father. The day remains unresolved: the copy types 10, with an ambiguous handwritten annotation, while a compilation gives 6. [Marriage copy and date qualification](#document-beauharnois-roy-domitilde-gendron-1838-marriage)
- **8 November 1838 — collateral birth and baptism:** Marie Philomène, daughter of Joseph Roy and Domithilde Gendron, was born and baptized at Saint-Clément. Sponsors were Pierre Gendron, whose identity and relationship are unstated, and Catherine Roy. George is not named. [Baptism copy](#document-beauharnois-marie-philomene-roy-1838-baptism)

## Targeted hospital and family routes — 21 November 2026
