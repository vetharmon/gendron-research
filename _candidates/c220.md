## Biography

George Gendron was baptized on 1 February 1828 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Joseph Gendron and Marie-Anne Guy. No later record has been securely identified. Searches of the 1851, 1861 and 1871 census indexes have not established a match.

## Documents

- **[Baptism, 1 February 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4253799](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4253799)** — His baptism at Beauharnois (St-Clément).

- **[Public Archives baptism transcription, page 419](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/430)** — George was born and baptized on 1 February 1828. Names the parents as Joseph Gendron and Marie-Anne Guy. This is a typed transcription of the parish register.

## Rejected match

The [George Gendron buried at Beauharnois on 29 August 1874](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/membership/LAFRANCE/img/Tag/d1p_01711053.jpg) was nine months old, the son of François Gendron and Alice Brisebois. The original records his death on 27 August. He is not the George born in 1828 to Joseph Gendron and Marie-Anne Guy.

## Targeted hospital and family routes — 21 November 2026

Julia Moore's documented death at Hôtel-Dieu on 30 September 1864 gives a more focused search than another broad spouse-name query. The [UQAM CHRS catalogue](https://chrs.uqam.ca/index.php/liste-des-jeux-de-donnees/) lists an **Hôtel-Dieu de Montréal admissions dataset for 1840–1921**. The [RHSJ historical archive](https://rhsj.org/dhier-a-aujourdhui/archives/) confirms that it holds hospital patient registers. Seek Julia/Julie under Moore and Gendron in the admission preceding her death, looking for birthplace, residence, relatives and cross-references. Individual entries were not accessible in this pass; the catalogue's date span does not prove that Julia is indexed.

A separate [NosOrigines compilation](https://www.nosorigines.qc.ca/GenealogieQuebec.aspx?genealogie=Audet_Virginie&pid=1934324) gives **24 March 1863, Notre-Dame, Montréal**, for Virginie Audet dit Lapointe's earlier marriage to Amable Bonneville. The [Amable profile](https://www.nosorigines.qc.ca/GenealogieQuebec.aspx?genealogie=Bonneville_Amable&pid=1896095) reports his burial there on **19 September 1864**. These are retrieval leads: the underlying originals have not been read, and neither establishes George's parents. Compare their witnesses and residences with the 1865 remarriage when those originals are available.

No new record connects the Montréal adult to Joseph Gendron and Marie-Anne Guy. The **3 versus 6 February 1865** marriage-date discrepancy remains open. **Needs work remains unchanged.**
