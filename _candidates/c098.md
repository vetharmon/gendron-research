## Biography

Charles Evode Gendron was born on 23 March 1822 and baptized the next day at Cap-St-Ignace (St-Ignace-de-Loyola), the son of Pierre Abraham Gendron and Marie Magdeleine Hudon. He married Thérèse Dagneau on 24 November 1840 at St-Jean-Port-Joli; the act names him (as Charles) as the minor son of Pierre Gendron and the late Magdeleine Hudon dite Beaulieu. On 9 October 1860 he stood as a witness at Yamaska for the marriage of his son Louis, and signed the act. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 9 October 1860.
