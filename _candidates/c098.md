## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2877993
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 24 March 1822 Cap-St-Ignace, act 2877993, image d1p_10051083.jpg, right middle/lower, names Charles Evode, born previous day, son of Pierre Abraham Gendron and Marie Magdeleine Hudon. Original marriage 24 November 1840 St-Jean-Port-Joli, act 13400878, image d1p_10390676.jpg bottom right continuing at top left of d1p_10390677.jpg, identifies Charles Gendron, minor son of Pierre Gendron and deceased Magdeleine Hudon dite Beaulieu, marrying Thérèse Dagneau, daughter of Jean Marie Dagneau and Marie Barbe Vallée. The father's shortened Pierre/Pierre Abraham and mother's Hudon/Hudon dite Beaulieu are bridged by the parents' original marriage 18 April 1809 St-Jean-Port-Joli, act 5712241, image d1p_10381025.jpg lower left continuing upper right, identifying Pierre Abraham Gendron and Marie Magdeleine Hudon dite Beaulieu; this bridge was also checked for C258. PRDH parent family 111845 corroborates the link. Original marriage of son Louis, 9 October 1860 Yamaska, act 13401019, image d1p_20400996.jpg, M23 left page, identifies Louis, minor son of Charles Gendron and Thérèse Dagneau of St-Guillaume. It explicitly records Charles Gendron, father of the groom, as a witness signing, with Charles Gendron's signature at the foot. This provides direct later-life participation in the separate family, supporting exclusion. The baptismal additional name Evode is absent in the later acts; the link rests on the combined parentage and family evidence, not an exact full-name match.
