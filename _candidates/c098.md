## Biography

Charles Evode Gendron was born on 23 March 1822 and baptized the next day at Cap-St-Ignace (St-Ignace-de-Loyola), the son of Pierre Abraham Gendron and Marie Magdeleine Hudon. He married Thérèse Dagneau on 24 November 1840 at St-Jean-Port-Joli; the act names him (as Charles) as the adult son (fils majeur) of Abraham Gendron and the late Magdeleine Hudon dite Beaulieu. The marriage’s apparent majeur reading conflicts with an age of about eighteen implied by the assigned baptism; it is preserved as a qualification, not silently changed to mineur. The parents’ 1809 marriage supplies the fuller father’s name Pierre Abraham. On 9 October 1860 he stood as a witness at Yamaska for the marriage of his son Louis, and signed the act.
