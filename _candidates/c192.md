## Biography

Narcisse Gendron was born at St-Michel on 16 December 1826 and baptized the same day at St-Vallier (St-Philippe-et-St-Jacques). His parents, Jacques Gendron, a farmer, and Catherine Denis, lived at St-Michel. No later record has been connected to Narcisse.

## Research lead

His parents match those of [Jacques, C037]({{ '/candidates/c037/' | relative_url }}) and [Ferdinand, C119]({{ '/candidates/c119/' | relative_url }}). Their records carry Catherine's surname as Denis, Denys and Lapierre. Jacques's family reaches Lambton by 1861; Ferdinand married at Lauzon in 1847 and had a child baptized at St-Michel in 1861.

The original **15 January 1850** Lambton marriage of **Phebee Gendron and François Xavier Rousseau** names Phebee's parents as the **late Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denys dite Lapierre of St-Michel**. **Jacques Gendron, brother of the bride**, attended. This independently confirms the Denis/Denys/Lapierre surname bridge and places Narcisse's brother at Lambton by January 1850.

Narcisse is not named in this marriage. He remains a possible candidate. The next checks are his widowed mother's household at St-Michel and his brother Jacques's and sister Phebee's households at Lambton, followed by records of Phebee's children for a possible appearance as godfather.
