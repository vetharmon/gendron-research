## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Narcisse Gendron was born at St-Michel on 16 December 1826 and baptized the same day at St-Vallier, the son of Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denis. [Baptism](#document-act-3359890)
- **Later household:** The 1851 St-Michel census lists him as a servant with a reported age of 24 at his next birthday, with Catherine Denis and the family of Ferdinand Gendron and Victoire Barras. The identification of Ferdinand as his brother comes from the family records discussed below, rather than a relationship column in this census. [Census](#document-source-006) · [Family-record comparison](#elimination)

## Elimination

The original census, stamped page 55, lists the following people together:

| Line | Name | Age at next birthday | Recorded description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ferdinand Gendron | 28 | Cultivateur; born at St-Michel |
| 2 | Victoire Barras | 23 | Wife of Ferdinand; born at Pointe-Lévy |
| 3 | Ferdinand Gendron | 5 | Son of the couple |
| 4 | Victoire Gendron | 1 | Daughter of the couple |
| 5 | Narcisse Gendron | 24 | Serviteur; born at St-Michel |
| 6 | François Gendron | 19 | Serviteur; born at St-Michel |
| 7 | Joseph Gendron | 17 | Serviteur; born at St-Michel |
| 8 | Catherine Denis | 52 | Rentière; widowed; born at St-Michel |

Narcisse’s baptism names Catherine Denis as his mother and gives St-Michel as his birthplace. His brother [Ferdinand, C119]({{ '/candidates/c119/' | relative_url }}) was born at St-Michel on 9 May 1823 to the same parents and married Victoire Lecours dite Barras at Lauzon on 19 October 1847. Catherine’s baptism in 1799 fits the census age of 52. These matching family members, the married couple’s names, Narcisse’s birthplace and his approximate age identify the census entry with C192.

The census does not state that Narcisse is Catherine’s son or Ferdinand’s brother; those relationships come from their baptismal and marriage records. His identification rests on the combined family match. He is recorded in Québec after Franklin Jondro had established his Missouri household with a wife and child. The recovered sheet has only columns 1–8. Narcisse lacks the mark beside the ages of the married couple, but no separate marital-status column was checked; his unmarried status should therefore remain an interpretation, not a literal transcription. **Solid elimination.**

## Family records

The original 15 January 1850 Lambton marriage of Phebee Gendron and François Xavier Rousseau names Phebee’s parents as the late Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denys dite Lapierre of St-Michel. Jacques Gendron, brother of the bride, attended. This confirms the Denis/Denys/Lapierre surname connection in the same family. Narcisse is not named in that marriage; his later record is the St-Michel census.
