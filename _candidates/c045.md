## Biography

Eustache Gendron was born on 31 July 1818 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Eustache Gendron and Marie Josephte Lefebvre. He died on 29 August 1818 and was buried at St-Hyacinthe on 31 August 1818. The burial act gives his age as twenty-five days — four days less than the twenty-nine days since his birth, a discrepancy the file notes — and calls his father the schoolteacher of the place (maître d'école), while his baptism and his parents' marriage call his father a cultivateur.
