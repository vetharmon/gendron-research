## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2476817
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism B252 upper left, 1 August 1818: Eustache born previous day, Eustache Gendron cultivateur and Marie Josephte Lefebvre; father signs. Original burial S133 left page, 31 August 1818: Eustache, twenty-five days, died avant hier (29 August), Eustache Gendron maître d’école du lieu and Josephte Lefevre. Corrected 10 November: the old notaire reading is wrong; it says schoolteacher. Twenty-nine days elapsed from birth to death, four more than stated; discrepancy remains explicit. Original parental marriage M42 left page, 11 August 1817, identifies the same parents with father cultivateur. NEW independent occupational corroboration: original 11 November 1822 Pierre baptism B424 bottom left continuing upper right on the same spread, act 2478745, explicitly calls Eustache Gendron maître d’école du lieu and names Josephte Lefebvre. The matching child, parental couple, locality, infant chronology and independent schoolteacher record support the burial correlation despite the four-day age discrepancy and varying farm/labor/teaching descriptions. No exclusive occupation or employment start date is inferred. Previously checked family 131116 corroborates rather than proves completeness. The 1824 Marie Louise baptism says journalier; it is not an instituteur bridge. The interim contrary progress statement was withdrawn. C106 Pierre remains unresolved; his baptism is supporting family evidence only. Four core originals plus retained follow-ups and full reasoning in C045-Evidence-2026-11-10.zip.
