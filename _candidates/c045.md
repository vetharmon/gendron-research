## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Eustache Gendron was born on 31 July 1818 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Eustache Gendron and Marie Josephte Lefebvre. [Baptism](#document-act-2476817)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 29 August 1818 and was buried at St-Hyacinthe on 31 August. The burial gives his age as twenty-five days, four days less than the twenty-nine days since his birth; both the recorded age and the calculated interval are retained. [Burial](#document-act-2482747) · [Birth date](#document-act-2476817)
- **Father's occupation:** The burial calls his father the schoolteacher of the place (maître d'école), while the baptism and parents' marriage call him a cultivateur. [Burial](#document-act-2482747) · [Baptism](#document-act-2476817) · [Parents' marriage](#document-act-5732213)
