## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 3303259
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 10 June 1828 at Berthierville names Charles, born the previous day, son of Charles Gendro and Josephte Charron. Original marriage 1 October 1849 there identifies Charles Gendrau, adult son of Charles Gendrau and Josephte Charron, marrying Angele Preville. Original baptism 8 July 1851 there names Marie Louise, born that day, child of Charles Gendrault and Angele Preville. Gendro/Gendrau/Gendrault are disclosed spelling variants. This matched parental and marital chain supports a separate continuing Quebec family from Franklin and his Missouri family. The child act does not expressly establish the father’s attendance.
