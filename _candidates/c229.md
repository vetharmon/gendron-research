## Biography

- Charles Gendreau was born on 9 June 1828 and baptized the next day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Charles Gendro and **Josephte Ducharme**, as written in the original. [Baptism](#document-act-3303259)

- He married Angele Preville on 1 October 1849 at Berthierville, where the act names him as the adult son of Charles Gendreau and **Josephte Charron**. The 1851 child’s baptism supplies the combined name **Josephte Charron dit Ducharme** for a godmother paired with Charles Gendrault, supporting the maternal surname connection. [Marriage](#document-act-13400783)

- Their daughter Marie Louise was born and baptized on 8 July 1851 at Berthierville. [Marie Louise baptism](#document-act-5627045)

- His surname is spelled Gendro in the baptism, Gendrau in the marriage and Gendrault in his daughter's baptism. [Baptism](#document-act-3303259) [Marriage](#document-act-13400783) [Marie Louise baptism](#document-act-5627045)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 1 October 1849 — LAFRANCE act 13400783](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400783)** — His marriage to Angele Preville at Berthierville. The act names his parents, Charles Gendreau and Josephte Charron, tying the groom to the 1828 baptism. His daughter Marie Louise’s [baptism on 8 July 1851](#document-act-5627045) names Charles Gendrault and **Josephte Charron dit Ducharme** as godparents. Although their relationship to the child is not stated, the combined surname provides a documented connection between Charron in the marriage and Ducharme in the 1828 baptism.
- **[Baptism, 10 June 1828 — LAFRANCE act 3303259](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3303259)** — His baptism at Berthierville: born the previous day, son of Charles Gendro and **Josephte Ducharme**. The old summary’s Charron wording was incorrect for this act; the 1851 baptism records Charron dit Ducharme for a godmother in the adult family.
