## Biography

Charles Gendreau was born on 9 June 1828 and baptized the next day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Charles Gendreau and Josephte Charron. He married Angele Preville on 1 October 1849 at Berthierville, where the act names him as the adult son of Charles Gendreau and Josephte Charron. Their daughter Marie Louise was born and baptized on 8 July 1851 at Berthierville. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 8 July 1851. His surname is spelled Gendro in the baptism, Gendrau in the marriage and Gendrault in his daughter's baptism.
