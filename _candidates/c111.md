## Biography

- Charles Gendron was baptized on 21 February 1823 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Toussaint Gendron and Claire Rolin. [Baptism](#document-act-2369974)
- A Charles Gendron of his age heads a household at St-Clément in 1851 with a wife Angèle, but that man's 1845 marriage names his parents as Charles Gendron and Catherine Hains — a different family. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
