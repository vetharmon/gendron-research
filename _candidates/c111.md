## Biography

Charles Gendron was baptized on 21 February 1823 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Toussaint Gendron and Claire Rolin. No later record identified. A Charles Gendron of his age heads a household at St-Clément in 1851 with a wife Angèle, but that man's 1845 marriage names his parents as Charles Gendron and Catherine Hains — a different family.

## Documents

- **[Baptism, 21 February 1823 — LAFRANCE act 2369974](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2369974)** — His baptism at Beauharnois, the only record attached to him in his file.

## Further record check

The Charles Gendron buried on 22 February 1823 was six months old and the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. His burial and C111’s baptism appear on facing pages of the same register image. They are different children.
