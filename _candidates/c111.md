## Compton comparison — original 1891 census, 26 November 2026

The original [1891 census, page 7, family 29](#document-census-1891-paquetteville-comparison), records **Charles Gendreau, 56**, married and a farmer, with **Marie Clara, 51**, explicitly his wife, and six children. Charles and the children are recorded as born in Québec; Marie Clara’s birthplace is **États-Unis**. Both of Charles’s parental birthplace columns carry Québec, but the parents are not named. The [division cover](https://data2.collectionscanada.gc.ca/1891/jpg/30953_148194-00545.jpg) identifies **Compton district 149, Hereford Municipality and Paquetteville Village, J3**; the individual page writes “149 Paquetteville.”

The couple and nearby Gendreau households support comparison with the previously traced Ely–Compton family. George, 30, and Laurent, 26, are in separate family 30; Edmond, 32, wife Celanire, 22, and daughter Celanire, 3, are in separate family 31. Their adjacency alone does not state their relationship to Charles.

The senior Charles’s age of **56** agrees with the previously recorded **46 in 1881** and the competing **June 1834 birth**. C111, baptized in February 1823, would have been **68**. This is further evidence for the younger comparison family, not a new later-life record of C111. The conflicting 1861 age remains part of the record, and the census supplies no parent-name bridge.

### Earlier burial research reconciled

The research log dated **19 November 2026** already records an original reading of **Saint-Venant-de-Hereford, 1914 S5**, Ancestry Drouin image **8/10, d13p_3120997**: Charles Gendreau, widower of Clara Perreault, died 9 October and was buried 12 October, aged **80 years and three months**. That is **earlier recorded work**; the image was **not reopened in this pass**. The later search-log statements that no such burial had been located conflict with that recorded reading and must not be used as the current research conclusion. The new 1891 census independently supports the younger age pattern.

The principal route for C111 remains his own family: **Claire Rollin’s remarried household and Zoé Gendron’s Louis Ranger family**. The reported Port Henry marriage remains a possible parent-naming test of the Compton comparison. **Needs work remains unchanged.**
