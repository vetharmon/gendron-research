## Biography

Pierre Gendron was baptized on 17 December 1830 at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Pierre Gendron and Louise Fontaine. No later record has been identified as his; he is not in the 1851 census index, and census searches in Canada (1861, 1871) and the United States (1850–1870) found no record that can be connected to him. A Peter Jendro who farmed in Barry County, Michigan, from 1859 until his death in 1907 is an unconfirmed lead — no record names his parents.
