## Biography

- Pierre Gendron was baptized on 17 December 1830 at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Pierre Gendron and Louise Fontaine. [Baptism](#document-act-4345489)
- A Peter Jendro who farmed in Barry County, Michigan, from 1859 until his death in 1907 is an unconfirmed lead — no record names his parents. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Research status

No later record has been identified as his; he is not in the 1851 census index, and census searches in Canada (1861, 1871) and the United States (1850–1870) found no record that can be connected to him. [Search log](#search-log)

Burial indexes 4352384 and 4352393 concern Narcisse (11 months, died 2/buried 4 December 1837) and Charles (4, died 10/buried 11 December 1837), not C281. That American identity is unproved here: FS tree G8YR-V6W gives birth 8 May 1828/death 11 March 1900, and its attached Rhode Island death index QG1X-DY9M names father Pierre Gendron and mother Lizotte, age 71. Four indexed child-death entries independently pair Joseph with Émelie/Emilia Bessette, but do not establish his Québec parents or a link to C281. Do not use the American family as a confirmed sibling bridge. That review did not establish a later identity for Pierre. The subsequent Peter Jendro lead remains an unconfirmed possible elimination, as qualified in the current assessment above.
