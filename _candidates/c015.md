## Biography

Hubert Gendreau was baptized on 2 October 1816 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Pierre Gendreau and M. Catherine Gamache. No later record identified. A Hubert Gendron heading a household at St-Casimir in 1861 is a namesake — his marriage names parents Louis Gendron and Marguerite Gervais of Ste-Anne.

## Documents

- **[Baptism, 2 October 1816 — LAFRANCE act 2870183](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2870183)** — His baptism at Montmagny (St-Thomas) — the only record so far connected to him.
- **[FamilySearch index record](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:X4Q4-7BSM?lang=en)** — The FamilySearch index entry for the same 2 October 1816 baptism, naming his parents as Pierre Gendreau and M. Catherine Gamache; a signed-in search on those parent names returned this baptism and nothing later.
- **[FamilySearch tree profile PFRY-D1R](https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/PFRY-D1R)** — His FamilySearch tree profile, which carries a single source and showed no dated death in the inspected preview.

## Family follow-up

A new family lead points toward Madawaska. Édouard Gendreau married Éléonore Bélanger at Saint-Basile on 8 January 1849. The original names his deceased parents as Pierre Gendreau and Marie Gamache and places their family in the parish of Cap, Lower Canada. Édouard resided at Saint-Basile. The parental names and a compiled family tree suggest that he was Hubert’s brother, but Hubert is not named in the marriage. This is a lead for research in Madawaska and Aroostook County, not evidence of Hubert’s own whereabouts.

## Census follow-up

The [1860 Aroostook census transcription](https://untreaty.upperstjohn.com/1860/T17R9-3.htm) lists Dubert Gendreau, age 12, born in Maine, on page 489, line 28. He cannot be Hubert born in 1816. The original image has not been inspected.

The [St-François parish census transcription](https://www.upperstjohn.com/1858/stfrancois58.htm), catalogued as 1858 but probably taken in December 1857, lists Gendreau households under Paschal, André, Euger and Zadé. It does not name every household member, so it cannot establish whether Hubert was present.

## Reported death

Lang’s tree identifies Hubert with Pierre Gendreau and Marie Catherine Gamache and reports death before 9 July 1866 at St Francis, Maine. Its single displayed citation is Ancestry Family Trees, without a death act. Other ranked trees repeat the same limit but give varying birth dates. The reported death is a lead, not evidence sufficient to eliminate C015. Needs work unchanged.
