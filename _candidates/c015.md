## Biography

Hubert Gendreau was baptized on 2 October 1816 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Pierre Gendreau and M. Catherine Gamache. No later record identified. A Hubert Gendron heading a household at St-Casimir in 1861 is a namesake — his marriage names parents Louis Gendron and Marguerite Gervais of Ste-Anne.
