## Family follow-up

A new family lead points toward Madawaska. Édouard Gendreau married Éléonore Bélanger at Saint-Basile on 8 January 1849. The original names his deceased parents as Pierre Gendreau and Marie Gamache and places their family in the parish of Cap, Lower Canada. Édouard resided at Saint-Basile. The parental names and a compiled family tree suggest that he was Hubert’s brother, but Hubert is not named in the marriage. This is a lead for research in Madawaska and Aroostook County, not evidence of Hubert’s own whereabouts.

## Census follow-up

The [1860 Aroostook census transcription](https://untreaty.upperstjohn.com/1860/T17R9-3.htm) lists Dubert Gendreau, age 12, born in Maine, on page 489, line 28. This entry concerns a child born about 1848 and does not identify Hubert baptized at Montmagny in 1816.

The [St-François parish census transcription](https://www.upperstjohn.com/1858/stfrancois58.htm), catalogued as 1858 but probably taken in December 1857, lists Gendreau households under Paschal, André, Euger and Zadé. It does not name every household member, so it cannot establish whether Hubert was present.

## Reported death

Lang’s tree identifies Hubert with Pierre Gendreau and Marie Catherine Gamache and reports death before 9 July 1866 at St Francis, Maine. Its single displayed citation is Ancestry Family Trees, without a death act. Other ranked trees repeat the same limit but give varying birth dates. The reported death is a lead, not evidence sufficient to eliminate C015. Needs work unchanged. ## Maternal-family record located — 21 November 2026

A [certified manuscript copy of the Saint-Basile register](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c3017/792) records **Marie Gamache’s burial on 21 August 1829**, S19, manuscript page 865. It gives her death as **26 July 1829**, age **45**, and her husband as **Pierre Jeandrault**, a *cultivateur* of the parish. The witnesses are Michel Cyr, François Martin and Laurent Terriault, without stated relationships. Two readers independently checked the entry.

The [certificate dated 9 November 1906](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c3017/342) identifies this volume as a supervised copy of the official registers. The entry supports a parental-family comparison with the Pierre Gendreau/Marie Gamache pair in Édouard’s already-read 1849 marriage. The copy gives **26 July** as the death date and **21 August** as the burial date. The copy says **Marie**, without Catherine, and does not name Hubert or establish that he lived at Saint-Basile.

Hubert’s reported death **before 9 July 1866** still requires its underlying record. The maternal burial and Édouard’s already-read marriage do not establish Hubert’s death or whereabouts. **Needs work remains unchanged.**
