## Biography

Joseph Alfred Gendron was born on 23 January 1829 and baptized the next day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, the son of Francois Gendron and Adelaide Vallée. On 3 March 1851 he married Anastasie Caille/Cadier at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, the act naming him as the minor son of Francois Gendron and Adelaide Vallée. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 3 March 1851.
