## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4566559
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 24 January 1829 at Ste-Anne-de-la-Perade names Joseph Alfred, born the previous day, son of Francois Gendron and Adelaide Valle. Original marriage 3 March 1851 there identifies Joseph Gendron, minor son of Francois Gendron and Adelaide Vallee, marrying Anastasie Caille/Cadier. Both pages of the marriage are saved. Matching parents and the unmarried form of this later marriage support exclusion from Franklin, already married with an infant in Missouri in 1850.
