## Biography

- Joseph Alfred Gendron was born on 23 January 1829 and baptized the next day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, the son of Francois Gendron and Adelaide Vallée. [Baptism](#document-act-4566559)
- On 3 March 1851 he married Anastasie Caille/Cadier at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, the act naming him as the minor son of Francois Gendron and Adelaide Vallée. [Marriage, 3 March 1851](#document-act-13400980)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 3 March 1851 — LAFRANCE act 13400980](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400980)** — His marriage to Anastasie Caille/Cadier at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (both pages of the act are saved). It names his parents, Francois Gendron and Adelaide Vallée, tying the groom to the 1829 baptism in the same parish.
- **[Baptism, 24 January 1829 — LAFRANCE act 4566559](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4566559)** — His baptism at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade: born the previous day, son of Francois Gendron and Adelaide Valle.
