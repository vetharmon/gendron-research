## Biography

François Hippolyte Gendron was baptized on 4 September 1818 at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier), the son of Joseph Gendron and Magdeleine Lehoullier. He died a small child and was buried at Batiscan on 5 April 1821, aged about two and a half.

- **[Baptism, 4 September 1818 — LAFRANCE act 2369133](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2369133)** — His baptism at Batiscan (right page), naming his parents, Joseph Gendron and Magdeleine Lehoullier.
