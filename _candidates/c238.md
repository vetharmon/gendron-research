## Biography

Anselme Gendreau was baptized on 13 September 1828 at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Francois Gendreau and Genevieve Bocage. Two adult identifications have been investigated. An Anselme who married Arthemise Dumas at Bic in 1861, appears in the 1881 census and was buried in 1911 is identified in another compiled tree as a different man — born 1 November 1833 at Montmagny, son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne. No matching Canadian census entry was identified in the recorded searches. The American lead is a probable identification: Samuel Jandron married Matilda Poyer at Burlington, Vermont, on 14 April 1849, and was at Plattsburgh, New York, for the censuses of 1850 (age 21, born Canada, with a Francis Gendron, 55, in the household), 1860 and 1870, and was buried there on 11 September 1879. The identification rests on that convergence — the Jandron spelling, the Anselme/Samuel pairing, the ages, and the Berthierville–Burlington–Plattsburgh corridor — and no record in the trail names his parents.
