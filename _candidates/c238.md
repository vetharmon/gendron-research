## Biography

- Anselme Gendreau was baptized on 13 September 1828 at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Francois Gendreau and Genevieve Bocage. [Baptism](#document-act-3303335)

- A probable American identification is Samuel Jandron, who married Matilda Poyer at Burlington, Vermont, on 14 April 1849. The 1850 Plattsburgh, New York census records him aged 21, born Canada, with Francis Gendron, 55, in the household; the existing research notes follow him in the 1860 and 1870 Plattsburgh censuses. He was buried there on 11 September 1879. The identification rests on the Jandron spelling, Anselme/Samuel name pairing, ages and Berthierville–Burlington–Plattsburgh connection; no record in this trail names his parents. ## Research status

Two adult identifications have been investigated. An Anselme who married Arthemise Dumas at Bic in 1861, appears in the 1881 census and was buried in 1911 is identified in another compiled tree as a different man — born 1 November 1833 at Montmagny, son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne. No matching Canadian census entry was identified in the recorded searches.

## Follow-up, 16 November 2026

The Vermont Genealogy Library church-marriage index returned **MarriageID 1977: Samuel Jandron and Matilda Poyer, Burlington, Cathedral**. Search the two surnames together. This gives a precise church-repertoire entry to pursue beyond the civil card.

The public index gives no date, parents or witnesses. The complete entry has not been obtained, so it does not yet connect Samuel to François Gendreau and Geneviève Bocage. No order or request was sent. **Likely unchanged.**
