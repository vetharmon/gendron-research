## Biography

Anselme Gendreau was baptized on 13 September 1828 at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Francois Gendreau and Genevieve Bocage. No later record has been connected to him in this file: the search behind it established no qualifying pre-1850 burial, and a family follow-up found no adult endpoint for him. Two later leads were examined and not adopted. An Anselme who married Arthemise Dumas at Bic in 1861, appears in the 1881 census and was buried in 1911 is identified in another compiled tree as a different man — born 1 November 1833 at Montmagny, son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne. A separate American tree lead under the name Anselme (Samuel) Jandro remains unverified. None of these men is merged with this child on name alone.
