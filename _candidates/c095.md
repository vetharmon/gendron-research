## Biography

Flavien Gendreau was born on 29 January 1822 and baptized the same day at Pointe-du-Lac (La-Visitation), the son of Francois Gendreau and Josephine Fafar. He may be the Fabien Gendreau who died on 6 January 1842, aged nineteen, and was buried at Pointe-du-Lac on 9 January 1842: that burial names parents Francois Gendreau and Josephte Fafard, and the age fits his 1822 birth. The father's name matches, and the mother's surname is the same in a variant spelling — but her given name differs (Josephine Fafar in the baptism, Josephte Fafard in the burial), and the burial gives the son's name as Fabien rather than Flavien. No record has been found that bridges either difference, so it is not certain the buried man and the baptized child are the same person.
