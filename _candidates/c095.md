## Biography

- Flavien Gendreau was born and baptized on 29 January 1822 at Pointe-du-Lac (La-Visitation), the son of François Gendreau and Josephine Fafar. [Baptism](#document-act-2567925)

- Fabien Gendreau also witnessed Joseph's burial on 2 December 1841, placing that name with the same family shortly before the January 1842 burial. The witness entry does not state his relationship or age; it corroborates the identification rather than establishing it alone. [Joseph Gendreau's burial](#document-act-4544333)

- He is identified with Fabien Gendreau, who died on 6 January 1842 and was buried in the same parish on 9 January, aged nineteen. The burial names François Gendreau and Josephte Fafard as his parents. [Burial](#document-act-4544337)

- The mother's given-name variation is independently documented in his brother [Joseph's records (C063)](/gendron-research/candidates/c063/): Josephine Fafar at baptism in 1820 and Josephte Fafare at burial in 1841. [Comparison baptism — Joseph](#document-act-2567835) [Joseph Gendreau's burial](#document-act-4544333)

## Research status

**Solid elimination — died before Franklin's documented Missouri family.** The burial age fits exactly in completed years: Flavien would have been nineteen, twenty-three days short of his twentieth birthday. The father, mother's surname and parish agree. Taken together, the original records support the identification despite Flavien/Fabien. No separate Fabien of these parents is documented in the reviewed project records; the surviving family-card transcription is incomplete, so this is not a claim of an exhaustive sibling reconstruction. The conclusion rests on the combined record evidence, not on requiring an explicit alias statement or accepting a compiled linkage alone.

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 9 January 1842 — LAFRANCE act 4544337](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4544337)** — His burial at Pointe-du-Lac (entry S1, lower left): Fabien, son of Francois Gendreau and Josephte Fafard, died three days earlier, on 6 January 1842, aged nineteen. The father's name and the mother's surname match Flavien's parents, and the age fits his birth. The record uses Josephte and Fabien; the identification and name differences are addressed above.
- **[Baptism, 29 January 1822 — LAFRANCE act 2567925](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2567925)** — His baptism at Pointe-du-Lac (entry B3, right page): born that day, son of Francois Gendreau and Josephine Fafar; his godparents were Francois Martin and Julie Gauthier.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 2551540](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/2551540)** — PRDH's compiled record for the same child.

- **[Joseph Gendreau's burial, 2 December 1841 — LAFRANCE act 4544333](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4544333)** — Entry S41, right page, re-read from the saved original on 15 November 2026: Joseph, aged twenty-one, son of François Gendreau and Josephte Fafare. Witnesses Germain Landry and Fabien Gendreau did not sign. Joseph's [1820 baptism, act 2567835](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2567835) names Josephine Fafar. These records support the maternal-name variation and the family association of Fabien; they do not explicitly call him Flavien.
