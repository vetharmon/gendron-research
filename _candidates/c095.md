## Biography

Flavien Gendreau was born and baptized on 29 January 1822 at Pointe-du-Lac (La-Visitation), the son of François Gendreau and Josephine Fafar. He is identified with Fabien Gendreau, who died on 6 January 1842 and was buried in the same parish on 9 January, aged nineteen. The burial names François Gendreau and Josephte Fafard as his parents.

**Solid elimination — died before Franklin's documented Missouri family.** The burial age fits exactly in completed years: Flavien would have been nineteen, twenty-three days short of his twentieth birthday. The father, mother's surname and parish agree. The mother's given-name variation is independently documented in his brother [Joseph's records (C063)](/gendron-research/candidates/c063/): Josephine Fafar at baptism in 1820 and Josephte Fafare at burial in 1841.

Fabien Gendreau also witnessed Joseph's burial on 2 December 1841, placing that name with the same family shortly before the January 1842 burial. The witness entry does not state his relationship or age; it corroborates the identification rather than establishing it alone.

Taken together, the original records support the identification despite Flavien/Fabien. No separate Fabien of these parents is documented in the reviewed project records; the surviving family-card transcription is incomplete, so this is not a claim of an exhaustive sibling reconstruction. The conclusion rests on the combined record evidence, not on requiring an explicit alias statement or accepting a compiled linkage alone.
