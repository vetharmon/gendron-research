## Biography

Flavien Gendreau was born on 29 January 1822 and baptized the same day at Pointe-du-Lac (La-Visitation), the son of Francois Gendreau and Josephine Fafar. A probable match is Fabien Gendreau, who died on 6 January 1842 and was buried at Pointe-du-Lac on 9 January, aged nineteen. The burial names Francois Gendreau and Josephte Fafard as his parents. The age and surname fit; the given names differ: Flavien/Fabien and Josephine/Josephte.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 9 January 1842 — LAFRANCE act 4544337](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4544337)** — The burial lead at Pointe-du-Lac (entry S1, middle right): Fabien, son of Francois Gendreau and Josephte Fafard, died three days earlier, on 6 January 1842, aged nineteen. The father's name and the mother's surname match Flavien's parents, and the age fits his birth — but the mother's given name is Josephte, not Josephine, and the son's name is Fabien, not Flavien.
- **[Baptism, 29 January 1822 — LAFRANCE act 2567925](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2567925)** — His baptism at Pointe-du-Lac (entry B3, left page): born that day, son of Francois Gendreau and Josephine Fafar; his godparents were Francois Martin and Julie Gauthier.
