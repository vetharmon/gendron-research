## Biography

Michel Gendron was born on 29 September 1830 and baptized the next day at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marguerite. He died on 25 April 1833, aged two and a half years, and was buried at St-Pie on 27 April 1833.
