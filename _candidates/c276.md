## Biography

- Michel Gendron was born on 29 September 1830 and baptized the next day at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marguerite. [Baptism](#document-act-4371834)
- He died on 25 April 1833, aged two and a half years, and was buried at St-Pie on 27 April 1833. [Burial, 27 April 1833](#document-act-4245066)

- **[Burial, 27 April 1833 — LAFRANCE act 4245066](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4245066)** — His burial at St-Pie (entry S44, middle right): died two days earlier, aged two and a half years, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marguerite — the same parents as his baptism.
