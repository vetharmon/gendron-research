## Biography

- Toussaint Gendron was born and baptized on 21 September 1828 at Beauharnois (St-Clément). The complete civil-register act directly names his parents as Toussaint Gendron, farmer, and Claire Rollin. The FamilySearch opening agrees; the earlier candidate index’s Nolin is not the reading of these originals. [Baptism](#document-act-4253870) [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Research status

A compiled result reports a death at Châteauguay on 30 October 1828, but no matching burial has been located — the Châteauguay register for that window was read, and the only boy buried there on 1 November is Pierre Lefebvre's son. The Beauharnois transcription for 28 October 1828 records an anonymous child of Léon Gendron and Catherine Cadot, a different family. His parents gave a younger son the same name: Toussaint, born 27 March 1835 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (godparents François Xavier Gendron and Josephine Laberge). The repeated name suggests an earlier death, but both children could have survived; it does not establish the older child’s death. The Toussaint buried at Beauharnois on 20 May 1836, at fourteen months, is the younger brother. He appears in neither the 1851 nor the 1861 census index.
