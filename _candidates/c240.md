## Biography

Toussaint Gendron was born and baptized on 21 September 1828 at Beauharnois (St-Clément). The Public Archives typed transcription names his parents as Toussaint Gendron and Claire Rollin; the earlier candidate index gave Nolin. A compiled result reports a death at Châteauguay on 30 October 1828, but no matching burial has been located. The Beauharnois transcription for 28 October names an anonymous child of Léon Gendron and Catherine Cadot, a different family. The Toussaint buried at Beauharnois on 20 May 1836 was fourteen months old and belongs to the later birth, not this 1828 child.
