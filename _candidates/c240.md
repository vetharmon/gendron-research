## Biography

Toussaint Gendron was baptized on 21 September 1828 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Toussaint Gendron and Claire Nolin. A compiled Généalogie Québec result gives his death as 30 October 1828 at Châteauguay, but that claim has not been verified against a burial act: targeted readings of the late-October registers found no matching burial — the Beauharnois spread for late October holds an anonymous child of Charles Gendron and Catherine Hebert, not Toussaint, and the Châteauguay pages for 23–31 October show no burial for him — and neither reading was a complete register-wide search. A later Toussaint born to this family in 1835 does not by itself settle what happened to this child.
