## Biography

- Paul Gendron was born on 5 August 1829 and baptized the next day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Francois Gendron and Josephte Masson. [Baptism](#document-act-3303524)
- On 17 January 1848 he married Adelaide Robillard at Ste-Élisabeth, the act naming him as the minor son of Francois Gendron and Josephte Masson. [Marriage, 17 January 1848](#document-act-13401049)
- On 3 May 1850 a son, Paul Alexis, born the previous day to Paul Gendron and Adelaide Robillard, was baptized at Ste-Élisabeth, with the father stated to be present. [Paul Alexis’s baptism](#document-act-5454834)
## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 17 January 1848 — LAFRANCE act 13401049](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401049)** — His marriage to Adelaide Robillard at Ste-Élisabeth (both pages of the act are saved). It names his parents, tying the groom to the 1829 baptism. His son's baptism belongs with this record: on 3 May 1850, at Ste-Élisabeth, Paul Alexis, born the previous day, was baptized as the son of Paul Gendron and Adelaide Robillard, the father stated present (LAFRANCE act 5454834).
- **[Baptism, 6 August 1829 — LAFRANCE act 3303524](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3303524)** — His baptism at Berthierville: born the previous day, son of Francois Gendron and Josephte Masson.
