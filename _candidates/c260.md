## Biography

Paul Gendron was born on 5 August 1829 and baptized the next day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Francois Gendron and Josephte Masson. On 17 January 1848 he married Adelaide Robillard at Ste-Élisabeth, the act naming him as the minor son of Francois Gendron and Josephte Masson. On 3 May 1850 a son, Paul Alexis, born the previous day to Paul Gendron and Adelaide Robillard, was baptized at Ste-Élisabeth, with the father stated to be present. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 3 May 1850.
