## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 3303524
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 6 August 1829 at Berthierville names Paul, born the previous day, son of Francois Gendron and Josephte Masson. Original marriage 17 January 1848 at Ste-Elisabeth identifies Paul Gendron, minor son of Francois Gendron and Josephte Masson, marrying Adelaide Robillard; both pages are saved. Original baptism 3 May 1850 there names Paul Alexis, born the previous day, son of Paul Gendron and Adelaide Robillard, with the father stated present. This matched parental and marital chain supports a separate continuing Quebec family from Franklin and Phebe’s Missouri household.
