## Biography

- Pierre Samuel Gendron was born and baptized on 31 August 1828 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Simon Gendron and Marie Louise Yon. [Baptism](#document-act-2996067)

- He married Louise Fournier on 13 May 1850 at St-Simon, where the act names him — as Samuel Gendron — the adult son of Simon Gendron and the deceased Marie Louise. Her surname in the faded marriage appears to be Yon but remains uncertain; the prior summary read Dion. [Marriage](#document-act-13497830) [Baptism](#document-act-2996067)
