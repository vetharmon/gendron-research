## Biography

Pierre Samuel Gendron was born and baptized on 31 August 1828 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Simon Gendron and Marie Louise Yon. He married Louise Fournier on 13 May 1850 at St-Simon, where the act names him — as Samuel Gendron — the adult son of Simon Gendron and Louise Dion; Yon and Dion are surname variants for his mother. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 13 May 1850.
