## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2996067
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 31 August 1828 at St-Hyacinthe names Pierre Samuel, born that day, son of Simon Gendron and Marie Louise Yon. Original marriage 13 May 1850 at St-Simon names Samuel Gendron, adult son of Simon Gendron and Louise Dion, marrying Louise Fournier. Yon/Dion are disclosed surname variants; the marriage image is faded and its continuation is saved. Matching paternal and maternal identities and the unmarried form of this marriage support exclusion from Franklin, already married with an infant in Missouri in 1850.
