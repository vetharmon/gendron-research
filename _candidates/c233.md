## Biography

- Pierre Samuel Gendron was born and baptized on 31 August 1828 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Simon Gendron and Marie Louise Yon. [Baptism](#document-act-2996067)

- He married Louise Fournier on 13 May 1850 at St-Simon, where the act names him as Pierre Samuel Gendron, adult son of Simon Gendron and the deceased Marie Louise. Her surname in the faded marriage appears to be Yon but remains uncertain; the prior summary read Dion. [Marriage](#document-act-13497830) [Baptism](#document-act-2996067)

- **Sainte-Rosalie, 1861 and 1871:** The original census records identify his household as P. S. Gendron, notary, with Louise Fournier in 1861, and Pierre Gendron, notary, with Louise in 1871. Four younger Gendrons remain in the same order with coherent ages, and Flavien Dupont appears in both households. This correlation extends his documented Québec life through 1871. ## Census identity — 28 November 2026

The **1871 Sainte-Rosalie Pierre 42 / Louise 44 household**, formerly an unassigned lead for other Pierres, is identified as this candidate. The **1861** original gives **P. S. Gendron, notary, 33**, **Louise Fournier, 34**, and Joseph, Saül, Célina and Adéline aged 10, nine, seven and five. In **1871**, Pierre is 42 and a notary; the same four younger Gendrons appear in order aged 19, 18, 16 and 14, followed by Aimé, seven, and Régina, six. **Flavien Dupont**, 14 in 1861, is still in the group in 1871, aged 24 and a law student. The latter family is numbered **125**, replacing a crossed-out **126**, in dwelling **102**. [1861 census](#document-census-1861-ste-rosalie-pierre-samuel-gendron) · [1871 census](#document-census-1871-ste-rosalie-pierre-samuel-gendron)

The 1850 marriage connects **Pierre Samuel Gendron** and **Louise Fournier** to the parents named in his 1828 baptism. The 1861 preceding declaration also names enumerator **Pierre Samuel Gendron** and bears **P. S. Gendron** signatures. These combined records support the identity; neither census expressly labels the individual family relationships. The 1861 ages are recorded for the **next birthday**, consistent with his 31 August 1828 birth. His grade remains **solidly eliminated**.
