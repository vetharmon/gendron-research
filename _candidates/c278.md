## Biography

Pierre Gendreau was born on 4 November 1830 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Abraham Gendreau and Marie Pelerin.

## Research lead

On **25 February 1862**, **Thomas Gendreau**, a schoolteacher living at St-Thomas, married **Rosalie Proulx** at St-Pierre-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud. The original marriage calls him the adult son of **Abraham Gendreau and the late Marie Pellerin**. His father Abraham attended. This is an adult record with the same parental names as Pierre's baptism.

The FamilySearch tree identifies Thomas as the child baptized Pierre on 4 November 1830 and cites census records for him. The next checks are the original 1851/52 and 1901 census entries and the couple's other children's baptisms, to establish whether Pierre and Thomas were one son or two. A NosOrigines entry also says he was baptized Pierre, but supplies a conflicting 1839 birth date. Pierre remains a possible candidate while that identity is checked.
