## Biography

- Pierre Gendreau was born and baptized on 4 November 1830 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Abraham Gendreau and Marie Pelerin. [Baptism](#document-act-4080568)

## Assessment

**Solid elimination — identification by combined family evidence.** Pierre is identified with Thomas Gendreau, who lived with Abraham Gendreau and Marie Pellerin in 1851–1852 and named those parents in his 1862 marriage. The sibling reconstruction resolves the question of whether Thomas could have been another recorded son.

The 1800–1850 LAFRANCE couple search, using Abraham Gendron and Pellerin with surname resemblance enabled and no maternal given-name restriction, returned twelve baptisms and an unnamed infant burial. Each baptism detail was checked individually at index level. The six named sons are:

| Son | Baptism | Distinguishing evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Jean Baptiste | 6 December 1813, act 2869826 | Original burial: died 4 February 1827, buried 6 February, aged fourteen; act 4083804. |
| Édouard | 16 March 1823, act 2870966 | Enumerated separately from Thomas in the 1851–1852 household. |
| François Ferdinand | 2 July 1825, act 4079847 | Original burial: died 22 November 1825, buried 25 November, aged five months; act 4083727. |
| François Eustache | 20 September 1828, act 4080280 | Original burial: died 29 July 1849, buried 31 July; act 4085529. |
| Pierre | 4 November 1830, act 4080568 | The remaining son compatible with the later Thomas. Original baptism re-read. |
| Herménégilde | 18 December 1837, act 4081632 | Enumerated separately from Thomas in the 1851–1852 household. |

The six daughters were Marie Marcelline (31 January 1816, act 2870077), Marie Claire (23 April 1817, 2870256), Marie Soulange (27 April 1820, 2870604), Henriette (28 October 1826, 4080026), Rosalie Angèle (22 May 1832, 4080792), and Marie Virginie (10 September 1835, 4081274). An unnamed infant was buried on 31 December 1821 (2872531). No additional son baptized Thomas appeared in this family search.

The original **1851–1852 census**, inspected on 14 November, lists **Thomas, 23**, unmarried, born at St-Thomas, with Abraham and Marie. Édouard and Herménégilde are separate entries. Thomas's **25 February 1862 marriage to Rosalie Proulx** names Abraham Gendreau and the late Marie Pellerin as his parents; Abraham attended as his father. In **1901**, Thomas is Albert Gendreau's widowed father, aged seventy.

The differing names and dates remain visible: the baptism says **Pierre, 4 November 1830**; the 1901 census says **Thomas, 1 May 1830**; the earlier census age of twenty-three at his next birthday is higher than expected for the baptism date. Eustache's burial reports twenty-two although his baptism would make him twenty years ten months. These are not exact-age matches. The identification rests on the parental couple, the independently distinguished brothers, the household, and the adult marriage together. No inspected record explicitly says “Pierre also called Thomas,” and the search cannot exclude an entirely unrecorded child. The combined evidence is accepted as sufficient to distinguish this candidate from Franklin.
