## Biography

Pierre Gendreau was born on 4 November 1830 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Abraham Gendreau and Marie Pelerin. Nothing more of his life has been connected to him: the search behind this register established no qualifying burial for him before 1850, and a later exact-parent search of the indexes returned only this baptism — a limited indexed search, not an exhaustive negative. A tree suggestion linking his baptism to a Thomas Gendreau is not an established adult identity for Pierre.
