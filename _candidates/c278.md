## Biography

Pierre Gendreau was born and baptized on 4 November 1830 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Abraham Gendreau and Marie Pelerin.

## Assessment

**Likely eliminated.** Pierre probably appears in later records as Thomas Gendreau. The parental couple, household and approximate birth year agree, but no original record yet expressly identifies Pierre and Thomas as the same person.

The original **1851–1852 census** lists **Thomas Gendreau, 23**, a day labourer born at St-Thomas, with **Abraham Gendreau and Marie Pellerin**. The household also contains Edouard, Hermenegilde, Claire, Angele and Clara. Thomas is marked unmarried and resident with the family. Edouard is the brother documented under [C115]({{ '/candidates/c115/' | relative_url }}).

On **25 February 1862**, Thomas, a **farmer living at St-Thomas**, married **Rosalie Proulx** at St-Pierre-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud. The original marriage names his parents as **Abraham Gendreau and the late Marie Pellerin**; Abraham attended as his father.

In the **1901 census**, Thomas appears as the widowed father in Albert Gendreau's household at St-Thomas, aged 70 and born **1 May 1830**. This agrees with Pierre's birth year but differs from his baptism's **4 November** date. The compiled NosOrigines birth year of 1839 is inconsistent with both census ages.

These records establish Thomas's separate life in Québec during Franklin's Missouri years. The remaining question is whether Thomas was Pierre or another son of Abraham and Marie. A complete check of the couple's children's baptisms, or an original record using both given names, is needed for a solid elimination.
