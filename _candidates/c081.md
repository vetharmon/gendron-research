## Biography

Charles Gendron was born on 25 June 1821 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Joachim Gendron and Charlotte Hemond. He died on 4 September 1821, aged two and a half months, and was buried at St-Pierre-les-Becquets on 6 September 1821; the burial names the same parents.
