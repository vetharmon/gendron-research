## Biography

Richard Auguste Gendreau was born on 28 August 1824 and baptized the same day at Cap-St-Ignace, the son of Charles Gendreau and Cecile Constance Fournier. He later appears in Iowa: an Iowa death certificate records an August Richard, born on 28 August in the same parish, to Chas Gendreau and Constance Fournier, who died on 7 June 1907 and was buried on 9 June 1907. The certificate gives his birth year as 1825, one year later than the baptism; the shared compound name, the exact month and day, the parental pair and the parish of origin are what tie the two records together.
