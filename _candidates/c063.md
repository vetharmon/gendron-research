## Biography

Joseph Gendreau was born on 30 March 1820 and baptized the next day at Pointe-du-Lac (La-Visitation), the son of Francois Gendreau and Josephine Fafard. He died on 30 November 1841 and was buried at Pointe-du-Lac on 2 December 1841, aged twenty-one; the burial names the same parents.
