## Biography

- Ambroise Gendron was born on 6 September 1827 and baptized the same day at Beauport, the son of Ambroise Gendron and Julie Laroche. [Baptism](#document-act-2662402)

- He married Esther Chamberland on 12 July 1852 at Beauport, the act describing him as the adult son of Ambroise Gendron and Julie Laroche. [Marriage](#document-act-13400856)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}
