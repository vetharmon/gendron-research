## Biography

Ambroise Gendron was born on 6 September 1827 and baptized the same day at Beauport, the son of Ambroise Gendron and Julie Laroche. He married Esther Chamberland on 12 July 1852 at Beauport, the act describing him as the unmarried adult son of Ambroise Gendron and Julie Laroche. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 12 July 1852.
