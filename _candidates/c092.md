## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2627758
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Three originals downloaded and manually read 9 November. Baptism B170 right middle, 21 October 1821 Chateauguay: Antoine born previous day, son of Joachim Gendron and Felicite Hebert. Marriage M7 upper left, 5 February 1850 Beauharnois: Antoine Gendron, adult son of Joachim Gendron and Felicite Hebert of Chateauguay, to Adelaide Hebert, adult daughter of Francois Hebert and deceased Francoise Couillard. The father Joachim is present as a marriage witness. Original child baptism B32 right lower/middle, 28 June 1860 Chateauguay: Joseph Ferdinand born same day, son of Antoine Gendron, journalier, and Adelaide Hebert of that parish. The act says the witnesses and father could not sign; no signature is attributed to Antoine. Exact parental pair links the baptism to the marriage, and the same marital pair continues in 1860, establishing a separate family identity incompatible with Franklin's documented Missouri family. PRDH profile 916759 and family 265630 corroborate the chain; the exclusion is based on the original acts.
