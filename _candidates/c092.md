## Biography

Antoine Gendron was born on 20 October 1821 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Joachim Gendron and Felicite Hebert. He married Adelaide Hebert on 5 February 1850 at Beauharnois; the act names him as the adult son of Joachim Gendron and Felicite Hebert of Châteauguay, and his father was present as a witness. His son Joseph Ferdinand was born and baptized on 28 June 1860 at Châteauguay, naming Antoine, a day labourer (journalier), and Adelaide Hebert as his parents. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 28 June 1860.
