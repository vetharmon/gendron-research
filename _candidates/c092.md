## Biography

- Antoine Gendron was born on 20 October 1821 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Joachim Gendron and Felicite Hebert. [Baptism](#document-act-2627758)

- He married Adelaide Hebert on 5 February 1850 at Beauharnois; the act names him as the adult son of Joachim Gendron and Felicite Hebert of Châteauguay, and his father was present as a witness. [Marriage](#document-act-13400862)

- His son Joseph Ferdinand was born and baptized on 28 June 1860 at Châteauguay, naming Antoine, a day labourer (journalier), and Adelaide Hebert as his parents. [Baptism of his son](#document-act-5507292)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 5 February 1850 — LAFRANCE act 13400862](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400862)** — His marriage to Adelaide Hebert at Beauharnois (entry M7, upper left). The act names him as the adult son of Joachim Gendron and Felicite Hebert of Châteauguay — the same parents as his baptism — and his father stood as a witness. The bride was the adult daughter of Francois Hebert and the late Francoise Couillard. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400862).
- **[Baptism of his son, 28 June 1860 — LAFRANCE act 5507292](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5507292)** — Baptism of Joseph Ferdinand at Châteauguay (entry B32): born that day, son of Antoine Gendron, a day labourer, and Adelaide Hebert of that parish. The act notes that the father could not sign.

Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/2627758).
