Original baptism 15 February 1825 Ste-Anne-de-la-Perade, act 4566129, d1p_25630828.jpg B8 upper right: Joseph Adolphe born previous day, son of Pierre Gendron and Angele Langlois. Original marriage 16 October 1849 St-Casimir, act 13400850, d1p_1648a0042.jpg M9 bottom left continuing top right on the same spread: Adolphe Gendron, adult son of Pierre Gendron and Angele Langlois, marries Celanire Garneau, daughter of Francois Garneau and Angele Perrault. Original baptism 20 November 1860 St-Casimir, act 5563305, d1p_1648a0317.jpg B77 upper right: Marie Emma Phelonie born that day, legitimate daughter of Adolphe Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie Celanire Garneau. The act includes the father among those who could not sign. Matching both parents bridges Joseph Adolphe to Adolphe; the same named marital couple continues as a distinct Quebec family into 1860, inconsistent with Franklin's documented Missouri family. The first given name Joseph is omitted in the adult acts, not treated as a contradictory identity.
