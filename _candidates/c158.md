## Biography

Joseph Adolphe Gendron was born on 14 February 1825 and baptized the next day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, the son of Pierre Gendron and Angèle Langlois. As Adolphe Gendron — the adult acts drop his first given name — he married Célanire Garneau on 16 October 1849 at St-Casimir; the marriage act names him as the adult son of Pierre Gendron and Angèle Langlois, which is what ties the groom to the 1825 baptism. His daughter Marie Emma Phélonie was born and baptized on 20 November 1860 at St-Casimir, the child of Adolphe Gendron, a cultivateur, and Marie Célanire Garneau; the father was among those who could not sign the act. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 20 November 1860.
