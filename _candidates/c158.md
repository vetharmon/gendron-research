## Biography

- Joseph Adolphe Gendron was born on 14 February 1825 and baptized the next day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, the son of Pierre Gendron and Angèle Langlois. [Baptism](#document-act-4566129)
- As Adolphe Gendron — the adult acts drop his first given name — he married Célanire Garneau on 16 October 1849 at St-Casimir; the marriage act names him as the adult son of Pierre Gendron and Angèle Langlois, which is what ties the groom to the 1825 baptism. [Marriage, 16 October 1849](#document-act-13400850)
- His daughter Marie Emma Phélonie was born and baptized on 20 November 1860 at St-Casimir, the child of Adolphe Gendron, a cultivateur, and Marie Célanire Garneau; the father was among those who could not sign the act. [Baptism of his daughter, 20 November 1860](#document-act-5563305)
