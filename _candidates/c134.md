## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 3 March 1824 and baptized the next day at St-François-du-Lac (St-François-Xavier), the son of Louis Gendron and Reine Bonenfant dit Abraham. He died on 5 March 1825, aged about one year, and was buried on 7 March 1825.
