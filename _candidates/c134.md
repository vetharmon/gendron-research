## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 3 March 1824 and baptized the next day at St-François-du-Lac (St-François-Xavier), the son of Louis Gendron and Reine Bonenfant dit Abraham. He died on 5 March 1825, aged about one year, and was buried on 7 March 1825.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 7 March 1825 — LAFRANCE act 4494823](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4494823)** — His burial (the archived image is from Yamaska): Louis, son of Louis Gendron and Reine Bonenfant, died two days earlier, aged about one year. The baptism gives the mother’s additional dit name Abraham; the burial does not repeat it.
