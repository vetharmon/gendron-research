## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Pierre Gendreau was born on 6 April 1826 and baptized the next day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Pierre Gendreau and Marie Gamache. [Baptism](#document-act-4079947)
- **Marriage:** The existing original-reading notes record his marriage to Barbe Caron at Ste-Luce, Frenchville, on 9 August 1852. He was living at St François, and the act names him as the adult son of the late Pierre Gendreau and the late Marie Gamache. [Archived marriage notes](#evidence-notes)
- **Later household:** The saved 1860 northern Maine census reading records Peter Gendreau, a 36-year-old farmer born in Canada, with Babe, 25, born in Maine, and Julie (6), Sophie (4), Louison (3) and Amos (8 months), all born in Maine. The wife-and-children identification comes from the household names together with the marriage; the census has no relationship column. [Archived census notes](#evidence-notes)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **Marriage, 9 August 1852, Ste-Luce, Frenchville** (no source link in this file; the original is in the evidence archive) — The act names Pierre as the adult son of the late Pierre Gendreau and the late Marie Gamache, of Cap St Ignace, Bas Canada, and Barbe Caron as the minor daughter of Joseph Caron and Emilie Nadeau of St François; Joseph Caron and Paschal Gendreau witnessed. The exact parental pair is what connects the groom to the 1826 baptism. The original's margin reads M11 where an abstract gives M10, and its page is 354.
- **Census, 1860, northern Maine** (no source link in this file; the original page is in the evidence archive) — Page 61, first six lines: Peter Gendreau, 36, farmer, born in Canada; Babe, 25, born in Maine; Julie, Sophie, Louison and Amos, all born in Maine. The 1860 form has no relationship column, so the household relationships are read from the order and names together with the 1852 marriage; the census age of 36 is two years more than the 34 counted from his April 1826 birth. A compiled death date of 14 August 1910 has been reported for him but was not verified in this research and is not relied on.
