## Biography

Pierre Gendreau was born on 6 April 1826 and baptized the next day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Pierre Gendreau and Marie Gamache. As an adult he was living at St François, and on 9 August 1852 he married Barbe Caron at Ste-Luce, Frenchville — the marriage act names him as the adult son of the late Pierre Gendreau and the late Marie Gamache. In the 1860 census of northern Maine he appears as Peter Gendreau, a 36-year-old farmer born in Canada, with Babe, 25, born in Maine, and children Julie (6), Sophie (4), Louison (3) and Amos (8 months), all born in Maine.

## Documents

The exact parental pair is what connects the groom to the 1826 baptism. The original's margin reads M11 where an abstract gives M10, and its page is 354.
- **Census, 1860, northern Maine** (no source link in this file; the original page is in the evidence archive) — Page 61, first six lines: Peter Gendreau, 36, farmer, born in Canada; Babe, 25, born in Maine; Julie, Sophie, Louison and Amos, all born in Maine. The 1860 form has no relationship column, so the household relationships are read from the order and names together with the 1852 marriage; the census age of 36 is two years more than the 34 counted from his April 1826 birth, and no exact age match is claimed. A compiled death date of 14 August 1910 has been reported for him but was not verified in this research and is not relied on.
