## Biography

Adrien Gendron was born and baptized on 24 July 1828 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Pierre Gendron, of Châteauguay, and Angelique Hebert. He married Marcelline Primeau on 17 October 1854 at Châteauguay, where the act names him as the adult son of Pierre Gendron and Angelique Hebert. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 17 October 1854.
