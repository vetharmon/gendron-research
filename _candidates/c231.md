## Biography

- Adrien Gendron was born and baptized on 24 July 1828 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Pierre Gendron, of Châteauguay, and Angelique Hebert. [Baptism](#document-act-4253850)

- He married Marcelline Primeau on 17 October 1854 at Châteauguay, where the act names him as the adult son of Pierre Gendron and Angelique Hebert. [Marriage](#document-act-13558713)
