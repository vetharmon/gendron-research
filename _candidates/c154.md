## Biography

Antoine was born on 27 November 1824 and baptized the next day at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité). His baptism names his parents as Jean Baptiste, a day labourer, and Archange Ducharme, and gives the father's name as dit Larochel; the candidate index recorded the surname as Gendron Larochelle. His parents' marriage at Contrecoeur on 22 November 1813 names his father in full: **Jean Baptiste Gautron Larochelle**, son of Charles Gautron Larochelle and Josephte Letendre. The family is Gautron dit Larochelle — Antoine is not a Gendron, and his baptism reached this register through an indexing error. No marriage or burial for Antoine himself has been proven.

## Research lead

One unconfirmed lead remains: a burial at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu on 12 July 1858 (act 5442916) for an Antoine Gendron, age 34, husband of Sarah Trahan. It names no parents, and no marriage of an Antoine to Sarah Trahan has been found; whether it belongs to this Antoine Gautron is unproven and no longer affects his standing here.
