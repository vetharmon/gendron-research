## Biography

Antoine was born on 27 November 1824 and baptized the next day at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité). His baptism names his parents as Jean Baptiste, a day labourer, and Archange Ducharme. The father's surname appears to read **Gaudron dit Larochel**; the candidate index records Gendron Larochelle. No later record has been connected to Antoine.

## Research lead

The MesAieux index lists an **1813 Contrecoeur marriage of Jean-Baptiste Gautron and Marie-Archange Ducharme**. The matching parental names and parish give a route to the family under Gautron, Gaudron and Larochelle. The original marriage and the couple's other children's records need comparison before deciding whether Antoine belongs to this surname family or to the Gendron candidates.
