## Biography

- Antoine Gautron dit Larochelle was born on 27 November 1824 and baptized the next day at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of Jean Baptiste, a day labourer, and Archange Ducharme. [Baptism](#document-act-2736955)
- He entered this research register under the indexed surname Gendron Larochelle. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Assessment

**Eliminated from the Gendron candidate list by correction of the family surname.** His parents' original marriage at Contrecoeur on **22 November 1813** names the groom **Jean Baptiste Gautron dit Larochelle**, a day labourer and son of **Charles Gautron and Josephte Letendre**, and the bride **Marie Archange Ducharme**. The parental names, dit name, occupation and parish connect this marriage to Antoine's baptism.

The original baptism's surname had been read as Gaudron; the marriage resolves the family as Gautron dit Larochelle. This assessment concerns the surname under which Antoine was included. His own later life has not been established.

## Remaining lead

Ember found an unconfirmed burial for Antoine Gendron, aged 34, husband of Sarah Trahan, at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu on 12 July 1858 (LAFRANCE act 5442916). It names no parents, and no matching marriage has been located. It is not used to identify Antoine or support this assessment.
