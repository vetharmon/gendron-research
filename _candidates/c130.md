## Biography

Isidore Gendron was born on 29 September 1823 and baptized the same day at St-Henri-de-Lauzon, the son of Pierre Gendron and Catherine Mercier. He married Marie Magdeleine Roger on 2 August 1841 at St-Césaire; the act names his parents, of Granby, and calls him of age, though the baptism makes him not quite eighteen. Their daughter Marie Domitille was born on 20 March 1855 and baptized three days later at Stukely-Nord, the family then of Orford. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 23 March 1855.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 2 August 1841 — LAFRANCE act 13400941](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400941)** — His marriage to Marie Magdeleine Roger at St-Césaire (entry M26). The act names his parents, Pierre and Catherine Mercier of Granby, which ties the groom to the 1823 baptism, and names his father Pierre and his brother François among the witnesses; it calls him majeur, although the baptism implies he was seventeen.
- **[Baptism of his daughter, 23 March 1855 — LAFRANCE act 5990005]** (no link recorded) — Marie Domitille, born 20 March, daughter of Isidore Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie Latouche Roger of Orford, baptized at Stukely-Nord (entry B32). It carries the couple's family to 1855.
