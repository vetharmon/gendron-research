## Biography

Édouard Adaucte Gendreau was baptized on 30 August 1823 at Cap-St-Ignace (St-Ignace-de-Loyola), the son of Pierre Gendreau and Catherine Gamache. He married Éléonore Bélanger on 8 January 1849 at St-Basile; the act gives his origin as Cap-St-Ignace and names his parents, by then deceased, as Pierre Gendreau and Marie Gamache. Their son Édouard was born on 3 November 1849 and baptized the next day.
