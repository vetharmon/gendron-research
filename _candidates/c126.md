## Biography

- Édouard Adaucte Gendreau was baptized on 30 August 1823 at Cap-St-Ignace (St-Ignace-de-Loyola), the son of Pierre Gendreau and Catherine Gamache. [Baptism](#document-act-2878108)
- He married Éléonore Bélanger on 8 January 1849 at St-Basile; the act gives his origin as Cap-St-Ignace and names his parents, by then deceased, as Pierre Gendreau and Marie Gamache. [Marriage, 8 January 1849](#document-act-6174935)
- Their son Édouard was born on 3 November 1849 and baptized the next day. [Baptism of his son, 4 November 1849](#document-act-6169879)
