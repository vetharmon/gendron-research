## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2878108
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original 1823 baptism names parents Pierre and Catherine Gamache. Their original 1804 marriage explicitly gives Marie Catherine Gamache, bridging Catherine/Marie usage. Edouard's 8 January 1849 marriage at St-Basile names deceased Pierre Gendreau and deceased Marie Gamache, gives his origin as du Cap, and marries him to Eleonore Belanger. Their son Edouard's baptism is 4 November 1849 (birth 3 November), beginning at the foot of d1p_31380338 and continuing atop d1p_31380339. The act viewer lands on the continuation. This combined parent-and-place identification and separate 1849 family supports exclusion under the previously accepted 1848–1849 family chronology criterion. No original post-1850 event or Maine death record has been verified. Acadie Familles' 1851 Andre entry is a compiled lead only and not relied on as original evidence.
