## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Jean Gendron was born and baptized on 2 February 1826 at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Joseph Gendron and Louise Mathieu. [Baptism](#document-act-4344400)
- **Marriage:** As Jean Baptiste Gendron he married Adeline Robitaille on 13 January 1846 at Sorel. [Marriage](#document-act-13400951)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 16 October 1846, aged twenty, a navigateur and the husband of Adeline, and was buried at Sorel the next day. [Burial](#document-act-4353714)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 13 January 1846 — LAFRANCE act 13400951](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400951)** — His marriage to Adeline Robitaille at Sorel. The act names the groom as Jean Baptiste Gendron, a minor, son of Joseph Gendron and Louise Mathieu — the baptism's parents, which is what bridges the child baptized as Jean to the adult of the 1846 records.
- **[Burial, 17 October 1846 — LAFRANCE act 4353714](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4353714)** — His burial at Sorel: Jean Baptiste Gendron, navigateur, husband of Adeline Robitaille, aged twenty, died the previous day. Naming his wife ties the burial to the marriage of nine months earlier.
- **[Baptism, 2 February 1826 — LAFRANCE act 4344400](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4344400)** — His baptism at Sorel: Jean, born that day, son of Joseph Gendron and Louise Mathieu.
