## Biography

Jean Gendron was born and baptized on 2 February 1826 at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Joseph Gendron and Louise Mathieu. As Jean Baptiste Gendron he married Adeline Robitaille on 13 January 1846 at Sorel. He died on 16 October 1846, aged twenty, a navigateur and the husband of Adeline, and was buried at Sorel the next day.
