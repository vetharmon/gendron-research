## Biography

Charles Sylvain Gendron was born on 19 February 1822 and baptized the same day at Cap-St-Ignace (St-Ignace-de-Loyola), the son of the late Joseph Gendron and Magdeleine Meule. He died on 24 August 1822, aged six months, and was buried at Montmagny on 26 August 1822, the burial naming the same parents.
