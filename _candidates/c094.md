## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born on 11 January 1822 and baptized the same day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Daniel. He died on 28 May 1822, aged about five months, and was buried at St-Hyacinthe on 30 May 1822, the burial naming the same parents.
