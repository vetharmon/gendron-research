## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born on 11 October 1824 and baptized the same day at Châteauguay, the son of Pierre Gendron and Angelique Hebert. He died on 25 July 1838, aged thirteen years, and was buried on 27 July 1838 at Beauharnois; the burial names the same parents.
