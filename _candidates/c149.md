## Biography

- Pierre Gendron was born on 11 October 1824 and baptized the same day at Châteauguay, the son of Pierre Gendron and Angelique Hebert. [Baptism](#document-act-2628390)
- He died on 25 July 1838, aged thirteen years, and was buried on 27 July 1838 at Beauharnois; the burial names the same parents. [Burial, 27 July 1838](#document-act-4258107) · [Baptism, 11 October 1824](#document-act-2628390)
