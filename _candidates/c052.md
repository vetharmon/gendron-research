## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 31 July 1819 and baptized the same day at Châteauguay, the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Anne Pare. He died on 25 September 1819, aged two months, and was buried at Châteauguay the following day.

- **[Baptism, 31 July 1819 — LAFRANCE act 2627324](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2627324)** — His baptism at Châteauguay (left page, entry B132), born that day, naming his parents, Pierre Gendron and Marie Anne Pare.
