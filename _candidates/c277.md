## Biography

- Francois Xavier Gendron was baptized on 3 October 1830 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin. [Baptism](#document-act-2996965)
- He died aged eighteen and was buried at Ste-Rosalie on 14 May 1849. [Burial, 14 May 1849](#document-act-4200748)
