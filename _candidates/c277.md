## Biography

Francois Xavier Gendron was baptized on 3 October 1830 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin. He died aged eighteen and was buried at Ste-Rosalie on 14 May 1849.
