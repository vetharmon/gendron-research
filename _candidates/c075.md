## Biography

Pierre Victor Gendreau was born and baptized on 22 February 1821 at St-Luc (St-Jean), the son of Louis Gendreau, a laboureur, and Marie Anne Girard. As Victor Gendreau, a cultivateur, he married Euphrosine Lareau on 27 February 1843 at Chambly; the act names him as the adult son of Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard of St-Luc, and her as the minor daughter of the late François Lareau and Sophie Tétreau. After Euphrosine's death he married again, as Victor Gendreault, on 10 February 1852 at Henryville, where the act names him as her widower and his bride as Rose Masson. The adult acts drop his first name Pierre, and the surname varies between Gendreau and Gendreault. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 10 February 1852.
