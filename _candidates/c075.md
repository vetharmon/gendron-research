## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2765259
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 22 February 1821 St-Luc, act 2765259, image d1p_02451234.jpg, B31 middle left, names Pierre Victor, born that day, son of Louis Gendreau, laboureur, and Marie Anne Girard. Original marriage 27 February 1843 Chambly, act 13497815, image d1p_11171302.jpg, M17 lower left, names Victor Gendreau, cultivateur, adult son of Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard of St-Luc, marrying Euphrosine Lareau, minor daughter of deceased François Lareau and Sophie Tétreau. Original remarriage 10 February 1852 Henryville, act 13400848, image d1p_02240289.jpg, M9 right middle, identifies Victor Gendreault, widower of Euphrosine Lareau, marrying Rose Masson. The matching parents and explicit former-spouse bridge establish a separate adult identity continuing in 1852, inconsistent with Franklin's Missouri family. Pierre is omitted in adult acts; Gendreau/Gendreault varies. PRDH profile 807086 corroborates both unions; the originals were manually read.
