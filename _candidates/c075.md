## Biography

- Pierre Victor Gendreau was born and baptized on 22 February 1821 at St-Luc (St-Jean), the son of Louis Gendreau, a laboureur, and Marie Anne Girard. [Baptism](#document-act-2765259)

- As Victor Gendreau, a cultivateur, he married Euphrosine Lareau on 27 February 1843 at Chambly; the act names him as the adult son of Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard of St-Luc, and her as the minor daughter of the late François Lareau and Sophie Tétreau. [Marriage](#document-act-13497815)

- After Euphrosine's death he married again, as Victor Gendreault, on 16 February 1852 at Henryville, where the act names him as her widower and his bride as Rose Masson. [Remarriage](#document-act-13400848)

- The adult acts drop his first name Pierre, and the surname varies between Gendreau and Gendreault. [Marriage](#document-act-13497815) [Remarriage](#document-act-13400848)
