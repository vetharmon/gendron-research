## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4588173
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 19 March 1826 at Chateauguay names Louis, born that day, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. Original burial begins 31 March 1826 on d1p_01760817.jpg and continues at the head of the left page on d1p_01760818.jpg: Louis died two days earlier; the continuation names the same parents and says quinze jours (fifteen days). The dated interval is ten days, so the five-day age discrepancy is retained, not silently corrected. PRDH family 116715 was checked on 9 November: one Louis is indexed, between Domitille born 30 April 1823 and Philippe born 2 June 1828. Matching name, both parents, parish and the immediately adjacent infant chronology, corroborated by the family sequence, support identifying the burial with this candidate despite the minor age conflict. Exclusion is an identity assessment from the combined evidence; it does not assert that the two ages agree.
