## Biography

Louis Gendron was born and baptized on 19 March 1826 at Châteauguay, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. He died on 29 March 1826 and was buried at Châteauguay on 31 March 1826. The burial's continuation gives his age as fifteen days, although the interval from his birth is ten days — that small discrepancy stands as recorded.
