## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Louis Gendron was born and baptized on 19 March 1826 at Châteauguay, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. [Baptism](#document-act-4588173)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 29 March 1826 and was buried at Châteauguay on 31 March 1826. The burial's continuation gives his age as fifteen days, although the interval from his birth is ten days. [Burial and continuation](#document-act-4592209) · [Birth date](#document-act-4588173)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 31 March 1826 — LAFRANCE act 4592209](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4592209)** — His burial at Châteauguay: the act begins on one page and continues at the head of the left page of the next, where it names the same parents as the baptism and gives his age as fifteen days, though the dated interval from his birth is ten days. The continuation page is also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/tag/d1p_01760818.jpg).
- **[Baptism, 19 March 1826 — LAFRANCE act 4588173](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4588173)** — His baptism at Châteauguay: Louis, born that day, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron.
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 116715](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/116715)** — PRDH's compiled family of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron, in which a single Louis is indexed, between Domitille, born 30 April 1823, and Philippe, born 2 June 1828 — the family sequence leaves no second Louis for the burial to belong to.
