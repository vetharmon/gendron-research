## Biography

- Joseph Gendron was born and baptized on 31 August 1830 at Marieville (St-Nom-de-Marie), the son of Joseph Gendron, a cultivateur, and Marie Commeau. [Baptism](#document-act-4510999)
- A proposed later match married Marie Adélaïde [Bonvouloir?] on 16 October 1861 at Ste-Brigide. The inspected marriage names the groom’s father as Joseph Gendreau, but his mother’s surname is uncertain, reading Marie [M…neau?]; it does not currently provide a verified exact match to Marie Commeau. [Marriage, 16 October 1861](#document-act-13400979)

## Assessment

The later marriage remains a likely identification. The father’s name agrees, but the original maternal surname cannot currently be read confidently enough to establish the parentage bridge. This is an unresolved reading, not a demonstrated different mother. An alternate marriage copy or another parent-linked original is needed. [Marriage](#document-act-13400979) · [Baptism](#document-act-4510999)

## Sainte-Brigide household followed to 1881 — 28 November 2026

The [original 1881 census](#document-census-1881-e008149251) supplies a strong continuation of the Sainte-Brigide household found in 1871. In district 64 Iberville, subdistrict F, division 1, page 20, house 91, family 98, lines 14–22, Joseph Gendron is 50, a farmer, and marked married. He is enumerated with Dorothée, 36, also marked married; Césarie, 17; Audena, 15; Napoléon, 8; Sylvia, 6; Arthur, 4; Rose Anna, 3; and Adélina, 1. The birthplace, religion, and origin columns carry ditto marks for Québec, Catholic, and French. The schedule does not state relationships or parentage.

The repeated Joseph/Césarie/Odéna–Audena group in the same parish strongly supports comparison with [1871 family 197](https://data2.collectionscanada.gc.ca/1871/jpg/4395448_00122.jpg), where Joseph was a 40-year-old widower and Césarie and Odéna were 7 and 4. The 1881 name is retained as **Audena**, and her reported age of 15 differs by one year from a straightforward ten-year progression from Odéna’s 1871 age. A [compiled Geneanet profile](#document-findingaid-joseph-dorothee-brigide-1871) gives a further locator: Joseph Gendron’s marriage to Dorothée Bédard on **17 October 1871 at Sainte-Brigide**. That profile associates Joseph with Joseph Gendron and Marie Comeau, but supplies no original image or cited source for the marriage. It is a lead for verification, not an original parentage bridge.

The new census makes the Dorothée Bédard route concrete but does not resolve the uncertain maternal surname in the proposed 1861 marriage. It also does not, by itself, identify this household as C275 rather than the comparison candidate [C266](../c266/). The original 1871 remarriage or a parent-naming act for one of the two older girls remains necessary. C275 retains its existing qualified **likely** grade.

The public MesAieux overview adds an **1886 Sainte-Brigide-d’Iberville marriage of Henri Mercure and Audina Gendron**. It supplies no exact day or parents, so Audina is not yet identified as either census girl. [Selected index abstract and translation](#document-findingaid-audina-mercure-brigide-1886)

The compiled tree calls the 1861 bride **Cécile Délima Bonvouloir**, whereas the preserved original reading is **Marie Adélaïde [Bonvouloir?]**, with the groom’s maternal surname **Marie [M…neau?]** still uncertain. The compiler’s names do not replace those readings.
