## Biography

Joseph Gendron was born on 30 August 1830 and baptized the next day at Marieville (St-Nom-de-Marie), the son of Joseph Gendron, a cultivateur, and Marie Commeau. He married Marie Adeline Bonvouloir on 16 October 1861 at Ste-Brigide; the marriage act names him as the adult son of Joseph Gendron and Marie Commeau, of Marieville. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 16 October 1861.
