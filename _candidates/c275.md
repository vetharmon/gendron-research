## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4510999
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original images rechecked 9 November 2026. Baptism B159 at lower left is dated 31 August 1830 and states Joseph was born the previous day, son of Joseph Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie Commeau; 30 August is the birth date, although the inventory/index had treated it as the baptism date. Marriage M15 starts at the foot of the left page and continues at the top of the right page on d1p_02420616.jpg (also repeated on d1p_02420617.jpg). It is dated 16 October 1861 and names Joseph Gendron, adult son of Joseph Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie Commeau, of this parish, marrying Marie Adeline Bonvouloir. The original does not support the previous reading 'deceased Emelie Comeau'; that reading is withdrawn. Matching original parental names and the ordinary first-marriage form in 1861 establish a separate adult marital identity incompatible with Franklin's established Missouri marriages and family. PRDH profile 1066563 and family 153665 are corroboration, not substitutes for the originals. Counted as excluded.
