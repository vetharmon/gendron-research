## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 30 March 1824 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Daniel. He died on 7 March 1825 and was buried on 9 March 1825 at St-Hyacinthe; the burial states his age as about eight months, though the dates give about eleven months.
