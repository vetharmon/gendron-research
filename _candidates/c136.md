## Biography

- Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 30 March 1824 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Daniel. [Baptism](#document-act-2479376)
- He died on 7 March 1825 and was buried on 9 March 1825 at St-Hyacinthe; the burial states his age as about eight months, though the dates give about eleven months. [Burial, 9 March 1825](#document-act-3002858)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 9 March 1825 — LAFRANCE act 3002858](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3002858)** — His burial at St-Hyacinthe (entry S29, beginning bottom left and continuing top right of the same spread): Jean Baptiste, died two days earlier, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Daniel. Its stated age of about eight months does not match the eleven months since his birth, a discrepancy the record leaves unresolved.
- **[Baptism, 31 March 1824 — LAFRANCE act 2479376](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2479376)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe (entry B101, beginning bottom left and continuing top right), born 30 March, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Daniel.
- **[PRDH-IGD family 140897](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/140897)** — PRDH's reconstruction of his parents' family, which has a single Jean Baptiste, between Louis (born 7 March 1823, died 31 July 1823) and Élisabeth (born 1 April 1825) — no competing indexed Jean Baptiste.
