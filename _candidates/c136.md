## Research assessment

Original baptism B101, 31 March 1824 at St-Hyacinthe, beginning bottom left and continuing top right: Jean Baptiste born 30 March, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Daniel. Original burial S29, 9 March 1825, d1p_03890833.jpg, begins bottom left and continues top right on the same spread: Jean Baptiste, died two days earlier, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Daniel. The burial says about eight months, whereas the dates yield about eleven months; that original conflict remains disclosed. New family check 9 November: family 140897 has a single Jean Baptiste, between Louis born 7 March 1823 (died 31 July 1823) and Elisabeth born 1 April 1825. This corroborates the named child and supplies no competing indexed Jean Baptiste. Matching both parents, name and parish plus the family sequence support identifying the burial with this candidate despite the approximate-age error. The exclusion is a combined identity assessment, not a claim of matching ages.
