## Biography

Joseph Adolphe Gendron was baptized on 21 September 1823 at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Louis Gendron and Magdeleine Lafrance. No later record identified. A Joseph Gendron of about his age in the parish's 1851 census is another couple's son.

## Documents

- **[Baptism, 21 September 1823 — LAFRANCE act 2913683](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2913683)** — His baptism at St-Pierre-les-Becquets; the index names his parents as Louis Gendron and Magdeleine Lafrance. The family records found around him are index leads: his parents' marriage at Gentilly in 1820 (act 5722237, the bride as Madeleine Dubois Lafrance), a sibling's baptism in 1821 (act 2913536), his mother's burial on 23 October 1823 (act 2914265), and his father's remarriage in 1827 (act 13401010). An 1838 marriage found in the same searches (act 13377976) belongs to a Marie Gendron of these parents, not to Joseph Adolphe.

## Namesake checks — 15 November 2026

The search under Adolphe produced two marriage leads that the compiled records assign to other families:

- [Adolphe Gendron and Aurélie Therrien, Wotton, 27 October 1863](https://www.nosorigines.qc.ca/GenealogieQuebec.aspx?genealogie=Gendron_Adolphe&pid=1865652): the compiled entry gives birth on 2 November 1840 and parents Hyacinthe Gendron and Marie Côté.
- [Adolphe Gendron and Marie-Éléonore Massicotte, Sainte-Geneviève-de-Batiscan, 20 June 1853](https://www.nosorigines.qc.ca/GenealogieQuebec.aspx?genealogie=Massicotte_Marie-Eleonore&pid=1621532): the compiled entry gives birth on 13 December 1827 and parents Joseph Gendron and Suzanne Lahaie.

These are secondary-source checks, not newly inspected original marriages. Neither supplies evidence for C129. The Adolphe/Joséphine Cloutier lead overlaps the existing [C145 investigation](/gendron-research/candidates/c145/) and must not be assigned to C129 from the given name alone.

Assessment remains **Needs work**.

The original **1861 St-Pierre-les-Becquets census, page 398**, lists **Charles Gendron, 87**, **Marie Gagnon, 75**, and **Joseph Gendron, 35**, unmarried, on lines 14–16. That is the competing Charles/Marie Gagnon family already distinguished in this investigation. The tree attachment is not evidence that C129 appears there.

The attached **1871** index lists Joseph, 46, with Réigne, 47, Marie, 84, Ermine, nine, and Augé children at St-Pierre-les-Becquets, page 42, family 149. Its original was not inspected in this pass, and no record connects that man to Louis Gendron and Madeleine Lafrance. That marriage provides a concrete comparison for the indexed 1871 Joseph/Reigne household.
