## Biography

Joseph Adolphe Gendron was baptized on 21 September 1823 at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Louis Gendron and Magdeleine Lafrance. No later record identified. A Joseph Gendron of about his age in the parish's 1851 census is another couple's son.

## Documents

- **[Baptism, 21 September 1823 — LAFRANCE act 2913683](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2913683)** — His baptism at St-Pierre-les-Becquets; the index names his parents as Louis Gendron and Magdeleine Lafrance. The family records found around him are index leads with no source links recorded here: his parents' marriage at Gentilly in 1820 (act 5722237, the bride as Madeleine Dubois Lafrance), a sibling's baptism in 1821 (act 2913536), his mother's burial on 23 October 1823 (act 2914265), and his father's remarriage in 1827 (act 13401010). An 1838 marriage found in the same searches (act 13377976) belongs to a Marie Gendron of these parents, not to Joseph Adolphe.
