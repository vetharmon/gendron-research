## Biography

Joseph Adolphe Gendron was baptized on 21 September 1823 at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Louis Gendron and Magdeleine Lafrance. Follow-up index searches traced his family — his parents' marriage in 1820, a sibling's baptism in 1821, his mother's burial on 23 October 1823, and his father's remarriage in 1827 — but none of those records is Joseph Adolphe himself, and no later record for him has been established.
