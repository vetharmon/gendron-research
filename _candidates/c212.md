## Biography

Narcisse Gendron was baptized on 10 October 1827 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Ignace Gendron and Josephte Gendron. No later record identified. The three 1851 census entries that might have been his family have all been read: at St-Urbain, an Ignace Gendron of 70 heads a household containing a Narcisse of 51; at Saint-Timothée, a Josephte Gendrau of 72 lives in an Antoine Deschamps household with no Gendron man present; and the unaged "N Gendron" at Saint-Timothée is an agricultural-schedule listing. He is in none of them.
