## Biography

Narcisse Gendron was baptized on 10 October 1827 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Ignace Gendron and Josephte Gendron. No later record identified. The 1851 index offers possible sightings of his parents at Saint-Timothée and St-Urbain, still to be read.
