## Biography

- Charles Gendron was born on 13 September 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Ours, the son of Michel Gendron and Claire Apolline Labossiere. [Baptism](#document-act-4653129)

- He married Victoire Chapdelaine dite Lariviere on 7 October 1850 at St-Jude, the act naming him as the adult son of Michel Gendron and Claire Labossiere. [Marriage](#document-act-13400877)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 7 October 1850 — LAFRANCE act 13400877](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400877)** — His marriage to Victoire Chapdelaine dite Lariviere at St-Jude. It names his parents, Michel Gendron and Claire Labossiere, tying the groom to the 1827 baptism.
- **[Baptism, 14 September 1827 — LAFRANCE act 4653129](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4653129)** — His baptism at St-Ours: born the previous day, son of Michel Gendron and Claire Apolline Labossiere.
