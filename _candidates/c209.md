## Biography

Charles Gendron was born on 13 September 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Ours, the son of Michel Gendron and Claire Apolline Labossiere. He married Victoire Chapdelaine dite Lariviere on 7 October 1850 at St-Jude, the act naming him as the adult son of Michel Gendron and Claire Labossiere. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 7 October 1850.
