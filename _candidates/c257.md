## Biography

Jacques Gendron was born and baptized on 19 June 1829 at Yamaska (St-Michel), the son of Jacques Gendron and Agathe Bonenfant. He died on 25 January 1831, aged nineteen months, and was buried at Yamaska the next day; the burial names the same parents. He was a younger same-name brother of the Jacques Gendron of entry C218, whose own burial predates this baptism.
