## Biography

- Jacques Gendron was born and baptized on 19 June 1829 at Yamaska (St-Michel), the son of Jacques Gendron and Agathe Bonenfant. [Baptism](#document-act-4490865)
- He died on 25 January 1831, aged nineteen months, and was buried at Yamaska the next day; the burial names the same parents. [Burial, 26 January 1831](#document-act-4495586) · [Baptism, 19 June 1829](#document-act-4490865)
- He was a younger same-name brother of the Jacques Gendron of entry C218, whose own burial predates this baptism. [Baptism](#document-act-4490865) · [Older Jacques, C218](../c218/)
