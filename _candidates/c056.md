## Biography

Pierre Gendreau was born on 20 October 1819 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Joseph Gendreau and Marie Angélique Bouchard. He died on 2 November 1819 and was buried at St-Hyacinthe the following day; the burial act gives his age as eleven days, two days less than the thirteen days since his birth.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 3 November 1819 — LAFRANCE act 2482976](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2482976)** — His burial at St-Hyacinthe (entry S173), naming the same child and parents; he had died the day before. The act's stated age of eleven days is two days short of the interval since his birth, and its closing witness and signature clause runs onto the following page, which is preserved in the evidence.
- **[Baptism, 21 October 1819 — LAFRANCE act 2477364](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2477364)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe, born the previous day, naming his parents, Joseph Gendreau and Marie Angélique Bouchard.

- **[PRDH-IGD family record 96576](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/96576)** — PRDH's compiled family for his parents; it shows an earlier Pierre, born and died in 1811, and this Pierre as the only indexed infant of the name in 1819.
