## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2477364
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 21 October 1819 names Pierre, born previous day, son of Joseph Gendreau and Marie Angélique Bouchard. Original burial 3 November 1819, S173, names the same child and parents, deceased previous day; the downloaded following page completes the witness/signature clause. Burial age eleven days differs by two days from the thirteen elapsed since birth. PRDH family 96576 shows an earlier Pierre born and dead in 1811 and this Pierre as the only indexed infant of that name in 1819. Matching original parentage and infant chronology support exclusion despite the small age error. The indexed family is corroborative, not proof of completeness.
