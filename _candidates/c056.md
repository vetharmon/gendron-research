## Biography

Pierre Gendreau was born on 20 October 1819 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Joseph Gendreau and Marie Angélique Bouchard. He died on 2 November 1819 and was buried at St-Hyacinthe the following day; the burial act gives his age as eleven days, two days less than the thirteen days since his birth.
