## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Elie Gendron was born and baptized on 20 August 1826 at Châteauguay, the son of Pierre Gendron and Angelique Hebert. [Baptism](#document-act-4588304)
- **Marriage:** A cultivateur, he married Marguerite Primeau on 16 September 1851 at Châteauguay; the act names him as the adult son of Pierre Gendron and Angelique Hebert, and Marguerite as the minor daughter of Hyacinthe Primeau and the deceased Josephte Duquet. [Marriage](#document-act-13557491)
- **Later family:** On 7 April 1861 their son Alphonse Israel was baptized at St-Louis-de-Gonzague; Elie was then a journalier and among those who could not sign the act. [Alphonse Israel's baptism](#document-act-6083585)

## Probable census household — 16 November 2026

Original LAC census images [e002296780](https://data2.collectionscanada.gc.ca/e/e092/e002296780.jpg) and [e002296781](https://data2.collectionscanada.gc.ca/e/e092/e002296781.jpg), St-Louis-de-Gonzague, C-1112, pages 3–4, lines 18–21, record E. Gendron, 25; M. Primeau, 18; J. Guérin, 23; and A. Gendron, 21. All four are marked married. This fits Élie and Marguerite Primeau, with Joachim Guérin and Angélique Gendron as a competing identification for the adjacent couple. The names remain initials and the census states no relationships. It was previously retained as an Étienne C181 lead because E.’s age matched; the original surnames and sexes provide a better explanation. C186’s existing solid assessment rests on his parent-naming marriage and later family acts, not this census alone.

## Census recheck — 23 November 2026

The original pages list E. Gendron, a day laborer with a reported age of 25 at his next birthday, followed by M. Primeau, J. Guérin, and A. Gendron. All four are marked married. The full-name marriage of Élie Gendron and Marguerite Primeau supports this as a possible household continuation, but the census initials and lack of explicit relationships do not independently prove the identification. The parent-linked marriage and child’s baptism remain the direct basis for Élie’s separate family.
