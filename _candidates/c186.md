## Biography

Elie Gendron was born and baptized on 20 August 1826 at Châteauguay, the son of Pierre Gendron and Angelique Hebert. A cultivateur, he married Marguerite Primeau on 16 September 1851 at Châteauguay; the act names him as the adult son of Pierre Gendron and Angelique Hebert, and Marguerite as the minor daughter of Hyacinthe Primeau and the deceased Josephte Duquet. On 7 April 1861 their son Alphonse Israel was baptized at St-Louis-de-Gonzague, Elie by then a journalier and among those who could not sign the act. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 7 April 1861.
