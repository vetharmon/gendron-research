## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Elie Gendron was born and baptized on 20 August 1826 at Châteauguay, the son of Pierre Gendron and Angelique Hebert. [Baptism](#document-act-4588304)
- **Marriage:** A cultivateur, he married Marguerite Primeau on 16 September 1851 at Châteauguay; the act names him as the adult son of Pierre Gendron and Angelique Hebert, and Marguerite as the minor daughter of Hyacinthe Primeau and the deceased Josephte Duquet. [Marriage](#document-act-13557491)
- **Later family:** On 7 April 1861 their son Alphonse Israel was baptized at St-Louis-de-Gonzague; Elie was then a journalier and among those who could not sign the act. [Alphonse Israel's baptism](#document-act-6083585)
