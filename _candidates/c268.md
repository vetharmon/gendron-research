## Biography

Pierre Zephirin Gendreau was born on 2 February 1830 and baptized the same day at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), the son of Pierre Gendreau and Marie Dulude. He died on 14 January 1838, aged seven years eleven months, and was buried at St-Pie on 16 January 1838.
