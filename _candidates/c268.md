## Biography

- Pierre Zéphirin Gendreau was born on 2 February 1830 and baptized the same day at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), the son of Pierre Gendrault and Marie Dusseau. [Baptism](#document-act-4371731)
- He died on 14 January 1838 and was buried at St-Pie on 16 January. [Burial, 16 January 1838](#document-act-4245422)
- The burial names his mother Marie Dussault and records his age as seven and a half; his baptism places him just under eight. [Burial, 16 January 1838](#document-act-4245422)
- He is the younger brother of [Pierre Gendron, C211](../c211/), born in 1827. [Baptism, 2 February 1830](#document-act-4371731)
- The burial belongs to this younger child. [Burial, 16 January 1838](#document-act-4245422)
