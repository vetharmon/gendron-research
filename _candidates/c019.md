## Biography

Jean Nestor Gendreau was baptized on 17 April 1817 at La Pocatière (Ste-Anne), in a joint baptism with his twin brother Hubert, the sons of Hubert Gendreau and Constance Perrault. He died within days and was buried with his brother on 21 April 1817; the joint burial names both twins explicitly, though its age wording — a few hours — differs from the four days since the baptism. Both entries are on the left page of the register.
