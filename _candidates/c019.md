## Biography

Jean Nestor Gendreau was baptized on 17 April 1817 at La Pocatière (Ste-Anne), in a joint baptism with his twin brother Hubert, the sons of Hubert Gendreau and Constance Perrault. He died within days and was buried with his brother on 20 April 1817; the joint burial names both twins explicitly and says they had died two days earlier, implying 18 April. The difficult age phrase refers to age at death, not the interval between baptism and burial. Both entries are on the left page of the register.
