## Biography

Benoni Gendron was born on 29 October 1821 and baptized the same day at Rimouski (St-Germain), the son of Prudans Gendron, a farmer (cultivateur), and Henriette Chamard. He married Adélaïde Morin on 22 January 1844 at Ste-Luce; the act names him (as Benois) as a farmer and the adult son of Prudent Gendron and Marie Henriette Chamard. His daughter Marie Angèle, born on 8 December 1856, was baptized the next day at St-Octave-de-Métis, naming Benoni and Adélaïde Morin as her parents.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 22 January 1844 — LAFRANCE act 13400870](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400870)** — His marriage to Adélaïde Morin at Ste-Luce (entry M2). He appears as Benois Gendron, a farmer and the adult son of Prudent Gendron and Marie Henriette Chamard — the same parents as his baptism, under fuller forms of their names (Prudans/Prudent, Henriette/Marie Henriette, and Bénonie/Benois/Bénoni are the forms the records use). The bride was a minor daughter of Pierre Morin and Magdelaine Ouellet.
- **[Baptism of his daughter, 9 December 1856 — LAFRANCE act 5759630](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5759630)** — Baptism of Marie Angèle at St-Octave-de-Métis (entry B46): born the previous day, daughter of Bénoni Gendron, a farmer, and Adélaïde Morin. The act notes the father was absent.
