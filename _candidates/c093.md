## Biography

Benoni Gendron was born on 29 October 1821 and baptized the same day at Rimouski (St-Germain), the son of Prudans Gendron, a farmer (cultivateur), and Henriette Chamard. He married Adélaïde Morin on 22 January 1844 at Ste-Luce; the act names him (as Benois) as a farmer and the adult son of Prudent Gendron and Marie Henriette Chamard. His daughter Marie Angèle, born on 8 December 1856, was baptized the next day at St-Octave-de-Métis, naming Benoni and Adélaïde Morin as her parents. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 9 December 1856.
