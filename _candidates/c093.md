## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2670918
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 29 October 1821 Rimouski, act 2670918, image d1p_17171131.jpg, bottom left, names Bénonie, born that day, son of Prudans Gendron, cultivateur, and Henriette Chamard. Original marriage 22 January 1844 Ste-Luce, act 13400870, image d1p_58910045.jpg, M2 bottom left continuing upper right on the same image, names Benois Gendron, cultivateur, adult son of Prudent Gendron and Marie Henriette Chamard, marrying Adélaïde Morin, minor daughter of Pierre Morin and Magdelaine Ouellet. Original child baptism 9 December 1856 St-Octave-de-Métis, act 5759630, image d1p_17401199.jpg, B46 upper right, names Marie Angèle, born previous day, daughter of Bénoni Gendron, cultivateur, and Adélaïde Morin; father absent. The original parents and continuing spouse/child record establish a separate family inconsistent with Franklin's documented Missouri household. Prudans/Prudent, Henriette/Marie Henriette and Bénonie/Benois/Bénoni are disclosed forms, not silently rendered as exact matches. PRDH profile 813705 and family 227512 corroborate the reconstruction. No attendance claim is made from the 1856 baptism. All three original scans are downloaded unchanged and cited in Scan-Citations.csv.
