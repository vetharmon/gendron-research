## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 12 November 1817 and baptized the next day at St-Henri-de-Lauzon (St-Henri), the son of Pierre Gendron and Catherine Mercier. He died in infancy and was buried on 27 July 1818 at Lauzon, aged about eight months; the burial names the same parents.
