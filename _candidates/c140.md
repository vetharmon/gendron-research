## Biography

François Xavier Gendreau was born on 12 May 1824 and baptized the next day at Saint-Joseph-de-Chambly, son of Joseph Gendreau and Pauline Provost ([baptism, act 2407220](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2407220)). His brother Joseph's [1811 baptism](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2603778) and [1832 marriage to Julienne Deniau](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400818) name the same parents, with the mother recorded as Apolline Prevost/Provost.

Joseph and Julienne's daughter [Zoé was baptized in 1837](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4440722); that act names her mother Julie Anne Daignault. Julienne's Massachusetts death record of 24 October 1882 names her parents Louis and Marguerite ([FamilySearch source profile](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/L2VP-HYR), exhibit E14-09).

## Documents

The [1850 West Stockbridge census](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MD9X-2TH), enumerated on 14 September, lists Joseph Jandru (39), Julian (47), Leander (16), Zoé (13), Joseph (9), William (6), and Francis (27), family 307. Joseph, Julienne, and Zoé identify the household. Francis's name, age, and placement connect him to François Xavier. Relationships are inferred from the family records; the census does not state them. Citation: page 113, folio 225, NARA M432, roll 306, exhibit E14-07.

The [Chambly burial of 12 April 1851](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5505626) names François Xavier Gendreau, a Massachusetts labourer, who drowned at Saint-Luc on 9 April. It records his age as 28 and names no parents. The full name, Massachusetts residence, and approximate age connect the burial to the census Francis. The recorded ages are one year high in 1850 and two years high in 1851. Citation: S20, Drouin image d1p_11180369, exhibit E14-08.

## Conclusion

Eliminated. The Québec family records identify Joseph's household in Massachusetts and connect Francis to his brother François Xavier. The 1851 burial completes that identification. Franklin was living with Phebe and their infant son Isaac in [Missouri in 1850](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:V5P7-TS5) and remained alive after 1851.
