## Biography

François Xavier Gendreau was baptized on 13 May 1824 at Chambly (St-Joseph), the son of Joseph Gendreau and Pauline-Apolline Provost. Earlier research has linked him, circumstantially, to a Francis in an 1850 household at West Stockbridge, Massachusetts, and to a Massachusetts labourer of the name who was buried at Chambly on 12 April 1851. Those links are not proven: the census entry does not state the household relationships, the burial does not name the man's parents, and the ages differ between the records. He is counted as eliminated on that attribution, with its circumstantial character stated plainly.
