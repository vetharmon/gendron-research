## Identification: the Québec brothers and the Massachusetts household

**François Xavier Gendreau** was born on **12 May 1824** and baptized on **13 May** at Saint-Joseph-de-Chambly, son of **Joseph Gendreau and Pauline Provost** ([E14-03/04, baptism act 2407220](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2407220)). The same parents, recorded as Joseph Gendreau and **Apolline Prevost/Provost**, appear in the baptism of **Joseph Gendreau (1811)** and his **1832 marriage to Julienne Deniau** ([E14-01](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2603778); [E14-05](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400818)). These original acts document the sibling relationship between Joseph and François Xavier. Joseph and Julienne's daughter **Zoé**, born in 1837, is independently documented in a baptism ([E14-06](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4440722)); her mother's name appears in that act as Julie Anne Daignault. Julienne's Massachusetts death record of **24 October 1882**, naming her parents Louis and Marguerite, further supports the identification of the Massachusetts mother with the woman in Joseph's Québec marriage ([FamilySearch source profile](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/L2VP-HYR), E14-09).

The **1850 census of West Stockbridge, Berkshire County, Massachusetts**, enumerated **14 September 1850**, lists **Joseph Jandru (39), Julian (47), Leander (16), Zoé (13), Joseph (9), William (6), and Francis (27)** in **family 307**. The match to the independently established Joseph–Julienne–Zoé family is unusually specific. Francis's placement with this family, his given name, and his approximately matching age strongly support identifying him as their younger brother François Xavier, born in 1824. The census records no kinship relationships; that conclusion is an inference from the correlated family evidence.

**[View the indexed 1850 census entry at FamilySearch](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MD9X-2TH)** — U.S. population census, West Stockbridge, Massachusetts, page 113 / folio 225, family 307; NARA M432, roll 306. This exact FamilySearch record identifier is preserved in the original illustrated evidence packet (E14-07); a FamilySearch login may be required to open the record and scan. The census's reference date was June 1, 1850, although the enumerator wrote the schedule in September.

## The 1851 death and comparison to Franklin

The **12 April 1851 burial at Saint-Joseph-de-Chambly** records a **François Xavier Gendreau, Massachusetts labourer, aged 28**, who drowned at **Saint-Luc on 9 April** ([E14-08, LAFRANCE 5505626](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5505626); Drouin image **d1p_11180369**). The distinctive combination of **full name, Massachusetts association, approximate age, and proximity to the 1850 Massachusetts household** strongly favors identifying the deceased with the census Francis. The burial gives no parents, and the 1850 and 1851 ages are somewhat higher than the 1824 birth implies; these discrepancies are retained rather than erased. A prior PRDH assignment of this burial to the 1823 Châteauguay François (C127) was reported corrected in July 2026, but **that database correction is not independent evidence of parentage**.

Meanwhile, **Franklin Johndro** was enumerated in **St. Clair County, Missouri, in 1850**, in a household with **Phebe and their infant son Isaac, born in Missouri** ([FamilySearch census record](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:V5P7-TS5)). Residence in different states on the 1850 census alone would not make the identities incompatible: travel was possible and the census nominal reference date was June 1. The important distinction is the **independently documented Missouri wife and child versus the reconstructed Massachusetts brother's family, combined with the 1851 death attribution**.

## Conclusion and evidence standard

**Solid elimination on cumulative original-record correlation.** The shared-parent baptism and marriage acts establish the sibling relationship. Joseph, Julienne and Zoé anchor the Massachusetts household; Francis's name, age and placement support identifying him as C140. The subsequent Massachusetts-resident drowning/burial supplies a coherent endpoint incompatible with Franklin's later documented Missouri life. **No single census or burial expressly states all the relationships**, so the identification is still inferential at those two links; the solid classification reflects the strength of their **combined, mutually corroborating** chain rather than a claim of a direct baptism-to-burial parentage match.
