## Biography

Charles Gendron was born on 2 May 1818 and baptized on 6 May 1818 at Île-Perrot (Ste-Jeanne-de-Chantal), the son of Joachim Gendron and Louise Lefebvre, of Beauharnois. He married Adélaïde Poineau, daughter of Louis Poineau and the late Angélique Degré, on 11 November 1845 at Ste-Martine; the act names his parents, then of St-Joachim. He appears at Beauharnois as the father of Charles Toussaint, born on 31 August 1848 and baptized the next day; the act describes Charles as a journalier (day labourer), of Ste-Martine. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 1 September 1848.
