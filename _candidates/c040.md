## Biography

Charles Gendron was born on 2 May 1818 and baptized on 6 May 1818 at Île-Perrot (Ste-Jeanne-de-Chantal), the son of Joachim Gendron and Louise Lefebvre, of Beauharnois. A Charles Gendron married Adélaïde Poineau, daughter of Louis Poineau and the late Angélique Degré, on 11 November 1845 at Ste-Martine. That act names Joachim Gendron and the late Angélique Lefèvre of St-Joachim as his parents, whereas the baptism names Louise Lefèvre. The shared father and maternal surname support the proposed identification, but the maternal given-name difference remains unresolved. The adult Charles and Adélaïde appear at Beauharnois as the parents of Charles Toussaint, born on 31 August 1848 and baptized the next day; the act describes Charles as a journalier (day labourer), of Ste-Martine.
