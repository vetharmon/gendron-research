Original baptism 6 May 1818 Île-Perrot, lower right, names Charles born 2 May, son of Joachim Gendron and Louise Lefebvre of Beauharnois. Marriage 11 November 1845 Ste-Martine, M28 upper right, names Charles, son of Joachim Gendron and Louise Lefebvre of St-Joachim, and Adélaïde Poineau, daughter of Louis Poineau and deceased Angélique Degré. Child baptism 1 September 1848 Beauharnois, B162 lower left, names Charles Toussaint born previous day, son of Charles Gendron, journalier, and Adélaïde Poineault of Ste-Martine. PRDH family 237022 also lists André born 1846. Matching parental identity through marriage to the distinct family continuing in late 1848 supports exclusion against Franklin’s wife and infant son in Missouri in 1850. This is a family-identity inference, not a claim that travel between Québec and Missouri was impossible.
