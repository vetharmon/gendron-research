## Biography

Francois Cyrille Gendreau was born on 9 August 1821 and baptized the next day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier). Family records in the existing research follow him through 1845. He may be the Cyrille Gendreau who married (Marie) Anne Viens — a published Vermont repertoire asserts the marriage took place at Sorel in 1842 and names a daughter, Marie Eugenie, born on 3 September 1848 at Essex and baptized on 23 June 1850 at St. Joseph, Burlington, as the couple's child. The original Vermont baptism has not been inspected, so that later-life trail remains a lead rather than a settled record.

- **[Vermont repertoire, publication PB044](https://www.vtgenlib.org/vermont/PDF/PB044.pdf)** — The Vermont French-Canadian Genealogical Society repertoire entry for Marie Eugenie Gendreau, born 3 September 1848 at Essex and baptized 23 June 1850 at St. Joseph, Burlington, naming Cyrille Gendreau and (Marie) Anne Viens as her parents, with the couple's asserted 1842 marriage at Sorel. It is a derivative publication; the original baptism behind it has not been inspected.
- **[Diocese of Burlington archives](https://www.vermontcatholic.org/about/chancery/archives/)** — The archives office holding the St. Joseph registers. Its page states that new genealogy requests are paused and public research access is closed, so the original record behind the repertoire entry cannot presently be obtained.
