## Follow-up, 16 November 2026

The original **1860 Essex, New York census** (printed page 49) has Joseph Jerdo, 40, and Marria, 36, both born in Canada, with Canadian-born Henry, 16, and Loiza, 15, followed by six New York-born children. Henry and Loiza are worth comparing with the Quebec children; the household does not itself name Marie Anne Viens. The original **1865 census**, page 9, household 54, writes the wife as **Mary, 43**, rather than the index’s Margot. The earlier log preserves the index reading; this is the original-image correction.

A surname-only search of the Vermont Genealogy Library baptism index found **ID 51288, Marie Eugene Gendreau, St Joseph, Burlington**. It may be the Marie Eugenie baptism already cited, but the public result gives no date or parents. Confirm those fields before attaching the entry. The library supplies published transcription pages, not necessarily original-register photographs. A second Burlington entry, **51290, Samuel Laurent Gendreau**, is unassigned; no family connection is claimed.

The cited PB044 PDF is the preliminary matter of the **Swanton and Highgate, VT Baptism & Marriage Repertoire (2008)**. The relevant entry is on printed **XXVI**, PDF page **26**, among records collected from other places. It explicitly names the known grandparents, François Gendreau/Geneviève Bocage and Joseph Viens/Geneviève Provencal. The original Burlington baptism remains the priority.

The GQ link for Marie Louise’s 1845 baptism, act 4349652, displayed an April register image in this inspection; the August target act was not found on it. Retain the index as a lead and locate a correct image before claiming a new original reading.

## New Jerdo family evidence — 25 November 2026

The original [Treasury claims report of 27 February 1889, page 32](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/SERIALSET-02613_00_00-003-0132-0000/pdf/SERIALSET-02613_00_00-003-0132-0000.pdf#page=32) explicitly names **Sorrel and Mary Ann Jerdo as the parents of the deceased Henry Jerdo**. This is new evidence about the Jerdo family, whose connection to C086 remains under investigation.

| Field in the report | Recorded value |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate or claim number | 67218 |
| Settlement number | 5452 |
| Fiscal year in which the expenditure was incurred | 1865 |
| Amount | $61.75 |
| Appropriation | Commutation of rations to prisoners of war in rebel States and soldiers on furlough |

The report is Senate Executive Document 132, 50th Congress, 2nd session, Serial Set 2613. **These numbers are not identified as pension numbers.** The 1889 report date does not establish that both parents were then alive. The entry gives neither the mother's maiden name nor the parents' birthplace or marriage.

The official **1892 Vermont revised roster**, [page 225](https://archive.org/details/cu31924080774148/page/225/mode/1up), records **Henry Jerdo of Burlington**, First Regiment Cavalry: enlisted **21 November 1863**, mustered **1 December 1863**, wounded and taken prisoner **29 June 1864**, and died at **Wilmington, North Carolina, 2 March 1865**. Its [national-cemetery list, page 768](https://archive.org/details/cu31924080774148/page/768/mode/1up), specifies **Company A, Cavalry**, at Wilmington. The two entries are sections of one official publication, not independent service records.

The alphabetical cards add the parents' names and the father's date; the earlier T289 table above retains what those particular cards actually show. Both T288 entries strike through the **Company B** service entry while retaining **Company A, 1st Vermont Cavalry**. This differs from T289's additional-service field and remains unresolved.

The named parents, together with the soldier's explicit **Jeandreau–Jerdo alias**, support correlating the pension claims with **Sorrel and Mary Ann Jerdo** in the Treasury ration-commutation report. That is a comparison across records: the Treasury entry itself supplies no military unit or pension numbers. None of these documents gives Mary A.'s maiden name or identifies Sorelle's Québec parents.

The exact next target is the father's **full dependent pension case, WC 220681 / WO 298858**, under Henry Jeandreau alias Henry Jerdo, together with the mother's **WO 233709**. The approved-case series is [National Archives RG 15, entry A1 12-A, NAID 300020](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/300020). A bounded search by the established surnames, claim numbers and unit did not identify a digitized full case or individual full-case catalogue record. This does not establish that the physical file is missing or that no digitization exists elsewhere. **The full pension papers remain unread.**

Evidence naming **Marie Anne Viens**, the **11 October 1842 Sorel marriage**, or the family's Québec origins would be especially useful in the full case. No new record yet proves that Sorrel Jerdo was Cyrille Gendreau or that Henry was the Québec-born Honoré. The existing New York attachment conflicts also remain unresolved. **C086 remains likely eliminated; the original 1850 Burlington baptism is still outstanding.**
