## Biography

Francois Cyrille Gendreau was born on 9 August 1821 and baptized the next day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier). Québec family records follow him through 1845. A published Vermont repertoire identifies a Cyrille Gendreau and (Marie) Anne Viens, married at Sorel in 1842, as parents of Marie Eugenie, born at Essex on 3 September 1848 and baptized at St. Joseph, Burlington, on 23 June 1850. This is a probable continuation of his family; the original Vermont baptism has not been inspected.
