Original baptism 4 May 1824 Beauharnois, act 2370094, B32 bottom right: Michel born previous day, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. Original marriage 8 August 1848 Beauharnois, act 13401030, M12 right page: Michel Gendron, adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny, marries Angele Nadeau, daughter of deceased Jean Baptiste Nadeau and Suzanne Poitras. Original 17 June 1861 Beauharnois baptism, act 6103891, B112 left page: Simeon born previous day, legitimate son of Michel Gendron, cultivateur, and Angele Nadeau; the father is among those who could not sign. Exact named parental linkage and the continuing married couple in the same parish support a distinct adult identity into 1861, incompatible with Franklin's documented Missouri family.
