## Biography

Michel Gendron was born on 3 May 1824 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. He married Angele Nadeau on 8 August 1848 at Beauharnois; the act names him an adult and again names his parents. Their son Simeon was born on 16 June 1861 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 17 June 1861.
