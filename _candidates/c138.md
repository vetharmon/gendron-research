## Biography

Michel Gendron was born on 3 May 1824 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. He married Angele Nadeau on 8 August 1848 at Beauharnois; the act names him an adult and again names his parents. Their son Simeon was born on 16 June 1861 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois.
