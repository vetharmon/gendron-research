## Biography

- Michel Gendron was born on 3 May 1824 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. [Baptism](#document-act-2370094)
- He married Angele Nadeau on 8 August 1848 at Beauharnois; the act names him an adult and again names his parents. [Marriage, 8 August 1848](#document-act-13401030)
- Their son Simeon was born on 16 June 1861 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois. [Baptism of his son, 17 June 1861](#document-act-6103891)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 8 August 1848 — LAFRANCE act 13401030](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401030)** — His marriage to Angele Nadeau at Beauharnois (entry M12, right page). The act names his parents, Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny, tying the groom to the 1824 baptism in the same parish. The mother’s name is inserted between lines; this act does not state whether the father could sign. Also at [Généalogie Québec (image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/6103891).
