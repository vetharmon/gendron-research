## Biography

Edouard Gendreau was born on 15 March 1823 and baptized the next day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Abraham Gendreau and Marie Pellerin. In the 1861 census of St-Thomas, Montmagny, an Edouard Gendreau — a carpenter (menuisier), aged 37 and unmarried — was enumerated in the household of his parents, following Abraham Gendreau, 75, and Marie Pellerin, 64, and a brother, Thomas, 26, and preceding his younger brother Herménégilde, 23. The census does not label his relationship to the head of household; the identification rests on the names, ages and household together, and on Herménégilde's baptism at Montmagny on 18 December 1837 to the same parents. A marriage at Montmagny on 28 January 1879, of an Edouard Gendreau, carpenter, son of the deceased Abraham Gendreau and the deceased Marie Pelletier, to Marie Normand, widow of Jean Baptiste Patry, may also be his, but the mother's surname there is Pelletier rather than Pellerin, so that record is not certain.
