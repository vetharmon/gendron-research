## Biography

- Charles Alexandre Gendron was born and baptized on 9 September 1828 at Châteauguay, the son of Nicolas Gendron and Suzanne Gaspard. [Baptism](#document-act-4588803)

- He married Julie Auger on 18 January 1858 at Châteauguay, where the act names him — as Charles Gendron — the adult son of Nicolas Gendron and Suzanne Auger. [Marriage](#document-act-13560102)

## Research status

Auger and Gaspard are the two forms of his mother's dit name, linked in the research notes through PRDH profile 1017918 (no source link is recorded for that profile in this file).
