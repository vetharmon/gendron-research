## Biography

- Charles Alexandre Gendron was born and baptized on 9 September 1828 at Châteauguay, the son of Nicolas Gendron and Suzanne Gaspard. [Baptism](#document-act-4588803)

- The adult identification is Charles Gendron, who married Julie Auger on 18 January 1858 at Châteauguay, son of Nicolas Gendron and Suzanne Auger. His parents' 21 July 1817 marriage names Susanne Auger as the daughter of Gaspard Auger and Susanne Poineau. The same father, mother's given name and local family context support the identification; the baptism's Gaspard may substitute the maternal grandfather's given name. That explanation is an inference, and no inspected act explicitly calls the mother Auger dit Gaspard. [Marriage](#document-act-13560102) [Parents' marriage](#document-act-5696286) [Baptism](#document-act-4588803)

## Research status

The solid grade is retained on the combined original-source family evidence. The maternal surname discrepancy remains visible: Suzanne Gaspard in the baptism, Suzanne Auger in the adult marriage, and Susanne Auger, daughter of Gaspard Auger, in the parental marriage. Earlier notes cited a PRDH profile for a dit-name explanation; that profile was not reopened, and the current explanation is based on the inspected parental act rather than a newly verified compiled alias.

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 18 January 1858 — LAFRANCE act 13560102](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13560102)** — Proposed marriage to Julie Auger at Châteauguay. The inspected 1817 parental marriage supplies a concrete family explanation through her father Gaspard Auger; it does not explicitly establish a dit name.

- **[Parents' marriage, 21 July 1817 — LAFRANCE act 5696286](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5696286)** — Nicolas Gendron married Susanne Auger, daughter of Gaspard Auger and Susanne Poineau, at Châteauguay. This original supports the family correlation while leaving the exact reason for the baptism's use of Gaspard inferential.
