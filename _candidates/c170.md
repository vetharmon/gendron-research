## Biography

Joseph Gendron was baptized on 10 November 1825 at St-Jude (St-Hyacinthe). A burial in the same parish may be his: on 15 January 1826 a child named Louis, son of Louis Gendron and Marie Gaudet, aged about two months, who had died on 12 January, was buried at St-Jude. The baptism names the child Joseph and the burial names him Louis; the same parents alone do not establish that the two records are the same child, and the original gives no compound "Joseph Louis" name — a FamilySearch index entry (X4Q9-Z6M3) does give that form, but it is an index reading, not the original.

- **[Baptism, 10 November 1825 — LAFRANCE act 4724636](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4724636)** — His baptism at St-Jude, naming the child Joseph. The difference between this name and the burial's Louis is the unresolved point of his file.
