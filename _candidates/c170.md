## Biography

Joseph Gendron was baptized on 10 November 1825 at St-Jude (St-Hyacinthe). He died in infancy, in all likelihood: a burial at St-Jude on 15 January 1826 names Louis, son of Louis Gendron and Marie Gaudet, who died on 12 January aged about two months — Joseph's exact age. Both originals have been read. The baptism names the child Joseph; the burial names him Louis, his father's name. FamilySearch indexes the baptism as Joseph Louis (X4Q9-Z6M3), but that compound name is absent from the original. Louis and Marie Gaudet baptized twelve children between 1820 and 1835; no other son named Louis appears before 1832, and Joseph has no record after his baptism.
