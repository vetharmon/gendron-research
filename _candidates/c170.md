## Biography


- **Baptism:** Joseph Gendron was baptized on 10 November 1825 at St-Jude (St-Hyacinthe). The original names the child Joseph; FamilySearch's compound index name Joseph Louis is absent from it. [Baptism](#document-act-4724636)
- **Probable infant burial:** A burial at St-Jude on 15 January 1826 names Louis, son of Louis Gendron and Marie Gaudet, who died on 12 January aged about two months, matching Joseph's age. Both originals were read; the child has different given names in the baptism and burial. [Burial](#document-act-4726108) · [Baptism](#document-act-4724636)

## Research status

The existing identification treats infant death as likely. FamilySearch's baptism index is X4Q9-Z6M3. Louis and Marie Gaudet baptized twelve children between 1820 and 1835; no other son named Louis appears before 1832, and Joseph has no record after his baptism. These family-sequence statements are retained from the existing research notes. [Record notes](#evidence-notes)
