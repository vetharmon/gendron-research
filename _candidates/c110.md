## Biography

- **Baptism:** Olivier Gendron was baptized at Saint-Régis on 12 February 1823, entry B17. The archival manuscript copy says he was eight months old, placing his birth about June 1822. [Baptism copy](#document-act-14094771)
- **Parents and residence:** The same copy names Thomas Gendron, a day labourer at an uncertain [O…densburg?] in New York, and Isabelle Texier (Tessier). It gives the father's residence, without explicitly stating the child's birthplace. [Baptism copy](#document-act-14094771)

No later record has been securely connected to him. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Accessible archival copy — 21 November 2026

The [LAC manuscript transcription on C-3025, image 626](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c3025/626), manuscript page 240, records **B17 on 12 February 1823**. Two readers independently read Olivier as born **eight months earlier**, son of **Thomas Gendron**, *journalier* of Ogdensburg, New York, and **Isabelle Texier**. This corroborates the birth estimate of about June 1822.

The sponsors are **Louis Robidoux** and **Catherine Robidoux**. The entry says that they and the father could not sign. It does not state the sponsors' relationship to Olivier or where Olivier was born. These named associates give a more specific route for reconstructing the Ogdensburg parental family.

The volume explicitly describes itself as copied. This is a manuscript transcription, not the autograph parish register. It supplies another accessible source for the same event, and does not establish any later appearance of Olivier. The [LAC catalogue](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/genealogy-family-history/birth-marriage-death-records/parish.html) gives coverage through 1830, so this recovery does not settle the reported Thomas-Olivier burial in 1831.

That 1831 name listing does not identify a parish, exact date, age or parents. Saint-Régis is therefore a place to investigate because of Olivier's baptism, not an established location of the reported burial. Its film gap must not be presented as proof of where that burial occurred or why the listing remains unresolved.

## Berthier comparison resolved in earlier research

The original-record work completed on 19 November 2026 identifies the Berthier navigator through his family. Julienne's burial explicitly names Olivier and Marcel as her brothers. Pierre Olivier's 14 June 1880 burial names **François Gendreau and Marie Lapierre** as his parents, distinguishing him from this son of Thomas Gendron and Isabelle Texier. Marie and Philippe in the 1871 household belong to Marcel's immediate family; they should not be described as Olivier's wife and son.

The earlier research also records discrepancies within that comparison: the burial gives Pierre Olivier an age of about seventy, older than the census ages suggest, and Marcel's 1859 marriage names Marie Bélanger as his mother. The stated sibling relationship may therefore be through their father. These differences remain documented; they do not supply a link to Thomas Gendron and Isabelle Texier.

The remaining work is to trace the **Ogdensburg parental family and the named Robidoux associates**, and to locate a full identifying record behind the reported 1831 Thomas-Olivier burial. The Warwick initial remains unassigned. **Needs work is unchanged**.


## Possible parental marriage — a comparison to test

A [typed transcription of a 19 September 1803 Châteauguay marriage](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2891/790), MG8-G6 volume 3, page 1123, records:

- **Thomas Jeandron**, adult son of Pierre Jeandron and Marie Des-Anges Rufiange.
- **Elisabeth Tessier**, minor daughter of François Tessier and Marguerite Sylvestre.
- Witnesses **Pierre Jeandron** and **François Tessier**, expressly the spouses' fathers. The occupation *laboureur* is attached to the fathers, not expressly to Thomas.

All are described as of the parish. This provides specific parents and witnesses for a possible parental couple, but the transcription does not name Olivier or Ogdensburg. The connection between Elisabeth in this marriage and Isabelle in the 1823 baptism remains to be demonstrated through intervening family records. Treat it as a precisely located comparison, not as an established parental marriage for C110.

## Michigan Durant comparison excluded — 26 November 2026

The newly read [Durant field-note worksheet no. 9, page 8](#document-durant-9-8-jandreau-comparison) identifies a different **Oliver Jandreau**. The form is headed *Census of Aug. 1, 1908* and bears the handwritten date **27 May 1909**, at **Drummonds Isle**. It names **Mary**, marked dead, and her spouse **Tom Jandreau**, P.O. Detour. Their sixth listed child is **Oliver Jandreau, age 12**, with P.O. **Harbor Spgs Mich.**

That age excludes this particular indexed Oliver as the child baptized at eight months old in February 1823. Tom’s age and parents are not entered, and this worksheet supplies no connection to **Thomas Gendron and Isabelle Texier**. The continuation was also read; the separate final and payment rolls were not. This is a new original reading of a rejected comparison, not a later record of C110.

The remaining work stays with the possible parental family, the Robidoux associates, the unlocalized 1831 Thomas-Olivier burial listing and the unassigned Warwick entry. **Needs work remains unchanged.**
