## Biography

Olivier Gendron was baptized on 12 February 1823 at St-Régis (St-François-Régis), the son of Thomas Gendron and Isabelle Tessier. No later record identified. An 'O Gendro' in an 1861 Warwick work household cannot be identified with him, and he appears in neither the Canadian censuses nor the US censuses of 1850 and 1860.
