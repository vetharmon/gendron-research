## Biography

Olivier Gendron was baptized on 12 February 1823 at Saint-Régis (B17). The original says he was eight months old, placing his birth about June 1822. His parents were Thomas Gendron, a labourer of Ogdensburg, New York, and Isabelle Texier (Tessier). The act gives the father's residence; it does not explicitly give the child's birthplace. No later record has been securely connected to him.
