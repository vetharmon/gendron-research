## Biography

- Antoine Gendron was born on 26 November 1827 and baptized the same day at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu, the son of Antoine Gendron and Archange Savaria. [Baptism](#document-act-4525240)

- He married Seraphine Trahan on 17 September 1849 in the same parish, the act naming the same parents. [Marriage](#document-act-13365707)

- A proposed later match is Antoine Gendron, husband of **Sarah Trahan**, who died on 9 July 1858 and was buried at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu on 12 July. The act gives his age as about 34, whereas this candidate would have been 30. Its phrase *vivant cultivateur* describes his lifetime occupation; it does not give a *dit Vital* alias. The spouse-name and age differences qualify this burial identification. [Burial](#document-act-5442916)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 12 July 1858 — LAFRANCE act 5442916](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5442916)** — Proposed burial at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu: Antoine Gendron, husband of **Sarah Trahan**, deceased on 9 July and aged about 34. The original reads *vivant cultivateur*, not *dit Vital*. The spouse’s given name differs from Séraphine in the marriage and the stated age is about four years too old. The parent-linked 1849 marriage remains the secure adult bridge; this burial is not treated as an independently established identity.
- **[Marriage, 17 September 1849 — LAFRANCE act 13365707](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13365707)** — His marriage to Seraphine Trahan at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu, naming his parents, Antoine Gendron and Archange Savaria. (Généalogie Québec's dropdown misnames the groom "Charron"; the original act clearly says Gendron.)
- **[Baptism, 26 November 1827 — LAFRANCE act 4525240](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4525240)** — His baptism at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu: born that day, son of Antoine Gendron and Archange Savaria.
