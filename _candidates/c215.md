## Biography

Antoine Gendron was born on 26 November 1827 and baptized the same day at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu, the son of Antoine Gendron and Archange Savaria. He married Seraphine Trahan on 17 September 1849 in the same parish, the act naming the same parents. He died on 9 July 1858 and was buried at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu on 12 July 1858, the burial naming him as Antoine Gendron dit Vital, husband of Seraphine Trahan.
