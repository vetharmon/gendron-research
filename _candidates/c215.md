## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4525240
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 26 November 1827 at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu names Antoine, born that day, son of Antoine Gendron and Archange Savaria. Original marriage 17 September 1849 there names the same parents and wife Seraphine Trahan. Original burial 12 July 1858 there identifies Antoine Gendron dit Vital, husband of Seraphine Trahan, deceased on the ninth. This matched baptism–marriage–burial chain establishes a death before Franklin’s documented later life and death in 1886. GQ’s dropdown misnames the groom Charron, but the original marriage clearly says Gendron.
