## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2870361
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Reinspection 9 November: original baptism B22 bottom left, 6 March 1818, Louis born same day, son of Francois Gendron and Marie Roger Couillard Despre. The earlier log's Marie Roze reading for the mother is corrected: the maternal line reads Roger; Marie Rose appears separately as the godmother later in the same entry. Original burial, 14 September 1833 Riviere-du-Loup St-Patrice, S32 lower left, names Louis aged fifteen, son of Francois Gendron, cultivateur of that parish, and Marie Roger D[...]. The final maternal surname is partly obscured at the inner page edge and is not claimed fully transcribed. Original parental marriage 8 October 1811 Montmagny names Francois Gendron and Marie Roger Couillard Despre, providing the full parental identity independently of the index. PRDH family 117045, checked again, lists a single Louis, born 6 March 1818, followed by Marie Sophie in 1819 and linked to the September 1833 burial. Corrected maternal given-name agreement, matching father and son, compatible fifteen-year age, original parental marriage and corroborative family sequence support the childhood-death identification. Counted as a supported exclusion; the incomplete burial surname remains explicit. The indexed sequence is corroboration, not proof that no unrecorded child existed.
