## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 6 March 1818 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of François Gendron and Marie Roger Couillard Despre. He died as a boy and was buried on 14 September 1833 at Rivière-du-Loup (St-Patrice), aged fifteen; the burial names his father, a cultivateur (farmer) of that parish, and his mother, though her final surname is partly cut off at the inner edge of the page.
