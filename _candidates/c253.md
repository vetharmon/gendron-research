## Biography

- Pierre Hercule Gendron was born and baptized on 22 May 1829 at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Ambroise Gendron and Genevieve Braconnier. [Baptism](#document-act-3303484)
- He died on 10 June 1829, aged nineteen days, and was buried at Berthierville the next day; the burial names the same child and the same parents. [Burial, 11 June 1829](#document-act-3308855) · [Baptism, 22 May 1829](#document-act-3303484)
