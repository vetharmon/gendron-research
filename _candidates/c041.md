## Biography

Jean Baptiste Olivier Gendron was born on 6 May 1818 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Archange Poirier. He died on 31 May 1819 and was buried the next day at St-Hyacinthe, aged one year; the burial names the same parents.
