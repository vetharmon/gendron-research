## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Jean Baptiste Olivier Gendron was born on 6 May 1818 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Archange, whose surname is written Roireau in the saved act; Poirier is the inherited name form in the candidate record. [Baptism](#document-act-2476727)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 30 May 1819 and was buried on 1 June 1819 at St-Hyacinthe, aged one year; the burial names Pierre Gendron and Archange Roireau dite Laliberté. [Burial](#document-act-2482881)
