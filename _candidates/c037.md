## Biography

Jacques Gendron was born on 5 March 1818 and baptized the next day at St-Michel-de-Bellechasse (La Durantaye), the son of Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denis. He married Angèle Bissonnet, daughter of Pierre Bissonnet and Marguerite Labrecque, on 28 July 1840 at La Durantaye (St-Michel); the act calls him the son of the late Jacques Gendron and of Catherine Denys dite Lapierre. He appears at Lambton as the father of Marie Williamine, born on 3 May 1861 and baptized on 22 May 1861, the act naming Jacques Gendron and Angèle Bissonet of that parish and noting the father absent.
