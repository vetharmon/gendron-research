Original baptism 6 March 1818 St-Michel-de-Bellechasse, left middle, names Jacques born previous day, son of Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denis. Original marriage 28 July 1840, M12 right page, names Jacques son of deceased Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denys dite Lapierre, marrying Angèle Bissonnet, daughter of Pierre Bissonnet and Marguerite Labrecque. Original baptism 22 May 1861 Lambton, B50 upper left, names Marie Williamine born 3 May, daughter of Jacques Gendron and Angèle Bissonet, of that parish; father absent.
