## Biography

Jacques Gendron was born on 5 March 1818 and baptized the next day at St-Michel-de-Bellechasse (La Durantaye), the son of Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denis. He married Angèle Bissonnet, daughter of Pierre Bissonnet and Marguerite Labrecque, on 28 July 1840 at La Durantaye (St-Michel); the act calls him the son of the late Jacques Gendron and of Catherine Denys dite Lapierre. He appears at Lambton as the father of Marie Williamine, born on 3 May 1861 and baptized on 22 May 1861, the act naming Jacques Gendron and Angèle Bissonet of that parish and noting the father absent. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 22 May 1861.
