## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Jacques Gendron was born on 5 March 1818 and baptized the next day at St-Michel-de-Bellechasse (La Durantaye), the son of Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denis. The baptism date is corrected in the register from seven to six March. [Baptism](#document-act-2529541)
- **Marriage:** He married Angèle Bissonnet, daughter of Pierre Bissonnet and Marguerite Labrecque, on 28 July 1840 at La Durantaye (St-Michel); the act calls him the son of the late Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denys dite Lapierre. [Marriage](#document-act-13400947)
- **Later family:** He appears at Lambton as the father of Marie Williamine, born and baptized on 22 May 1861; the act names Jacques Gendron and Angèle Bissonet of that parish and notes the father absent. [Marie Williamine's baptism](#document-act-5744889)
