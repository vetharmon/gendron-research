## Biography

Pierre Aime Gendron was born and baptized on 25 August 1825 at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, the son of Abraham Gendron and Henriette Morand Grimard. As an adult he appears under the name Aime Gendron, a cultivateur living at St-Casimir. He married Marguerite Auger on 7 February 1853 at Deschambault. After her death he married again, on 24 February 1868 at St-Alban, where the act names him as the widower of Marguerite Auger and his bride as Marie Vitaline Auger. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 24 February 1868.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 7 February 1853 — LAFRANCE act 13400852](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400852)** — His marriage to Marguerite Auger at Deschambault (left page). The act names him as Aime Gendron, cultivateur of St-Casimir, adult son of Abraham Gendron and Henriette Grimard, and the bride as the daughter of Jean Auger and Marguerite Trottier; those parents tie the groom to the 1825 baptism ("Grimard" is part of the mother's baptismal surname, Morand Grimard, not a different name). He signed the act. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400852).
- **[Remarriage, 24 February 1868 — Généalogie Québec act 13605745](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13605745)** — His marriage to Marie Vitaline Auger at St-Alban (entry M3, bottom left, continuing at the top right of the same spread). The act names him as Aime Gendron of St-Casimir, widower of the late Marguerite Auger — naming his first wife is what links this groom to the 1853 marriage — and the bride as the daughter of Thomas Auger and Seraphine Hamelin.
- **[Baptism, 25 August 1825 — LAFRANCE act 4566198](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4566198)** — His baptism at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (entry B77, bottom left): Pierre Aime, born that day, son of Abraham Gendron and Henriette Morand Grimard. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4566198).
