## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4566198
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 25 August 1825 Ste-Anne-de-la-Perade, act 4566198, d1p_25630839.jpg B77 bottom left: Pierre Aime born that day, son of Abraham Gendron and Henriette Morand Grimard. Original marriage 7 February 1853 Deschambault, act 13400852, d1p_16581485.jpg left page: Aime Gendron, cultivateur domiciled at St-Casimir, adult son of Abraham Gendron and Henriette Grimard, marries Marguerite Auger, daughter of Jean Auger and Marguerite Trottier. Aime signs. Original remarriage 24 February 1868 St-Alban, act 13605745, d1p_1635a0170.jpg M3 bottom left continuing top right on the same spread: Aime Gendron of St-Casimir, adult widower of deceased Marguerite Auger, marries Marie Vitaline Auger, daughter of Thomas Auger and Seraphine Hamelin. Both parents identify the 1853 groom; Grimard is already part of the baptismal mother's surname, so this is an abbreviation rather than an unsupported new alias. The explicit first-wife link then establishes a separate adult Quebec marital history into 1868, incompatible with Franklin's documented Missouri family. Pierre is omitted in the adult given name; identification rests on parentage and spouse linkage.
