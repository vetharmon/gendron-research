Original remarriage 24 February 1868 St-Alban, act 13605745, d1p_1635a0170.jpg M3 bottom left continuing top right on the same spread: Aime Gendron of St-Casimir, adult widower of deceased Marguerite Auger, marries Marie Vitaline Auger, daughter of Thomas Auger and Seraphine Hamelin. Both parents identify the 1853 groom; Grimard is already part of the baptismal mother's surname, so this is an abbreviation rather than an unsupported new alias. Pierre is omitted in the adult given name; identification rests on parentage and spouse linkage.
