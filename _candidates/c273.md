## Biography

- Celestin Gendron was born on 24 June 1830 and baptized the same day at Ste-Élisabeth (Seigneurie Dautray), the son of Celestin Gendron and Michelle Dupuis. [Baptism](#document-act-4306932)
- He married Adeline Miville dite Deschenes on 2 October 1860; the marriage act calls him the adult son of Celestin Gendron and Rachel Dupuis and places his residence at Ste-Melanie. [Marriage, 2 October 1860](#document-act-13400874)
- A son, Joseph Thomas, born on 7 September 1861, was conditionally baptized on 8 September 1861 after his grandmother, Madame Celestin Gendron, had already given him emergency baptism; his grandfather Celestin Gendron was godfather. [Baptism of his son, 8 September 1861](#document-act-5458480)
- His sister Emerence's 1829 baptism names her mother Michel Dupuis, while her 1851 marriage names her Rachel Dupuis. [Emerence’s baptism](#document-act-4306602) · [Emerence’s marriage](#document-act-13351544)
- These family records support identifying the two names with the same mother. [Emerence’s baptism](#document-act-4306602) · [Emerence’s marriage](#document-act-13351544) · [Parents’ marriage](#document-act-13415827)
## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 2 October 1860 — LAFRANCE act 13400874 (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400874)** — His marriage to Adeline Miville dite Deschenes (entry M20, left page): the groom is the adult son of Celestin Gendron and Rachel Dupuis, residing at Ste-Melanie. The father's name and the mother's Rachel form tie the groom to the household of the 1830 baptism.
- **[Baptism of his son, 8 September 1861 — LAFRANCE act 5458480 (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5458480)** — Joseph Thomas, born the previous day, son of Celestin Gendron and Adeline Miville dite Deschenes (entry B78, beginning bottom right of one image and finishing top left of the [continuation image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/tag/d1p_01510715.jpg)); the godfather is the grandfather Celestin Gendron. The child had already received emergency baptism from his grandmother, Madame Celestin Gendron. The act calls his father a voiturier and states that father and godfather could not sign, although the 1860 marriage explicitly says both spouses signed.
- **[Baptism, 24 June 1830 — LAFRANCE act 4306932](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4306932)** — His baptism at Ste-Élisabeth (entry B112, middle left): born that day, naming his parents, Celestin Gendron and Michelle Dupuis. The [register image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4306932) is the same act at Généalogie Québec.
- **[Baptism of his sister Emerence, 1 March 1829 — LAFRANCE act 4306602 (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4306602)** — Emerence's baptism at Ste-Élisabeth (entry B37, lower right), naming the parents as Celestin Jeandron and Michel Dupuis — the Michel form of the mother's name in an original act of the same household.
- **[Marriage of his sister Emerence, 14 January 1851 — LAFRANCE act 13351544 (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13351544)** — Emerence Gendron's marriage to Pierre Bonnin at Ste-Melanie (entry M3, upper left): the act names her father Celestin Gendron and her mother as Rachel Dupuis, with the father present as a witness and a brother, David Gendron, also named. Together with her 1829 baptism, it supports the Michel-to-Rachel variation within this family. The marriage calls Emerence a minor, although the proposed 1829-born bride would have been 21; this age classification remains a qualification to the sibling comparison.
- **[Parents' marriage, 11 February 1828 — LAFRANCE act 13415827 (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13415827)** — The marriage of Celestin Gendron and his wife: the original act gives the mother's name as "Richel," while its index combines the names.
