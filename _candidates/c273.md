## Biography

Celestin Gendron was born on 24 June 1830 and baptized the same day at Ste-Élisabeth (Seigneurie Dautray), the son of Celestin Gendron and Michelle Dupuis. He married Adeline Miville dite Deschenes on 2 October 1860; the marriage act calls him the adult son of Celestin Gendron and Rachel Dupuis and places his residence at Ste-Melanie. A son, Joseph Thomas, born on 7 September 1861, was baptized on 8 September 1861, with the child's grandfather Celestin Gendron as godfather. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 8 September 1861.

The mother's two names are a variation documented across the family's own records rather than a contradiction: his older sister Emerence's baptism in 1829 gives the mother as Michel Dupuis, while Emerence's marriage in 1851 names her as Rachel Dupuis. No original record explicitly equates Michelle and Rachel or writes a combined name, so the identification rests on that family correlation.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 2 October 1860 — LAFRANCE act 13400874 (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400874)** — His marriage to Adeline Miville dite Deschenes (entry M20, left page): the groom is the adult son of Celestin Gendron and Rachel Dupuis, residing at Ste-Melanie. The father's name and the mother's Rachel form tie the groom to the household of the 1830 baptism.
- **[Baptism of his son, 8 September 1861 — LAFRANCE act 5458480 (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5458480)** — Joseph Thomas, born the previous day, son of Celestin Gendron and Adeline Miville dite Deschenes (entry B78, beginning bottom right of one image and finishing top left of the [continuation image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/tag/d1p_01510715.jpg)); the godfather is the grandfather Celestin Gendron. The act states that father and godfather could not sign, although the 1860 marriage bears an apparent groom's signature — an inconsistency the research file discloses and does not rely on.
- **[Baptism, 24 June 1830 — LAFRANCE act 4306932](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4306932)** — His baptism at Ste-Élisabeth (entry B112, middle left): born that day, naming his parents, Celestin Gendron and Michelle Dupuis. The [register image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4306932) is the same act at Généalogie Québec.
- **[Baptism of his sister Emerence, 1 March 1829 — LAFRANCE act 4306602 (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4306602)** — Emerence's baptism at Ste-Élisabeth (entry B37, lower right), naming the parents as Celestin Jeandron and Michel Dupuis — the Michel form of the mother's name in an original act of the same household.
- **[Marriage of his sister Emerence, 14 January 1851 — LAFRANCE act 13351544 (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13351544)** — Emerence Gendron's marriage to Pierre Bonnin at Ste-Melanie (entry M3, upper left): the act names her father Celestin Gendron and her mother as Rachel Dupuis, with the father present as a witness and a brother, David Gendron, also named. Together with her 1829 baptism, it documents the Michel-to-Rachel variation within one family.
- **[Parents' marriage, 11 February 1828 — LAFRANCE act 13415827 (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13415827)** — The marriage of Celestin Gendron and his wife: the original act gives the mother's name as "Richel," while its index combines the names — a combined form that must not be quoted as though the original act itself wrote it.
