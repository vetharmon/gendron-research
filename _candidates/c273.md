## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4306932
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Follow-up 9 November 2026: The maternal-name variation is now supported by an independent sibling chain, not by treating an index as a literal compound name. Original 1 March 1829 baptism of Emerence at Ste-Elisabeth, act4306602, d1p_01330255.jpg, B37 lower right, records Celestin Jeandron and Michel Dupuis. Original 14 January1851 Ste-Melanie marriage of Emerence Gendron to Pierre Bonnin, act13351544, d1p_01510459.jpg, M3 upper left, names father Celestin Gendron and mother Rachel Dupuis. Her father is present as a witness; the act also names David Gendron as her brother. The marriage calls Emerence a minor without giving an exact age; the baptismal birth would make her21 years10months, so the minority wording is not claimed as exact age agreement. This independently matching child/parental household provides corroboration for the Michel/Michelle-to-Rachel variation in the candidate's own records. Original C273 baptism24June1830 B112 middle left names Celestin, born that day, father Celestin Gendron and mother Michelle Dupuis. Original2October1860 marriage M20 left page identifies adult son Celestin Gendron and Rachel Dupuis, residing Ste-Melanie, marrying Adeline Miville dite Deschenes. Original8September1861 child baptism B78 starts bottom right of d1p_01510714.jpg and finishes top left of d1p_01510715.jpg: Joseph Thomas, born previous day, son Celestin Gendron/Adeline Miville dite Deschenes; godfather Celestin Gendron explicitly called grandfather. The father and godfather are stated unable to sign, whereas the1860 act bears an apparent groom signature. This inconsistency remains disclosed and literacy is not used as an exclusion argument. Parentage, the independently corroborated maternal-name variation and a separate continuing Quebec family in1860-1861 support exclusion from Franklin. The maternal identification remains a reasoned correlation across family originals; no reviewed original explicitly equates Michelle and Rachel or writes a combined name. The1828 parental marriage original says Richel; its index combines names and must not be quoted as an original compound-name bridge. All seven unchanged originals and detailed limitations retained in C273-Sibling-Bridge-2026-11-09.zip.
