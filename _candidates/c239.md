## Biography

Francois Eustache Gendreau was born and baptized on 20 September 1828 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Abraham Gendreau, a farmer, and Marie Pelerin. He died on 29 July 1849 and was buried at Montmagny on 31 July 1849, a farmer like his father, under the name Eustache Gendreau.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 31 July 1849 — LAFRANCE act 4085529](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4085529)** — His burial at Montmagny (lower left, entry S50): Eustache Gendreau, a farmer, died two days earlier, son of Abraham Gendreau and Marie Pellerin. Two differences from the baptism are left as recorded: the burial drops his first given name, Francois, and states his age as twenty-two, against twenty years and ten months elapsed since the baptism. The matching parents, parish and retained given name Eustache are what identify the buried man with this child.
- **[Baptism, 20 September 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4080280](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4080280)** — His baptism at Montmagny (upper left): born the same day, son of Abraham Gendreau, farmer, and Marie Pelerin.
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 119136](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/119136)** — PRDH's compiled family contains only one Eustache, between Henriette (born 26 October 1826) and Pierre (born 4 November 1830); an earlier brother, Francois Ferdinand (born 1 July 1825), died as an infant on 22 November 1825, so there is no competing surviving Eustache in the family sequence.
