## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 18 October 1822 and baptized the same day at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Michel Gendron and Apolline Labossiere. He died on 3 November 1822 and was buried at St-Ours the next day. The burial states his age as fifteen days, one day less than the sixteen days between his birth and death — a small discrepancy in the register, with the child, parents, parish and immediate chronology otherwise matching.
