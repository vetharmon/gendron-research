## Biography

- Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 19 October 1822 and baptized the same day at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Michel Gendron and Apolline Labossiere. [Baptism](#document-act-2631979)

- He died on 3 November 1822 and was buried at St-Ours the next day. The burial states his age as fifteen days, consistent with birth on 19 October and death on 3 November. [Burial](#document-act-2633423) [Baptism](#document-act-2631979)
