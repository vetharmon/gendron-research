## Biography and current assessment

François Régis Gendron was baptized on 7 March 1828 at Beauharnois (Saint-Clément), son of **Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny**. The proposed later identification is **Régis Gendron, age 23, voyageur**, associated with his parents in the 1851/1852 Saint-Clément census, with a **Haut Canada / Upper Canada** notation. The household is the family PRDH reconstructs as family 139402 — twelve listed children through 1844, among whom François Régis is the only son named François — and the census called 1851 was enumerated in January 1852.

That twelve-child list is not a complete family reconstruction: Dina's 1875 marriage, inspected below, also names this couple as her parents. The unexplained younger census entries should therefore remain open identification questions.

The existing **likely** assessment continues to rest on that census identification. The searches below have not supplied a second adult record securely identifying him. His marital history remains unresolved. His absence from witness lists or later index results does not establish death or independently eliminate him.
