## Biography

Francois Regis Gendron was baptized on 7 March 1828 at Beauharnois (St-Clément). He is counted as eliminated on his 1851 census sighting and the absence of any later record — but no adult record has been securely connected to this child, and whether he married is unknown. That gap is the uncertainty in this elimination. The census record itself is not linked in this file.
