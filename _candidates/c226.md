## Biography

- François Régis Gendron was born and baptized on 7 March 1828 at Saint-Clément-de-Beauharnois, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. [Baptism](#document-act-4253808)

- A probable later record is the 1851/1852 census: Régis Gendron, 23 at his next birthday, a voyageur, follows Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. The residence column says “Haut Canada” (Upper Canada), while the continuation marks him as a male resident family member and leaves the absent-member columns blank. The family link is a correlation; no explicit son relationship is stated. [Census — Régis Gendron](#document-source-002)

## Research status

No second adult record has been identified. PRDH family 139402 lists twelve children, with François Régis as the only son named François. Dina's 1875 marriage also names this couple as her parents, adding a child absent from that list.

### Family lead through Pierre Gendron / Peter Plush

[Brother Pierre Gendron, C172](../c172/), born in 1825 to the same parents, is probably Peter Plush of Missouri and Michigan. His documented American family used Gendron/Jondron as well as Plush, and the parental family used the dit name Peloche. The 1849 St. Charles marriage, 1853 baptism of daughter Aspasie and 1860 Delta County household now provide a specific family route to investigate.

Check the American family's Catholic baptisms and marriages for a sponsor or witness named Régis or François, and search the St. Charles and Delta County records under Gendron, Jondron, Peloche, Ploche and Plush. No record currently places Régis in either locality; this is a new search route through his probable American brother, not an additional appearance or an elimination of Régis.
