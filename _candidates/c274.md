## Biography

Raymond Gendron was born and baptized on **13 August 1830** at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud. The saved original baptism, **B34**, names his parents as **Joseph Gendron**, *cultivateur*, and **Marguerite Allair**. It says he was born that day. No later record has been connected to him.

## Documents

- **[Baptism, 13 August 1830](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4185082)** — LAFRANCE act 4185082; [FamilySearch register image](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G99Q-ZND4).
- **[Hilaire Gendron’s marriage, 22 April 1872](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13637744)** — St-Jean-de-Matha, M11. Hilaire, a merchant living at St-Michel-des-Saints, was the adult son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire of St-Hugues. He married Henriette Comptois. The original was inspected on 15 November 2026. Raymond is not named; this is a record of his family, not evidence of his own later life.

## Assessment

No match was identified in the earlier searches of the 1851, 1861 and 1871 census indexes. Those search results do not establish his absence from Canada or support an elimination.

## Rejected matches checked 16 November 2026

The [Raymond Gendron buried at Beauharnois on 17 April 1889](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L999-RLB5) was one year old, the son of Louis Gendron and Sophie Lepage. The original, S29, was reread; this confirms the rejection already recorded in the private notes on 14 November. He is not the Raymond baptized in 1830.

Three burial indexes found through Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire also concern other children: an unnamed male baptized in emergency on the day of his burial, 16 April 1831 (act 4186621); Felonise, aged two years and three and a half months, buried 15 October 1846 (4378794); and Jean Baptiste, aged nine, buried 1 February 1847 (4378809). These were index checks; the originals were not inspected in this pass. No later record was connected to Raymond, and his assessment remains **needs work**.
