## Biography

Raymond Gendron was baptized on 13 August 1830 at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud (St-François-de-Sales); the baptism index names his parents as Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allair. No later record identified; he is in neither the 1851 nor the 1861 census index.
