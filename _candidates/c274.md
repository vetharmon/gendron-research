## Biography

Raymond Gendron was baptized on 13 August 1830 at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud (St-François-de-Sales); the baptism index names his parents as Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allair. Nothing more of his life has been connected to him. A later search of his parents' records, extended to 1918, brought up marriages of other children of the couple and two burials that were examined and are not his — an anonymous male infant buried in 1831, and Felonise, buried in 1846 aged two years — and no record of Raymond himself was adopted from it. These follow-up checks rest on indexes rather than newly verified originals, and his later life remains open.
