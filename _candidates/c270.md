## Biography

Augustin Gendron was born on 10 May 1827 and baptized the same day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Francois Gendron, a cultivateur, and Josephte Masson. He died on 31 May 1830, aged three years, and was buried at Berthierville on 1 June 1830. His burial act was incorrectly presented as a baptism in the LAFRANCE index, which generated this duplicate entry; Augustin himself is candidate C200.

## Documents

This is the act the index also lists as a "baptism" of 1 June 1830 — the error that created this entry.
- **[Baptism, 10 May 1827 — LAFRANCE act 3303028](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3303028)** — His actual baptism at Berthierville (upper left): born that day, naming his parents, Francois Gendron and Josephte Masson. The child, father, parish and age in the two acts match, confirming a single short life rather than two children.
