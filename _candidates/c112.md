## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born on 26 February and baptized on 1 March at Rimouski (St-Germain), the son of Prudence (Prudent) Gendron and Henriette Chamard. The year written in the baptism act appears to read 1822, but the register around it, and the indexes, place the act in 1823. On 30 January 1849 he married Rose Migneau; the act names him as a cultivateur and the adult son of Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard, and Rose as the minor daughter of the deceased Antoine Migneau and Marie Anne Tremblay. (The indexes give the marriage year as 1847; the act itself reads 1849.) His son Francois was born on 29 January 1853 and baptized the next day at Rimouski, the child of Pierre Gendron and Rosalie Migneault.
