## Biography

- Pierre Gendron was born on 26 February and baptized on 1 March at Rimouski, son of Prudence (Prudent) Gendron and Henriette Chamard. The act's written year appears to be 1822; its register context and indexes place it in 1823. [Baptism](#document-act-2671060)
- He married Rose Migneau on 30 January 1849. The act calls him a farmer and adult son of Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard, and Rose the minor daughter of deceased Antoine Migneau and Marie Anne Tremblay. The index gives 1847, while the act reads 1849. [Marriage](#document-act-13401062)
- Their son François was born on 29 January 1853 and baptized the next day at Rimouski; the mother is named Rosalie Migneault. [François's baptism](#document-act-5656712)
