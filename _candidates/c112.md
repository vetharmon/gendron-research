## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2671060
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Three originals inspected and downloaded 9 November. Baptism at top right, 1 March, names Pierre born 26 February, son of Prudence Gendron and Henriette Chamard. IMPORTANT YEAR CONFLICT: the act's written year appears mil huit cent vingt deux (1822), while the enclosing register, neighboring acts and PRDH/LAFRANCE assign 1823. The stable candidate date is retained as the indexed register placement, not claimed as an exact transcription. Marriage M10 right middle, 30 January, names Pierre Gendron, cultivateur, adult son of Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard, marrying Rose Migneau, minor daughter of deceased Antoine Migneau and Marie Anne Tremblay. IMPORTANT INDEX CONFLICT: the marriage original explicitly reads quarante neuf (1849), consistent with its 1849 register; PRDH/LAFRANCE index 1847. Bride's maternal wording Marie Anne Tremblay differs from the PRDH reconstructed Madeleine Marie Tremblay; that reconstruction is not substituted for the original. Child baptism B9 upper right, 30 January 1853 Rimouski, names Francois born previous day, son of Pierre Gendron and Rosalie Migneault; no attendance or signature of the father is claimed. Original parental pair links baptism and marriage despite Prudence/Prudent spelling, and the matching spouse surname plus Rose/Rosalie form and continuing family support exclusion from Franklin's Missouri identity. The 1849 marriage and 1853 family establish a separate marital identity; exclusion does not depend on deciding the baptism's one-year discrepancy. PRDH profile 864308/family 254175 corroborate but do not override the manuscript discrepancies.
