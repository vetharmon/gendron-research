## Biography

Joseph Gendron was born on 7 September 1822 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Joseph Gendron, a cultivateur, and Therese Vien. On 19 October 1847 he married Zoe Laroque at Ste-Rosalie; the act names him as the adult son of the deceased Joseph Gendron and Therese Viens, and his brother Francois is also named in it. He appears again at St-Hyacinthe on 18 September 1858, when his son Francois was baptized on the day of his birth; by then Joseph was working as a journalier, and the act notes that he could not write.
