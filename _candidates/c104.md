## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2478670
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Three originals downloaded and manually read 9 November. Baptism B349 lower left, 8 September 1822 St-Hyacinthe: Joseph born previous day, son of Joseph Gendron, cultivateur, and Therese Vien. Marriage M11 lower right, 19 October 1847 Ste-Rosalie: Joseph Gendron, cultivateur, adult son of deceased Joseph Gendron and Therese Viens, to Zoe Laroque, minor daughter of deceased Charles Laroque and Josephte Cadoret; Francois Gendron is named as groom's brother. Child baptism B183 right middle/lower, 18 September 1858 St-Hyacinthe Cathedral: Francois born same day, son of Joseph Gendron, journalier, and Zoe Laroque of that parish. IMPORTANT: the act states that the father could not write; the visible Josephte Gendron signature is the godmother's, not the father's. This corrects an initial progress description before close inspection. Matching parental pair in baptism/marriage and the continuing distinct marital family in 1858 support exclusion. Vien/Viens is a spelling variation, retained as read. PRDH profile 851661 and family 247525 corroborate the chain. The 1858 record supplies family evidence, not a claimed paternal signature.
