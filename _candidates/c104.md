## Biography

- Joseph Gendron was born on 7 September 1822 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Joseph Gendron, a cultivateur, and Therese Vien. [Baptism](#document-act-2478670)

- On 19 October 1847 he married Zoe Laroque at Ste-Rosalie; the act names him as the adult son of the deceased Joseph Gendron and Therese Viens, and his brother Francois is also named in it. [Marriage](#document-act-13497824)

- He appears again at St-Hyacinthe on 18 September 1858, when his son Francois was baptized on the day of his birth; by then Joseph was working as a journalier, and the act records that he could not sign. [Baptism of his son](#document-act-5434908)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 19 October 1847 — LAFRANCE act 13497824](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13497824)** — His marriage to Zoe Laroque at Ste-Rosalie (lower right, entry M11). The act names his parents, Joseph Gendron and Therese Viens, tying the groom to the 1822 baptism, and names the bride as the minor daughter of the deceased Charles Laroque and Josephte Cadoret. Also at [Généalogie Québec (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13497824). PRDH's compiled profile (no. 851661) and family (no. 247525) corroborate the chain; no link is recorded for them.
- **[Baptism of his son, 18 September 1858 — Généalogie Québec act 5434908](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5434908)** — Francois, born the same day, son of Joseph Gendron and Zoe Laroque, baptized at St-Hyacinthe Cathedral (right side, entry B183). The act states that the father could not write; the Josephte Gendron signature on the act is the godmother's, not his.
- **[Baptism, 8 September 1822 — LAFRANCE act 2478670](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2478670)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe (lower left, entry B349): born the previous day, son of Joseph Gendron and Therese Vien. Also at [Généalogie Québec (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/2478670).
