## Biography

Philippe Gendron was born on 1 June 1828 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. He died on 19 June 1828 and was buried at Châteauguay on 21 June 1828, aged about three weeks.
