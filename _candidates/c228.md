## Biography

- Philippe Gendron was born and baptized on 2 June 1828 at Châteauguay, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. [Baptism](#document-act-4588729)

- He died on 18 June 1828 and was buried at Châteauguay on 20 June 1828, aged about three weeks. [Burial](#document-act-4592535)
