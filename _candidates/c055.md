## Biography

Edouard Gendron was born on 10 September 1819 and baptized the same day at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud (St-François-de-Sales), the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Anne Ruelle. He died on 15 October 1819, aged one month and six days, and was buried at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud on 17 October 1819.
