## Biography

Abraham Gendron — the index records him as Abraham Jandron — was baptized on 17 September 1817 at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Jean Jandron, a forgeron (blacksmith), and Charlotte Ladebauche. No qualifying burial before 1850 has been established for him, and a limited follow-up search on 10 November 2026 found no later endpoint; what became of him is not known.
