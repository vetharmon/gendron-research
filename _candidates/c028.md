## Biography

Abraham Gendron — the index records him as Abraham Jandron — was baptized on 17 September 1817 at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Jean Jandron, a forgeron (blacksmith), and Charlotte Ladebauche. No later record identified; he is in neither the 1851 nor the 1861 census index.
