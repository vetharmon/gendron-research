## Biography

Abraham Gendron — the index records him as Abraham Jandron — was baptized on 17 September 1817 at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Jean Jandron, a forgeron (blacksmith), and Charlotte Ladebauche. He has no later record in Québec: no marriage or burial, and he is in none of the Canadian censuses of 1851, 1861 or 1871.

A man who may be him appears in Vermont. An Abraham Johndro, born in Canada, married Amelia Campano at Johnson, Vermont on 20 July 1839, giving his residence as Burlington. In 1850 he is at Burlington, aged 34, working as a hatter, with Amelia, 30, also born in Canada, and three Vermont-born children. By 1870 he is at Orange, New Jersey — then the centre of the felt hat trade — as Abraham Johndrew, 56, working in a felt hat factory, with his wife Emily, 49, and five children, the older ones born in Vermont and the younger in New Jersey. He died at Newark on 15 December 1881, recorded as Abm. Jondreau, aged 67, a hatter, born in Canada.

The identification is possible, not proven. His American ages imply a birth in 1814–1816, a year or three before this baptism, and no American record names his parents: the New Jersey death record gives them as unknown, and the Vermont marriage card leaves the parent fields blank. No other Abraham Gendron or Jandron of his generation appears in the Québec registers.
