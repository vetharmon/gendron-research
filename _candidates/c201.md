## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 10 June 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon. He married Adélaïde Auger on 11 August 1851 at Deschaillons; the act names him as the adult son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon of St-Pierre, and Adélaïde as the daughter of Barthélemy Auger and Germaine Laliberté. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 11 August 1851.
