## Biography

- Louis Gendron was born on 10 June 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon. [Baptism](#document-act-3367413)

- He married Adélaïde Auger on 11 August 1851 at Deschaillons; the act names him as the adult son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon of St-Pierre, and Adélaïde as the daughter of Barthélemy Auger and Germaine Laliberté. [Marriage](#document-act-13401009)
