## Biography

Louis Narcisse Gendreau was born on 7 September 1820 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Francois Gendreau and Angelique Clavet. He died on 4 January 1821, aged four months, and was buried at Montmagny on 7 January 1821; the burial names the same parents.
