## Biography

- Louis Narcisse Gendreau was born on 7 September 1820 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Francois Gendreau and Angelique Clavet. [Baptism](#document-act-2870654)

- He died on 4 January 1821, aged four months, and was buried at Montmagny on 7 January 1821; the burial names the same parents. [Burial](#document-act-2872472)
