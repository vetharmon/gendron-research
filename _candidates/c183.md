## Assessment and research

Louis Brunelle's **inventory, minute 3796, 9 May 1831**, concerns the community of property of **Pierre Gendron and deceased Marie Louise Daniel** at Saint-Hyacinthe. Pierre acts in his own interest and as tutor to **Élisabeth Gendron, aged six**, a minor child of that marriage. **François Daniel** appears as her **maternal grandfather and subrogated tutor**. The opening subsequently describes the minor as entitled to claim as heir in her mother's succession and cites a tutorship certificate dated **7 May 1831**. The exact inheritance formula is not included in the selected partial transcription. [Inventory](#document-source-007)

The immediately following **public sale, minute 3797**, also dated **9 May 1831**, identifies the father as tutor to **“l’enfant issu de son mariage”** with deceased Marie Louise Daniel and refers to the inventory made that day. The singular child wording reinforces the inventory's guardianship context. These are **related acts from the same estate proceeding**, not independent family enumerations. Both complete acts were inspected, including their covers and closings; neither names the younger Pierre. [Sale](#document-brunelle-3797-1831-05-09)

C183 would have turned **five** on the date of these proceedings. A surviving minor son of the deceased mother would be expected to need representation in this estate alongside his six-year-old sister. His absence from this specific guardianship context therefore supports **probable childhood death before 9 May 1831**. That conclusion is an inference about the scope of the records. An omission or separate representation remains possible. Neither act uses an observed **seule** or **unique** to declare Élisabeth the sole surviving child, and neither expressly states C183's death. The absence of a burial or other direct death evidence keeps the assessment at **likely**. [Baptism](#document-act-2995012) · [Inventory](#document-source-007) · [Sale](#document-brunelle-3797-1831-05-09)

### Identifying the parental family

C183's original baptism names **Marie Daniel**. The estate's fuller **Marie Louise Daniel** is supported by the previously audited originals within this Saint-Hyacinthe family: **C136 Jean-Baptiste's baptism of 31 March 1824** names Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Daniel, while his linked **burial of 9 March 1825** names Pierre Gendron and Marie Daniel. The burial's stated age of approximately eight months differs from the approximately eleven months elapsed since the linked birth; that discrepancy remains recorded. These are prior original readings, compared for this assessment, rather than newly inspected parish acts. [C136 baptism](#document-act-2479376) · [C136 burial](#document-act-3002858) · [C136's full records](../c136/)

**C114 Louis's 8 March 1823 baptism** also names Marie Louise Daniel. **C094 Pierre's 11 January 1822 baptism** gives Marie Daniel and sponsors François Daniel and Josephte Simoneau. C094's baptism does not state sponsor kinship; the 1831 inventory itself supplies François Daniel's relationship as maternal grandfather. The repeated parental couple, parish and maternal name forms support identifying the estate family with C183's parents while retaining the wording of each source. ## Next identifying records

The priority is the **7 May 1831 tutorship certificate** cited in the inventory, issued through the **Court of King's Bench for the district of Montréal**. Its original application, appointments and any attached family testimony could establish which minor children were represented and test the inference drawn from the inventory. The certificate itself has not been inspected, and no conclusion about its contents is assumed. A burial for Pierre between his **9 May 1826 birth** and **9 May 1831** would provide direct evidence.
