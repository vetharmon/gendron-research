## Biography

## Exact estate target located — 21 November 2026

[BAnQ’s official inventory index](https://www2.banq.qc.ca/archives/genealogie_histoire_familiale/ressources/bd/recherche.html?id=INVENTAIRE_MONTREAL_20170823&2=Gendron&3=&4=&5=&6=&7=Daniel&8=&9=&10=&11=&12=&13=&21=) lists **Pierre Gendron and Marie-Louise Daniel**, with the woman marked **deceased**, residence **Saint-Hyacinthe**, and act date **9 May 1831**. The archival reference is **Archives nationales à Montréal, CN602,S16, greffe de Louis Brunelle**.

This is an inspected official finding aid, not a reading of the original inventory. It gives no minute number, heirs, children or guardian. The date falls five years after C183’s birth; it must not be mistaken for evidence that he was alive on his fifth birthday.

The fuller maternal name has an independently read family comparison. C114 Louis’s saved **8 March 1823 baptism, B95**, names **Marie Louise Daniel** and sponsors **Louis Langelier and Marie Bernard**. C094 Pierre’s **11 January 1822 baptism, B20**, names **Marie Daniel** and sponsors **François Daniel and Josephte Simoneau**. Neither act states sponsor kinship. These source-specific names help correlate the estate index with the family while preserving C183’s own baptismal wording.
