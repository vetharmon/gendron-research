## Biography

François Gendron was born on 6 April 1823 and baptized the next day at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), son of François Geandron and Marie Anne Dupont. On 5 August 1845 he married Mathilde Saulnier; the marriage calls his mother Élisabeth Dupont. His brother Antoine’s marriage on 30 September 1851 names their mother Marie Anne Élisabeth Dupont and expressly names François and Joseph Gendron as Antoine’s brothers. Antoine’s 1826 baptism uses Marie Anne Dupont. These original family acts connect the maternal name forms.

François remarried at Contrecoeur on 30 September 1856 as the widower of Mathilde Saulnier, marrying Catherine Renolds. The available opening of that act was checked; its following-page closing remains unverified.
