## Biography

- François Gendron was born on 6 April 1823 and baptized the next day at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), son of François Geandron and Marie Anne Dupont. [Baptism](#document-act-2736829)
- On 5 August 1845 he married Mathilde Saulnier; the marriage calls his mother Élisabeth Dupont. [First marriage, 5 August 1845](#document-source-005)
- His brother Antoine’s marriage on 30 September 1851 names their mother Marie Anne Élisabeth Dupont and expressly names François and Joseph Gendron as Antoine’s brothers. [Brother Antoine’s marriage, 30 September 1851](#document-act-13400860)
- Antoine’s 1826 baptism uses Marie Anne Dupont. [Antoine’s baptism, 14 September 1826](#document-act-4594379)
- These original family acts connect the maternal name forms. [First marriage](#document-source-005) · [Antoine’s marriage](#document-act-13400860) · [Antoine’s baptism](#document-act-4594379)
- François remarried at Contrecoeur on 30 September 1856 as the widower of Mathilde Saulnier, marrying Catherine Renolds. [Record](#document-act-13400915)
- The available opening of that act was checked; its following-page closing remains unverified. [Remarriage opening](#document-act-13400915)
## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Baptism, 7 April 1823 — act 2736829](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2736829)** — Born the previous day to François Geandron and Marie Anne Dupont; sponsors Antoine Gendron and Marguerite Fortier. The saved image is of the parish register photographed at the presbytery.
- **[First marriage, 5 August 1845](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L99Q-M1BD)** — François, adult son of François Gendron and Élisabeth Dupont, marries Mathilde Saulnier. Both the opening (image 627 of 644) and the [continuation](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-899Q-M1R8) were inspected.
- **[Brother Antoine’s marriage, 30 September 1851 — act 13400860](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400860)** — Antoine Gendron and Hermine Cormier. The groom’s mother is named Marie Anne Élisabeth Dupont; François and Joseph Gendron are identified as his brothers.
- **[Antoine’s baptism, 14 September 1826 — act 4594379](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4594379)** — Born and baptized that day to François Gendron and Marie Anne Dupont. Together with his marriage, this supplies the maternal-name bridge.
- **[Remarriage, 30 September 1856, Contrecoeur, M13](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L99Q-9Q92)** — François Gendron, widower of Mathilde Saulnier, and Catherine Renolds. The Drouin/LAFRANCE image is another filming of this parish original, not independent civil-copy corroboration.
