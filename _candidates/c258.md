## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2970204
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 23 June 1829 at L'Islet names Remi, born the previous day, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Beaulieu. Original marriage 25 January 1848 at St-David names Jeremie Gendron, minor son of Pierre Abraham Gendron and deceased Magdeleine Beaulieu, marrying Hermine Allard. New original bridge inspected 9 November: parents' marriage, 18 April 1809 St-Jean-Port-Joli, d1p_10381025.jpg, lower left continuing at upper right, identifies Pierre Abraham Gendron and Marie Magdeleine Hudon dite Beaulieu. This supplies the fuller paternal name and the compound maternal name behind Pierre/Pierre Abraham and Marie/Magdeleine. Original child baptism 6 October 1851 at St-David, beginning bottom right on d1p_20211245.jpg and ending top left on d1p_20211246.jpg, names Louis Remi, born the previous day, son of Remi Gendron and Hermine Allard. Thus Remi recurs in the adult household with the same wife as Jeremie. PRDH family 111845 corroborates the parental family and profile 854521 links the events. The original parental marriage plus the repeated Remi-Hermine Allard household support the identity chain and a separate continuing family in 1851. Remi/Jeremie is disclosed as a name variation inferred from this combined evidence, not as identical wording in the baptism and marriage. No claim of the father's attendance is made from the child's baptism.
