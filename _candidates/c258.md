## Biography

- Remi Gendron was born on 22 June 1829 and baptized the next day at L'Islet (Notre-Dame-du-Bon-Secours), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Magdeleine Hudon dite Beaulieu. [Baptism](#document-act-2970204)
- On 25 January 1848 he married Hermine Allard at St-David, the act naming the groom Jeremie Gendron, adult son of Pierre Abraham Gendron and the deceased Magdeleine Beaulieu; the baptism itself names his mother Marie Magdeleine Hudon dite Beaulieu; the parents’ marriage is cited below for the father’s fuller name. [Marriage](#document-act-13400971) · [Parents’ marriage](#document-act-5712241)
- On 6 October 1851 a son, Louis Remi, born the previous day to Remi Gendron and Hermine Allard, was baptized at St-David. [Louis Remi’s baptism](#document-act-5353117)
- The family records connect the names Remi and Jeremie. [Marriage](#document-act-13400971) · [Louis Remi’s baptism](#document-act-5353117)
## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 25 January 1848 — LAFRANCE act 13400971](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400971)** — His marriage to Hermine Allard at St-David, naming the groom Jeremie, adult son of Pierre Abraham Gendron and the deceased Magdeleine Beaulieu. His son's baptism belongs with this record: on 6 October 1851, at St-David, Louis Remi, born the previous day, was baptized as the son of Remi Gendron and Hermine Allard (LAFRANCE act 5353117, beginning bottom right of one page and ending top left of the next) — the same wife under the name Remi.
- **[Parents' marriage, 18 April 1809 — Généalogie Québec act 5712241](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5712241)** — The marriage of Pierre Abraham Gendron and Marie Magdeleine Hudon dite Beaulieu at St-Jean-Port-Joli (lower left of the page, continuing at upper right). It gives the father's fuller name and the mother's compound name, bridging Pierre/Pierre Abraham and Marie/Magdeleine Beaulieu between the 1829 baptism and the 1848 marriage.
- **[Baptism, 23 June 1829 — LAFRANCE act 2970204](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2970204)** — His baptism at L'Islet: born the previous day, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Magdeleine Hudon dite Beaulieu, with the father named Pierre and the mother’s full name written out.
- **[PRDH-IGD family 111845](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/111845)** — PRDH's compiled record for his parents' family; PRDH's profile 854521 links his own events, corroborating the parental family behind the chain above.

- **[Original-source audit, 23 November 2026 — marriage act 13400971](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400971)** — The original calls Jérémie an adult son, correcting the former “minor” reading. The 1829 child would have been 18, so the legal-age description is inconsistent with that baptism. The mother’s full name is already present in the baptism, and the 1851 child’s baptism names Rémi with the same wife, Hermine Allard.
