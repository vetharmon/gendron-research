## Biography

- Remi Gendron was born on 22 June 1829 and baptized the next day at L'Islet (Notre-Dame-du-Bon-Secours), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Beaulieu. [Baptism](#document-act-2970204)
- On 25 January 1848 he married Hermine Allard at St-David, the act naming the groom Jeremie Gendron, minor son of Pierre Abraham Gendron and the deceased Magdeleine Beaulieu; his parents' own marriage (below) supplies the fuller forms of both parents' names behind the shorter forms in his baptism. [Marriage](#document-act-13400971) · [Parents’ marriage](#document-act-5712241)
- On 6 October 1851 a son, Louis Remi, born the previous day to Remi Gendron and Hermine Allard, was baptized at St-David. [Louis Remi’s baptism](#document-act-5353117)
- The family records connect the names Remi and Jeremie. [Marriage](#document-act-13400971) · [Louis Remi’s baptism](#document-act-5353117)
## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 25 January 1848 — LAFRANCE act 13400971](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400971)** — His marriage to Hermine Allard at St-David, naming the groom Jeremie, minor son of Pierre Abraham Gendron and the deceased Magdeleine Beaulieu. His son's baptism belongs with this record: on 6 October 1851, at St-David, Louis Remi, born the previous day, was baptized as the son of Remi Gendron and Hermine Allard (LAFRANCE act 5353117, beginning bottom right of one page and ending top left of the next) — the same wife under the name Remi.
- **[Parents' marriage, 18 April 1809 — Généalogie Québec act 5712241](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5712241)** — The marriage of Pierre Abraham Gendron and Marie Magdeleine Hudon dite Beaulieu at St-Jean-Port-Joli (lower left of the page, continuing at upper right). It gives the father's fuller name and the mother's compound name, bridging Pierre/Pierre Abraham and Marie/Magdeleine Beaulieu between the 1829 baptism and the 1848 marriage.
- **[Baptism, 23 June 1829 — LAFRANCE act 2970204](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2970204)** — His baptism at L'Islet: born the previous day, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Beaulieu, in the shorter forms of his parents' names.
- **[PRDH-IGD family 111845](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/111845)** — PRDH's compiled record for his parents' family; PRDH's profile 854521 links his own events, corroborating the parental family behind the chain above.
