## Biography

Remi Gendron was born on 22 June 1829 and baptized the next day at L'Islet (Notre-Dame-du-Bon-Secours), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Beaulieu. On 25 January 1848 he married Hermine Allard at St-David, the act naming the groom Jeremie Gendron, minor son of Pierre Abraham Gendron and the deceased Magdeleine Beaulieu; his parents' own marriage (below) supplies the fuller forms of both parents' names behind the shorter forms in his baptism. On 6 October 1851 a son, Louis Remi, born the previous day to Remi Gendron and Hermine Allard, was baptized at St-David. The Remi/Jeremie difference between the baptism and the marriage is a name variation inferred from this combined evidence, not the same wording in both acts. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 6 October 1851.

His son's baptism belongs with this record: on 6 October 1851, at St-David, Louis Remi, born the previous day, was baptized as the son of Remi Gendron and Hermine Allard (LAFRANCE act 5353117, beginning bottom right of one page and ending top left of the next) — the same wife under the name Remi, though his file's sources carry no link for that act.
