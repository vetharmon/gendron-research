## Biography

- Thomas Gendreau was born on 11 November 1829 and baptized the next day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne. [Baptism](#document-act-4080442)
- He died on 27 November 1829, fifteen days old, and was buried at Montmagny on 30 November 1829. [Burial, 30 November 1829](#document-act-4083984)
