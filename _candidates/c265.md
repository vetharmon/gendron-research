## Biography

Thomas Gendreau was born on 11 November 1829 and baptized the next day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne. He died on 27 November 1829, fifteen days old, and was buried at Montmagny on 30 November 1829.
