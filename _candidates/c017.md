## Biography

Antoine Élisée Gendron was born on 3 February 1817 and baptized the same day at Neuville (St-François-de-Sales), the son of Pierre Gendron and Christine Anger. On 9 July 1838 he married Julie Clermont at Neuville. After her death he married again, on 24 January 1843 at Neuville, where the act names him as the widower of Julie Clermont and his bride as Marie Anne Vézina. He appears again as the father of Joseph Éleusippe, born and baptized on 9 August 1861 at Québec (St-Roch), with Marie Anne Vézina as the mother. Re-reading that original confirms the father's full name as **Antoine Élisée Gendron**, his occupation as **menuisier** (joiner), and his signature as **Élisée Gendron**.

## Winchester family comparison — 21 November 2026

The Massachusetts family investigated under **[C034 Amand Gendron]({{ '/candidates/c034/' | relative_url }})** has a strong alternative identification in C017's family. The original **1880 Winchester census**, ED 385, sheet 45, lists **Antoine Gendron, 63, carpenter**, wife **Marie A., 59**, children **Charles E. and Marie E., 32, explicitly marked twins**, **Marie Elvina, 28**, and **Joseph Eleusippe, 17**, plus nephew **Victor Le Conte, 23**. All were recorded as born in Canada. The uncommon son's name now has an exact, independently inspected Québec baptism in C017's established family.

The **[6 November 1942 Winchester Star, page 1, column 5](https://archive.org/details/WinStar_100242_122542/page/n42/mode/1up)** supplies another original comparison. Its obituary reports **Joseph E. Gendron's death on 3 November 1942, aged 81**, at 22 Calumet Road. It says he was born in **Québec City**, moved to Winchester in **1866 at age 5**, and married **Florence Avis Holbrook in 1890**; their son **Courtenay H.** is named. This origin and chronology fit the 1861 St-Roch baptism, but the obituary gives neither his parents nor an exact birth date.

This is a **strong alternate-family correlation, not a completed migration proof**. The 1861 Québec record securely belongs to C017, while the Winchester household and obituary remain comparison records. The next decisive checks are Joseph's **1890 marriage** and **3 November 1942 death registration** for **Antoine Élisée and Marie Anne Vézina**, supported by the Cap-Santé twin and Malvina baptisms. The original census does not identify Marie E. as Mrs. Joseph Moffett or establish the nephew's parents. C017's existing **solid elimination**, based on his documented Québec family, remains unchanged.
