## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Antoine Élisée Gendron was born on 3 February 1817 and baptized the same day at Neuville (St-François-de-Sales), the son of Pierre Gendron and Christine Anger. [Baptism](#document-act-2485321)
- **First marriage:** On 9 July 1838 he married Julie Clermont at Neuville. The marriage writes his mother's given name as Justine, where the baptism has Christine. [Marriage](#document-act-13400894) · [Baptism](#document-act-2485321)
- **Remarriage:** On 24 January 1843 at Neuville, the act names him as the widower of Julie Clermont and his bride as Marie Anne Vézina. [Remarriage](#document-act-13400895)
- **Later family:** He appears again as the father of Joseph Éleusippe, born and baptized on 9 August 1861 at Québec (St-Roch), with Marie Anne Vézina as the mother. The act gives the father's full name as **Antoine Élisée Gendron**, his occupation as **meublier** (furniture maker), and his signature as **Élisée Gendron**. [Son's baptism](#document-act-5676294)

It says he was born in **Québec City**, moved to Winchester in **1866 at age 5**, and married **Florence Avis Holbrook in 1890**; their son **Courtenay H.** is named. This origin and chronology fit the 1861 St-Roch baptism, but the obituary gives neither his parents nor an exact birth date.

This is a **strong alternate-family correlation, not a completed migration proof**. The 1861 Québec record securely belongs to C017, while the Winchester household and obituary remain comparison records. The next decisive checks are Joseph's **1890 marriage** and **3 November 1942 death registration** for **Antoine Élisée and Marie Anne Vézina**, supported by the Cap-Santé twin and Malvina baptisms. The original census does not identify Marie E. as Mrs. Joseph Moffett or establish the nephew's parents. C017's existing **solid elimination**, based on his documented Québec family, remains unchanged.
