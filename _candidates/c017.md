## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2485321
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 3 February 1817 Neuville, bottom right, names Antoine Élisée born that morning, son of Pierre Gendron and Christine Anger. Original marriage 9 July 1838 Neuville, left with closing signatures top right on the same spread, names Élisée, son of Pierre Gendron and Justine Angé, marrying Julie Clermont. The mother’s Christine/Justine variation is disclosed; PRDH reconstructs her as Christine Justine Lefebvre Angers. Original remarriage 24 January 1843, M2 bottom left continuing upper right on same spread, names Élisée as widower of Julie Clermont, marrying Marie Anne Vézina. Original child baptism 9 August 1861 Québec St-Roch, B691 left middle, names Joseph Éleusippe born that day, son of Élisée Gendron, menuisier, and Marie Anne Vézina; father signs. Consistent paternal identity, spouse bridge, signed later record and family sequence (PRDH family 222711) support exclusion. Christine/Justine is treated as a maternal given-name variation, not silently transcribed as identical.
