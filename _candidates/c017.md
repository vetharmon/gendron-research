## Biography

Antoine Élisée Gendron was born on 3 February 1817 and baptized the same day at Neuville (St-François-de-Sales), the son of Pierre Gendron and Christine Anger. On 9 July 1838 he married Julie Clermont at Neuville. After her death he married again, on 24 January 1843 at Neuville, where the act names him as the widower of Julie Clermont and his bride as Marie Anne Vézina. He appears again as the father of Joseph Éleusippe, born and baptized on 9 August 1861 at Québec (St-Roch), with Marie Anne Vézina as the mother; that act describes him as a menuisier (joiner) and he signed the entry. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 9 August 1861.
