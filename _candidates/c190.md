## Biography

Germain Gendron was born on 9 October 1826 and baptized on 11 October 1826 at Rimouski (St-Germain), the son of Prudent Gendron and Christine Chamard. A cultivateur, he married Catherine Drapeau on 9 January 1849 at Ste-Luce; the act names him as the adult son of Prudent Gendron and Christine Chamard, and Catherine as the adult daughter of Joseph Drapeau and Marguerite Charette. Their daughter Marie Eugénie, born 20 May 1861, was baptized on 25 May 1861 at Baie-des-Sables. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 25 May 1861.
