## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Germain Gendron was born on 9 October 1826 and baptized on 11 October 1826 at Rimouski (St-Germain), the son of Prudent Gendron and Christine Chamard. [Baptism](#document-act-3061727)
- **Marriage:** A cultivateur, he married Catherine Drapeau on 9 January 1849 at Ste-Luce; the act names him as the adult son of Prudent Gendron and Christine Chamard, and Catherine as the adult daughter of Joseph Drapeau and Marguerite Charette. [Marriage](#document-act-13400924)
- **Later family:** Their daughter Marie Eugénie, born 20 May 1861, was baptized on 25 May 1861 at Baie-des-Sables. [Marie Eugénie's baptism](#document-act-5496929)
