## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 3061727
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 11 October 1826 Rimouski St-Germain, act 3061727, image d1p_17171280.jpg, bottom right, names Germain, born 9 October, son of Prudent Gendron and Christine Chamard. PRDH's act abstract spells the father Prudence; the original is read as Prudent. Original marriage 9 January 1849 Ste-Luce, act 13400924, image d1p_58910228.jpg, M3 right middle, identifies Germain Gendron, cultivateur, garçon majeur, son of Prudent Gendron and Christine Chamard, marrying Catherine Drapeau, adult daughter of Joseph Drapeau and Marguerite Charette. Both original parents match. This establishes Germain's separate unmarried identity and marriage to Catherine in January 1849, inconsistent with Franklin's established Missouri household with Phebe and a six-month-old child in 1850. Exclusion rests on the combined original baptism and marriage, not his given name or geographic distance. PRDH profile 863336 and family 253663 corroborate the union and list subsequent children from 1850 to 1861. The linked child baptism, act 5496929, has now been downloaded and manually read: 25 May 1861 Baie-des-Sables, image d1p_17040156.jpg, B33 upper left, Marie Eugénie born 20 May, parents Germain Gendron and Catherine Drapeau. This corroborates the exclusion; her birth date differs from the baptism date.
