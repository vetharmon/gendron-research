## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2369993
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Four original scans inspected and downloaded 9 November. Baptism B37 upper left, 18 March 1823: Paul born previous day, Francois Gendron, journalier, and Josephte Bougie. Marriage M18 begins bottom right of d1p_01700889.jpg and continues upper left of d1p_01700890.jpg, 17 September 1844: Paul Gendron, son of Francois Gendron and Josephte Bougie, marries Josephte Brossois, minor daughter of Augustin Brossois and Josephte Marleau; Paul Gendron signs the continuation. Original burial S129 right middle, 6 September 1854: Paul Gendron of that parish, drowned accidentally, transported on the coroner's certificate, husband of Josephte Brossois, aged thirty-one. Both parents link baptism to marriage; spouse and compatible age link marriage to burial. The 1854 death excludes this candidate from Franklin, who lived until 1886. No exact death day is inferred from the burial day. PRDH profile 819685 corroborates the chain.
