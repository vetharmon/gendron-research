## Biography

- Paul Gendron was born on 17 March 1823 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Francois Gendron, a journalier, and Josephte Bougie. [Baptism](#document-act-2369993)
- On 17 September 1844 he married Josephte Brossois, the minor daughter of Augustin Brossois and Josephte Marleau; the marriage act names his parents, and he signed the act's continuation himself. [Marriage, 17 September 1844](#document-act-13401048)
- He drowned accidentally and was buried on 6 September 1854, aged thirty-one; the burial names him as the husband of Josephte Brossois and reports the accidental drowning as stated in the coroner’s certificate. [Burial, 6 September 1854](#document-act-6104366)
- The burial day is the day recorded; no exact day of death is given. [Burial, 6 September 1854](#document-act-6104366)
