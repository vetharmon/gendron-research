## Biography

Paul Gendron was born on 17 March 1823 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Francois Gendron, a journalier, and Josephte Bougie. On 17 September 1844 he married Josephte Brossois, the minor daughter of Augustin Brossois and Josephte Marleau; the marriage act names his parents, and he signed the act's continuation himself. He drowned accidentally and was buried on 6 September 1854, aged thirty-one; the burial names him as the husband of Josephte Brossois and notes that his body was transported on the coroner's certificate. The burial day is the day recorded; no exact day of death is given.
