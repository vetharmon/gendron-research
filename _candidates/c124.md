## Biography

- Joseph Gendron was born on 2 August 1823 and baptized the same day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Joachim Gendron and Marie Spenard. [Baptism](#document-act-2913671)
- He died on 8 September 1823, aged five weeks, and was buried the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets. [Burial, 9 September 1823](#document-act-2914260)
