## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Honore Gendron was born and baptized on 9 April 1827 at Châteauguay, the son of Nicolas Gendron and Susanne Gaspard. [Baptism](#document-act-4588459)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 25 September 1827, aged five months, and was buried two days later at Châteauguay. [Burial](#document-act-4592478)
