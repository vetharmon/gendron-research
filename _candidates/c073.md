## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 7 December 1820 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Louis Gendron and Marie Gaudet. He died on 18 December 1820, aged eleven days, and was buried at St-Hyacinthe on 20 December 1820; the burial names the same parents.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 20 December 1820 — LAFRANCE act 2483240](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2483240)** — His burial at St-Hyacinthe (entry S297): he died two days earlier, aged eleven days, and the act names the same parents as his baptism. The preceding entry, S296, begins with crossed-out Gendron and Gaudet wording but was corrected to another family; S297 is this child's act.
- **[Baptism, 8 December 1820 — LAFRANCE act 2477843](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2477843)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe (entry B404): born the previous day, son of Louis Gendron and Marie Gaudet.
