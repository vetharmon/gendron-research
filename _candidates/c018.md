## Biography

Marcel Gendron was born on 8 February 1817 and baptized the next day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of François Gendron and Marie Roger Couillard Després. He died on 6 July 1817 and was buried at Montmagny on 8 July; the burial names the same child and the same parents, and gives his age as six months, though about five months had elapsed since his birth.
