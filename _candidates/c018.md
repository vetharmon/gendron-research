## Biography

Marcel Gendron was born on 8 February 1817 and baptized the next day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of François Gendron and Marie Roger Couillard Després. He died on 6 July 1817 and was buried at Montmagny on 8 July; the burial names the same child and the same parents, and gives his age as six months, though about five months had elapsed since his birth.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 8 July 1817 — LAFRANCE act 2872214](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2872214)** — His burial at Montmagny: he had died two days earlier, and the act names the same parents as the baptism. Its stated age of six months sits against the roughly five months elapsed since his birth, an approximation noted in the file.
- **[Baptism, 9 February 1817 — LAFRANCE act 2870232](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2870232)** — His baptism at Montmagny: born the previous day, son of François Gendron and Marie Roger Couillard Després. His parents' own marriage act, dated 8 October 1811 at Montmagny, spells the mother's name Marie Roger Couillard Despré in full, confirming the unusual reading.

- **[PRDH-IGD family record 117045](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/117045)** — PRDH's compiled family for François Gendron and Marie Roger Couillard Després, in which Marcel is the only child listed in the relevant sequence.
