## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2870232
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Baptism 9 February 1817 names Marcel, born previous day, son of François Gendron and Marie Roger Couillard Després. Burial 8 July 1817 names the same child and parents, deceased two days earlier. Stated six months differs from approximately five elapsed months. The parents’ original marriage, 8 October 1811 Montmagny, explicitly names Marie Roger Couillard Despré, resolving the unusual maternal reading. PRDH family 117045 lists only this Marcel in the relevant sequence. Matching original parentage plus family chronology support the exclusion despite the approximate age; the indexed family is corroboration, not proof of completeness.
