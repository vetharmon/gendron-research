## Biography

Francois Olivier Gendron was baptized on 21 September 1829 at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier), the son of Joseph Gendron and Susanne Lepelle dit Lahaie. He died aged about one and a half years and was buried at Batiscan on 3 May 1831; the burial names the same parents.
