## Biography

- Francois Olivier Gendron was baptized on 21 September 1829 at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier), the son of Joseph Gendron and Susanne Lepelle dit Lahaie. [Baptism](#document-act-4549373)
- He died aged about one and a half years and was buried at Batiscan on 3 May 1831; the burial names the same parents. [Burial, 3 May 1831](#document-act-4550177) · [Baptism, 21 September 1829](#document-act-4549373)
