## Biography

- Frederic Gendron was born on 6 May 1824 and baptized two days later at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of François Gendron and Rose Despres. [Baptism](#document-act-2871106)
- He died on 3 July 1824, aged two months, and was buried on 5 July 1824 at Montmagny. [Burial, 5 July 1824](#document-act-2872695)
