## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 10 March 1825 and baptized on 13 March 1825 at Iberville (St-Athanase-de-Bleury), the son of Louis Gendron and Marguerite Besset. He may be the Jean Baptiste Gendron who was buried on 28 March 1825 at St-Luc, having died four days earlier, on 24 March: that burial names the parents as Louis Gendron and Marguerite Brissette. The father's name and the timing fit an infant death a fortnight after the baptism, but the mother's surname differs — Besset against Brissette — the burial states no age, and no record in this file bridges the two surnames, so it is not certain the buried child is this one.
