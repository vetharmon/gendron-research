## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 10 March 1825 and baptized on 13 March 1825 at Iberville (St-Athanase-de-Bleury), the son of Louis Gendron and Marguerite Besset. A probable burial at St-Luc on 28 March 1825 names Jean Baptiste Gendron, who died on 24 March, son of Louis Gendron and Marguerite Brissette. The burial gives no age. The mother's surname differs from Besset in the baptism.
