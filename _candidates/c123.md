## Biography

Joseph Léon Gendron was born on 27 July 1823 and baptized the same day at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud, the son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire. He married Geneviève Magnant on 19 February 1849 at St-Hugues. After her death he married again, on 4 April 1853 at St-Hugues, where he was living at St-Denis and the act names him as the widower of Geneviève Magnan and his bride as Apolline Brodeur. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 4 April 1853.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 19 February 1849 — LAFRANCE act 13497825](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13497825)** — His marriage to Geneviève Magnant at St-Hugues (entry M12, right page). The act names him Joseph Gendron, cultivateur, adult son of Joseph Gendron, journalier, and Marguerite Allaire — the parentage that ties the groom to the 1823 baptism, though the adult acts drop his second name, Léon.
- **[Remarriage, 4 April 1853 — LAFRANCE act 13499522](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13499522)** — His marriage to Apolline Brodeur at St-Hugues (entry M19, left page). Naming him as the widower of Geneviève Magnan (the spelling varies from the 1849 act) is what links this marriage to the first one, and it carries his documented life to 1853.
- **[Baptism, 27 July 1823 — LAFRANCE act 2587525](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2587525)** — His baptism at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud (upper right of the image), born that day, son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire. Also at [Généalogie Québec (image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/2587525).
