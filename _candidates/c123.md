## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2587525
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 27 July 1823 St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud, act 2587525, image d1p_10320345.jpg upper right, names Joseph Léon, born that day, son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire. Original marriage 19 February 1849 St-Hugues, act 13497825, image d1p_03791184.jpg, M12 right page, identifies Joseph Gendron, cultivateur, adult son of Joseph Gendron, journalier, and Marguerite Allaire, marrying Geneviève Magnant, minor daughter of deceased François Magnant and Marguerite Bouré of Berthier. Original remarriage 4 April 1853 St-Hugues, act 13499522, image d1p_03791364.jpg, M19 left page, identifies Joseph Gendron, cultivateur domiciled at St-Denis, widower of Geneviève Magnan, marrying Apolline Brodeur, daughter of Augustin Brodeur Lavigne and Apolline Messier. The matching parents in the 1849 act and explicit previous-wife link in 1853 establish a separate adult marital identity continuing after Franklin's Missouri household was documented. Léon is omitted in adult acts and Magnant/Magnan varies; these differences are disclosed. PRDH profile 865546 corroborates the two unions, but the original acts supply the identity chain.
