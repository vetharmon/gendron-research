## Biography

Joseph Léon Gendron was born on 27 July 1823 and baptized the same day at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud, the son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire. He married Geneviève Magnant on 19 February 1849 at St-Hugues. After her death he married again, on 4 April 1853 at St-Hugues, while domiciled at Saint-David and the act names him as the widower of Geneviève Magnan and his bride as Apolline Brodeur.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 19 February 1849 — LAFRANCE act 13497825](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13497825)** — His marriage to Geneviève Magnant at St-Hugues (entry M12, right page). The act names him Joseph Gendron, cultivateur, adult son of Joseph Gendron, journalier, and Marguerite Allaire — the parentage that ties the groom to the 1823 baptism, though the adult acts drop his second name, Léon.
- **[Remarriage, 4 April 1853 — LAFRANCE act 13499522](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13499522)** — His marriage to Apolline Brodeur at St-Hugues (entry M17, left page). Naming him as the widower of Geneviève Magnan (the spelling varies from the 1849 act) is what links this marriage to the first one, and it carries his documented life to 1853.
- **[Baptism, 27 July 1823 — LAFRANCE act 2587525](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2587525)** — His baptism at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud (upper right of the image), born that day, son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire. Also at [Généalogie Québec (image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/2587525).
- **PRDH-IGD profile 865546** (no link recorded) — PRDH's compiled profile for him, corroborating the two marriages above.

## Original remarriage re-read — 21 November 2026

The complete saved **4 April 1853** act was independently re-read. Its margin is **M17**, and it twice gives **Saint-David** as Joseph’s residence, correcting the earlier M19 and Saint-Denis readings. The witness clause explicitly identifies **Joseph Gendron as the groom’s father** and **Hilaire Gendron as his brother**. **Pierre Noël Gendron** also signs.

These details refine the established marriage record and the family’s locations. Raymond is not named in this complete act or its signatures. The separate saved **19 February 1849** marriage image ends before its witness section; its missing continuation must not be treated as a completed witness check. Joseph’s existing elimination is unchanged.
