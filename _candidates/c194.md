Original baptism 8 January 1827 St-Hyacinthe, B7 lower right, names Godefroy born that day, son of Laurent Gendreau and Louise Allard. Original marriage 2 February 1852 St-Pie, M2 bottom right with continuation top left on following page, names Godefroy son of Laurent Gendreau and deceased Louise Allard of St-Hyacinthe, marrying Éléonore Denonville, daughter of Jean Baptiste Denonville and deceased Opportune Guy; groom signs.
