## Biography

Godefroy Gendreau was born and baptized on 8 January 1827 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Laurent Gendreau and Louise Allard. He married Éléonore Denonville on 2 February 1852 at St-Pie; the act names him as the son of Laurent Gendreau and the deceased Louise Allard of St-Hyacinthe, and Éléonore as the daughter of Jean Baptiste Denonville and the deceased Opportune Guy, and Godefroy signed the act. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 2 February 1852.
