## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Godefroy Gendreau was born and baptized on 8 January 1827 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Laurent Gendreau and Louise Allard. [Baptism](#document-act-2995291)
- **First marriage:** He married Éléonore Denonville on 2 February 1852 at St-Pie; the act names him as the son of Laurent Gendreau and the deceased Marie Louise Allard of St-Hyacinthe, and Éléonore as the daughter of Jean Baptiste Denonville and the deceased Opportune Guy. Godefroy signed the act. [Marriage](#document-act-13400810)
- **Remarriage:** On 23 June 1885, as a blacksmith living at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Roxton and widower of Éléonore Denonville, he married Osithe Beaudry, widow of Chrysostome Viens, at St-Valérien-de-Milton. The named former wife connects him to the 1852 marriage. His nephew Désiré Chaput and niece Marie Chaput are identified in the act, which he signed G. Gendreau. [Remarriage](#document-source-007) · [First marriage](#document-act-13400810)
