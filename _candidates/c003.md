## Biography

Emilien Gendreau was baptized on 9 February 1816 at L'Acadie (Ste-Marguerite-de-Blairfindie). No burial, marriage or other later record has been connected to him. A compiled NosOrigines tree associates this baptism with Sophronie Fontaine, an asserted marriage in 1853 at Hogansburg, New York, and a death in 1903 in Glengarry, Ontario. A Hogansburg parish register does record the marriage of an Emilian Jandreau to Sophronia Fontaine on 8 February 1853, but the act names no parents, so it does not establish that the groom was this child; an Ontario death index entry for an Emilien Gendran, aged 89, at Lochiel in 1903, implying birth about 1813/14, likewise names no parents. The lead remains unproven.
