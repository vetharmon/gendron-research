## Biography

Emilien Gendreau was born on 8 February 1816 and baptized the next day at L'Acadie (Ste-Marguerite-de-Blairfindie), the son of Jacques Gendro and Genevieve St Yves. He is probably the Millen who lived at Lochiel, Glengarry, Ontario. The 1891 census records Millen, aged 75, with his wife Sophronie, aged 65; LAC indexes his given name as William. The 1901 census records a Quebec-born widower, Millan Gandron, aged 87, with a birth date of 8 February 1814. The birthday matches the baptism exactly, while the year differs by two years. The 1891 age implies 1816.

The name forms, matching birthday and Ontario household support a likely elimination. The adult records do not name Jacques Gendreau and Genevieve St Yves, and the birth-year discrepancy remains. This is not a solid elimination.

A Hogansburg parish register records Emilian Jandreau marrying Sophronia Fontaine on 8 February 1853, without naming their parents. The compiled NosOrigines tree links that couple to this baptism. A 1903 Ontario death index names Emilien Gendran, aged 89, at Lochiel, again without parents. Those records remain supporting leads; the tree's identification is not treated as proof.
