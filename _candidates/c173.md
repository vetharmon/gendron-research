## Biography

Paul Gendron was born on 7 January 1826 and baptized the next day at St-Jude (St-Hyacinthe), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Charbonneau. He may be the Paul buried at St-Jude on 9 February 1826: that act names a Paul, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Chardonais, aged about one month, who died the day before. The father, the parish and the age all fit the baptism, but the mother's surname differs — Charbonneau in the baptism, Chardonais in the burial — and no record has been found that gives either name as an alias of the other, so it is not certain the buried child is this Paul.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 9 February 1826 — LAFRANCE act 4726113](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4726113)** — The proposed burial at St-Jude: the act begins at the bottom right of the page and names Paul, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Chardonais, aged about one month; its continuation records that he died the day before and names the witnesses, with no alias for the mother. An alternate parish copy (FamilySearch film 005471045, image 221) gives the same names and age; it is held in the evidence archive but has no source link here.
- **[Baptism, 8 January 1826 — LAFRANCE act 4724655](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4724655)** — His baptism at St-Jude (entry B2, middle left): Paul, born the previous day, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Charbonneau. The mother's surname here is the other side of the conflict.
