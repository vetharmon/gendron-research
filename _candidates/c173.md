## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Paul Gendron was born on 7 January 1826 and baptized the next day at St-Jude (St-Hyacinthe), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Charbonneau. [Baptism](#document-act-4724655)
- **Probable burial:** A burial at St-Jude on 9 February 1826 names Paul, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Chardonais, who died the previous day aged about one month. The mother's surname differs from Charbonneau in the baptism. [Burial](#document-act-4726113) · [Baptism](#document-act-4724655)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 9 February 1826 — LAFRANCE act 4726113](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4726113)** — The proposed burial at St-Jude: the act begins at the bottom right of the page and names Paul, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Chardonais, aged about one month; its continuation records that he died the day before and names the witnesses, with no alias for the mother. An alternate parish copy (FamilySearch film 005471045, image 221) gives the same names and age; it is held in the evidence archive but has no source link here.
- **[Baptism, 8 January 1826 — LAFRANCE act 4724655](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4724655)** — His baptism at St-Jude (entry B2, middle left): Paul, born the previous day, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Charbonneau. The mother's surname here is the other side of the conflict.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 2681725](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/2681725)** — PRDH's compiled record for this baptism.

## Follow-up, 16 November 2026

16 November 2026: Same parental searches as C132 found only the two known Chardonais burials and nine Pierre Gendron/Charbonneau acts. Original baptism 4724655 rechecked: Paul born 7 January, baptized 8 January 1826, Pierre Gendron/Marie Charbonneau. Original burial 4726113 and continuation rechecked: buried 9 February, died previous day, aged about one month, Pierre Gendron/Marie Chardonais; witnesses Francois Bouvier and Francois Gammon (reading). Exact age, name, father and parish support identification, but no positive bridge for mother's surname found. Likely elimination unchanged.

### Later family record

The [marriage of Hedwidge Gendron and François Proulx, 2 July 1866 at St-Jude](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13593539), names her parents as Pierre Gendron and Marie Charbonneau of the parish. Pierre attended as the bride's father. The original was inspected on 16 November 2026. It names neither Paul nor Gabriel and supplies no Chardonais alias; Paul's assessment remains unchanged.
