## Biography

Charles Gendron was born and baptized on 14 July 1821 at Beauharnois (St-Clément). His baptism names his parents as Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Ense; his marriage names them as Charles Gendron and Catherine Hense. The two forms belong to the same couple: a Jean Baptiste Jeandron married Catherine Henns at Châteauguay on 4 September 1809, and a Charles Gendron of St-Clément, widower of Catherine Ense, remarried at Vaudreuil on 8 May 1838 — the same deceased wife ties the father's two names together, and PRDH reconstructs him as Jean Baptiste Charles Gendron. Charles himself married Angele Gendron, daughter of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Poirier, on 17 February 1846. He appears again with Angele as the father of Delima, born and baptized on 18 April 1861 at Beauharnois. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 18 April 1861.

- **[Father's remarriage, 8 May 1838 — Généalogie Québec act 13550408](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13550408)** — Charles Gendron, habitant of St-Clément-de-Beauharnois and widower of Catherine Ense, marries Victoire Leger at Vaudreuil. The act begins at the foot of one register page and continues on the next ([page image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/tag/d1p_1122b0709.jpg)). Naming the same deceased wife is what identifies this Charles with the father named Jean Baptiste in the 1821 baptism.
