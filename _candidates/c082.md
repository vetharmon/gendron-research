## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2369748
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Upgraded after an original spouse-linked paternal bridge was acquired. Original baptism 14 July 1821 Beauharnois, act 2369748, B81 right middle: Charles born that day, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Ense. Original marriage act 13400879, M5 bottom left continuing upper right, opens 17 February 1846 although indexed 1845: Charles Gendron, adult son of Charles Gendron and Catherine Hense, marries Angele Gendron, daughter of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Poirier. The given-name and manuscript/index-year differences remain disclosed.
New bridge: original 8 May 1838 Vaudreuil marriage, act 13550408, begins at the foot of the right page on d1p_1122b0709.jpg and continues at the top left of d1p_1122b0710.jpg. It names Charles Gendron, habitant of St-Clement-de-Beauharnois, widower in his first marriage of Catherine ens, marrying Victoire Leger. The already saved original 4 September 1809 Chateauguay marriage, act 5696062, names Jean Baptiste Jeandron marrying Catherine Henns. The same distinctive deceased first wife, family locality and parental couple support identifying Charles in 1838 with Jean Baptiste in 1809. This is an original spouse bridge, not a claim that either act literally writes the compound Jean Baptiste Charles. The new bridge explains why their son Charles's baptism and marriage use different paternal given names. PRDH profile 604039 independently reconstructs that father as Jean Baptiste Charles and links those two marriages; this is corroboration, not the sole identification evidence.
Original 18 April 1861 Beauharnois baptism, act 6102545, B69 upper right: Delima born that day, daughter of Charles Gendron and Angele Gendron. The linked parental household, adult marriage and continuing distinct family in 1861 support exclusion from Franklin's Missouri identity. The mother's Ense/Hense/Henns/ens spelling variations are retained. The father's 1782 baptism was viewed as a potential lead but not downloaded or used to claim an explicit compound given name.
