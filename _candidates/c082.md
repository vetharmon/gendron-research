## Biography

- Charles Gendron was born and baptized on 14 July 1821 at Beauharnois (St-Clément). His baptism names his parents as Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Ense; his marriage names them as Charles Gendron and Catherine Hense. [Baptism](#document-act-2369748) [Marriage](#document-act-13400879)

- The proposed connection between those father-name forms rests on the family records: Jean Baptiste Jeandron married Catherine Henns at Châteauguay on 4 September 1809, and Charles Gendron of St-Clément, widower of Catherine Ense, remarried at Vaudreuil on 8 May 1838. The corresponding wife’s name, locality and relatives support the inference, but none of the inspected acts explicitly calls the father Jean Baptiste Charles. [Parents' marriage](#document-act-5696062) [Father's remarriage](#document-act-13550408)

- Charles himself married Angele Gendron, daughter of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Poirier, on 17 February 1846. [Marriage](#document-act-13400879)

- He appears again with Angele as the father of Delima, born and baptized on 18 April 1861 at Beauharnois. [Baptism of his daughter](#document-act-6102545)

- **[Baptism of his daughter, 18 April 1861 — LAFRANCE act 6102545](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/6102545)** — The baptism of Delima at Beauharnois (entry B69): born that day, daughter of Charles Gendron and Angele Gendron. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/6102545).
- **[Baptism, 14 July 1821 — LAFRANCE act 2369748](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2369748)** — His baptism at Beauharnois (entry B81): born that day, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Ense — the father's given name as this act gives it, against Charles in the marriage above. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/2369748).
- **[Father's remarriage, 8 May 1838 — Généalogie Québec act 13550408](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13550408)** — Charles Gendron, habitant of St-Clément-de-Beauharnois and widower of Catherine Ense, marries Victoire Leger at Vaudreuil. The act begins at the foot of one register page and continues on the next ([page image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/tag/d1p_1122b0709.jpg)). The corresponding deceased wife’s name and locality support the proposed identification with the father named Jean Baptiste in 1821; this is a family-correlation inference, not an explicit alias statement.
- **[Parents' marriage, 4 September 1809 — LAFRANCE act 5696062](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5696062)** — Jean Baptiste Jeandron marries Catherine Henns at Châteauguay — the earliest record of the parental couple, with the mother's surname in its Henns spelling (it also appears as Ense and Hense). Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5696062).
