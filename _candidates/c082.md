## Biography

Charles Gendron was born and baptized on 14 July 1821 at Beauharnois (St-Clément). His baptism names his parents as Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Ense; his marriage names them as Charles Gendron and Catherine Hense. The proposed connection between those father-name forms rests on the family records: Jean Baptiste Jeandron married Catherine Henns at Châteauguay on 4 September 1809, and Charles Gendron of St-Clément, widower of Catherine Ense, remarried at Vaudreuil on 8 May 1838. The corresponding wife’s name, locality and relatives support the inference, but none of the inspected acts explicitly calls the father Jean Baptiste Charles. Charles himself married Angele Gendron, daughter of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Poirier, on 17 February 1846. He appears again with Angele as the father of Delima, born and baptized on 18 April 1861 at Beauharnois.
