## Biography

- Louis Gendron was born on 10 December 1828 and baptized on 11 December 1828 at Contrecoeur, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Gervais. [Baptism](#document-act-4594561)
- A probable match is Louis Norbert Gendron, who married Adeline Salmon at St-Timothée on 29 April 1851; the marriage names the same parents. The added name Norbert remains part of the identification question. [Marriage](#document-act-13557362) · [Baptism](#document-act-4594561)

## Research status

The original baptism confirms birth on 10 December 1828. The earlier compiled family lists Louis and Norbert separately. A compiled tree's estimate of birth in 1826 and death on 19 November 1892 remain unverified. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 29 April 1851 — Généalogie Québec act 13557362](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13557362)** — A possible match: Louis Norbert Gendron, adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Gervais, marries Adeline Salmon at St-Timothée (entry M15). The parents match the baptism exactly; the added name Norbert and PRDH's separate rows for Louis and Norbert are what keep the identification unresolved.
- **[Baptism, 11 December 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4594561](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4594561)** — His baptism at Contrecoeur (lower right, entry B96, a faint entry), naming his parents, Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Gervais. Register image at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4594561).

- **[1851 census, St-Timothée, page 81](https://data2.archives.ca/e/e092/e002296320.jpg)** — Norbert Gendron, a labourer aged 22 at his next birthday, appears next to Adeline Salmon and near Jean B. Gendron, 75, and Marie Gervais, 50. These adjacent entries support the adult couple and family association, but the page has no explicit parent–child relationship column and does not resolve whether this Norbert is Louis baptized in 1828.
