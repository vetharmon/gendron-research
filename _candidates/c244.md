## Biography

- Louis Gendron was baptized on 11 December 1828 at Contrecoeur, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Gervais. [Baptism](#document-act-4594561)
- A probable match is Louis Norbert Gendron, who married Adeline Salmon at St-Timothée on 29 April 1851; the marriage names the same parents. The added name Norbert remains part of the identification question. [Marriage](#document-act-13557362) · [Baptism](#document-act-4594561)

## Research status

The earlier compiled family gives a birth date of 10 December 1828 and lists Louis and Norbert separately. A compiled tree's estimate of birth in 1826 and death on 19 November 1892 remain unverified. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
