## Biography

Louis Gendron was baptized on 11 December 1828 at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Gervais; PRDH's family record gives his birth as 10 December 1828. He may be the Louis Norbert Gendron who married Adeline Salmon on 29 April 1851 at St-Timothée — that act names the groom as the adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Gervais. If that groom was this child, he has been connected to Canadian records until at least 29 April 1851. But PRDH carries Louis (born 10 December 1828) and Norbert (birth unknown, with this marriage) as two separate rows, and the two have not been independently resolved into one man; a compiled tree's estimate that the adult was born in 1826 and died on 19 November 1892 is a lead, not verified vital evidence.
