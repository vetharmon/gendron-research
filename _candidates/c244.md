## Biography

Louis Gendron was baptized on 11 December 1828 at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Gervais; PRDH's family record gives his birth as 10 December 1828. PRDH gives his birth as 10 December 1828. A probable match is Louis Norbert Gendron, who married Adeline Salmon at St-Timothée on 29 April 1851; that marriage names the same parents. PRDH lists Louis and Norbert separately. A compiled tree's birth estimate of 1826 and death date of 19 November 1892 remain unverified.
