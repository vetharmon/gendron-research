## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2692109
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original date correction resolves the apparent age conflict. Baptism second entry left, 10 August 1817: Jean Baptiste born previous day, Jean Baptiste Gendrau and Marie Euphrosine Courtemanche. Burial bottom left clearly opens Le quatre d'avril mil huit cent dix huit: 4 APRIL 1818, not 4 February as in the PRDH abstract. It names Jean Baptiste, died two days earlier, eight months, Jean Baptiste Gendro and Marie Courtemanche. The interval from 9 August to 2 April is seven months twenty-four days, compatible with the rounded eight months. Both original identities and chronology support exclusion. Filename corrected; indexed dates retained in the abstract as an explicitly documented discrepancy.
