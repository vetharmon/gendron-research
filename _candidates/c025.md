## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendreau was born on 9 August 1817 and baptized the next day at St-Denis-sur-Richelieu (St-Denis), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Marie Euphrosine Courtemanche. He died on 2 April 1818 and was buried on 4 April 1818 at St-Ours, aged eight months.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 4 April 1818 — LAFRANCE act 2633046](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2633046)** — His burial at St-Ours (bottom left of the page). The register dates the act 4 April 1818 and says he died two days earlier, aged eight months, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendro and Marie Courtemanche; from his birth on 9 August the interval is seven months and twenty-four days, matching the stated age. It closes his life in infancy.
- **[Baptism, 10 August 1817 — LAFRANCE act 2692109](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2692109)** — His baptism at St-Denis-sur-Richelieu (second entry on the left): born the previous day, son of Jean Baptiste Gendrau and Marie Euphrosine Courtemanche.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 2446989](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/2446989)** — PRDH's compiled record for the same child. Its abstract dates the burial 4 February, but the original register reads 4 April 1818, as noted in the burial entry above.
