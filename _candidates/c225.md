## Biography

- Louis Gendron was born on 23 February 1828 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Eustache Gendron, a day labourer, and Josette Lefebvre. [Baptism](#document-act-2995823)

- A probable match is Louis Gendron, a blacksmith who married Domithilde Lacroix at Sherbrooke on 16 April 1855. The marriage names Eustache Gendron and Louise Lefebvre as his parents. The mother's given name differs from Josette in the baptism. [Marriage](#document-act-13501366) [Baptism](#document-act-2995823)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 16 April 1855 — LAFRANCE act 13501366](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13501366)** — A possible match: Louis Gendron, a blacksmith, son of Eustache Gendron and Louise Lefebvre, marries Domithilde Lacroix at Sherbrooke. The father’s name and the groom’s adult status are consistent with the proposed match, but the mother is Louise rather than Josette in the baptism summary; no exact age is given in the marriage. The identification remains uncertain.
- **[Baptism, 24 February 1828 — LAFRANCE act 2995823](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2995823)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe: born the previous day, son of Eustache Gendron and Josette Lefebvre. The prior research summary reports that the continuation gives the father as a day labourer and spells the mother Josette Lefevre. That continuation was not available in the image inspected on 23 November 2026, so those clauses remain unverified in this pass; only the opening and previous-day birth were directly read.
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 131116](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/131116)** — PRDH's compiled family for the baptism gives the mother as Marie Josephe/Josette Lefebvre, supporting Josette; it does not supply an original record bridging Josette and Louise.
