## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 23 February 1828 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Eustache Gendron, a day labourer, and Josette Lefebvre. He may be the Louis Gendron, a blacksmith, who married Domithilde Lacroix on 16 April 1855 at Sherbrooke — that act names the groom as the son of Eustache Gendron and **Louise** Lefebvre. If that groom was this child, he has been connected to Canadian records until at least 16 April 1855. But the mother's given name differs between the baptism (Josette) and the marriage (Louise), and the two names have not been reconciled, so it is not certain the groom and the child are the same man.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 16 April 1855 — LAFRANCE act 13501366](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13501366)** — The proposed later-life record: Louis Gendron, a blacksmith, son of Eustache Gendron and Louise Lefebvre, marries Domithilde Lacroix at Sherbrooke. Same father and right age, but the mother is Louise, not Josette, which leaves the identification uncertain.
- **[Baptism, 24 February 1828 — LAFRANCE act 2995823](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2995823)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe: born the previous day, son of Eustache Gendron and Josette Lefebvre. The entry's continuation gives the father as a day labourer and spells the mother Josette Lefevre; the mother's name in this act is one side of the conflict described above.
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 131116](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/131116)** — PRDH's compiled family for the baptism gives the mother as Marie Josephe/Josette Lefebvre, supporting Josette; it does not supply an original record bridging Josette and Louise.
