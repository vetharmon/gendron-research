## Biography

Joseph Gendron was born on 16 February 1825 and baptized the following day at Sainte-Anne-de-la-Pérade, son of François Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie-Josephte Tessier. He married Betsy Connely (Élisabeth Connolly), daughter of Michael Connely and Mary Hamilton, at Saint-Casimir on 3 September 1861.

His marriage names his father and identifies Olivier Leduc and Hilaire Dusablon as his brothers-in-law. Olivier's marriage at Sainte-Anne-de-la-Pérade on 15 February 1847 names his wife Phébée Gendron as the daughter of François Gendron and Josephte Tessier. That marriage connects Joseph's adult household to his baptismal family. Joseph's own marriage omits his mother's name.
