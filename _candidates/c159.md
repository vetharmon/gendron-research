## Biography

- Joseph Gendron was born on 16 February 1825 and baptized the following day at Sainte-Anne-de-la-Pérade, son of François Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie-Josephte Tessier. [Baptism](#document-act-4566132)
- He married Betsy Connely (Élisabeth Connolly), daughter of Michael Connely and Mary Hamilton, at Saint-Casimir on 3 September 1861. [Marriage, 3 September 1861](#document-source-004)
- His marriage names his father and identifies Olivier Leduc and Hilaire Dusablon as his brothers-in-law. [Joseph’s marriage](#document-source-004)
- Olivier's marriage at Sainte-Anne-de-la-Pérade on 15 February 1847 names his wife Phébée Gendron as the daughter of François Gendron and Josephte Tessier. [Phébée Gendron’s marriage, 15 February 1847](#document-source-005)
- That marriage connects Joseph's adult household to his baptismal family. [Joseph’s marriage](#document-source-004) · [Phébée’s marriage](#document-source-005) · [Baptism](#document-act-4566132)
- Joseph's own marriage omits his mother's name. [Joseph’s marriage](#document-source-004)

- **[Baptism, 17 February 1825 — B11](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L99Q-C9CP)** — DGS 5471259, image 530 of 616, right page near the bottom. Joseph was born the previous day, son of François Gendron and Marie-Josephte Tessier. The father is described as a cultivateur. This is LAFRANCE act 4566132, checked against the original image.
- **[Marriage, 3 September 1861 — M15](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-899Q-S96J-X)** — DGS 5470936, image 400 of 622. Joseph Gendron, adult son of François Gendron of Sainte-Anne, marries Betsy Connely at Saint-Casimir. The entry begins at lower left and continues at upper right. The continuation names Olivier Leduc and Hilaire Dusablon as “tous deux beaux-frères de l’époux.”
- **[Phébée Gendron’s marriage, 15 February 1847 — M5](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L99Q-C38J)** — DGS 5471260, image 757 of 808, left page. Olivier Leduc marries Phébée Gendron, minor daughter of François Gendron, cultivateur, and Josephte Tessier, all of this parish. This establishes the identity of the brother-in-law named in 1861.
