## Biography

Christophe Gendron was born on 8 January 1825 and baptized the same day at Châteauguay, the son of François Xavier Gendron and Jeanne Alain. He died on 15 August 1826, aged one and a half years, and was buried on 17 August 1826 at Châteauguay; the burial names the same parents, his mother's surname there spelled Allain.
