## Biography

- Pierre Gendron was born on 21 December 1822 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron, a cultivateur, and Marie Poirier. [Baptism](#document-act-2369957)
- On 28 November 1848 he married Lucie Farrel at Beauharnois; the act names him as the adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Poirier, and the bride as the adult daughter of Jean Baptiste Farrell and Marie Ouimet, of St-Timothee. [Marriage, 28 November 1848](#document-act-13401053)
- His son Jean Baptiste was born on 17 March 1861 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois; by then Pierre was working as a journalier. [Baptism of his son, 18 March 1861](#document-act-6103856)
