## Biography


- **Baptism:** Jean Baptiste Gendreau was baptized on 26 April 1816 at Varennes (Ste-Anne), the son of Joseph Gendreau and Apolline Provost. [Baptism](#document-act-2604544)
- **Possible family inventory:** A 5 April 1830 inventory index names Joseph Gendreau, deceased, of Longueuil, and Appoline Prévost. The parental-family match needs the original, especially because it conflicts with part of the provisional 1838 marriage reading; the index does not name Jean Baptiste. [Inventory index](#document-demers-gendreau-prevost-1830-index)
- **Adult appearance:** On 6 October 1840 he stood witness at Chambly, with his brother Joseph, at the marriage of their sister Domitille Gendreau to Michel Dubuc; the act names both men as brothers of the bride. [Sister's marriage](#document-source-004)

## Family follow-up — 15 November 2026

The published [Longueuil burial index hosted by Michel Pratt](https://michelpratt.quebec/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Sepultures.pdf#page=217), PDF page 217, lists Louis Gendreau, son of Joseph Gendreau and Apolline Provost, buried on 14 January 1826, aged eighteen. Witnesses are Joseph Gendreau and Michel Paré. The table was visually checked; the original parish act was not inspected in this pass. This is a record of Jean Baptiste's family, not a later record of Jean Baptiste himself.

The same page lists Joseph Gendreau, buried 22 September 1829, aged forty-eight, with Jn-Baptiste Gendreau and Antoine Dalpé as witnesses. It gives no parents or spouse for Joseph. Neither the deceased's identification as Jean Baptiste's father nor the witness's identification as this candidate is established by that entry alone. An older Jean-Baptiste Gendreau also appears in the index, buried 2 July 1832, aged fifty-eight. Follow the original Longueuil acts and the family's later marriages; do not assign the 1829 witness to C006 without further identification.

Assessment remains **Needs work**.

## Possible parental inventory — 5 April 1830

The official [Montréal inventory index](#document-demers-gendreau-prevost-1830-index), read on **27 November 2026**, lists **Joseph Gendreau, deceased, of Longueuil**, and **Appoline Prévost**, with an inventory dated **5 April 1830** before **Joseph Demers**, **CN601,S123**. The names and locality closely match Jean Baptiste's parental family. This is a precise original-record target, but the index supplies no children and the inventory has not been read.

The [Demers catalogue](#document-demers-catalogue-1830-1831) places that date in **acquisition 1971-00-000, container 3980, March 1830–June 1831, microfilm 3219**. **Container 9190** lists a **repertoire covering 1814–1833** and an **index covering 1814–1828**. The supplied digital link reached a CAPTCHA; no inventory image, act number or heir clause was obtained.

**The 1838 reading needs comparison with this index.** The preserved French reading of [Marie Louise's marriage](#document-source-005) includes “de l'agrément du père de l'épouse” and names “Joseph le Gendrault” among the witnesses. Its witness list does not explicitly say that Joseph is her father. If the 1830 indexed couple is this family, the father's-consent wording is inconsistent with Joseph already being deceased. The existing French transcription and English translation remain provisional and are preserved unchanged; the index does not establish which reading or identification is wrong. A relationship for the 1838 Joseph witness must not be supplied from his name alone.

The unassigned **22 September 1829 Longueuil burial of Joseph Gendreau** is a related comparison target. Its published index gives no spouse or parents, so the inventory index does not by itself identify that deceased man or the Jean-Baptiste witness as members of this family. Read the original burial, the 1830 inventory and the 1838 marriage together. The 1840 marriage's reference to the late Joseph is chronologically compatible with the estate lead, but does not resolve the earlier discrepancy. Assessment remains **Needs work**.
