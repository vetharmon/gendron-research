## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendreau was baptized on 26 April 1816 at Varennes (Ste-Anne). No record after his baptism has been connected to him. Follow-up among his family's records found later marriages naming his sisters, but no verified adult record for Jean Baptiste; a burial opened in that follow-up, dated 27 August 1816, names a François aged seven, not Jean Baptiste, and is not attached to him.
