## Biography


- **Baptism:** Jean Baptiste Gendreau was baptized on 26 April 1816 at Varennes (Ste-Anne), the son of Joseph Gendreau and Apolline Provost. [Baptism](#document-act-2604544)
- **Adult appearance:** On 6 October 1840 he stood witness at Chambly, with his brother Joseph, at the marriage of their sister Domitille Gendreau to Michel Dubuc; the act names both men as brothers of the bride. [Sister's marriage](#document-source-004)

## Research status

No later record has been identified. Two same-name 1851 census entries at St-Grégoire proved to be agricultural-schedule listings. They have not been identified as this candidate. [Search log](#search-log)

## Family follow-up — 15 November 2026

The published [Longueuil burial index hosted by Michel Pratt](https://michelpratt.quebec/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Sepultures.pdf#page=217), PDF page 217, lists Louis Gendreau, son of Joseph Gendreau and Apolline Provost, buried on 14 January 1826, aged eighteen. Witnesses are Joseph Gendreau and Michel Paré. The table was visually checked; the original parish act was not inspected in this pass. This is a record of Jean Baptiste's family, not a later record of Jean Baptiste himself.

The same page lists Joseph Gendreau, buried 22 September 1829, aged forty-eight, with Jn-Baptiste Gendreau and Antoine Dalpé as witnesses. It gives no parents or spouse for Joseph. Neither the deceased's identification as Jean Baptiste's father nor the witness's identification as this candidate is established by that entry alone. An older Jean-Baptiste Gendreau also appears in the index, buried 2 July 1832, aged fifty-eight. Follow the original Longueuil acts and the family's later marriages; do not assign the 1829 witness to C006 without further identification.

Assessment remains **Needs work**.


## Longueuil baptism-index follow-up — 15 November 2026

The published [Saint-Antoine-de-Longueuil baptism index](https://michelpratt.quebec/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Baptemes-paroisse-Saint-Antoine.pdf) lists Domitille Gendreau, daughter of Joseph Gendreau and Apolline Provost, baptized 7 March 1822. Her godparents are Jean-Baptiste Gendreau and Marie Vadenet. The same godparent names appear together in entries dated 25 May 1817, 15 January 1818 and 30 March 1818, when C006 was an infant. ### Documented brother Joseph and the Massachusetts family

Joseph's parentage and marriage are already documented in [François Xavier Gendreau's file, C140](../c140/). The documented [1850 West Stockbridge, Massachusetts household](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MD9X-2TH) contains Joseph Jandru, Julian, their children and Francis, identified in C140's file as their brother François Xavier. The citation is Berkshire County, page 113, folio 225, family 307, NARA M432 roll 306, enumerated 14 September 1850. These family records provided a concrete Massachusetts search route for Jean Baptiste. They do not place him in that household or establish his later identity.

The documented 1850 West Stockbridge household served as the control. No C006 match was identified within that search.
