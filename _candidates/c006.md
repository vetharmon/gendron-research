## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendreau was baptized on 26 April 1816 at Varennes (Ste-Anne), the son of Joseph Gendreau and Apolline Provost. No later record identified. Two 1851 census entries under his name at St-Grégoire proved to be agricultural-schedule listings, not a household.
