## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendreau was baptized on 26 April 1816 at Varennes (Ste-Anne), the son of Joseph Gendreau and Apolline Provost. No later record identified. Two same-name 1851 census entries at St-Grégoire proved to be agricultural-schedule listings. They have not been identified as this candidate.

## Family follow-up — 15 November 2026

The published [Longueuil burial index hosted by Michel Pratt](https://michelpratt.quebec/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Sepultures.pdf#page=217), PDF page 217, lists Louis Gendreau, son of Joseph Gendreau and Apolline Provost, buried on 14 January 1826, aged eighteen. Witnesses are Joseph Gendreau and Michel Paré. The table was visually checked; the original parish act was not inspected in this pass. This is a record of Jean Baptiste's family, not a later record of Jean Baptiste himself.

The same page lists Joseph Gendreau, buried 22 September 1829, aged forty-eight, with Jn-Baptiste Gendreau and Antoine Dalpé as witnesses. It gives no parents or spouse for Joseph. Neither the deceased's identification as Jean Baptiste's father nor the witness's identification as this candidate is established by that entry alone. An older Jean-Baptiste Gendreau also appears in the index, buried 2 July 1832, aged fifty-eight. Follow the original Longueuil acts and the family's later marriages; do not assign the 1829 witness to C006 without further identification.

Assessment remains **Needs work**.


## Longueuil baptism-index follow-up — 15 November 2026

The published [Saint-Antoine-de-Longueuil baptism index](https://michelpratt.quebec/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Baptemes-paroisse-Saint-Antoine.pdf) lists Domitille Gendreau, daughter of Joseph Gendreau and Apolline Provost, baptized 7 March 1822. Her godparents are Jean-Baptiste Gendreau and Marie Vadenet. The same godparent names appear together in entries dated 25 May 1817, 15 January 1818 and 30 March 1818, when C006 was an infant. These appearances cannot be treated as evidence of C006's adult life or used to identify the 1829 burial witness. The index text was checked; the original baptism acts were not inspected. No later record of C006 was established, and his assessment remains **Needs work**.

### Possible brother Joseph

The [Apolline Provost compilation](https://genealogie.quebec/stemma4web/info/424395) lists a son Joseph born in 1811. The [Julienne Daigneau FamilySearch profile](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/L2VP-HYR/julienne-d%C3%A9niau-1805-1882) lists an age-compatible Joseph Gendreau ([94Z1-QVN](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/94Z1-QVN)) who married her at Longueuil on 30 October 1832. Their children Joseph and Vitaline also appear with both parents in the [Longueuil burial index](https://michelpratt.quebec/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Sepultures.pdf#page=217). The profile cites an 1880 U.S. census entry under Julia and Joseph Jandro. Check the 1832 marriage for the groom’s parents before assigning the couple or the U.S. trail to C006’s family. The profile gives conflicting Michigan and Massachusetts locations, so verify the census locality from the return. No later record of C006 was established.
