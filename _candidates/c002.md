Baptism 30 January 1816, born previous day, names Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard. Marriage 16 June 1840 at L’Acadie identifies Julien Gendreau with those parents and wife Marie Anne Vien. Remarriage 8 February 1864 at Henryville identifies Julien Gendron as widower of Marie Anne Viens, marrying Flavie Asché. These original acts establish a separate adult marital identity continuing into 1864, incompatible with Franklin’s documented Missouri family.
