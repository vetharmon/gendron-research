## Biography

Julien Louis Gendreau was born on 29 January 1816 and baptized the next day at St-Luc, the son of Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard. He married Marie Anne Viens on 16 June 1840 at L'Acadie (Ste-Marguerite-de-Blairfindie). After her death he married again, on 8 February 1864 at Henryville (St-Georges), where the act names him as the widower of Marie Anne Viens and his bride as Flavie Asché. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 8 February 1864.
