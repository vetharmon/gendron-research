## Biography

Eustache Adolphe Gendron was born on 13 September 1824 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Joachim Gendron and Celeste Spenard. He may be the Eustache Gendron who married Josephine Cloutier on 23 August 1847 at Trois-Rivières, a link PRDH makes (profile 849755, family 246532). But the marriage act calls the groom a journalier, son of Eustache Gendron, journalier, and the late Marie Spenard of St-Grégoire: the father's given name differs from the baptism, and the mother is Marie rather than Celeste. No record in this file bridges either difference, so it is not certain the groom and the child are the same man.
