## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2913760
CURRENT STATUS: Qualified later-life lead — not counted
PRDH profile 849755 and family 246532 link the 1824 candidate to the 23 August 1847 Trois-Rivieres marriage to Josephine Cloutier, act 13400899. Original baptism act 2913760, d1p_2512a1246.jpg left page: Eustache Adolphe born 13 September, baptized 14 September 1824, son of Joachim Gendron and Celeste Spenard. The original marriage M28, d1p_25420495.jpg left page, calls the groom Eustache Gendron, journalier, son of Eustache Gendron, journalier, and deceased Marie Spenard of St-Gregoire. The father's given name differs, and the mother is Marie rather than Celeste. The database linkage does not independently resolve either difference. Both originals are retained; no later child act was acquired for this proposed chain, and it is not counted as an exclusion.
