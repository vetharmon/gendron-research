## Biography

Eustache Adolphe Gendron was born on 13 September 1824 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Joachim Gendron and Celeste Spenard. Eustache Gendron, who married Josephine Cloutier at Trois-Rivières on 23 August 1847, is probably this man. Both copies of the marriage act name the groom as a journalier, son of Eustache Gendron and the late Marie Spenard of St-Grégoire. His mother matches his baptismal mother: her full name was Marie Celeste Spenard, and her children's acts name her Celeste and Marie interchangeably. No Eustache baptized to a father Eustache appears in the records, and the groom's name, age, and mother all fit this baptism; PRDH also connects the records (profile 849755, family 246532). The difference is the father's given name: the marriage writes Eustache, the baptism writes Joachim. The couple had children at Trois-Rivières until at least 1860.
