## Biography

- **Birth and baptism:** Eustache Adolphe Gendron was born on 13 September 1824 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets, son of Joachim Gendron and Celeste Spenard. [Baptism](#document-act-2913760)
- **Proposed marriage:** Eustache Gendron married Josephine Cloutier at Trois-Rivières on 23 August 1847. The inspected marriage describes him as a day labourer and names his parents as Eustache Gendron and deceased Marie Spenard of St-Grégoire. [Marriage](#document-act-13400899)
- **Identity qualification:** The marriage is a probable connection. The research notes identify Celeste and Marie as forms of Marie Celeste Spenard, but the father's given name differs: Joachim in the baptism and Eustache in the marriage. [Baptism](#document-act-2913760) · [Marriage](#document-act-13400899) · [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
- **Later family:** The existing research notes place Eustache and Josephine's children at Trois-Rivières through at least 1860. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Research context

The earlier research found no Eustache baptized to a father Eustache and recorded a PRDH association (profile 849755, family 246532). Those compiled associations do not resolve the original-record father-name conflict.
