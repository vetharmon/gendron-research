## Biography

- Christophe Gendreau was born and baptized on 9 December 1830 at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. [Baptism](#document-act-4371859)
- He died on 8 July 1839 and was buried at St-Damase on 10 July, aged eight. [Burial, 10 July 1839](#document-act-4374807)

## Identification

**Solid elimination — childhood death.** The baptism and burial originals agree on Christophe’s name, his father Joseph Gendreault, his mother’s surname Chaput, and the parish. His birth date makes him eight years and nearly seven months old at death, consistent with the burial’s age of eight.

The burial names his mother **Thérèse Chaput**, whereas his baptism names **Louise Chaput**. This is present in the original, not just the index. Pierre’s original baptism at St-Damase on 1 April 1839, three months before Christophe’s burial, also names Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput.

A separate LAFRANCE search for Gendreau and Chaput, without given names, found 21 acts across all parishes and roles for 1780–1861. They trace a family beginning with Julien Gendron and Louise Thècle Chaput’s 1825 marriage, with later children recorded under Joseph and Louise. The 12 baptism results run from 1825 to 1846. Eleven baptism detail records, including Christophe’s, were inspected; Christophe was the only child of that name among them. The final 1846 detail record was not inspected because a viewing limit intervened. Its result row names Joseph and Louise. Thérèse appears only in Christophe’s burial among the 21 results, and no separate Joseph–Thérèse couple was identified in that search.

The matching identity details and family sequence support attributing the burial to C280. The maternal given name is treated as an apparent clerical discrepancy. Thérèse has not been established as another name used by Louise; the discrepancy remains part of the evidence.
