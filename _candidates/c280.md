## Biography

Christophe Gendreau was born on 9 December 1830 and baptized the same day at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), the son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. A probable burial at St-Damase on 10 July 1839 names Christophe, who died on 8 July aged eight, son of Joseph Gendreault and Therese Chaput. The mother's given name differs from Louise in the baptism.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 10 July 1839 — LAFRANCE act 4374807](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4374807)** — A possible match: burial at St-Damase (entry S50, lower left) of a Christophe who died two days earlier, aged eight, son of Joseph Gendreault and Therese Chaput. Age and father fit the 1830 baptism; the mother's name is the open point.
- **[Baptism, 9 December 1830 — LAFRANCE act 4371859](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4371859)** — His baptism at St-Damase (middle left): born the same day, naming his parents, Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. The mother's name in this act is the other side of the conflict.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 2812760](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/2812760)** — PRDH's compiled record for Christophe, consistent with the baptism.
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 153389](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/153389)** — PRDH's reconstruction of Joseph Gendreault's family: Christophe, born 1830, is its only indexed child of that name, and the mother is Louise Chaput throughout.
