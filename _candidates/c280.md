## Biography

Christophe Gendreau was born on 9 December 1830 and baptized the same day at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), the son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. He may be the Christophe who died on 8 July 1839, aged eight years, and was buried at St-Damase on 10 July 1839 — that burial names the parents as Joseph Gendreault and **Therese** Chaput. The father's name, the parish and the age all fit, but the mother's given name differs between the two acts, and no independent evidence has been found that Therese was the same mother as Louise, so it is not certain the buried child was this one.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 10 July 1839 — LAFRANCE act 4374807](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4374807)** — The proposed later record: burial at St-Damase (entry S50, lower left) of a Christophe who died two days earlier, aged eight, son of Joseph Gendreault and Therese Chaput. Age and father fit the 1830 baptism; the mother's name is the open point.
- **[Baptism, 9 December 1830 — LAFRANCE act 4371859](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4371859)** — His baptism at St-Damase (middle left): born the same day, naming his parents, Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. The mother's name in this act is the other side of the conflict.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 2812760](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/2812760)** — PRDH's compiled record for Christophe, consistent with the baptism.
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 153389](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/153389)** — PRDH's reconstruction of Joseph Gendreault's family: Christophe, born 1830, is its only indexed child of that name, and the mother is Louise Chaput throughout — which neither confirms nor bridges the Therese of the burial act.
