## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Gabriel Gendron was born on 30 July 1817 and baptized the next day at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville (St-Jean-Baptiste), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Choinière dit Sabourin. [Baptism](#document-act-2760336)
- **Marriage:** He married Marie Langevin, daughter of François Langevin and Charlotte Martel, on 16 January 1849 at St-Damase. [Marriage](#document-act-13554382)
- **Later family:** He appears at St-Damase as the father of Damase, born on 30 October 1861 and baptized the next day; the act includes Gabriel among those declaring they cannot sign. [Damase's baptism](#document-act-5413275)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 16 January 1849 — LAFRANCE act 13554382](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13554382)** — His marriage to Marie Langevin at St-Damase (entry M3). - **[Baptism, 31 July 1817 — LAFRANCE act 2760336](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2760336)** — His baptism at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville (entry B94, bottom right, with the final signature clause on the following image): born the previous day, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Choinière dit Sabourin. Also at [PRDH-IGD](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Acte/2760336).
- **[PRDH-IGD family 253794](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/253794)** — PRDH's compiled family for Gabriel Gendron and Marie Langevin, corroborating the children of the marriage.
