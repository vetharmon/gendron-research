## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2760336
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 31 July 1817 St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville, B94 at bottom right with final signature clause on following image, names Gabriel, born previous day, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Choinière dit Sabourin. Marriage 16 January 1849 St-Damase, M3, names those parents and bride Marie Langevin, daughter of François Langevin and Charlotte Martel. Baptism 31 October 1861 St-Damase, B93, names Damase, born previous day, son of Gabriel Gendron and Marie Langevin; father is included among those declaring they cannot sign. PRDH family 253794 corroborates the intervening children. Matching parentage and the continuing separate family support exclusion from Franklin’s Missouri household.
