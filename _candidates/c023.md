## Biography

Gabriel Gendron was born on 30 July 1817 and baptized the next day at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville (St-Jean-Baptiste), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Choinière dit Sabourin. He married Marie Langevin, daughter of François Langevin and Charlotte Martel, on 16 January 1849 at St-Damase. He appears at St-Damase as the father of Damase, born on 30 October 1861 and baptized the next day; the act includes Gabriel among those declaring they cannot sign. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 31 October 1861.
