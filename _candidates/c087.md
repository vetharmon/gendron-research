## Biography

Camille Gendron was born on 18 August 1821 and baptized the same day at Châteauguay, the son of Joseph Gendreau and Marie Anne Guy. He died on 5 May 1829, aged eight, and was buried on 8 May 1829 at Beauharnois, the burial naming the same parents.
