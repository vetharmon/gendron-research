## Biography

Pierre Gendron was baptized on 11 November 1822 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire). No burial before 1850 was established for him in this search, and no later record has been confirmed as his. A lead drawn from PRDH (profile 798111) gives a marriage at Notre-Dame, Montréal, on 13 May 1842 to Monique Henault Deschamps Huneau, with no children listed in the compiled family (no. 219181), but the original act has not been inspected, so the lead remains unverified.
