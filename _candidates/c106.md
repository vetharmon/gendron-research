## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born and baptized on 11 November 1822 at St-Hyacinthe, son of Eustache Gendron, maître d’école, and Josephte Lefebvre. On 13 May 1842 he married Monique Huneau at Notre-Dame, Montréal. The marriage identifies Pierre as a minor son of Eustache Gendron and the late Josephte Lefebvre, of St-Hyacinthe. His age implied by the baptism, about nineteen and a half, agrees with the minor description.

## Assessment

**Likely elimination.** The baptism and inspected 1842 marriage establish Pierre's Canadian identity. A probable continuation appears in Illinois: a recorded deed of 8 December 1869 names Pierre Gendron of St. Anne, Kankakee County, and Monique, his wife. In the 1870 Kankakee city census, Peter Gendron, 49, a paint manufacturer, appears with Monique, 52; both were born in Canada. Pierre's age is within two years of the 1822 baptism, and Monique's age agrees with her adult status at the 1842 marriage.

The couple's names, compatible ages and Canadian origins support the identification. A nearby cemetery index for Monique **Honult** Gendron supplies a possible spelling of Huneau, but its underlying burial record has not been inspected. The Illinois records do not yet name Pierre's parents or independently establish Monique's maiden name. This is a likely identification, not a solid elimination.

## Widow’s deed and family lead

On **16 March 1889**, Monique Gendron, explicitly described as a widow, transferred the **same Papineau parcel** from the 1869 deed to **Ovila Henault**. The recorded instrument is in Iroquois County Book 106, page 565, number 106126, filed on 21 March 1889. Ovila was to pay her $80 annually for life, pay the taxes and assessments, and pay a $1,000 mortgage due on 4 March 1891. He could not sell or assign the property during her life. Default would void the deed. [Recorded deed](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C3QT-491S-P?view=fullText&lang=en&groupId=M9XF-GLD).

This parcel match connects the widow to the 1869 Illinois couple independently of the unverified 1880 census index.

Ovila’s original **30 May 1888** marriage to Josephine Mayhew identifies him as 28, born in Canada, resident at Papineau, son of **Michael Henault and Adelina Montpetit**. [Marriage register, page 216](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:9392-SDNH-R?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AKF29-9SV&action=view&lang=en).

The [FamilySearch tree for Ovila](https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/details/LVYL-7WR) places him in the family of Michel Hunault and **Françoise** Montpetit. That Michel is assigned to Michel Hunault and Véronique-Monique Longtin, a possible connection to C106’s wife. The tree is a finding aid: **Adelina versus Françoise needs reconciliation**, and neither Illinois original states a relationship between Ovila and Monique. A nephew relationship remains a hypothesis.

**Next records:** Ovila’s reported baptism at Beauharnois on or about 25 May 1861; Michel Hunault’s 3 March 1851 marriage to Françoise Montpetit; Monique’s own birth or a family act connecting her to Michel Hunault and Longtin. Assessment remains **Likely elimination**.

The original [3 March 1851 marriage, M10](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L993-WFDR?view=explore), Saint-Clément-de-Beauharnois, DGS 005469009, image 39 of 310, names **Michel Hainault, widower of Françoise Daigneau**, and **Françoise Montpetit**, minor daughter of Jean Baptiste Montpetit and Angélique Daoust. Michel’s parents are not named. This verifies the marriage lead but does not reconcile Adelina versus Françoise in Ovila’s Illinois marriage.

The inspected May 1861 sequence did not yield Ovila’s claimed baptism. In particular, B91 on image 303 is **Alexandre Montpetit**, not Ovila. The tree’s reported 25 May 1861 Beauharnois birth remains unverified. Next check Michel’s first marriage on 17 September 1844 for his parents and locate Ovila’s original baptism before using the proposed nephew relationship.


The original [17 September 1844 marriage, M19](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G993-W68M?cat=80302&view=explore&lang=en&groupId=TH-1971-27996-9833-23), Saint-Clément-de-Beauharnois, DGS 005469008, image 340 of 606, identifies **Michel Hainault, voyageur**, as the adult son of **Michel Hainault, journalier, and Véronique Longtin**, of that parish. The late-May/early-June **1860** sequence was also checked to test the year implied by Ovila’s age in 1888; no baptism was verified there. The nephew relationship remains unproved.
