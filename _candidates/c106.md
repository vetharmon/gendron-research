## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born and baptized on 11 November 1822 at St-Hyacinthe, son of Eustache Gendron, maître d’école, and Josephte Lefebvre. On 13 May 1842 he married Monique Huneau at Notre-Dame, Montréal. The marriage identifies Pierre as a minor son of Eustache Gendron and the late Josephte Lefebvre, of St-Hyacinthe. His age implied by the baptism, about nineteen and a half, agrees with the minor description.

## Assessment

**Possible elimination.** The original marriage supplies a parent-and-parish connection to the baptism, rather than a database linkage alone. It establishes a useful adult identity to follow. It does not yet establish a separate continuing life during Franklin’s Missouri marriages and family. Monique’s later life, a child’s record, a later census or Pierre’s death is still needed. An 1842 marriage alone does not rule out a subsequent change of spouse or residence.
