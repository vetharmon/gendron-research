## Biography

Pierre Gendreau was baptized on 22 February 1816 at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies (L'Islet). No later record identified.

## Documents

- **[Baptism, 22 February 1816 — LAFRANCE act 2415990](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2415990)** — His baptism at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies (L'Islet) — the only record so far connected to him.

## Family follow-up

The original burial of Marie Louise Gendro at Saint-Pierre-les-Becquets on 8 April 1824 names her parents as Jean Baptiste Gendro, shoemaker of that parish, and deceased Marie Claire Caron. She died on 6 April, aged four. This is Pierre’s sister, not a burial for Pierre.

An indexed remarriage at L’Islet on 21 August 1821 (act 5711531) names Jean Baptiste Jandro as the widower of Marie Claire Carron and the husband of Marie Josette Bossé. His next marriage, at Saint-Roch-des-Aulnaies on 19 October 1841 (act 13555005), was checked in the original. It names him as a shoemaker, widower of Josephte Bossé, marrying Scholastique Caron, widow of Pierre Sénéchal; both resided in the parish. Pierre is not named among the witnesses.

These records give a route for following the family through Saint-Pierre-les-Becquets and back to Saint-Roch-des-Aulnaies. They do not establish Pierre’s own whereabouts after baptism. **Needs work remains unchanged.**

## Wider record search

A follow-up search on 16 November 2026 reviewed 44 LAFRANCE burial result rows for Pierre Gendron/Gendreau, 1816–1861, without requiring a father’s name. Detailed index checks distinguished Pierre Flavien buried at Trois-Rivières in 1826 (act 4387172, mother Josephte Comeau) and Pierre buried at Québec in 1836 (act 3333788, father Nicolas). The Beauharnois marriage of 1848 (act 13401053) names mother Marie Poirier; the Sainte-Anne-des-Monts marriage of 1864 (act 13581871) names parents Jean Gendron and Lucie Tiollan. These checks were of the index entries, not fresh inspections of original scans. No later record was securely connected to C004.
