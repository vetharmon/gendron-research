## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4473260
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 8 September 1826 L'Acadie, act 4473260, image d1p_02291511.jpg, B228 middle right, names Laurent, born that day, son of Hubert Gendreau and Marie Pereau. Original marriage 10 October 1854 St-Jean-sur-Richelieu, act 13558705, image d1p_02550042.jpg, M13 bottom left continuing upper right on the same image, identifies Laurent Gendron, adult son of Hubert Gendreau and Constance Perreault, marrying Aurélie Messier, adult widow of Duncan David Lys. The mother's Marie/Constance variation is explicitly bridged by the original parental marriage 9 January 1815 La Pocatière, act 5702361, image d1p_18291028.jpg, middle left: Hubert Gendrault marries Marie Constance Perrault, daughter of Lambert Perrault and Marie Anne Bois. PRDH family 124775 corroborates the parental household and lists one Laurent in the displayed child sequence. Pereau/Perrault/Perreault and Gendreau/Gendrault/Gendron are disclosed surname spellings. The combined originals establish a separate 1854 marital identity inconsistent with Franklin's established Missouri family. The 1854 groom is described by parentage without a previous-spouse designation; PRDH profile 975481 classifies it as his first marriage.
