## Biography

Laurent Gendreau was born and baptized on 8 September 1826 at L'Acadie (Ste-Marguerite-de-Blairfindie), the son of Hubert Gendreau and Marie Pereau. He married Aurélie Messier on 10 October 1854 at St-Jean-sur-Richelieu; the act names him as the adult son of Hubert Gendreau and Constance Perreault, and Aurélie as the adult widow of Duncan David Lys. His parents' marriage, on 9 January 1815 at La Pocatière, names his mother as Marie Constance Perrault, which bridges the Marie/Constance variation in her name between his baptism and his marriage. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 10 October 1854.
