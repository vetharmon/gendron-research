## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Laurent Gendreau was born and baptized on 8 September 1826 at L'Acadie (Ste-Marguerite-de-Blairfindie), the son of Hubert Gendreau and Marie Pereau. [Baptism](#document-act-4473260)
- **Marriage:** He married Aurélie Messier on 10 October 1854 at St-Jean-sur-Richelieu; the act names him as the adult son of Hubert Gendreau and Constance Perreault, and Aurélie as the adult widow of David Lys. [Marriage](#document-act-13558705)
- **Mother's name:** His parents' marriage on 9 January 1815 at La Pocatière names his mother as Marie Constance Perrault, bridging the Marie/Constance variation between his baptism and marriage. [Parents' marriage](#document-act-5702361) · [Baptism](#document-act-4473260) · [Marriage](#document-act-13558705)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **Original-image correction (23 November 2026):** The marriage calls Aurélie the widow of *défunt David Lys* (the late David Lys); *défunt* is not a second given name. [Marriage](#document-act-13558705)

- **[Marriage, 10 October 1854 — Généalogie Québec act 13558705](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13558705)** — His marriage to Aurélie Messier at St-Jean-sur-Richelieu (entry M13, bottom left continuing upper right). The act gives his parentage as Hubert Gendreau and Constance Perreault. PRDH (profile 975481) classifies this as his first marriage; that profile is not among the linked sources.
- **[Baptism, 8 September 1826 — LAFRANCE act 4473260](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4473260)** — His baptism at L'Acadie (entry B228, middle right): born that day, son of Hubert Gendreau and Marie Pereau. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4473260).
- **[Parents' marriage, 9 January 1815 — Généalogie Québec act 5702361](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5702361)** — Hubert Gendrault marries Marie Constance Perrault, daughter of Lambert Perrault and Marie Anne Bois, at La Pocatière (middle left). It supplies the mother's full name behind the Marie/Constance variation; PRDH's compiled family record (family 124775) corroborates the parental household but is not among the linked sources. The family surname appears across the acts as Gendreau, Gendrault and Gendron, and the mother's as Pereau, Perrault and Perreault.
