## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 2 February 1829 and baptized the next day at St-Damase, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marguerite Godin. His father was a journalier residing at St-Césaire. His brother Noël was born and baptized at St-Damase on 25 December 1831.

## Assessment and research

**Likely elimination.** The original census of Goshen, Vermont, taken on 10 September 1850, lists John Jondereau, 21, a Canadian-born laborer, with John, 48; a woman indexed as Lagott, 43; Vitalia, 19; Christmas, 17; Sophia, 10; Maria, 5; Lacoon, 3; and Mason, 1. John’s age fits C247. The older John and the possible Noël/Christmas connection give the household more support than a name-and-age match alone. Noël would have been 18, about one year older than Christmas’s recorded age.

Sophie’s original St-Damase baptism, B145 of 31 December 1843, supplies a second sibling match. It explicitly states that she was born on 15 June 1840 to Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marguerite Godin. Her birth year fits Sophia, ten, in the Goshen household. The baptism year must not be used as her birth year. Together with the John/Jean Baptiste and Noël/Christmas matches, this supports a likely identification of the family.

The census does not state relationships or the woman’s maiden name. Lagott has not been connected to Marguerite Godin, so the identification remains likely rather than solid. Vitalia and the younger household members also remain to be independently identified. A separate John Jondro, 22, lived with Diana, 16, at Brandon in 1850. A John Johndro also died at Weybridge on 30 December 1863, aged 33 years 11 months; his death card leaves his parents blank. Neither the Brandon household nor the Weybridge death has been connected to C247.

## Further lead

A [transcription of Goshen’s 1850 mortality schedule](https://mail.newhorizonsgenealogicalservices.com/mortality-schedules/1850-mortality-schedule-vt-addison.htm) lists Amelia Jondereau, twelve, born in Canada, died in April. The reporting year runs from 1 June 1849 to 31 May 1850, placing the reported April death in 1850. The original has not been inspected and no parentage has been established. She is not assigned to Claire or another child in C247’s family.
