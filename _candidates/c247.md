## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 2 February 1829 and baptized the next day at St-Damase, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marguerite Godin. His father was a journalier residing at St-Césaire. His brother Noël was born and baptized at St-Damase on 25 December 1831.

## Assessment and research

**Likely elimination.** The original census of Goshen, Vermont, taken on 10 September 1850, lists John Jondereau, 21, a Canadian-born laborer, with John, 48; a woman indexed as Lagott, 43; Vitalia, 19; Christmas, 17; Sophia, 10; Maria, 5; Lacoon, 3; and Mason, 1. John’s age fits C247. The older John and the possible Noël/Christmas connection give the household more support than a name-and-age match alone. Noël would have been 18, about one year older than Christmas’s recorded age.

Sophie’s original St-Damase baptism, B145 of 31 December 1843, supplies a second sibling match. It explicitly states that she was born on 15 June 1840 to Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marguerite Godin. Her birth year fits Sophia, ten, in the Goshen household. The baptism year must not be used as her birth year. Together with the John/Jean Baptiste and Noël/Christmas matches, this supports a likely identification of the family.

The census does not state relationships or the woman’s maiden name. Lagott has not been connected to Marguerite Godin, so the identification remains likely rather than solid. Vitalia and the younger household members also remain to be independently identified. A separate John Jondro, 22, lived with Diana, 16, at Brandon in 1850. A John Johndro also died at Weybridge on 30 December 1863, aged 33 years 11 months; his death card leaves his parents blank. Neither the Brandon household nor the Weybridge death has been connected to C247.

## Further lead

A [transcription of Goshen’s 1850 mortality schedule](https://mail.newhorizonsgenealogicalservices.com/mortality-schedules/1850-mortality-schedule-vt-addison.htm) lists Amelia Jondereau, twelve, born in Canada, died in April. The reporting year runs from 1 June 1849 to 31 May 1850, placing the reported April death in 1850. The original has not been inspected and no parentage has been established. She is not assigned to Claire or another child in C247’s family.

## Sibling index reconstruction — 16 November 2026

A FamilySearch parent search and LAFRANCE couple search found the following indexed children of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marguerite Godin: Michel, baptized 30 September 1830 (4371834), buried 27 April 1833 (4245066); Noël, 25 December 1831 (4371979); Marie Alphonsine, May 1833 (4241461; FamilySearch indexes 12 May while LAFRANCE gives 11 May), buried 7 January 1834 (4245102); Adéline, 24 June 1835 (4241832); Claire, 26 July 1837 (4242198); Sophie, 31 December 1843 (4373654). These were index observations at that stage. Sophie’s original was subsequently read, as described above. The original 1850 Goshen household index was rechecked: it indexes the seventeen-year-old as Christinas, previously read as Christmas. No Vitalia baptism was established. The original subsequently resolved Sophie’s birth date as 15 June 1840, strengthening the Goshen identification to likely.

## Vermont family follow-up — 16 November 2026

Henry Johndroe’s original first-marriage card records his marriage to Mary Catherine Pratt at Leicester on 25 September 1869. He was nineteen, a farmer born in Goshen, and his parents were John Johndroe and Margaret. The viewer title says Johnson, but the town on the card is Leicester. His December 1885 second-marriage card, to Lizzie Standage, names his parents John Johndroe and Margaret Gordon; his age is thirty-three. The printed bride/groom labels on that card are reversed. His Fairfax death card of 20 January 1914 names John Johndroe and **Judith Gordon**, with his age as sixty-five years, two months and thirteen days. These age and maternal-name discrepancies need reconciliation. The tree’s identification of Goshen’s Mason as Henry is a lead, not an independently verified relationship.

The reported death of Marguerite Godin at Salisbury on 24 November 1878 also needs correction. The original records **Margret Johndrow, seventy-seven**, married, born in Canada, with her father’s surname written Gandett/Gaudett and no mother named. The published age of sixty-eight is not supported by this card. This death is not attached to C247’s mother.

C247 remains a likely elimination on the previously established John, Noël/Christmas and Sophie/Sophia comparison. The new Vermont cards do not yet establish the missing maternal bridge.
