## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 2 February 1829 and baptized the next day at St-Damase, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marguerite Godin. His father was a journalier residing at St-Césaire. His brother Noël was born and baptized at St-Damase on 25 December 1831.

## Assessment and research

**Possible elimination.** The original census of Goshen, Vermont, taken on 10 September 1850, lists John Jondereau, 21, a Canadian-born laborer, with John, 48; a woman indexed as Lagott, 43; Vitalia, 19; Christmas, 17; Sophia, 10; Maria, 5; Lacoon, 3; and Mason, 1. John’s age fits C247. The older John and the possible Noël/Christmas connection give the household more support than a name-and-age match alone. Noël would have been 18, about one year older than Christmas’s recorded age.

The census does not state relationships or the woman’s maiden name. Lagott has not been connected to Marguerite Godin, so this is not a confirmed identification. A separate John Jondro, 22, lived with Diana, 16, at Brandon in 1850. A John Johndro also died at Weybridge on 30 December 1863, aged 33 years 11 months; his death card leaves his parents blank. Neither the Brandon household nor the Weybridge death has been connected to C247.
