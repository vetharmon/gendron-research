## Biography

Frederic Gendron was born and baptized on 10 July 1827 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Francois Gendron, a cultivateur, and Marie Roger Despres. He died on 4 August 1827, aged twenty-five days, and was buried two days later at Montmagny; the burial shortens his mother's name to Roger Despres. His parents had married at Montmagny on 8 October 1811. An older brother, also named Frederic, had been born in May 1824 and died in July 1824.
