## Biography

- Frederic Gendron was born and baptized on 10 July 1827 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Francois Gendron, a cultivateur, and Marie Roger Despres. [Baptism](#document-act-4080114)

- He died on 4 August 1827, aged twenty-five days, and was buried two days later at Montmagny; the burial shortens his mother's name to Roger Despres. [Burial](#document-act-4083827)

- His parents had married at Montmagny on 8 October 1811. [Parents' marriage](#document-act-5715647)

- The existing research notes record an older brother, also named Frederic, born in May 1824 and dead in July 1824. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
