## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4080114
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Reinspection 9 November: original baptism 10 July 1827, B104 lower right, names Frederic born that day, son of Francois Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie Roger Despres. The earlier uncertain Marie Mage reading is corrected to Marie Roger on enlarged inspection. Original burial S32 middle left reads Le six Aout, 6 August 1827, not 10 August as the preceding log said: Frederic died 4 August, aged twenty-five days, son of Francois Gendron and Roger Despres. Twenty-five elapsed days from 10 July to 4 August agree exactly. Original parental marriage 8 October 1811 Montmagny names Francois Gendron and Marie Roger Couillard Despre, bridging the fuller maternal identity. PRDH family 117045 corroborates this 1827 child; the earlier Frederic born May 1824 had already died in July 1824 and cannot be the twenty-five-day-old infant. Matching child and father, corrected maternal reading, shortened maternal wording in burial, exact infant chronology and original parental marriage support exclusion. No maternal-name conflict is silently erased: baptism says Marie Roger Despres; burial shortens this to Roger Despres. The old saved burial filename contains the incorrect 1827-08-10 date and is retained only as a legacy reference; this evidence package contains a byte-identical copy with corrected 1827-08-06 filename.
