## Biography

Celeste Gendron was baptized on 10 June 1822 at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville (St-Jean-Baptiste), the daughter of Francois Gendron and Archange Chagnon — the baptism records her as Celeste (née), although the candidate list carried her name in the masculine form Celestin. She was buried on 25 October 1841 at St-Damase, the burial naming her as the legitimate daughter of Francois Gendron and Archange Chagnon and giving her age as twenty.
