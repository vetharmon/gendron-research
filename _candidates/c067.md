## Biography

- Jean Baptiste Gendron was baptized on 7 August 1820 at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Charbonault. [Baptism](#document-act-2631616)

- In the 1851 census, enumerated in January 1852, a Jean Baptiste Gendron, 32, appears in the Saint-Jude household associated with Pierre Gendron, 68, and Marie Charbonneau, 53. His identification as their son rests on correlation with the baptism; the schedule does not state that relationship. [1851 census](#document-source-003) [Baptism](#document-act-2631616)
