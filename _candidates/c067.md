## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was baptized on 7 August 1820 at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Charbonault. In the 1851 census, enumerated in January 1852, he was living at Saint-Jude in his parents' household — Pierre Gendron, 68, and Marie Charbonneau, 53 — recorded as their son, age 32, with his sister Edvige, 20, and the widow Marie Fortin, 80, also in the household.
