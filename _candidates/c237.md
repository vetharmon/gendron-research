## Biography

Joseph Gendron was baptized on 12 September 1828 at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of Francois Gendron and Marianne Dupont. A burial at Contrecoeur on 13 October 1828 may be his: it names a Joseph aged one month, son of Francois Gendron — but gives the mother as Isabelle Dussault, not Marianne Dupont. Both originals were re-read in the research and the conflict stands; no record has been found that bridges the two mothers' names, so it is not established that the buried child was this one.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 13 October 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4596550](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4596550)** — The proposed death record: Joseph, aged one month, son of Francois Gendron and Isabelle Dussault, buried at Contrecoeur (left middle, entry S33). The age and father fit the baptism; the mother does not, which is why this burial remains a lead rather than his established death.
- **[Baptism, 12 September 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4594528](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4594528)** — His baptism at Contrecoeur (top right, entry B63), naming his parents, Francois Gendron and Marianne Dupont. The mother's name in this act is one side of the conflict described above.
