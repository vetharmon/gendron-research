## Biography

Joseph Gendron was baptized on 12 September 1828 at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of Francois Gendron and Marianne Dupont. He died in infancy, in all likelihood: a burial at Contrecoeur on 13 October 1828 names Joseph, aged one month, son of Francois Gendron and Isabelle Dussault. The age and father match the baptism; the mother differs from Marianne Dupont. Both original acts have been read. No marriage of a Francois Gendron to an Isabelle Dussault is recorded, and Francois and Marianne baptized a second son named Joseph at Contrecoeur in December 1829.
