## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 17 February 1828 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets, the son of Louis Gendron and **Josephte Milburge**, as written in the baptism. Two readers independently re-examined the saved original on 21 November 2026 and read **Milburge**, correcting the earlier transcription **Millange**. The record does not establish whether Milburge functions as a surname or an additional given name.

A probable later match is Louis Gendron, who married Marie Phébé Lacroix in the same parish on 8 January 1855. The marriage calls his parents the deceased Louis Gendron and the deceased **Emilie Boucher**. The mother's identity remains unresolved, so the assessment stays **Likely eliminated**.

The two acts repeat the name **Dieudonné Boucher** in a sponsor or witness role. That is a useful person to identify through additional records; it does not by itself establish that Josephte Milburge and Emilie Boucher were the same woman. PRDH's compiled link between the baptism and marriage is also insufficient to resolve the difference.
