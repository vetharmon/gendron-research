## Biography

Louis Gendron was baptized on 18 February 1828 at St-Pierre-les-Becquets, the son of Louis Gendron and Josephte Millange. He may be the Louis Gendron who married Marie Phebee Lacroix on 8 January 1855 in the same parish — that act names the groom as the son of Louis Gendron and the deceased Emilie Boucher. PRDH links the two records, but the mother's name differs between them (Josephte Millange in the baptism, Emilie Boucher in the marriage) and no further original record bridging the two names has been brought in, so it is not certain the groom was this child. If he was, he has been connected to Canadian records until at least 8 January 1855.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 8 January 1855 — LAFRANCE act 13401016](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401016)** — The proposed later-life record: Louis Gendron marries Marie Phebee Lacroix at St-Pierre-les-Becquets. The act names his father as Louis Gendron, matching the baptism, but his mother as the deceased Emilie Boucher, where the baptism names Josephte Millange — the difference that leaves the identification open.
- **[Baptism, 18 February 1828 — LAFRANCE act 3367473](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3367473)** — His baptism at St-Pierre-les-Becquets, naming his parents as Louis Gendron and Josephte Millange. PRDH links this baptism to the 1855 marriage (profile 978798), but that compiled link is not itself an original-record bridge between the two mothers' names.
