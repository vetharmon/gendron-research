## Biography

Louis Joseph Odilon Gendron was born on 26 June 1822 and baptized the next day at Trois-Rivières (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron (written Jeandron), a navigator (navigateur), and Marie Josette Cormier. He may be the Louis Odilon Gendron who married Marie Aurélie Archambault on 14 October 1844 at Sorel — that act names the groom as the adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Josephe Comeau — and who died on 26 March 1858 and was buried at Sorel on 28 March, a grocer (épicier) aged thirty-five whose wife was Aurélie Archambault. The wife's name links the 1844 marriage to the 1858 burial, but the groom's mother is Comeau where the baptism gives Cormier, and that conflict is unresolved.
