## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2459894
CURRENT STATUS: Qualified later-life evidence — not conclusive
Three originals downloaded and inspected: baptism 27 June 1822 Trois-Rivières (act 2459894, d1p_25410317.jpg) names Louis Joseph Odilon, born previous day, father Jean Baptiste Jeandron, navigateur, and mother Marie Josette Cormier. Marriage 14 October 1844 Sorel (act 13401021, d1p_20091281.jpg, M36 lower left) names Louis Odilon Gendron, adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Josephe Comeau, marrying Marie Aurélie Archambault. Burial 28 March 1858 Sorel (act 6025406, d1p_20100800.jpg, S47 upper right) names Louis Odilon Gendron, épicier, age thirty-five, husband of Aurélie Archambault, died previous day before yesterday (26 March). The spouse bridge links the adult marriage and burial, but Cormier/Comeau in the maternal surname is an unresolved original-record conflict. PRDH profile 820657 merges these events; that reconstruction is not used to erase the conflict. Not counted as excluded. All three scans retained for further comparison.
