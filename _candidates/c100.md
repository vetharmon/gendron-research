## Biography

Louis Joseph Odilon Gendron was born on 26 June 1822 and baptized the next day at Trois-Rivières (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron (written Jeandron), a navigator (navigateur), and Marie Josette Cormier. A probable match is Louis Odilon Gendron, who married Marie Aurélie Archambault at Sorel on 14 October 1844. The marriage names Jean Baptiste Gendron and Josephe Comeau as his parents. Aurélie's husband, a grocer aged thirty-five, died on 26 March 1858 and was buried at Sorel on 28 March. The mother's surname differs between the baptism and marriage: Cormier/Comeau.

## Follow-up, 16 November 2026

The original 1844 marriage calls Louis Odilon a notary and names his mother Josephe Comeau. The original 1822 baptism still reads Marie Josette Cormier. Two earlier baptisms at Trois-Rivières name Jean Baptiste Gendron, a navigator, and a mother named Comeau: [Pierre Joseph Fabien, 3 December 1813](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2458720), and [Marie Louise Christine, 6 July 1816](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2459067). These originals were inspected, as was [Jean Baptiste's burial at Sorel, 14 September 1853](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/6024672), which names his wife Josephte Commeau.

The matching father's name and occupation support the probable family connection. None of these records explicitly resolves Cormier/Comeau. The assessment remains **likely eliminated**.
