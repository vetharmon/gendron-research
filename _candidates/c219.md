## Biography

Noel Gendreau was born on 13 January 1828 and baptized the same day at St-Luc, the son of Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard. He married Isabelle Bertrand on 4 August 1846 at La Prairie, the act naming him as the minor son of Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard. Their daughter Marie Denise was born and baptized on 1 April 1850 at St-Luc, the act naming Noel Gendrault and Elisabeth Bertrand as her parents and stating that the father was present. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 1 April 1850.
