## Biography

- Noel Gendreau was born on 13 January 1828 and baptized the same day at St-Luc, the son of Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard. [Baptism](#document-act-4723532)

- He married Isabelle Bertrand on 4 August 1846 at La Prairie, the act naming him as the minor son of Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard. [Marriage](#document-act-13400836)

- Their daughter Marie Denise was born and baptized on 1 April 1850 at St-Luc, the act naming Noel Gendrault and Elisabeth Bertrand as her parents and including the father in the closing statement that the participants did not know how to sign. [Marie Denise baptism](#document-act-5825838)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 4 August 1846 — LAFRANCE act 13400836](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400836)** — His marriage to Isabelle Bertrand at La Prairie. It names his parents, Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard, tying the groom to the 1828 baptism. His daughter Marie Denise’s [baptism at St-Luc on 1 April 1850](#document-act-5825838) names the parents as Noel Gendrault and Elisabeth Bertrand — Isabelle/Elisabeth and Gendreau/Gendrault are the variants as written — and includes the father in the closing statement that the participants did not know how to sign.
- **[Baptism, 13 January 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4723532](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4723532)** — His baptism at St-Luc: born that day, son of Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard.
