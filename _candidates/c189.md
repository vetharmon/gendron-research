## Biography

Remi Gabriel Gendreau was baptized on 2 October 1826 at Iberville (St-Athanase-de-Bleury), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendreault and Euphrosine Courtemanche. No later record identified; he is in neither the 1851 nor the 1861 census index.
