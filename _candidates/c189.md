## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Rémi Gabriel Gendreau was born on 30 September 1826 and baptized on 2 October at Iberville (St-Athanase-de-Bleury), the legitimate son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau, a cultivator in the parish, and Euphrosine Courtemanche. The original baptism, B118, was inspected on 15 November 2026. [Baptism](#document-act-4686940)

## Research status

No later record has been identified for him. [Search log](#search-log)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Original baptism, 2 October 1826](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4686940)** — B118, image d1p_02250718.jpg. Records his birth on 30 September and names both parents.
- **[Parents’ marriage index, 8 July 1816](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5731209)** — Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Marie Euphrosine Courtemanche at Saint-Denis-sur-Richelieu. Original not inspected.

## Family follow-up

Searching without the mother’s given name found additional records under Marie Courtemanche. Two Montréal marriages provide leads: [Émilie Gendreau and Michael Hennessey, 20 February 1844](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13412145), and [Jean Baptiste Gendron and Mary Laughlin, 7 July 1851](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400962). Both name Jean Baptiste Gendreau/Gendron and Marie Courtemanche as parents. The 1844 original was subsequently inspected on 19 November 2026: M35 names Émilie Gendreau, adult daughter of Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Marie Courtemanche, despite the Beaudreau index transcription. The original 1851 marriage was also inspected: its witnesses are the groom’s father Jean Baptiste Gendron, Michael Hennessy and Bridget Walsh. Neither record identifies Rémi.

The [8 January 1846 burial of Marie Courtemanche](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/3324155), also inspected in the original, records her death on 6 January, age 52, as the wife of Jean Baptiste Gendron, a labourer. Rémi is not named. The compound and shortened maternal names are now documented in the earlier C025 infant records, as detailed below. Connecting the specific Montréal adults to Rémi’s parental family still needs further documentation. They direct the next search toward the Montréal households and children’s records; they do not eliminate Rémi.

No match was identified in the earlier Canadian census index searches for 1851, 1861 and 1871. This does not establish that he was absent from Canada.

The established C025 baptism and burial pair supports the use of **Marie Euphrosine** and **Marie** for the same mother. The St-Ours residence in C107 adds family context. These are reasons to search both maternal-name forms; they do not prove that every Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Marie Courtemanche couple is this family.

The [8 July 1816 parental marriage](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5731209) remains an exact unread original target. Its parents, residences and witnesses should be compared with these earlier records, then with parent-linked baptisms of the Montréal adults. No later record of Rémi Gabriel has been established. The separate **Magdeleine** reading in C107's 1827 burial is not resolved by the Marie/Euphrosine evidence.
