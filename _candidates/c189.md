## Biography

Rémi Gabriel Gendreau was born on 30 September 1826 and baptized on 2 October at Iberville (St-Athanase-de-Bleury), the legitimate son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau, a cultivator in the parish, and Euphrosine Courtemanche. The original baptism, B118, was inspected on 15 November 2026. No later record has been identified for him.

## Documents

- **[Original baptism, 2 October 1826](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4686940)** — B118, image d1p_02250718.jpg. Records his birth on 30 September and names both parents.
- **[Parents’ marriage index, 8 July 1816](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5731209)** — Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Marie Euphrosine Courtemanche at Saint-Denis-sur-Richelieu. Original not inspected.

## Family follow-up

Searching without the mother’s given name found additional records under Marie Courtemanche. Two Montréal marriages provide leads: [Émilie Gendreau and Michael Hennessey, 20 February 1844](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13412145), and [Jean Baptiste Gendron and Mary Laughlin, 7 July 1851](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400962). Both name Jean Baptiste Gendreau/Gendron and Marie Courtemanche as parents. The 1844 entry was read in the index. The original 1851 marriage was inspected: its witnesses are the groom’s father Jean Baptiste Gendron, Michael Hennessy and Bridget Walsh. Rémi is not named.

The [8 January 1846 burial of Marie Courtemanche](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/3324155), also inspected in the original, records her death on 6 January, age 52, as the wife of Jean Baptiste Gendron, a labourer. Rémi is not named. The connection between these Marie Courtemanche records and Euphrosine at Rémi’s baptism needs further documentation. They direct the next search toward the Montréal households and children’s records; they do not eliminate Rémi.

No match was identified in the earlier Canadian census index searches for 1851, 1861 and 1871. This does not establish that he was absent from Canada.
