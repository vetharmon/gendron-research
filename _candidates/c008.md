## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born on 14 May 1816 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Joseph Gendron and Therese Vien. He died on 22 January 1817, aged eight months, and was buried at St-Hyacinthe the next day; the burial names the same parents (the mother's name spelled Vient there).
