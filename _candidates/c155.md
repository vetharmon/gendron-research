## Biography

- François Xavier Gendreau was baptized on 3 December 1824 at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), son of Laurent Gendreau and Marie Louise Allard. [Baptism](#document-act-2382639)
- The baptism says he was born the preceding day, implying 2 December. [Baptism, 3 December 1824](#document-act-2382639)
- On 23 January 1850 at St-Hyacinthe he married Émilie Benoit; that original identifies him as the adult son of Laurent Gendreau and the late Louise Allard of St-Damase. [Record](#document-source-005) · [Baptism, 3 December 1824](#document-act-2382639)
- On 11 August 1868 he married Vitaline Jourdain as the widower of Émilie Benoit. [1868 remarriage](#document-act-13608161)
- The matching parents and parish in 1850, followed by the named deceased spouse in 1868, supply the direct original-record chain. [Baptism](#document-act-2382639) · [1850 marriage](#document-source-005) · [1868 remarriage](#document-act-13608161)
