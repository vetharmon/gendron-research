## Biography

François Xavier Gendreau was baptized on 3 December 1824 at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe). Prior research recorded in this file identifies him as the François Xavier Gendreau of PRDH record 915693 who, as the widower of Émilie Benoit, married Vitaline Jourdain on 11 August 1868. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 11 August 1868.
