## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 30 June 1816 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. [Baptism](#document-act-2626701)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 15 July 1816, aged fifteen days, and was buried at Châteauguay the next day; the burial names the same parents. [Burial](#document-act-2629789)
