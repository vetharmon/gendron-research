## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 30 June 1816 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. He died on 15 July 1816, aged fifteen days, and was buried at Châteauguay the next day; the burial names the same parents.
