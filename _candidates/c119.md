## Biography

Ferdinand Gendron was born on 9 May 1823 and baptized the same day at La Durantaye (St-Michel), the son of Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denys. On 19 October 1847 he married Victoire Lecours dite Barras at Lauzon; the marriage act names his parents as Jacques Gendron and Catherine Lapierre, of St-Michel. His daughter Marie Nely was born on 3 May 1861 and baptized the next day at St-Michel, the child of Ferdinand Gendron and Victoire Barras; the father was absent at the baptism. The two forms of his mother's surname — Denys in his baptism and in her own marriage of 1816, Lapierre in his marriage of 1847 — are bridged through her father's later records: Pierre Denys's remarriage of 1828 and his remarriage of 1839, the second of which names him Pierre Denys dit Lapierre.

- **[Grandfather's remarriage, 16 September 1828 — Généalogie Québec act 13378654](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13378654)** — Pierre Denys, widower of Marie Corriveau, marries Marie Louise Bolduc at St-Gervais (entry M36, bottom left, continuing upper right).
