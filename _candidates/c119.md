## Biography

- Ferdinand Gendron was born on 9 May 1823 and baptized the same day at La Durantaye (St-Michel), the son of Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denys. [Baptism](#document-act-2529941)
- On 19 October 1847 he married Victoire Lecours dite Barras at Lauzon; the marriage act names his parents as Jacques Gendron and Catherine Lapierre, of St-Michel. [Marriage, 19 October 1847](#document-act-13400901)
- His daughter Marie Nely was born on 3 May 1861 and baptized the next day at St-Michel, the child of Ferdinand Gendron and Victoire Barras; the father was absent at the baptism. [Baptism of his daughter, 4 May 1861](#document-act-5371556)
- The two forms of his mother's surname — Denys in his baptism and in her own marriage of 1816, Lapierre in his marriage of 1847 — are bridged through her father's later records: Pierre Denys's remarriage of 1828 and his remarriage of 1839, the second of which names him Pierre Denys dit Lapierre. [Parents’ marriage](#document-act-5687823) · [Grandfather’s 1828 marriage](#document-act-13378654) · [Grandfather’s 1839 marriage](#document-act-13378703) · [Ferdinand’s marriage](#document-act-13400901)

- **[Grandfather's remarriage, 16 September 1828 — Généalogie Québec act 13378654](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13378654)** — Pierre Denys, widower of Marie Corriveau, marries Marie Louise Bolduc at St-Gervais (entry M36, bottom left, continuing upper right). - **[Grandfather's remarriage, 10 February 1839 — Généalogie Québec act 13378703](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13378703)** — Pierre Denys dit Lapierre, widower of Marie Louise Bolduc, marries Scholastique Thibault at St-Charles (lower left). This is the bridge record: it attaches the dit name Lapierre to the same Pierre Denys, in an original act.
- **[Grandparents' marriage, 8 October 1787 — LAFRANCE act 344065](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/344065)** — The marriage of Pierre Denis and Marie Charlotte Corrivaux (left side). Also at [Généalogie Québec (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/344065).
