## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2529941
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Upgraded after an original maternal-family dit-name bridge was acquired. Original 9 May 1823 St-Michel baptism, act 2529941, lower left: Ferdinand born that day, son of Jacques Gendron and Catherine Denys. Original 19 October 1847 Lauzon marriage, act 13400901, M41 lower left continuing upper right: Ferdinand Gendron, adult son of Jacques Gendron and Catherine Lapierre of St-Michel, marries Victoire Lecours dite Barras. Original 4 May 1861 St-Michel baptism, act 5371556, B28 left middle: Marie Nely born previous day, daughter of Ferdinand Gendron and Victoire Barras; father absent. These separate-family records were already saved, but the Denis/Lapierre link was previously qualified.
The original 20 February 1816 St-Michel parental marriage, act 5687823, names Catherine Denis, daughter of Pierre Denis and Marie Corriveau, marrying Jacques Gendron. New original 25 May 1799 St-Michel baptism, act 424361, bottom right, names Marie Catherine, born previous day, daughter of Pierre Denis and Marie Charlotte Corriveau. New original 8 October 1787 marriage of Pierre Denis and Marie Charlotte Corrivaux, act 344065, d1p_31120407.jpg left middle, corroborates that parental couple; the viewer folder says 1788, while the act itself is 1787.
The decisive surname bridge is the original 16 September 1828 St-Gervais remarriage, act 13378654, M36 bottom left continuing upper right: Pierre Denys, widower of Marie Corriveau, marries Marie Louise Bolduc. Original 10 February 1839 St-Charles remarriage, act 13378703, lower left, explicitly calls him Pierre Denys dit Lapierre, widower of Marie Louise Bolduc, marrying Scholastique Thibault. The exact second-wife bridge links the explicit dit surname to the Pierre Denis household identified through Catherine's baptism and marriage. This establishes an original basis for the family's Denis/Lapierre usage, rather than relying only on the database reconstruction. No claim is made that Catherine's 1799 baptism or 1816 marriage itself contains Lapierre, or that Charlotte is present in every version of her mother's name. Matching the named family, inherited dit surname and Ferdinand's continuing marital family into 1861 supports exclusion from Franklin's Missouri identity. PRDH profiles 631566/223044 and families 64912/128127 provide additional corroboration.
