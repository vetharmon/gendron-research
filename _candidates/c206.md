## Biography

Michel Nazaire Gendron was born on 9 August 1827 and baptized the same day at Marieville, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Felicite Bachang dite Vertefeuille. He married Domitille Barriere on 10 October 1848 at Mont-St-Gregoire; the act gives him as aged 21, an adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Felicite Bachand, and her as aged 24, daughter of the deceased Charles Barriere and the deceased Dorothee Viens. In the 1861 census of Ste-Marie parish, Rouville, he appears as Michel Gendron, a journalier aged 34, beside Domithilde Barriere, aged 37 — the census names no relationships, so the pairing rests on the matching names, ages and household grouping. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 1861.
