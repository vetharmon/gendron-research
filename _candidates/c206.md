## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4510182
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism B198, 9 August 1827 at Marieville, names Michel Nazaire, born that day, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Felicite Bachang dite Vertefeuille. Original marriage M22, 10 October 1848 at Mont-St-Gregoire, identifies Michel Nazaire Gendron, age 21, adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Felicite Bachand, marrying Domitille Barriere, age 24, daughter of deceased Charles Barriere and deceased Dorothee Viens. The original 1861 census, Ste-Marie parish, Rouville, arrondissement 2, handwritten p.12 / stamped 247, lines 33-34, lists Michel Gendron, journalier, 34, and Domithilde Barriere, 37, adjacent, both married and Catholic, with one male and one female in the family columns. The ages are explicitly age at next birthday, consistent with the 1827 birth and the bride's 1848 age. The census has no relationship column: identification as the married pair rests on the matching husband and wife's names, ages, local geography and family grouping, rather than an explicit wife label. Combined with the matched parental baptism-to-marriage link, this establishes a separate continuing Quebec marital identity in 1861, incompatible with Franklin's documented Missouri family. C206 is now counted as excluded. The unsourced FamilySearch profile date 9 October 1827 is not adopted; the original baptism says 9 August.
