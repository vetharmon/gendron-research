## Census lead reviewed

The original 1851 Saint-Louis-de-Gonzague census was re-read on 16 November 2026: E. Gendron, 25; M. Primeau, 18; J. Guérin, 23; and A. Gendron, 21, all marked married. It does not contain the previously described four-person Gendron sibling group. [Élie Gendron, C186](../c186/), married to Marguerite Primeau, provides a stronger competing identity for E. Gendron; Joachim Guérin and Angélique Gendron fit the other two entries. The census uses initials and does not state relationships, so the group is not adopted as a later record of Étienne. Citation: LAC, reel C-1112, pages 3–4, lines 18–21, images e002296780 and e002296781.
