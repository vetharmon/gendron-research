## Biography

Etienne Gendron was baptized on 2 April 1826 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Daigneau. No later record has been securely identified. An E. Gendron of exactly his age heads a household at Saint-Louis-de-Gonzague in 1851, but the schedule gives initials only, and no marriage of an Étienne Gendron appears in the indexes to corroborate it.
