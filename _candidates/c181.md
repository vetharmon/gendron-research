No later record has been securely identified. The 26 January 1828 Joseph infant burial is now a specific unresolved childhood-death lead; see the dated section below. [Search log](#search-log)

## January 1828 burial lead reopened — 28 November 2026

A complete **later archival typed copy** records the burial of **Joseph**, aged **one and a half**, on **26 January 1828**, son of **Joseph Gendron, cultivateur, and Marguerite Daigneau**. He had died **about three weeks earlier**. The witnesses are François and Antoine Hyneault, who could not sign. The contemporary burial manuscript has not yet been inspected. [Full copied act and English translation](#document-burial-joseph-gendron-1828-01-26)

The matching parents and timing reopen the earlier compiled **5 January death / 26 January burial** claim. The copy does not supply an exact 5 January death date. It names **Joseph**, whereas the newly read baptism names **Étienne**, born and baptized **2 April 1826**. Étienne would have been about **twenty-one months** old in early January 1828, compared with the burial's eighteen months. Neither discrepancy is silently resolved. This is an **unresolved childhood-death lead**, not an established burial of C181 or an elimination. [Baptism and complete English translation](#document-act-4253604)

The new discovery screen covered **C-2889 images 356–434**, 79 typed frames after the April 1826 baptism through the start of 7 March 1828. It found this burial but no separate same-parent Joseph baptism. OCR was used to find possible entries, followed by selected visual readings; this does **not** establish an exhaustive absence. The next checks are the contemporary 26 January 1828 burial manuscript, births before April 1826 and the complete sibling sequence. **Needs work remains unchanged.**

## Parental-family records — 28 November 2026

A newly read later typed archival copy records an **unnamed child of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Daigneau**, privately baptized at home and buried at St-Clément on **7 July 1824**, aged **half an hour**. The child had died “avant-hier,” giving **5 July 1824** as the death date. The act does not explicitly state the child's sex. Antoine and François Héneault were present and could not sign. This infant was already dead before Étienne's birth in April 1826 and cannot be the Joseph buried in January 1828. [Complete copied burial and English translation](#document-burial-unnamed-gendron-1824-07-07)

The complete typed baptism of the already known **Joseph, born 3 May and baptized 4 May 1806** at Châteauguay, is now supplied in French and English. It names **Joseph Jeandron and Marguerite Deniau** as parents and **Benjamin Cousino and Marie Huot** as sponsors. This Joseph remains a **probable older sibling**: the parental-name variants are consistent with the family, but a later explicit relationship has not been established. The expanded text adds no secure later-life record for him. [Complete copied baptism and English translation](#document-baptism-joseph-jeandron-1806-05-04)

These family records were checked jointly with [Paul, C042](../c042/). They establish no later survival for either candidate and do not resolve the Joseph/Étienne name conflict in the 1828 burial. **Needs work remains unchanged.**
