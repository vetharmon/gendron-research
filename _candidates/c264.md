## Biography

Joseph Gendron was baptized on 7 November 1829 at Châteauguay, the son of Antoine Gendron and Marie Anne Lefebvre. His mother died on 22 March 1850 and was buried at Châteauguay two days later, aged 44. His father remarried there in 1851 and again in 1857, and in 1861 was living at St-Joachim-de-Châteauguay with his third wife, Josèphe Rufiange, and their five young children. Joseph, who would have been 22 in 1851 and 31 in 1861, is in neither census household and has no Québec marriage or burial to 1861.

## Ancestry follow-up — 16 November 2026

The Jarvis1 Ancestry tree identifies **Joseph Earnest Gendron** with the **7 November 1829** Châteauguay baptism and parents **Antoine Gendron and Marie Anne Lefebvre**, then places him at **Kennebec, Ontario**, married to **Mary Ann Clark**. This is an unverified match.

The attached original **1901 census**, Addington, Kennebec, page 14, lines 47–50, records Joseph as a married farmer aged **67**, born in Québec on **15 November 1833**, with Mary Ann and their children Frank, 16, and Florence, 19. It gives no parents. The birth date differs from C264’s baptism, and no inspected record bridges the Ontario father to the Châteauguay child. Later index entries name Joseph and Mary Ann as parents of their children; they do not identify Joseph’s own parents. His own marriage or death record is the next useful target. **Needs work remains unchanged.**
