## Biography

Joseph Gendron was baptized on 7 November 1829 at Châteauguay, the son of Antoine Gendron and Marie Anne Lefebvre. His mother died on 22 March 1850 and was buried at Châteauguay two days later, aged 44. His father remarried there in 1851 and again in 1857, and in 1861 was living at St-Joachim-de-Châteauguay with his third wife, Josèphe Rufiange, and five young children. The three oldest children predate the 1857 marriage; their mothers require individual baptismal identification. No entry in either household has been securely identified as this Joseph, who would have been about 22 in 1851 and 31 in 1861. The recorded Québec searches have established no marriage or burial for him through 1861.
