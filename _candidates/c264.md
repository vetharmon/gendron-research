## Biography

Joseph Gendron was baptized on 7 November 1829 at Châteauguay, the son of Antoine Gendron and Marie Anne Lefebvre. No later record identified. His family remained at Châteauguay — his father remarried there in 1851 and again in 1857 — but Joseph, who would have been 22 in 1851, is not in his father's census household and has no Québec marriage or burial to 1861.

- **[Parents' marriage, 30 June 1828 — LAFRANCE act 13400864](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400864)** — Antoine Gendron marries Marie Anne Lefebvre at Châteauguay.

- **[Father's third marriage, 24 November 1857 — LAFRANCE act 13560046](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13560046)** — Antoine Gendron, widower of Catherine Tranquille, marries again at Châteauguay. The family is documented there into 1857; Joseph is not.
