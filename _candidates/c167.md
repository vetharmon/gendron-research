## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Francois Xavier Gendron was born on the evening of 10 July 1825 and baptized on 11 July 1825 at Neuville (St-François-de-Sales), the son of Pierre Gendron and Christine Angé. [Baptism](#document-act-4358565)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 23 August 1825 (the burial says three days earlier) and was buried in the same parish on 26 August 1825, with a recorded age of one month and fourteen days. [Burial](#document-act-4360360)
