## Biography

Francois Xavier Gendron was baptized on 11 July 1825 at Neuville (St-François-de-Sales), the son of Pierre Gendron and Christine Angé. He died in infancy and was buried in the same parish on 26 August 1825, aged one month and fourteen days.
