## Biography

Theophile Gendreau was baptized on 21 May 1826 at Iberville (St-Athanase-de-Bleury), the son of Jacques Gendreau and Genevieve St Yves. No later record has been connected to him: searches found adult men of his name — fathers in records with wives named Delima Gagnon and Odille Quenneville — but none has a demonstrated link to this baptism, and none was adopted as his later life. He is in neither the 1851 nor the 1861 census index.
