## Biography

Théophile Gendreau was baptized on 21 May 1826 at Iberville (St-Athanase-de-Bleury), the son of Jacques Gendreau and Geneviève St-Yves. No later record has been connected to him. Searches of the 1851, 1861 and 1871 Canadian census indexes did not locate him; those search results do not establish that he was absent from Canada.

Two original records extend the documented family history. His brother Jacques was buried at Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu on 15 August 1834, aged eleven, the son of Jacques Gendreau, a labourer, and Geneviève St-Yves, of that parish. His sister Rose married Jean-Baptiste Gaboury at Iberville on 3 November 1836. The marriage names Jacques Gendreault and Geneviève St-Yves as her parents and places Rose in Iberville. Neither record names Théophile.

## Namesakes checked

The adult men associated with Délima Gagnon and Odille Quenneville are no longer open leads for this candidate. FamilySearch tree previews point to men born around 1850; those tree birth dates have not been checked against originals. More specifically, the Ontario marriage index for Théophile’s marriage to Adile Quenneville at Belle River on 25 October 1875 gives age twenty and parents Julien Gendreau and Marie Poulliot, unlike C184.

Rose’s 1836 marriage is indexed under **Gaudreau**, while the handwritten entry reads **Gendreault**. This is a useful search variant for the family, not evidence that every Gaudreau namesake belongs to it. Two later Théophile Gaudreau leads were checked at index level: the 20 August 1855 Saint-Bruno marriage names Jean-Baptiste Gaudreau and Adélaïde Racine as parents; the 20 November 1858 L’Acadie burial names Charles Godreau and Geneviève Lavoie. Neither matches C184’s parents. A Gaudreau/Théophile search for 1826–1905 with father Jacques returned only an 1858 baptism at Saint-Roch-des-Aulnaies, not a later record of C184.
