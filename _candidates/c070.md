## Biography

Francois Gendron was baptized on 30 September 1820 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Francois Gendron and Josette Bougie. He married Francoise Hebert on 16 November 1847; the marriage act repeats his parents' names. He and Francoise appear again as the parents of Joseph Emery, baptized on 15 December 1861. The register writes the father's name as Firmin in that one act; the index reads François. No Firmin Gendron appears anywhere in the Beauharnois registers between 1840 and 1870, and Joseph Emery is the eighth child baptized to Francois and Francoise at St-Clément between 1848 and 1861 — the name in the 1861 act is a slip.
