## Biography

Francois Gendron was baptized on 30 September 1820 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Francois Gendron and Josette Bougie. He married Francoise Hebert on 16 November 1847; the marriage act repeats his parents' names. He and Francoise appear again as the parents of Joseph Emery, baptized on 15 December 1861. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 15 December 1861.
