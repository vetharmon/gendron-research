## Biography

- Francois Gendron was baptized on 30 September 1820 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Francois Gendron and Josette Bougie. [Baptism](#document-act-2369639)

- According to the existing research packet described below, he married Francoise Hebert on 16 November 1847; the marriage act repeats his parents’ names. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

- The existing research identifies him and Francoise as the parents of Joseph Emery, baptized on 15 December 1861. The checked register writes the father’s name as Firmin; the research notes report François in the index. This identity connection depends on the family comparison described below. [Joseph Emery baptism](#document-act-6103932) [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Research status

No Firmin Gendron appears anywhere in the Beauharnois registers between 1840 and 1870, and Joseph Emery is the eighth child baptized to Francois and Francoise at St-Clément between 1848 and 1861 — the name in the 1861 act is a slip.

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Baptism, 30 September 1820 — LAFRANCE act 2369639](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2369639)** — His baptism at Beauharnois (St-Clément), naming his parents, Francois Gendron and Josette Bougie. The 1847 marriage is documented in the earlier research packet (exhibits E06-01 to E06-04); no separate source link for it is recorded in this file.
- **[Baptism of his son Joseph Emery, 15 December 1861 — LAFRANCE act 6103932](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/6103932)** — The eighth child baptized to Francois Gendron and Francoise Hebert at St-Clément. The register writes the father's name as Firmin in this act (see the biography note); the mother is Francoise Hebert, and the godfather was Charles Gendron.
