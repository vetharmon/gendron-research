Burial states five months versus six elapsed months. PRDH family 138875 shows parents married 13 May 1823, this Onésime as their first indexed child and the next child Joseph born 16 February 1825, with no competing indexed Onésime. The original parentage and tightly bounded family chronology support exclusion while preserving the one-month age discrepancy. The family reconstruction is corroborative, not independently complete.
