## Biography

Onésime Gendron was baptized on 2 August 1823 at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, the son of François Gendron and Josephte Tessier. He died on 4 February 1824 and was buried the next day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade; the burial states his age as five months, though six months had elapsed since his baptism.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 5 February 1824 — LAFRANCE act 2938268](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2938268)** — His burial at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, naming Onésime and the same parents as the baptism, François Gendron and Josephte Tessier. The stated age of five months sits one month short of the six months elapsed since the baptism, a small discrepancy the record leaves as it stands.
- **[Baptism, 2 August 1823 — LAFRANCE act 2937256](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2937256)** — His baptism at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, son of François Gendron and Josephte Tessier.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 2585277](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/2585277)** — PRDH's compiled record for the same child.
- **[PRDH-IGD family 138875](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/138875)** — PRDH's reconstruction of his parents' family: François Gendron and Josephte Tessier married on 13 May 1823, Onésime as their first indexed child, and the next child, Joseph, born on 16 February 1825, with no competing indexed Onésime.
