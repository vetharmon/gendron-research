## Biography

- Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 14 February 1828 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Basile Gendron and Claire Primeau. [Baptism](#document-act-4588677)

- He married Esther Dupuis on 18 February 1851 at Châteauguay, the act naming him as the adult son of Basile Gendron and Claire Primeau. [Marriage](#document-act-13557286)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 18 February 1851 — LAFRANCE act 13557286](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13557286)** — His marriage to Esther Dupuis at Châteauguay. It names his parents, Basile Gendron and Claire Primeau, tying the groom to the 1828 baptism.
- **[Baptism, 15 February 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4588677](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4588677)** — His baptism at Châteauguay: born the previous day, son of Basile Gendron and Claire Primeau.
