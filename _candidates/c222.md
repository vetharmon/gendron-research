## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 14 February 1828 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Basile Gendron and Claire Primeau. He married Esther Dupuis on 18 February 1851 at Châteauguay, the act naming him as the adult son of Basile Gendron and Claire Primeau. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 18 February 1851.
