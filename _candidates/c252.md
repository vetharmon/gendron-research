## Biography

- Prudent Gendron was born on 6 April 1829 and baptized the next day at Saint-Hyacinthe, son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin dite Valcourt. [Baptism](#document-act-2996305)
- In the 1871 Sainte-Rosalie census, Prudent, aged 42, appears in family 98 with Jacques Gendron, 68; Lucie, 71; Perpétue, 31; and Simon, 38. [1871 census](#document-source-003)
- The household names and his age connect him to the baptismal family. [1871 census](#document-source-003) · [Baptism](#document-act-2996305)
- The census does not state relationships or Lucie's maiden name. [1871 census](#document-source-003)
- The 1901 Saint-Hyacinthe census records Prudent as single, with the exact birth date 6 April 1829. [1901 census](#document-source-004)
- Together with the 1871 household, this identifies him as a separate man alive fifteen years after Franklin Jondro's death on 13 April 1886. [1871 census](#document-source-003) · [1901 census](#document-source-004) · [Franklin’s research record](../c127/)
- He is eliminated. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
- The 1901 entry states age 72, although the written birth date makes him 71 on the census reference date of 31 March. [1901 census](#document-source-004)
- His relationship to the household head is abbreviated as pensionnaire; the entry does not identify him as the head's brother. [1901 census](#document-source-004)
