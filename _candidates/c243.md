## Biography

Francois Napoleon Gendreau was baptized on 11 December 1828 at Montmagny, son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagné. The 1871 census at Saint-Fabien lists Napoléon Gendreau, 42, a clerk, in the household of Augustin, 68, and Julie, 72. In 1881, Napoléon Gendrow, 51, also a clerk, appears in Alexander Grant's household at Saint-Edmond and Lac-au-Saumon.

The matching names, occupation, region, and ages support a probable identification. The relationship to Augustin and Julie is inferred from the household; neither census states his parentage. No matching entry has been found in the 1891 or 1901 censuses.

The original marriages of Charles (1851), Thomas (1856), Seneville (1858), and Anselme (1861) identify the same parents and place the family at Saint-Fabien. Julie Gagné was buried there on 25 November 1872, wife of Augustin Gendreau; Augustin was buried there on 1 December 1885, widower of Julie Gagné. These records strengthen the identification of the older census couple. None names Napoléon as a witness, so this review leaves the assessment at likely eliminated.

## Research checked

16 November 2026: all six family records above were read from the original register images, including the continuation of Thomas's marriage. Seven full-page images were saved privately. The marriages and burials establish the family's residence and relationships but provide no additional record naming Napoléon. Status unchanged; no new elimination counted.

Further review on 16 November 2026: Philomène's 1861 marriage and Charles's 1868 remarriage were read in full, including both continuations. Three more full-page images saved (ten total). Neither names Napoléon. The LAFRANCE index for Napoléon Gendron's 1873 marriage to Luce Michaud at Saint-Octave-de-Métis (act 13649553) names Germain Gendron and Delina Dubé as parents: this is a different family, not a later record for C243. That original was not inspected. No new elimination counted.

16 November 2026, extended family follow-up: three original marriages of Charles Gendreau and Arthémise Morency's children were read. Marie / Josaphat Roy dit Lauzon, Saint-Fabien 7 January 1890 M1 (d1p_17290206.jpg), names the fathers Joseph Roy dit Lauzon and Charles Gendreau as witnesses. Adélard / Emma Côté, Saint-Fabien 16 July 1895 M3 (d1p_17290357.jpg), names Louis Côté, bride's father, and Thomas Gendron, groom's uncle; Thomas signs. Lydia / Joseph Roy dit Lauzon, same date M4 and same image, names Pierre Roy dit Lauzon, groom's father, and Josaphat Roy, groom's brother. None names the older Napoléon. LAFRANCE acts 13837466 and 13837481 duplicate Lydia's single marriage; they are not two events. The reported 12 May 1876 birth of Charles's son Napoléon remains an unverified lead: Saint-Anaclet images d1p_17230667.jpg–d1p_17230669.jpg, spanning April into late May, did not yield the baptism. This limited search is not proof of absence. Status and elimination count unchanged.
