## Biography

Francois Napoleon Gendreau was baptized on 11 December 1828 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagné. In 1871 a Napoléon Gendreau, 42, a clerk, was enumerated at Saint-Fabien (Rimouski) in the household of Augustin, 68, and Julie, 72 — his parents' names and ages. In 1881 a Napoléon Gendrow, 51, again a clerk, appears in Alexander Grant's household at Saint-Edmond and Lac-au-Saumon. The given name, surname, occupation, region and near-consistent ages support one continuing adult, and he is counted as eliminated on that chain — but no record in it names his parents, so the connection to the baptism is a correlation, not a proof; that is the uncertainty. He has not been found in the 1891 or 1901 censuses.
