## Biography

- François Napoléon Gendreau was born and baptized on 11 December 1828 at Montmagny, son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagné. [Baptism](#document-act-4080306)
- The 1871 census at Saint-Fabien lists Napoléon Gendreau, 42, a clerk, in the household of Augustin, 68, and Julie, 72. [1871 census, Saint-Fabien](#document-source-002)
- In 1881, Napoléon Gendrow, 51, also a clerk, appears in Alexander Grant's household at Saint-Edmond and Lac-au-Saumon. [1881 census, Saint-Edmond and Lac-au-Saumon](#document-source-003)
- A further adult-record lead appears in two May 1881 Québec hotel-arrival notices: Nap./N. Gendreau is listed alongside A. Grant, with Amqui explicit in the later notice. Neither identifies his parents. [10 May notice](#document-newspaper-1881-05-10) · [12 May notice](#document-newspaper-1881-05-12)
- The matching names, occupation, region, and ages support a probable identification. [Baptism](#document-act-4080306) · [1871 census](#document-source-002) · [1881 census](#document-source-003)
- The relationship to Augustin and Julie is inferred from the household; neither census states his parentage. [1871 census](#document-source-002) · [1881 census](#document-source-003)
- The original marriages of Charles (1851), Thomas (1856), Seneville (1858), and Anselme (1861) identify the same parents and place the family at Saint-Fabien. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
- Julie Gagné was buried there on 25 November 1872, wife of Augustin Gendreau; Augustin was buried there on 1 December 1885, widower of Julie Gagné. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
- These records strengthen the identification of the older census couple. [Family records](#evidence-notes)
- None names Napoléon as a witness, so this review leaves the assessment at likely eliminated. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Research checked

### Hotel arrivals — May 1881

[Le Courrier du Canada, 10 May 1881, page 2, column 6](#document-newspaper-1881-05-10), lists **A. Grant, Anguin; Nap. Gendreau, do** under arrivals at the Mountain Hill House in Québec. **Anguin** is the printed spelling; the following *do* repeats that place. [L’Électeur, 12 May 1881, page 2, column 4](#document-newspaper-1881-05-12), lists **A Grant, N Gendreau, Amqui** under arrivals at Hotel Dion, côte Lamontagne. Both original notices and issue dates were checked on 25 November 2026.

The names and Amqui locality fit the existing lead for **Napoléon Gendrow in Alexander Grant's 1881 census household**. This is a correlation: neither newspaper gives age, parents, occupation or an explicit employment relationship. Several surrounding guests occur in both notices, so the notices may reflect the same or related arrival reporting; they do not establish two separate journeys. The hotel headings differ and their relationship has not been established. A record explicitly connecting this adult to **Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagné** is still needed. **Likely eliminated** is retained.
