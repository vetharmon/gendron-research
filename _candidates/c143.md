## Biography

Joseph Gendron was born on 21 August 1824 and baptized the next day at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud, the son of Jacques Gendron and Geneviève Goulette. His parental names match those of Thomas (C178), baptized at Berthier-sur-Mer in 1826. No later record has been connected to Joseph.

## Assessment and research

The LAFRANCE parental-couple search indexes Geneviève Goulet’s burial at St-Hugues on 31 August 1837 and Jacques Gendron’s remarriage there on 10 January 1843. The original 1843 remarriage and its continuation were inspected during the follow-up for Thomas (C178) on 16 November 2026. They identify Jacques as widower of Geneviève Goulet and his new wife as Louise Chartier. The 1837 burial remains an index lead. These family records point the search for both sons toward St-Hugues; the remarriage does not identify a later record of either son.
