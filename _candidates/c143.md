## Biography

Joseph Gendron was born on 21 August 1824 and baptized the next day at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud, the son of Jacques Gendron and Geneviève Goulette. His parental names match those of Thomas (C178), baptized at Berthier-sur-Mer in 1826. No later record has been connected to Joseph.

## Assessment and research

The LAFRANCE parental-couple search indexes Geneviève Goulet’s burial at St-Hugues on 31 August 1837 and Jacques Gendron’s remarriage there on 10 January 1843. The original 1843 remarriage and its continuation were inspected during the follow-up for Thomas (C178) on 16 November 2026. They identify Jacques as widower of Geneviève Goulet and his new wife as Louise Chartier. The 1837 burial remains an index lead. These family records point the search for both sons toward St-Hugues; the remarriage does not identify a later record of either son.

Three readers agree that the surname ends in **u**, not the **n** produced by OCR. The locators put both residences on the south side of Portage, west of West Street, but at different descriptions. Retain the numerals as printed; their unit has not been established. Neither entry gives an age, birthplace, spouse, parent or relationship. They do not establish a shared household or connect either man to C143 or the earlier census households.

The corresponding alphabetical position in [Bellis’s 1892–1893 directory](http://web.archive.org/web/20240604070955/https://www3.uwsp.edu/library/archives/stevensPointCityDirectories/Portage%20County%20Directory%201892-1893.pdf), **printed page 38, PDF page 45**, was also visually checked. It passes from Jakusz through James and Janiszeski to Januskey without a Jandrou/Jandron entry. This limited check does not establish that the men died, moved, or are absent under another spelling.

Use the **Louis Jandrou, plasterer** entry and its Portage Street locator to seek a marriage, death or other record naming his parents. Compare any result with **Louis/Lewis, born about 1851–1853**, before joining the census households. A separate record must then connect Joseph to **Jacques Gendron and Geneviève Goulette**. **C143 remains Needs work.**
