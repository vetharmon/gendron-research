## Biography

- Ambroise Gendron was born on 17 September 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe, the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin dite Valcourt. [Baptism](#document-act-2995659)

- He married Rosalie Cote on 12 January 1852 at Ste-Rosalie, the act describing him as the adult son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin Valcourt. [Marriage](#document-act-13497817)
