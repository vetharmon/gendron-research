## Biography

Ambroise Gendron was born on 17 September 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe, the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin dite Valcourt. He married Rosalie Cote on 12 January 1852 at Ste-Rosalie, the act describing him as the unmarried adult son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin Valcourt. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 12 January 1852.
