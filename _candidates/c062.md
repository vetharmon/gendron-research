## Biography

Pierre Benjamin Gendron was born on 18 December 1819 and baptized the same day at Cap-St-Ignace (St-Ignace-de-Loyola), the son of Magloire Gendron and Magdeleine Beaulieu. He died on 17 July 1821, aged nineteen months, and was buried at Cap-St-Ignace the following day.

- **[Baptism, 18 December 1819 — LAFRANCE act 2877805](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2877805)** — His baptism at Cap-St-Ignace (lower right), born that day, naming his parents, Magloire Gendron and Magdeleine Beaulieu.
