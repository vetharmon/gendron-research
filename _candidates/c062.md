## Biography

- Pierre Benjamin Gendron was born on 18 December 1819; the baptismal ceremonies were supplied that day at Cap-St-Ignace (St-Ignace-de-Loyola), the son of Abraham Gendron and Magdeleine Beaulieu. [Baptismal ceremonies supplied](#document-act-2877805)

- He died on 13 July 1821, aged eighteen months and some days. On 18 July the priest supplied the funeral ceremonies after the burial; the act does not state the burial date. [Funeral ceremonies supplied](#document-act-2878981)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Funeral ceremonies supplied, 18 July 1821 — LAFRANCE act 2878981](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2878981)** — The priest supplied funeral ceremonies at Cap-St-Ignace (second entry on the right page) to a child already buried there. He died on 13 July, aged eighteen months and some days; the act names the same parents as the baptismal-ceremonies entry.
- **[Baptismal ceremonies supplied, 18 December 1819 — LAFRANCE act 2877805](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2877805)** — The baptismal ceremonies were supplied at Cap-St-Ignace (lower right), born that day, naming his parents, Abraham Gendron and Magdeleine Beaulieu.
