## Biography

- Joseph Antoine Julien Gendreau was born on 6 February 1829 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Laurent Gendreau and Marie Louise Allard. [Baptism](#document-act-2996224)
- He died on 14 July 1831 and was buried at St-Hyacinthe on 16 July 1831, aged two and a half years; the burial names the same parents. [Burial, 16 July 1831](#document-act-3004051) · [Baptism, 7 February 1829](#document-act-2996224)
