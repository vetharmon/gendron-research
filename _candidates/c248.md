## Biography

Joseph Antoine Julien Gendreau was born on 6 February 1829 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Laurent Gendreau and Marie Louise Allard. He died on 14 July 1831 and was buried at St-Hyacinthe on 16 July 1831, aged two and a half years; the burial names the same parents.
