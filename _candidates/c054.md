## Biography

- Alexis Gendron was born on 4 September 1819 and baptized the following day at Champlain, son of Alexis Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie Julie Adam, residents of Batiscan. The original baptism is B31, folio 461. [Baptism](#document-act-2419075)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Baptism, 5 September 1819 — LAFRANCE act 2419075](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2419075)** — His baptism at Champlain, the only record connected to him so far.


## Younger namesake — 15 November 2026

The [original Batiscan baptism of 31 January 1829](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-899M-RS2Y) names another Alexis, born the previous day, son of Alexis Gendron and Julie Adam. This is [C246]({{ '/candidates/c246/' | relative_url }}), a distinct younger child.

The repeated name supports a possible childhood death of the older boy. It does not prove one: both sons could have survived. No burial has been located. FamilySearch searches for Alexis Gendron with an exact death-year range of 1819–1829, with and without the parents, returned no matching burial. No complete original-register burial sweep has been made.

**Assessment: possible elimination.** The next task is to find a burial or another record identifying the older Alexis.
