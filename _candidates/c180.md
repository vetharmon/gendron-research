Original baptism 29 March 1826 Napierville, act 3245011, image d1p_02350434.jpg, B85 lower left, names Vital, born previous day, son of Vital Gendron, cultivateur, and Rosalie Fortin. Original marriage 25 September 1849 L'Acadie, act 13401077, image d1p_02300909.jpg, M10 upper/middle right, names Vital Gendron, adult son of Vital Gendron and Rosalie Fortin, marrying Marie Flavie Vétu Latremouille, daughter of Nicolas Vétu Latremouille and deceased Geneviève Brault. Original baptism 19 December 1860 St-Valentin, act 5494863, image d1p_02500535.jpg, B114 upper right, names Vital Euclide, born previous day, son of Vital Gendron, journalier, and Flavie Latremouille. This supplies continuing separate-family evidence after Franklin's Missouri household was documented. The 1860 act's closing wording refers to the father declaring inability to sign. PRDH family 256864 corroborates the continuing couple; the household's indexed child date 18 December is birth, while baptism is 19 December. The exclusion rests on linked original parentage and family records, not Vital's given name alone.
