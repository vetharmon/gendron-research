## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Vital Gendron was born on 28 March 1826 and baptized the next day at Napierville (St-Cyprien-de-Léry), the son of Vital Gendron, a cultivateur, and Rosalie Fortin. [Baptism](#document-act-3245011)
- **Marriage:** He married Marie Flavie Vétu Latremouille on 25 September 1849 at L'Acadie. [Marriage](#document-act-13401077)
- **Later family:** He appears at St-Valentin on 19 December 1860 as the father of Vital Euclide, his son by Flavie, then working as a journalier; the act records that he declared he could not sign. [Vital Euclide's baptism](#document-act-5494863)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 25 September 1849 — Généalogie Québec act 13401077](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13401077)** — His marriage to Marie Flavie Vétu Latremouille at L'Acadie (entry M10, upper right). The act names him as the adult son of Vital Gendron and Rosalie Fortin, tying the groom to the 1826 baptism, and the bride as the daughter of Nicolas Vétu Latremouille and the late Geneviève Breau (as written in this copy).
- **[Baptism of his son, 19 December 1860 — Généalogie Québec act 5494863](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5494863)** — Vital Euclide, born the previous day at St-Valentin (entry B112, upper right), son of Vital Gendron, journalier, and Flavie Latremouille. It carries his family to 1860. PRDH's compiled family (no. 256864) corroborates the continuing couple, but has no source link in this file.
