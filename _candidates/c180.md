## Biography

Vital Gendron was born on 28 March 1826 and baptized the next day at Napierville (St-Cyprien-de-Léry), the son of Vital Gendron, a cultivateur, and Rosalie Fortin. He married Marie Flavie Vétu Latremouille on 25 September 1849 at L'Acadie. He appears at St-Valentin on 19 December 1860 as the father of Vital Euclide, his son by Flavie, by then working as a journalier; the act records that he declared he could not sign. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 19 December 1860.
