## Biography

- Francois Regis Gendron was baptized on 4 June 1829 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Poirier. [Baptism](#document-act-4253937)
- He died aged five years and was buried at Beauharnois on 12 March 1834; the burial names the same parents. [Burial, 12 March 1834](#document-act-4257826) · [Baptism, 4 June 1829](#document-act-4253937)
