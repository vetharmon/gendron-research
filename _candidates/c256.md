## Biography

Francois Regis Gendron was baptized on 4 June 1829 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Poirier. He died aged five years and was buried at Beauharnois on 12 March 1834; the burial names the same parents.
