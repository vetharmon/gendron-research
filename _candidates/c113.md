## Biography

Toussaint Gendron was born on 7 March 1823 and baptized the next day at St-Jude, the son of Louis Gendron and Marie Gaudet. He died on 17 March 1823 and was buried at St-Jude on 19 March. The burial states his age as nine days, one day less than the ten days between his birth and death.
