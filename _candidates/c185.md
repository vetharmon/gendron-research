## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Charles Gendron was born on 30 June 1826 and baptized the next day at Québec (Notre-Dame-de-Québec), the son of Charles Gendron and Angelique Letellier. [Baptism](#document-act-3286937)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 8 September 1826, aged two months and eight days, and was buried the next day at Québec. [Burial](#document-act-3041803)
