## Biography

Édouard Gendron was born on 4 August 1817 and baptized the same day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Ambroise Gendron and Marie Massicot. He married Marie Anne Trudel, daughter of Jean Baptiste Trudel and Judith Cornellier, on 21 May 1844 at Ste-Mélanie. He appears at Ste-Mélanie as the father of Marie Amanda, born on 8 July 1859 and baptized the next day; the act expressly records the father present and unable to sign. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 9 July 1859.
