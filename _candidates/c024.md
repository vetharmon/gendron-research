## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2374426
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 4 August 1817 Berthierville, B79 lower left, names Édouard born that day, son of Ambroise Gendron and Marie Massicot. Marriage 21 May 1844 Ste-Mélanie, M6 lower right, names those parents and bride Marie Anne Trudel, daughter of Jean Baptiste Trudel and Judith Cornellier. Baptism 9 July 1859 Ste-Mélanie, B44 lower right, names Marie Amanda, born previous day, daughter of Édouard Gendron and Marie Anne Trudel; expressly records the father present and unable to sign. PRDH family 229114 corroborates intervening children. The original parent-to-marriage-to-family chain supports exclusion.
