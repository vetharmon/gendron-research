## Biography

Zephirin Gendreau was born on 6 August 1821 and baptized the same day at Nicolet (St-Jean-Baptiste), the son of Julien Gendreau, a workman (ouvrier), and Marie Jutras. He married Marguerite Morency on 23 February 1841 at Sherbrooke (St-Michel); the act calls him a farmer (cultivateur) and a minor son of Julien Gendreau and Marie Jutras, then of Trois-Rivières. His son Joseph Alphonse, born on 25 July 1861, was baptized at Sherbrooke on 4 August 1861, naming Zephirin and Marguerite Morency as his parents. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 4 August 1861.
