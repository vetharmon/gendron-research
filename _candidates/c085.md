Original marriage 23 February 1841 Sherbrooke St-Michel, act 13501365, image d1p_03210103.jpg, M3 upper right, identifies Zéphirin Gendreau, cultivateur, minor son of Julien Gendreau and Marie Jutras of Trois-Rivières, marrying Marguerite Morency, minor daughter of Marcel Morency and Marguerite Vallière. Original baptism of Joseph Alphonse, 4 August 1861 Sherbrooke, act 5966185, image d1p_03210723.jpg, B93 upper left, names Zéphirin Gendreau, cultivateur, and Marguerite Morency as parents; child born 25 July.
