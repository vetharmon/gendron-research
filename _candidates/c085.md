## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2770561
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 6 August 1821 Nicolet, act 2770561, image d1p_25551025.jpg, bottom left, names Zéphirin, born that day, son of Julien Gendraut, ouvrier, and Marie Jutras; father signs. Original marriage 23 February 1841 Sherbrooke St-Michel, act 13501365, image d1p_03210103.jpg, M3 upper right, identifies Zéphirin Gendreau, cultivateur, minor son of Julien Gendreau and Marie Jutras of Trois-Rivières, marrying Marguerite Morency, minor daughter of Marcel Morency and Marguerite Vallière. Original baptism of Joseph Alphonse, 4 August 1861 Sherbrooke, act 5966185, image d1p_03210723.jpg, B93 upper left, names Zéphirin Gendreau, cultivateur, and Marguerite Morency as parents; child born 25 July. The baptism's 4 August date is distinct from the indexed family birth date 25 July. Matching birth parents, linked spouse and continuing separate household support exclusion from Franklin's Missouri identity. PRDH profile 788594 and family 214109 corroborate the reconstruction. No inference of later physical attendance is necessary for this exclusion. Original source images downloaded unchanged and cited in Scan-Citations.csv.
