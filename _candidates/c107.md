## Biography

Joseph Gendreau was born on 2 December 1822 and baptized the same day at St-Denis-sur-Richelieu, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Marie Courtemanche. A probable burial at Iberville on 8 May 1827 names Joseph Gendreau, aged five, son of Jean Baptiste Gendreault and Magdeleine Courtemanche. The mother's given name differs from Marie in the baptism.

## Documents

The age and the father's name fit the 1822 baptism; the mother's given name, Magdeleine against Marie, is the unresolved point.
- **[Baptism, 2 December 1822 — LAFRANCE act 2692933](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2692933)** — His baptism at St-Denis-sur-Richelieu (upper left, entry B157), on the day of his birth, naming his parents as Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Marie Courtemanche.

## Residence and name comparison — 21 November 2026

The original baptism B157 expressly describes the father as **cultivateur à St-Ours**, although Joseph was baptized at St-Denis. This gives a specific residence to compare with the earlier family records: [C025's 1818 burial](../c025/) also places Jean Baptiste Gendro and Marie Courtemanche at St-Ours.

C025's own baptism gives the mother's fuller name **Marie Euphrosine Courtemanche**. That compound/short-name pair does not resolve **Magdeleine Courtemanche**, which remains plainly written in Joseph's proposed 1827 burial. The residence correlation therefore improves the family reconstruction while leaving this candidate **Likely eliminated**.
