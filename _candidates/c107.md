## Biography

Joseph Gendreau was born on 2 December 1822 and baptized the same day at St-Denis-sur-Richelieu, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Marie Courtemanche. He may be the Joseph Gendreau, aged five, who was buried at Iberville on 8 May 1827; that burial names the parents as Jean Baptiste Gendreault and Magdeleine Courtemanche. The father's name matches, but the mother's given name differs between the two acts — Marie in the baptism, Magdeleine in the burial — so it is not certain the buried child was this one.
