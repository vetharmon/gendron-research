## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Amable Gendron was born on 23 January 1826 and baptized the next day at St-Constant (La Prairie), the son of Antoine Gendron and Marie Louise Roy. [Baptism](#document-act-3067896)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 11 July 1826, with a recorded age of six months, and was buried at St-Constant the next day. [Burial](#document-act-3072378)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 12 July 1826 — LAFRANCE act 3072378](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3072378)** — His burial at St-Constant (upper right of the page): Amable, died the previous day, with a recorded age of six months, son of Antoine Gendron and Louise Roy. The parents match the baptism. The original says six months; this rounded age is close to the five months and eighteen days implied by the birth and death dates. The earlier five-month paraphrase has been corrected.
