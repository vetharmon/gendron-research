## Biography

Hubert Gendreau was baptized on 17 April 1817 at La Pocatière (Ste-Anne), in a joint baptism with his twin brother Jean Nestor, the sons of Hubert Gendreau and Constance Perrault. He died within days and was buried with his brother on 21 April 1817; the joint burial names both twins explicitly. His death is documented by that shared act rather than by a separate burial entry of his own.
