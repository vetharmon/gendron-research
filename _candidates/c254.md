## Biography

Prudent Gendron was born on 23 May 1829 and baptized the next day at Rimouski (St-Germain), the son of Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard. On 4 September 1854 he married Marie Bosse at Ste-Flavie, the act naming him as the adult son of Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 4 September 1854.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 4 September 1854 — LAFRANCE act 13401072](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401072)** — His marriage to Marie Bosse at Ste-Flavie (the saved continuation page completes the act). It names his parents, Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard, tying the groom to the 1829 Rimouski baptism.
- **[Baptism, 24 May 1829 — LAFRANCE act 3062064](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3062064)** — His baptism at Rimouski: born the previous day, son of Prudent Gendron (written Geandron in the act) and Henriette Chamard.
