Original baptism 24 May 1829 at Rimouski names Prudent, born the previous day, son of Prudent Gendron/Geandron and Henriette Chamard. Original marriage 4 September 1854 at Ste-Flavie identifies Prudent Gendron, adult son of Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard, marrying Marie Bosse. The saved continuation closes the marriage. Matching parents and the unmarried form of this later marriage support exclusion from Franklin, already married with an infant in Missouri in 1850.
