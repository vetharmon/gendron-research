## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Joseph Julien Gendreau was born and baptized on 24 November 1825 at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), the son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. [Baptism](#document-act-4371163)
- **Marriage:** He married Henriette Diamos dit Beausoleil on 5 November 1850 at St-Damase. [Marriage](#document-act-13557125)
- **Later family:** He appears there again as the father of Damase Siméas, born and baptized on 15 February 1861, his son by Henriette; the baptism expressly says that the father was absent. It names him as the father but does not establish his presence at the ceremony. [Damase Siméas's baptism](#document-act-5413938)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **Original-image correction (23 November 2026):** The 1861 baptism ends *Le père absent*. Its statement that the sponsors could not sign does not apply to the absent father. [Damase Siméas’s baptism](#document-act-5413938)

- **[Marriage, 5 November 1850 — LAFRANCE act 13557125](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13557125)** — His marriage to Henriette Diamos dite Beausoleil at St-Damase (entry M30, left page). The act names him as the adult son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput, tying the groom to the 1825 baptism, and the bride as the minor daughter of Paul Diamos dit Beausoleil and Josephte Yon. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13557125).
- **[Baptism of his son, 15 February 1861 — LAFRANCE act 5413938](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5413938)** — Damase Siméas, born that day at St-Damase (entry B11, right page), the legitimate son of Julien Gendreault and Henriette Diamos dite Beausoleil. It carries his family at St-Damase to 1861. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5413938).
- **[Baptism, 24 November 1825 — LAFRANCE act 4371163](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4371163)** — His baptism at St-Damase (middle left of the page): Joseph Julien, born that day, son of Joseph Julien Gendreault, cultivateur, and Louise Thecle Chaput. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4371163).
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 270046](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/270046)** — PRDH's compiled family for Joseph Julien and Henriette, which corroborates the children born to the couple between the 1850 marriage and the 1861 baptism.
