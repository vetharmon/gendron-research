## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4371163
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 24 November 1825 St-Damase identifies Joseph Julien, born that day, son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. Original marriage 5 November 1850 St-Damase names Joseph Julien, son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput, and bride Henriette Diamos dit Beausoleil. Original baptism of Damase Siméas, 15 February 1861, names Julien Gendreault and Henriette Diamos dit Beausoleil as parents; father absent. PRDH family 270046 corroborates the intervening children. The marriage and continuing distinct family establish an identity separate from Franklin’s Missouri household; the child baptism is not represented as proof of the father’s physical presence.
Additional original-record corroboration, 9 November 2026 (no new exclusion counted):
Original baptism 24 November 1825 St-Damase, act 4371163, d1p_3660577.jpg middle left: Joseph Julien born that day, son of Joseph Julien Gendreault, cultivateur, and Louise Thecle Chaput. Original marriage 5 November 1850 St-Damase, act 13557125, d1p_3670038.jpg M30 left page: Joseph Julien Gendrault, adult son of Joseph Gendrault and Louise Chaput, marries Henriette Diamos dite Beausoleil, minor daughter of Paul Diamos dit Beausoleil and Josephte Yon. Original baptism 15 February 1861 St-Damase, act 5413938, d1p_3670435.jpg B11 right page: Damase Simeas born the same day, legitimate son of Julien Gendreault and Henriette Diamos dite Beausoleil. The father is among those who could not sign. The parental names and parish bridge the 1825 child to the 1850 groom; abbreviated paternal and maternal given names are disclosed, not silently expanded. The uncommon double given name, matching parentage and continuing spouse-linked family support a distinct Quebec identity into 1861, incompatible with Franklin's documented Missouri family.
