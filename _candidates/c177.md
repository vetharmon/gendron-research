## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 3 March 1826 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Simon Gendron, a cultivateur, and Marie Louise Dion. He died on 2 August 1826, aged about five months, and was buried at St-Hyacinthe on 4 August 1826.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 4 August 1826 — LAFRANCE act 3003172](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3003172)** — His burial at St-Hyacinthe (entry S129, right page): Jean Baptiste, died two days earlier, aged about five months, son of Simon Gendron and Louise Dion. Name, both parents, parish and the age all match the baptism.
- **[Baptism, 4 March 1826 — LAFRANCE act 2994921](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2994921)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe (entry B66, bottom left): born the previous day, son of Simon Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie Louise Dion; his father signed the act. An audit of the original confirmed the mother is Marie Louise Dion — the Marie Angelique Talbot of the preceding entry belongs to a different child's baptism.
