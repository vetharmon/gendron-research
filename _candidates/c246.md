## Biography

- The civil register records Alexis Gendron as born the morning of 31 January 1829 and baptized that day at Batiscan, son of Alexis Gendron, cultivateur, and Julie Adam. [Baptism](#document-act-4549352)
- He appears in adult records as Eusèbe Gendron. [1855 marriage](#document-act-13400898) · [1901 census](#document-source-005)
## Assessment

**Eliminated.** The identification rests on the same parental couple in the reported 1855 original reading and closely matching birth dates. On 9 January 1855, Eusèbe Gendron of St-François-Xavier-de-Batiscan married Sophie Vermette at Ste-Geneviève-de-Batiscan. Marriage M2 names his parents as Alexis Gendron and Julie Adam.

The original 1901 census lists Eusèbe with his wife Sophie and son Aimé at St-François-Xavier-de-Batiscan. It gives his birth date as **30 January 1829**, one day earlier than the inspected civil baptism, which says he was born the morning of 31 January. The census records him as a married household head, aged 72. These records establish a separate continuing life in Québec.

No record inspected uses both Alexis and Eusèbe together. The identification is based on the matching parents, nearby birth date, locality and continuing marriage, rather than a linked family tree. The reported 1905 burial remains uninspected and is not needed for this assessment.

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- [Original baptism, 31 January 1829](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-899M-RS2Y) — the earlier parish-copy reading reported a birth the previous day. The civil copy inspected on 23 November 2026 instead says “né le matin” on 31 January; this difference requires checking against the parish copy. Parents Alexis Gendron and Julie Adam are clear.
- [Original marriage, 9 January 1855](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400898) — M2, civil register, Ste-Geneviève-de-Batiscan; names both parents. Read 15 November 2026.
- [Original 1901 census](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSS1-G93W-5) — page 21, household 168, lines 23–25; image 1586. Reported birth date and household with Sophie and Aimé. Read 15 November 2026.

## Family connection

His parents also had [Alexis C054]({{ '/candidates/c054/' | relative_url }}), born in September 1819. The two original baptisms establish separate children with the same first name. This identification of the younger son does not establish the older son’s fate.

- **[Civil baptism audit, 23 November 2026](https://numerique.banq.qc.ca/patrimoine/details/52327/4425985)** — The inspected civil original says “Alexis né le matin”, following the date 31 January 1829. The previous exact-date claim of 30 January is therefore not supported by this copy. The 1855 marriage and 1901 census originals were not available for reinspection in this part of the audit; their earlier readings remain explicitly attributed above.
