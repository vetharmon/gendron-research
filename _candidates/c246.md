## Biography

Alexis Gendron was born on 30 January 1829 and baptized the next day at Batiscan, son of Alexis Gendron, cultivateur, and Julie Adam. He appears in adult records as Eusèbe Gendron.

## Assessment

**Eliminated.** The identification rests on the same parental couple and an exact birth-date match across original records. On 9 January 1855, Eusèbe Gendron of St-François-Xavier-de-Batiscan married Sophie Vermette at Ste-Geneviève-de-Batiscan. Marriage M2 names his parents as Alexis Gendron and Julie Adam.

The original 1901 census lists Eusèbe with his wife Sophie and son Aimé at St-François-Xavier-de-Batiscan. It gives his birth date as **30 January 1829**, exactly matching C246’s original baptism. The census records him as a married household head, aged 72. These records establish a separate continuing life in Québec.

No record inspected uses both Alexis and Eusèbe together. The identification is based on the matching parents, exact birth date, locality and continuing marriage, rather than a linked family tree. The reported 1905 burial remains uninspected and is not needed for this assessment.
