## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born on 5 March 1825 and baptized the next day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of François Gendron, a journalier, and Josephte Maçon. He married Sophie Sarrazin on 7 September 1841 at Berthierville; the act names him as the minor son of François Gendron and Josephte Maçon, and the bride as the adult daughter of Étienne Sarrazin and Magdeleine Brisset Dupas. Their son Joseph Prime, two days old, was baptized on 10 August 1850 at Ste-Élisabeth, the act describing Pierre as a journalier domiciled at Berthier. PRDH's family record lists further children in 1852, 1855 and 1859, as indexed leads that were not inspected in the underlying research. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 10 August 1850.
