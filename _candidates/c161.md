## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Pierre Gendron was born on 5 March 1825 and baptized the next day at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of François Gendron, a journalier, and Josephte Maçon. [Baptism](#document-act-3302502)
- **Marriage:** He married Sophie Sarrazin on 7 September 1841 at Berthierville; the act names him as the minor son of François Gendron and Josephte Maçon, and the bride as the adult daughter of Étienne Sarrazin and Magdeleine Brisset Dupas. [Marriage](#document-act-13401067)
- **Later family:** Their son Joseph Prime, two days old, was baptized on 10 August 1850 at Ste-Élisabeth; the act describes Pierre as a journalier domiciled at Berthier. [Joseph Prime's baptism](#document-act-5454885)

## Research status

PRDH's family record lists further children in 1852, 1855 and 1859, as indexed leads that were not inspected in the underlying research.
