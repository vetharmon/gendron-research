## Biography

Joseph Gendreau was born on 24 January 1823 and baptized the next day at La Présentation, the son of Laurent Jeandreau and Lisette Allard. The baptism describes his father as a cultivateur residing at St-Damase. The combined evidence identifies him with Joseph Gendreau, who married Marie Célina Lavimodière at St-Hyacinthe on 2 February 1863 and later lived at Ste-Madeleine. He died there on 4 October 1898 and was buried on 6 October, recorded as a cultivateur, husband of Célina Lavimodière, aged 76.

## Identification

**Solid elimination**, based on the correlated original records and the exclusion of the younger same-family Joseph.

The 1823 baptism names **Lisette Allard**, while the 1863 marriage names the deceased **Louise Allard**, wife of the deceased Laurent Gendreau. Both readings are retained. The identification rests on the same paternal name and maternal surname, the St-Damase–St-Hyacinthe setting, compatible age, and the later family associations. The Laurent Gendreau/Allard couple-index search recorded on 16 November returned 28 acts; the 1823 baptism was the only returned entry using Lisette, while the others used Marie Louise, Louise or Marie. That indexed pattern supports the family reconstruction but is not an explicit alias statement.

At [Marie Delvina’s baptism on 17 March 1885](https://www.ancestry.com/imageviewer/collections/1091/images/d13p_4000372), Joseph and Célina’s daughter had **Godefroy Gendreau of Roxton Falls** and **Édesse Gendreau** as godparents. Godefroy’s [23 June 1885 remarriage](https://www.ancestry.com/imageviewer/collections/1091/images/d1p_02810379) identifies him as a blacksmith of St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Roxton and the widower of Éléonore Denonville. That former wife connects him to [C194’s established 1852 marriage](/gendron-research/candidates/c194/), which names Laurent Gendreau and the deceased Marie Louise Allard as his parents. The name, place and signature support identifying him with the March godfather. Godfroy appears again among the witnesses to Joseph’s 1898 burial.

Édesse’s [26 July 1831 marriage to Thomas Chaput](https://www.ancestry.com/imageviewer/collections/1091/images/d13p_3900126) also names Laurent Gendreau and Marie Louise Allard as her parents. This gives a second sponsor name belonging to the same parental family. The 1885 baptism does not name the godmother’s spouse, and neither that baptism nor Joseph’s burial states a sibling relationship. The associations are supporting identity evidence, with those limits preserved.

The [1898 burial](https://www.ancestry.com/imageviewer/collections/1091/images/d13p_4000634) gives age 76, closely compatible with the 1823 birth: Joseph would have been 75 years and eight months. The younger [Joseph Antoine Julien, C248](/gendron-research/candidates/c248/), born in February 1829 to Laurent Gendreau and Marie Louise Allard, died in July 1831; his original burial names the same parents and gives age two and a half. He cannot be the 1863 groom. The compiled 1828 birth estimate supplies no separate baptism, and the original 1863 marriage calls Joseph majeur without a numerical age.

The conclusion treats Lisette/Louise as an isolated maternal given-name discrepancy within this correlated family. It does not turn either source reading into a documented alias. Joseph’s continuing life in Québec is distinct from Franklin Jondro’s.

## Earlier follow-up

The original baptism and both pages of the 1863 marriage were reread on 16 November 2026. Godefroy’s original 1852 marriage and continuation were also examined. The continuation calls **Pierre Gendreau Godefroy’s brother**; it does not name Joseph. That work established the family context, while the later original records inspected on 18 November supplied the additional associations and age evidence used in the present conclusion.
