## Biography

Joseph Gendreau was born on 24 January 1823 and baptized the next day at La Présentation, the son of Laurent Gendreau and Lisette Allard. He may be the Joseph Gendreau who married Marie Celina Lavimodiere on 2 February 1863 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame); that act names the groom as the adult son of the deceased Laurent Gendreau and the deceased Louise Allard. The father's name and the parish region match, but the mother's given name differs — Lisette in the baptism, Louise in the marriage — and no original record has been found that bridges the two names, so it is not certain the groom was this child.
