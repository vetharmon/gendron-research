## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2789504
CURRENT STATUS: Qualified later-life lead — not counted
Three original scans inspected and downloaded 9 November. Baptism 25 January 1823, lower left, names Joseph born previous day, son of Laurent Jeandreau and Lisette Allard. Marriage M4, 2 February 1863 St-Hyacinthe Notre-Dame, opens lower right on d1p_03911207.jpg and continues upper left on d1p_03911208.jpg: Joseph Gendreau, adult son of deceased Laurent Gendreau and deceased Louise Allard, to Marie Celina Lavimodiere. The original maternal given names Lisette/Louise differ; PRDH profile 4558024 reconstructs Louise, but no independent original alias bridge was established. Retain as qualified, not excluded. Marriage continuation saved completely, including the maternal name; no inference is based on an incomplete opening alone.
