## Biography

Joseph Gendreau was born on 24 January 1823 and baptized the next day at La Présentation, the son of Laurent Gendreau and Lisette Allard. A probable match is Joseph Gendreau, who married Marie Celina Lavimodiere at St-Hyacinthe on 2 February 1863. The marriage names the deceased Laurent Gendreau and Louise Allard as his parents. The mother's given name differs from Lisette in the baptism.
