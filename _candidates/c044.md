## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Joseph Prudent Gendron was born on 11 July 1818 and baptized the same day at St-Pierre-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud (St-Pierre-du-Sud), the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Anne Ruel. [Baptism](#document-act-2920193)
- **First marriage:** He married Marie Constance Boulé on 20 August 1839 at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud. [Marriage](#document-act-13401001)
- **Remarriage:** On 20 August 1866 at St-Hugues, the act describes him as a cultivateur and Lieutenant Capitaine de Milice, widower of Constance Boulé, and his bride as Henriette Petit dit Gobin. [Remarriage](#document-act-13594654)
