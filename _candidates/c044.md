## Biography

Joseph Prudent Gendron was born on 11 July 1818 and baptized the same day at St-Pierre-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud (St-Pierre-du-Sud), the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Anne Ruel. He married Marie Constance Boulé on 20 August 1839 at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud. After her death he married again on 20 August 1866 at St-Hugues, where the act describes him as a cultivateur and lieutenant of militia, widower of Constance Boulé, and his bride as Henriette Petit dit Gobin.
