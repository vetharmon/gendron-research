## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2920193
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 11 July 1818 names Joseph Prudent, born that day, son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Anne Ruel. Marriage 20 August 1839 at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud names those parents and bride Marie Constance Boulé. Remarriage 20 August 1866 at St-Hugues identifies Prudent Gendron, cultivateur and lieutenant of militia, widower of Constance Boulé, marrying Henriette Petit dit Gobin. The continuous marital identity and original signatures establish a separate adult from Franklin.
