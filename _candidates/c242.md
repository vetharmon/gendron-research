## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendreau was born on 20 October 1828 and baptized the next day at L'Acadie (Ste-Marguerite-de-Blairfindie), the son of Hubert Gendreau and Marie Perrou. He married Adeline Messier on 13 November 1854 at St-Jean-sur-Richelieu, where the act names him — as Jean Baptiste Gendron, a voyageur — the adult son of the deceased Hubert Gendron and Constance Perrault, and names Laurent Gendron as his brother. His mother's two given names, Marie and Constance, are joined by his parents' own marriage record (below): the bride of 1815 was Marie Constance Perrault. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 13 November 1854.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 13 November 1854 — LAFRANCE act 13558756](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13558756)** — His marriage to Adeline Messier at St-Jean-sur-Richelieu (right page, entry M20): Jean Baptiste Gendron, a voyageur, adult son of the deceased Hubert Gendron and Constance Perrault, with his brother Laurent Gendron named in the act. Gendreau/Gendron and Perrou/Perrault are the surname spellings of the two acts.
- **[Parents' marriage, 9 January 1815 — Généalogie Québec act 5702361](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5702361)** — His parents' marriage at La Pocatière (middle left) names Hubert Gendrault and Marie Constance Perrault. The bride's compound name is what connects the Marie Perrou of the 1828 baptism to the Constance Perrault of the 1854 marriage.
- **[Baptism, 21 October 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4473750](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4473750)** — His baptism at L'Acadie (right page, entry B185): born the previous day, son of Hubert Gendreau and Marie Perrou.
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 124775](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/124775)** — PRDH's compiled family corroborates Jean Baptiste and his brother Laurent in the same household.
