## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4473750
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism B185, right page, 21 October 1828 at L'Acadie: Jean Baptiste, born the previous day, son of Hubert Gendreau and Marie Perrou. Original marriage M20, right page, 13 November 1854 at St-Jean-sur-Richelieu: Jean Baptiste Gendron, voyageur, adult son of deceased Hubert Gendron and Constance Perrault, marrying Adeline Messier; Laurent Gendron is explicitly the groom's brother. New original bridge inspected 9 November: parents' marriage, 9 January 1815 La Pocatiere, d1p_18291028.jpg, middle left, names Hubert Gendrault and Marie Constance Perrault. The compound maternal name independently connects Marie and Constance; Perrou/Perrault and Gendreau/Gendron are disclosed surname spellings. Family 124775 corroborates Jean Baptiste and his brother Laurent in the same household. The 1854 adult marital identity, linked through the original parental marriage, supports exclusion from Franklin's Missouri identity.
