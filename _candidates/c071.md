## Biography

- Eusebe Horace Gendreau was born and baptized on 14 October 1820 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Pierre Gendro, a cultivateur, and Marguerite Delorme; the baptism itself writes the family name as Gendro. [Baptism](#document-act-2477792)

- His parents' marriage at St-Hyacinthe on 10 January 1820 names Pierre Rondo, son of Etienne Rondo and Marie Ursule Lapointe, and Marguerite Delorme, daughter of Joseph Delorme and Marie Guilbert. [Parents’ marriage](#document-act-5732373)

- Eusebe Rondeau, who married Brigitte Combes dit Brindamour at St-Charles-sur-Richelieu on 2 September 1839, is in all likelihood this man: the groom was a minor son of Pierre Rondeau and Marguerite Delorme, and his father witnessed the marriage. The existing research notes also record children of the couple at St-Charles. [Marriage](#document-act-13476068) [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

- The difference is the father's surname: the 1820 baptism writes Pierre Gendro; the parents' marriage and the son's marriage write Pierre Rondeau. [Baptism](#document-act-2477792) [Parents’ marriage](#document-act-5732373) [Marriage](#document-act-13476068)

- The existing research notes report the later family-name form Bondo at the 1845 St-Charles baptism of the couple’s daughter Emilie Malvina. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Research status

No other Pierre and Marguerite Delorme couple appears in the records, no marriage for a Pierre Gendro or baptism for Eusebe Rondeau was identified in those searches — this baptism is the only one that fits the groom.

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 2 September 1839 — LAFRANCE act 13476068](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13476068)** — Eusebe Rondeau, minor son of Pierre Rondeau and Marguerite Delorme, marries Brigitte Combes dit Brindamour at St-Charles-sur-Richelieu (entry M6), his father present as a witness. The parents match this candidate's baptism; the father's surname is written Rondeau here and Gendro in the baptism.
- **[Baptism, 14 October 1820 — LAFRANCE act 2477792](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2477792)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe (entry B353): born that day, son of Pierre Gendro and Marguerite Delorme. His parents' own marriage in the same parish on 10 January 1820 (archived image, LAFRANCE act 5732373) names the groom as Pierre Rondo — the earliest appearance of the Rondeau form of the name.

## Follow-up, 16 November 2026

**Status remains likely eliminated.** The original baptism of Émilie Malvina, 27 July 1845 at St-Charles-sur-Richelieu (act 4720798, B44), records her birth on 25 July and names Eusèbe Bondo and Brigitte Brindamour. This confirms the child previously known from the index.

A later marriage at St-Dominique on 18 October 1864 (act 13581736, M13) names a bride indexed as Madeleine Rondeau, but her signature reads **Melvina Rondeau**. Her parents are Eusèbe Rondeau and **Mélie Comble**. Eusèbe is present as her father; the bride is described as a minor. The 1845 birth would fit that description. This is a plausible continuation of the family, but the mother's Mélie/Brigitte difference has not been resolved. It is not yet treated as a firm link to C071.

Two other originals were checked for family witnesses: Marguerite Delorme’s burial at St-Charles on 12 November 1842 (act 4721840, S28, continuing onto the next image), and Louis Rondeau’s marriage to Émérence Forcier at St-Hyacinthe on 2 February 1847 (act 13476124, M12). The former names the widow of Pierre Rondeau, dead two days, aged 54; the latter names the deceased parents Pierre Rondeau and Marguerite Delorme. Neither supplied a witness explicitly identifying Eusèbe as a son or brother.
