## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2477792
CURRENT STATUS: Qualified later-life lead — not counted
Qualified Rondeau-family lead, not counted as an exclusion. Original baptism B353,14October1820 St-Hyacinthe, names Eusebe Horace born that day, father Pierre Gendro, cultivateur, and mother Marguerite Delorme; the Gendro surname is genuinely present. Newly downloaded original parental marriage M3,10January1820 in the same parish, names Pierre Rondo, minor son of Etienne Rondo and Marie Ursule Lapointe, marrying Marguerite Delorme, minor daughter of Joseph Delorme and Marie Guilbert. Newly downloaded original marriage M6,2September1839 St-Charles-sur-Richelieu, names Eusebe Rondeau, minor son of Pierre Rondeau and Marguerite Delorme, marrying Brigitte Combes dit Brindamour; father Pierre Rondeau is a witness. This supports the proposed Rondeau reconstruction but does not independently correct the1820 surname or establish a later conflicting identity. The1839 marriage names Brigitte;1848/1850/1874 index leads name Emilie/Emelie Brindamour, a second unresolved difference. These leads are not adopted as a continuing spouse chain. FamilySearch baptism index X43B-SBGB also gives Gendreau; film005471178 reports no images, so no second original baptism copy was obtained. PRDH774119 remains a reconstruction lead, not proof of its own linkage.
