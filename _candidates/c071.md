## Biography

Eusebe Horace Gendreau was born and baptized on 14 October 1820 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Pierre Gendro, a cultivateur, and Marguerite Delorme; the baptism itself writes the family name as Gendro. His parents' marriage at St-Hyacinthe on 10 January 1820 names Pierre Rondo, son of Etienne Rondo and Marie Ursule Lapointe, and Marguerite Delorme, daughter of Joseph Delorme and Marie Guilbert. A possible later match is Eusebe Rondeau, who married Brigitte Combes dit Brindamour at St-Charles-sur-Richelieu on 2 September 1839. That groom was a minor son of Pierre Rondeau and Marguerite Delorme; his father witnessed the marriage. The surname changes from Gendro to Rondeau, and later index entries name the wife Emilie or Emelie Brindamour. These differences remain unresolved.
