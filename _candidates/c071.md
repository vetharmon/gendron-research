## Biography

Eusebe Horace Gendreau was born and baptized on 14 October 1820 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Pierre Gendro, a cultivateur, and Marguerite Delorme; the baptism itself writes the family name as Gendro. His parents had married in the same parish on 10 January 1820, but that act names the groom as Pierre Rondo, son of Etienne Rondo and Marie Ursule Lapointe, and the bride as Marguerite Delorme, daughter of Joseph Delorme and Marie Guilbert. He may be the Eusebe Rondeau who married Brigitte Combes dit Brindamour on 2 September 1839 at St-Charles-sur-Richelieu — that act names the groom as a minor son of Pierre Rondeau and Marguerite Delorme, with Pierre Rondeau present as a witness. The parents match, but the surname changes from Gendro to Rondeau between the records and no record bridges the two spellings, so it is not certain the groom was this child. Later index leads naming an Emilie or Emelie Brindamour add a second unresolved difference, in the wife's given name.
