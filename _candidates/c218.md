## Biography

- Jacques Gendron was born on 8 January 1828 and baptized the same day at Yamaska, the son of Jacques Gendron and Agathe Bonenfant. [Baptism](#document-act-4490455)

- He died an infant, on 17 September 1828, and was buried at Yamaska on 17 September 1828, aged eight months. [Burial](#document-act-4495343)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 17 September 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4495343](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4495343)** — His burial at Yamaska (bottom left of the page): Jacques, died the previous day, aged eight months, with the same parents as the baptism. He died before the same parents gave the name Jacques to a later son, baptized in June 1829; the two boys' separate burials keep the siblings from being conflated.
- **[Baptism, 8 January 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4490455](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4490455)** — His baptism at Yamaska (top left of the page): born the same day, son of Jacques Gendron and Agathe Bonenfant.
