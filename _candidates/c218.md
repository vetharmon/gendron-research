## Biography

Jacques Gendron was born on 8 January 1828 and baptized the same day at Yamaska, the son of Jacques Gendron and Agathe Bonenfant. He died an infant, on 17 September 1828, and was buried at Yamaska on 18 September 1828, aged eight months.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 18 September 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4495343](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4495343)** — His burial at Yamaska (bottom left of the page): Jacques, died the previous day, aged eight months, with the same parents as the baptism. He died before the same parents gave the name Jacques to a later son, baptized in June 1829; the two boys' separate burials keep the siblings from being conflated.
- **[Baptism, 8 January 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4490455](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4490455)** — His baptism at Yamaska (top left of the page): born the same day, son of Jacques Gendron and Agathe Bonenfant.
