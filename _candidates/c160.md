## Biography

- Clément Gendron was born on 6 March 1825 and baptized the same day at Île-Perrot (Ste-Jeanne-de-Chantal), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Poirier, of Beauharnois. [Baptism](#document-act-3293078)
- He died on 18 February 1834, aged nine years, and was buried the next day at Beauharnois; the burial names the same parents, and the stated age agrees with a baptism just under nine years earlier. [Burial, 19 February 1834](#document-act-4257818) · [Baptism, 6 March 1825](#document-act-3293078)
