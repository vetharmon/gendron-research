## Biography

Clément Gendron was born on 6 March 1825 and baptized the same day at Île-Perrot (Ste-Jeanne-de-Chantal), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Poirier, of Beauharnois. He died on 18 February 1834, aged nine years, and was buried the next day at Beauharnois; the burial names the same parents, and the stated age agrees with a baptism just under nine years earlier.
