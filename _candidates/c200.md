## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 3303028
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism, 10 May 1827, Berthierville, upper left: Augustin, born that day, legitimate son of Francois Gendron, cultivateur, and Josephte Masson. Original burial, 1 June 1830, same parish, upper left: Augustin, died the previous day, aged three years, legitimate son of Francois Gendron and Josephte Macon. The matching child, father, maternal name variant, parish and age establish a childhood death. The burial is incorrectly presented as a baptism in the LAFRANCE index and generated spurious candidate C270. Original images read directly; C200 excluded, C270 removed as a duplicate/indexing artifact, not a second deceased person.
