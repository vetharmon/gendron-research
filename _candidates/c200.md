## Biography

Augustin Gendron was born and baptized on 10 May 1827 at Berthierville (Ste-Geneviève-de-Berthier), the son of Francois Gendron, a cultivateur, and Josephte Masson. He died on 31 May 1830, aged three years, and was buried the next day at Berthierville; the burial names his mother as Josephte Macon.
