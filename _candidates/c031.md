## Biography

François Xavier Gendreau was born on 4 December 1817 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of François Gendreau and Marie Angélique Angèle Clavet. He became a notary. He married Julie Fitzback, daughter of Charles Fitzback and Geneviève Pepin Lachance, on 12 May 1840 at Montmagny, and their children are recorded through 1851. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 1851.
