## Biography

François Xavier Gendreau was born on 4 December 1817 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of François Gendreau and Marie Angélique Angèle Clavet. He became a notary. He married Julie Fitzback, daughter of Charles Fitzback and Geneviève Pepin Lachance, on 12 May 1840 at Montmagny, and their children are recorded through 1851.
