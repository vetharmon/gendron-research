## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** François Xavier Gendreau was born on 4 December 1817 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of François Gendreau and Marie Angélique Clavet; the marriage later calls his mother Angèle Clavet. [Baptism](#document-act-2870331) · [Marriage](#document-act-13400808)
- **Notary and marriage:** He married Julie Fitzback, daughter of Charles Fitzback and Geneviève Lachance, on 12 May 1840 at Montmagny; the marriage describes him as a notary. [Marriage](#document-act-13400808)
- **Later family:** Their children are recorded through 1851. The inspected baptisms of François Magloire on 6 February 1850 and Édouard on 8 March 1851 name the same adult couple. [François Magloire's baptism abstract](#document-act-4737098) · [Édouard's baptism abstract](#document-act-4737261)
