## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2736952
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Upgraded 9 November after a new original maternal-name bridge was found; the former qualified status and its reason are preserved in prior snapshots. Original baptism 10 November 1824 Contrecoeur, act 2736952, bottom left: Jean Baptiste born that day, son of Francois Gendron and Marie Anne Dupont. Original marriage act 13400958, d1p_1143b0235.jpg right page, reads 11 June 1850, not the indexed 11 February 1850: Jean Baptiste Gendron, cultivateur, adult son of Francois Gendron and deceased Elisabeth Dupont, marries Marie Louise Chagnon, daughter of Andre Chagnon and deceased Marie Louise Cheval dite St-Jacques. The maternal variation is now bridged by the original 30 September 1851 marriage of Antoine Gendron (C188), act 13400860: his parents are explicitly Francois Gendron and deceased Marie Anne Elisabeth Dupont. Antoine's 1826 baptism uses Francois Gendron and Marie Anne Dupont, matching Jean Baptiste's 1824 parental couple. The original 8 February 1819 Contrecoeur marriage, act 5742877, d1p_1145b0542.jpg middle left, names Francois Gendron and Marie Anne Dupont; it corroborates the couple but does not itself include Elisabeth. PRDH family 135307 independently reconstructs one Jean Baptiste born 1824 and Antoine born 1826 in that household. The identification is a combined original family assessment, not a claim that Elisabeth was written in the 1824 baptism or that the database alone resolved it.
Original child baptism 27 October 1851 St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu, act 5442307, d1p_1143b0282.jpg middle left: Marie Louise Rosanna born that day, legitimate daughter of Jean Baptiste Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie Louise Larose dite Chagnon. This original explicitly bridges the wife's Chagnon/Larose names. Original 27 September 1860 Contrecoeur baptism, act 5441204, d1p_1145b1553.jpg B 77 upper right: Damiens born that day, legitimate son of Jean Baptiste Gendron, cultivateur, and Louise Larose. The father's stated inability to sign is retained. The named parental household, new compound-name bridge, and continuing separate marital family support exclusion from Franklin's documented Missouri identity. The June/February marriage date discrepancy remains explicitly disclosed.
