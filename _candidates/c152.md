## Biography

- Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 10 November 1824 and baptized the same day at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of François Gendron and Marie Anne Dupont. [Baptism](#document-act-2736952)
- He married Marie Louise Chagnon on 11 June 1850 — the original act reads June, though the marriage is indexed under 11 February 1850 — and the act calls him a cultivateur and the adult son of François Gendron and the late Elisabeth Dupont. [Marriage, 11 June 1850](#document-act-13400958)
- His mother's fuller name comes from the marriage of his brother Antoine on 30 September 1851, which names their parents as François Gendron and the late Marie Anne Elisabeth Dupont; the parents' own marriage, on 8 February 1819 at Contrecoeur, names François Gendron and Marie Anne Dupont. [Antoine’s marriage](#document-act-13400860) · [Parents’ marriage](#document-act-5742877)
- Jean Baptiste's daughter Marie Louise Rosanna was born and baptized on 27 October 1851 at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu, her mother appearing there as Marie Louise Larose dite Chagnon, and his son Damiens was born and baptized on 27 September 1860 at Contrecoeur, his mother appearing there as Louise Larose. [Marie Louise Rosanna’s baptism](#document-act-5442307) · [Damiens’s baptism](#document-act-5441204)
