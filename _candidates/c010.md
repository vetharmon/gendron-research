## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Pierre Gendron was born on 28 June 1816 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Ignace Gendron and Josephte Gendron. [Baptism](#document-act-2626698)
- **Marriage:** On 25 October 1836 he married Émilie Thuot dit Duval at Beauharnois (St-Clément); the act names him as the minor son of Ignace Gendron and the late Josephte Gendron. [Marriage](#document-act-13401070)

## Research status

The inherited notes report that PRDH's compiled family lists no children; that statement is retained as research history, not used as original-record evidence. No later record — census entry, child's baptism or burial — has been connected to him. Same-name agricultural-schedule entries in the Châteauguay–St-Clément area have not been identified as this candidate. [Search log](#search-log)

## Census follow-up

The 1851 St-Clément census entry indexed as Pierre Gendron, age 30 (LAC item **22968965**, district 1, subdistrict 10, page 63, line 50, reel C-1112, image e002296500), was checked with the adjoining nominal page 65. ## The proposed 1834 maternal burial

The [compiled Josephte Gendron profile](https://www.nosorigines.qc.ca/GenealogieQuebec.aspx?genealogie=Gendron_Josephte&pid=1288361) gives **27 August 1834** as her death date at Beauharnois. The corresponding [typed burial entry](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/698) and its [continuation](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2889/699), **Saint-Clément volume 2, pages 686–687**, name **Marguerite Gendron, wife of Ignace Gendron**. She was buried **28 August 1834**, having died the previous day, aged **fifty**. The copy describes the cause as *de la maladie courante*; it does not name a specific disease. Witnesses **François Haynault and Norbert Clément** have no stated relationship to her.

The difference between **Marguerite** in this copy and **Josephte** in Pierre’s baptism and 1836 marriage remains unresolved. The shared husband’s name and the compiled date do not establish an alias. Keep this burial as a family comparison until the handwritten act and independent evidence identify the woman. Neither Pierre nor [Narcisse, C212](../c212/), is named in the entry, and it supplies no evidence of either man’s whereabouts.

The handwritten burial is the next precise check, followed by a record that identifies the deceased woman’s parents or explicitly joins the two given names. Pierre’s latest established event remains his **25 October 1836 marriage to Émilie Thuot dit Duval**. **Needs work remains unchanged.**

## Suzanne’s family records and the parental census comparison — 28 November 2026

Two complete acts in a **later typed archival copy** of the Saint-Clément de Beauharnois register strengthen the collateral-family trail. The **[19 November 1835 marriage](#document-suzanne-gendron-vincent-auge-1835)** names **Suzanne Gendron**, a minor, as daughter of **Ignace Gendron**, a day labourer, and **deceased Josephte Gendron**, matching the recorded parental names of Pierre C010 and Narcisse C212. Ignace is explicitly the bride’s father among the witnesses. Hyacinthe Fobert and Toussaint d’Arpentigny have no stated relationship. The groom is **Vincent Augé**, with his mother written **Jeannes Gendron**. Neither Pierre nor Narcisse is named.

The **[11 February 1841 marriage revalidation](#document-suzanne-gendron-vincent-auge-1841)** explicitly refers to the same 19 November 1835 marriage. It acknowledges **Marie Virginie, born 17 October 1836**, and **Charles, born 30 August 1838**, as the couple’s legitimate children. Jean Ginestet, François Bombardier and Angélique Gendron are witnesses without stated kinship. The 1835 act records **fourth-to-fourth-degree consanguinity** and a **17 November 1835 dispensation**; the 1841 act instead describes a **third-to-third-degree diriment impediment**, a missing required earlier dispensation and a new **9 February 1841 dispensation**. Both readings are retained without harmonizing the difference. Full selected French text and English translations appear in the source cards; the contemporary parish manuscripts were not newly inspected.

The already known **[1851 Saint-Urbain census page](#document-1851-sturbain-ignace-gendron)** has now been retrieved through the public LAC images and read more fully. Its selected nominal columns give:

| Line | Name | Occupation | Birthplace | Age at next birthday | Sex |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 47 | Ignace Gendron | Farmer | Beauharnois | 70 | Male |
| 48 | Amable Lamadeleine | Blank | Châteauguay | 61 | Female |
| 49 | Adeline Bertrand | Blank | St Philom. [Saint-Philomène] | 17 | Female |

This fuller reading is consistent with the **15 November withdrawal** of the old “Narcisse Gendron, 51” interpretation. It is not a new discovery of that error. Only **nominal columns 1–8, lines 47–50**, are transcribed in the source card. The continuation’s corresponding marks sit one printed row lower and require editorial alignment; no explicit spouse or parent relationship or fixed household size is inferred.

The **[compiled Ignace profile](https://gw.geneanet.org/ra41dr07?lang=en&n=gendron&p=ignace)** places a remarriage to **Amable Lamadeleine on 30 July 1844 at Sainte-Martine**, and gives Ignace’s **death on 9 August and burial on 11 August 1856 at Beauharnois**. The **[compiled Amable profile](https://gw.geneanet.org/ra41dr07?lang=en&n=lamadeleine&p=amable)** repeats the remarriage. These originals remain unread. The adjacent census names make this a useful comparison, but **Ignace’s census age of 70 and Beauharnois birthplace differ from the compiled May 1786 birth at Châteauguay**. Amable’s stated census age of 61 also does not exactly match her compiled 24 November 1792 birth. The census man is therefore **not established as the candidates’ father**.

The first identifying targets are the **1844 Sainte-Martine remarriage** and **1856 Saint-Clément burial**, checking spouse, parentage and any explicitly named sons. The two typed Suzanne acts and this census comparison do not securely locate Pierre or Narcisse after their existing last certain records. **Needs work remains unchanged.**
