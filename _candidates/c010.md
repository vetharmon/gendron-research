## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born on 28 June 1816 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Ignace Gendron and Josephte Gendron. On 25 October 1836 he married Émilie Thuot dit Duval at Beauharnois (St-Clément); the act names him as the minor son of Ignace Gendron and the late Josephte Gendron. No children are listed for the couple in PRDH's family record, and no later record — census entry, child's baptism or burial — has been connected to him.
