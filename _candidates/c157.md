## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 3367256
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original 21 January 1825 St-Pierre-les-Becquets entry, act 3367256, d1p_2512a1256.jpg top left, records the supplied ceremonies of baptism for Joseph, born that day and previously ondoye by the midwife, legitimate son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon. Original marriage 15 July 1861 in the same parish, act 13400992, d1p_2513a1035.jpg M13 upper left: Joseph Gendron, adult son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon, marries Reine Mailhot, widow of Charles Laplante. The groom is identified by parents rather than a former wife; PRDH profile 1062118 and family 345590 classify this as his first marriage. Both parental names and parish agree, supporting a separate adult marital identity in Quebec in 1861, incompatible with Franklin's documented Missouri family. The original baptism is accurately characterized as supplied baptismal ceremonies, not as the initial emergency baptism. The bride's widowhood must not be attributed to Joseph.
