## Biography

- Joseph Gendron was born on 21 January 1825 at St-Pierre-les-Becquets, son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon. The midwife baptized him in emergency at birth, and the church supplied the baptismal ceremonies the same day. [Baptism](#document-act-3367256)
- The existing research connects him with Joseph Gendron's marriage to Reine Mailhot in the same parish on 15 July 1861. Conflicting page locators and descriptions of the mother and bride's previous husband require comparison; those details remain qualified. [Marriage and source-check note](#document-act-13400992)

## Research context

The earlier compiled assessment treated this as Joseph's first marriage (PRDH profile 1062118, family 345590).

## Marriage locator discrepancy — 23 November 2026

The provisional marriage transcription retained under C129 cites [FamilySearch image 286, 3QS7-8993-XZKH](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-8993-XZKH). The C157 source list and research Sheet instead record [image 287, 3QS7-L993-XZND](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L993-XZND). Both are associated in the existing notes with LAFRANCE act 13400992, but the exact image-to-act correspondence has not been reconciled.

C129's provisional text names Joseph's mother **Marie Lapierre** and Reine Mailhot's deceased husband **Léon Auger**. C157's earlier description gives **Marie Gagnon** and **Charles Laplante**. These are conflicting readings/descriptions, not verified differences between copies. The original locators and raw text are retained for comparison; the candidate grades are unchanged.
