## Biography

Joseph Gendron was born on 21 January 1825 at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon; he had been ondoyé by the midwife at his birth, and the ceremonies of baptism were supplied in church the same day. He married Reine Mailhot, the widow of Charles Laplante, on 15 July 1861 in the same parish; the marriage act identifies him by his parents, and PRDH (profile 1062118, family 345590) classifies it as his first marriage. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 15 July 1861.
