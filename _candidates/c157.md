## Biography

- Joseph Gendron was born on 21 January 1825 at St-Pierre-les-Becquets, son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon. The midwife baptized him in emergency at birth, and the church supplied the baptismal ceremonies the same day. [Baptism](#document-act-3367256)
- He married Reine Mailhot at Saint-Pierre-les-Becquets on 15 July 1861. The civil act names his parents as Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon and identifies Reine as the widow of Léon Auger; these details were independently confirmed in the civil image on 27 November 2026. The reported parish-copy maternal reading is separately qualified below. [Marriage and source-check note](#document-act-13400992)

The earlier compiled assessment treated this as Joseph's first marriage (PRDH profile 1062118, family 345590).
