Original baptism 17 October 1824 St-Francois-de-la-Riviere-du-Sud, act 2587580: Edouard born that day, son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Anne Ruel. The provider act link opens d1p_10320360.jpg, whose last baptism is Pierre Magloire Beaudoin on 5 October; Edouard's actual complete entry is in the middle of the left page on the next spread, d1p_10320361.jpg. Both are saved and the direct correct-image citation is provided. Original marriage 6 October 1846 St-Hugues, act 13497820, M10 right page: Edouard Gendron, adult son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Anne Ruel of St-Francois-de-la-Riviere-du-Sud, marries Dorothee Laflamme, daughter of Philibert Laflamme and deceased Marie Louise Jerome. Edouard signs. Original 10 October 1854 St-Hugues baptism, act 5773507, B114 bottom left: George Edouard born that day, legitimate son of Edouard Gendron and Dorothee Laflamme; the father signs again. Matching both parents and stated parish of origin bridge the candidate to a distinct marriage and continuing family. His signed records also differ from Franklin's reported inability to write. The linked Quebec family, rather than literacy alone, supports exclusion.
