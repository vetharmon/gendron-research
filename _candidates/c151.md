## Biography

Edouard Gendron was born on 17 October 1824 and baptized the same day at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud, the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Anne Ruel. He married Dorothée Laflamme on 6 October 1846 at St-Hugues; the act names him as the adult son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Anne Ruel of St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud, and Edouard signed it. Their son George Edouard was born and baptized on 10 October 1854 at St-Hugues, and Edouard signed that act as the father. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 10 October 1854.
