## Biography

Charles Gendron was born on 24 August 1822 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. He died on 21 February 1823, aged six months, and was buried at Beauharnois on 22 February 1823, the burial naming the same parents.

- **[Baptism, 25 August 1822 — LAFRANCE act 2369901](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2369901)** — His baptism at Beauharnois (entry B86, lower left): born the previous day, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny.
