## Biography

Charles Gendron was born on 24 August 1822 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Chalifou. He died on 21 February 1823, aged six months, and was buried at Beauharnois on 22 February 1823, the burial naming the same parents.
