## Biography

Thomas Gendron was born on 17 December 1817 and baptized the next day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Joseph Gendron and Magdeleine Minville. He died on 19 July 1818 and was buried the next day at Montmagny, aged about seven months; the burial names the same parents.
