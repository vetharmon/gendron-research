Original baptism 14 July 1819 Grondines is on the following image after the indexed opening: left middle, Casimir born previous day, son of Louis Gendron and Françoise Tessier, domiciled Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade. Original marriage 9 May 1859 Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, M4 upper right, names Casimir, adult son of deceased Louis Gendron and deceased Françoise Tessier, marrying Angèle Tessier. The marriage image is faded but the relevant names and date are readable in the original at enlarged scale. This independently parented adult marriage in 1859 supports a distinct identity from Franklin’s established Missouri marriage and household. The index-opening image is retained for provenance; it does not contain the target baptism.
