## Biography

Casimir Gendron was born on 13 July 1819 and baptized the next day at Grondines (St-Charles), the son of Louis Gendron and Françoise Tessier, who were domiciled at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade. He married Angèle Tessier on 9 May 1859 at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade; the act names him as the adult son of the late Louis Gendron and the late Françoise Tessier.
