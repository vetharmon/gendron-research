## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Jacques Gendron was born on 1 July 1825 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin. [Baptism](#document-act-2994671)
- **First marriage:** He married Marie Hermine Lemonde on 28 April 1846 at Ste-Rosalie. [Marriage and continuation](#document-act-13497823)
- **Remarriage:** On 18 July 1859 at St-Hyacinthe, the act names him as a widower of Ste-Rosalie and his bride as Rose Bernier. [Remarriage](#document-act-13400945)
