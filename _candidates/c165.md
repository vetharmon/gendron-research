## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Jacques Gendron was born on 1 July 1825 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin. [Baptism](#document-act-2994671)
- **First marriage:** He married Marie Hermine Lemonde on 28 April 1846 at Ste-Rosalie. [Marriage and continuation](#document-act-13497823)
- **Remarriage:** On 18 July 1859 at St-Hyacinthe, the act names him as a widower of Ste-Rosalie and his bride as Rose Bernier. [Remarriage](#document-act-13400945)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 28 April 1846 — LAFRANCE act 13497823](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13497823)** — His marriage to Marie Hermine Lemonde at Ste-Rosalie (entry M13, bottom right, continuing at the top left of the next page). The act names him as the minor son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin, tying the groom to the 1825 baptism, and the bride as the daughter of Louis Lemonde and Marie Anne Christine Tanguay. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13497823), with the continuation page at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/tag/d1p_04040455.jpg).
- **[Remarriage, 18 July 1859 — Généalogie Québec act 13400945](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13400945)** — His marriage to Rose Bernier at St-Hyacinthe (entry M15, top left). The act names him as Jacques Gendron of Ste-Rosalie, widower of Marie Hermine Lemonde — naming his first wife is what links this groom to the 1846 marriage.

Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/2994671).
