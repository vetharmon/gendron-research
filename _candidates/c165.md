## Biography

Jacques Gendron was born on 1 July 1825 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin. He married Marie Hermine Lemonde on 28 April 1846 at Ste-Rosalie. After her death he married again, on 18 July 1859 at St-Hyacinthe, where the act names him as a widower of Ste-Rosalie and his bride as Rose Bernier. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 18 July 1859.
