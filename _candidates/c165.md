Original baptism 2 July 1825 St-Hyacinthe, act 2994671, d1p_03890876.jpg B212 lower right: Jacques born previous day, son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin. Original marriage 28 April 1846 Ste-Rosalie, act 13497823, d1p_04040454.jpg M13 bottom right continuing on d1p_04040455.jpg top left: Jacques Gendron, minor son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Luce Morin, marries Marie Hermine Lemonde, daughter of Louis Lemonde and Marie Anne Christine Tanguay. Original remarriage 18 July 1859 St-Hyacinthe, act 13400945, d1p_03910996.jpg M15 top left: Jacques Gendron of Ste-Rosalie, adult widower of Marie Hermine Lemonde, marries Rose Bernier. The original spouse bridge links the 1859 groom to the 1846 marriage; matching both parents links that first marriage to this baptism. This distinct adult marital history into 1859 supports exclusion from Franklin's Missouri identity. Jacques signs in these marriage records; literacy is additional corroboration rather than the sole basis of exclusion.
