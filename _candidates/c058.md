## Biography

Joseph Octave Gendron was born on 18 November 1819 and baptized on 20 November 1819 at Rimouski (St-Germain), the son of Pierre Gendron and Euphrosine St-Laurent. He married Florence Rouleau on 22 February 1841 at Rimouski; the act names him as Octave, adult son of the late Pierre Noël Gendron and the late Euphrosine St-Laurent. He appears as the father of Elzéar, born on 23 September 1861 and baptized the next day at St-Octave-de-Métis, the act noting the father absent.
