## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born and baptized on 28 May 1821 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Chalifou. He died on 23 July 1821, aged two months, and was buried at Beauharnois the next day; the burial names the same parents.
