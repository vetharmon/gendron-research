## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born and baptized on 28 May 1821 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. A Jean Baptiste Gendron, aged two months, died on 23 July 1821 and was buried at Beauharnois the next day, but that act names Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Chalifou as his parents. The matching child and father names, parish and age make this a possible burial identification; the different mother’s given name and surname remain unresolved.

The act names Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Chalifou; the baptism names Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. It must not be described as naming the same parents.
