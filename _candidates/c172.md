## Biography

Pierre Gendron was baptized on 24 December 1825 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. Online trees identify him as a Peter Plush, giving his exact birth date and parents; no record confirming that connection has been found. He is not in his parents' 1851 household at St-Clément, and no marriage records him as their son.
