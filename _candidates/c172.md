## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born and baptized on 24 December 1825 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean-Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. He is absent from his parents' 1851 St-Clément household. The checked 1843–1855 marriage indexes did not identify him as their son.

## Possible match: Peter Plush

Possible match: Peter Plush, husband of Margaret, recorded in Delta County, Michigan, in 1860 and Cunningham Township, Chariton County, Missouri, in 1880. Both originals give Canadian birth and ages 33/53, about one year below Pierre's expected completed ages of 34/54. The 1880 census gives both parents' birthplaces as Canada. Children born in Missouri about 1851–1853 place the family there during Franklin's Missouri years. Claire Chatigny's 1839 petition identifies her husband as Jean-Baptiste Gendron dit Peloche, connecting C172's parental family to that dit name. Peloche/Ploche becoming Plush is plausible but unproved. Trees assign Peter the exact birth date 24 December 1825 and the same parents, but no independent American record yet confirms those details. This is a possible identification and possible elimination, not a proved identity.

Peter was a labourer in 1860 and a farmer in 1880. His wife Margaret was also born in Canada. The 1860 household includes two older children born in Missouri and two younger children born in Michigan. His age, Canadian origins, farming occupation and early Missouri family resemble Franklin's circumstances, but those similarities do not establish a relationship or common migration.

The next step is an original marriage, death, baptism or other record linking Peter Plush to Jean-Baptiste Gendron and Claire Chatigny. The reported 1848 Missouri marriage to Margaret Lucie Cathrine Greffe comes from compiled genealogy and remains unverified.
