## Biography

Casimir Gendron was born on 6 December 1819 and baptized the same day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (Ste-Anne), the son of François Gendron and Marie Vachon. He married Rose Delima Fraser on 3 November 1841 in the same parish. He appears as the father of Adolphe, born on 9 February 1861 and baptized the next day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade.

## Documents

It names his parents, François Gendron and Marie Vachon, tying the groom to the 1819 baptism.
