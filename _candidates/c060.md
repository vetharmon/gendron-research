## Biography

- Casimir Gendron was born on 6 December 1819 and baptized the same day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (Ste-Anne), the son of François Gendron and Marie Vachon. [Baptism](#document-act-2936878)

- He married Rose Delima Fraser on 3 November 1841 in the same parish. [Marriage](#document-act-13400872)

- He appears as the father of Adolphe, born on 9 February 1861 and baptized the next day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade. [Adolphe baptism](#document-act-6015654)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 3 November 1841 — LAFRANCE act 13400872](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400872)** — His marriage to Rose Delima Fraser at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (left page, entry M25). It names his parents, François Gendron and Marie Vachon, tying the groom to the 1819 baptism.
- **Baptism of his son, 10 February 1861 — LAFRANCE act 6015654** — Adolphe, born the day before at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (bottom left, entry B8, with its closing continuation at the top of the right page on the same image), son of Casimir Gendron and Rose Delima Fraser. It is the latest record connected to Casimir, and is used as family evidence without claiming the father attended. [Register locator](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/6015654). The separately saved file named Continuation contains later entries, not the target act.
- **[Baptism, 6 December 1819 — LAFRANCE act 2936878](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2936878)** — His baptism at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (upper left), born that day, naming his parents, François Gendron and Marie Vachon.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 793440](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/793440)** — PRDH's compiled record for Casimir.
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 216688](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/216688)** — PRDH's compiled family for Casimir and Rose Delima Fraser, corroborating the sequence of records.
