## Biography

Thomas Gendreau — the baptism records him as Thomas Gendro — was born on 13 September 1817 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Pierre Gendron, a journalier (day labourer), and Marie Chrétien. He died in infancy and was buried on 13 August 1818 at Marieville (St-Nom-de-Marie), aged eleven months; the burial names him as the legitimate son of Pierre Gendron, by then described as a cultivateur (farmer), and Marie Chrétien.
