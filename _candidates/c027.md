## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 5632279
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Infant-death exclusion: original baptism 13 September 1817 records Thomas Gendro, born that day, parents Pierre Gendro, journalier, and Marie Chrétien. Original Marieville (St-Nom-de-Marie) burial 13 August 1818, S39, act 2719593, records Thomas Gendron, eleven months, legitimate son of Pierre Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie Chrétien. Matching child, both parents and age support identity across the different parishes and occupations. Burial age agrees with eleven calendar months from birth to burial; no precise death date is adopted from the overstruck clause. The indexed death year 1813 is incompatible with the original burial year 1818 and is not used. Reasoned correlation, not an explicit burial statement of birthplace.
