## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Thomas Gendreau, recorded as Thomas Gendro, was born on 13 September 1817 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Pierre Gendron, a journalier (day labourer), and Marie Chrétien. [Baptism](#document-act-5632279)
- **Burial:** He died in infancy and was buried on 13 August 1818 at Marieville (St-Nom-de-Marie), aged eleven months; the burial names him as the legitimate son of Pierre Gendron, then described as a cultivateur (farmer), and Marie Chrétien. The phrase giving an exact death day is crossed out. [Burial](#document-act-2719593)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 13 August 1818 — LAFRANCE act 2719593 (register image)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/2719593)** — His burial at Marieville (entry S39; left page, second entry), aged eleven months — eleven calendar months from his birth — naming both parents. The burial does not state his birthplace, so the identification rests on the matching name, both parents and age across the two parishes; the indexed death year 1813 is incompatible with the original burial year 1818 and is not used.
- **[Baptism, 13 September 1817 — LAFRANCE act 5632279](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5632279)** — His baptism at Montmagny (right page, bottom): born that day, son of Pierre Gendro, journalier, and Marie Chrétien.
