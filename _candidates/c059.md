## Biography

Olivier Gendron was born on 20 November 1819 and baptized the next day at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Jean Gendron, a blacksmith, and Charlotte Casavant dit Ladebauche. He died on 14 October 1821 and was buried at St-Hyacinthe on 16 October 1821, aged about two; the burial act names the same father, with the same occupation, and his mother as Charlotte Casavan.
