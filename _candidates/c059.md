## Biography

- Olivier Gendron was born on 20 November 1819 and baptized the next day at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Jean Gendron, a blacksmith, and Charlotte Casavant dit Ladebauche. [Baptism](#document-act-2523914)

- He died on 14 October 1821 and was buried at St-Hyacinthe on 16 October 1821, aged about two; the burial act names the same father, with the same occupation, and his mother as Charlotte Casavan. [Burial](#document-act-2483441)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 16 October 1821 — LAFRANCE act 2483441](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2483441)** — His burial at St-Hyacinthe (middle left, entry S196): he had died two days before, and the act names his father Jean Gendron, blacksmith, as in the baptism, and his mother as Charlotte Casavan. It gives his age as two years; he was in fact just under two.
- **[Baptism, 21 November 1819 — LAFRANCE act 2523914](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2523914)** — His baptism at Sorel (middle left), born the previous day, naming his parents, Jean Gendron, blacksmith, and Charlotte Casavant dit Ladebauche.
