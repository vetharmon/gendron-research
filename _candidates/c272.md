## Biography

- Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 24 June 1830 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Vincent Gendron and Genevieve Laberge. [Baptism](#document-act-4080509)
- He died two days later, on 26 June 1830, and was buried at Montmagny on 28 June 1830. [Burial, 28 June 1830](#document-act-4084012)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 28 June 1830 — LAFRANCE act 4084012](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4084012)** — His burial at Montmagny (entry S26, beginning bottom left and continuing top right): died 26 June, two days old, son of Vincent Gendron and Genevieve Laberge — the same parents as his baptism.
