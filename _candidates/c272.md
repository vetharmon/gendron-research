## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 24 June 1830 and baptized the same day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Vincent Gendron and Genevieve Laberge. He died two days later, on 26 June 1830, and was buried at Montmagny on 28 June 1830.

It closes his life four days after it began.
- **[Baptism, 24 June 1830 — LAFRANCE act 4080509](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4080509)** — His baptism at Montmagny (entry B49, middle right): born the same day, naming his parents, Vincent Gendron and Genevieve Laberge.
