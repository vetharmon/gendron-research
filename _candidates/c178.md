## Biography

Thomas Gendron was baptized on 7 March 1826 at Berthier-sur-Mer (Notre-Dame-de-l'Assomption), the son of Jacques Gendron and Genevieve Goulette. No later record identified; he is in neither the 1851 nor the 1861 census index.

## Research update — 15 November 2026

C143 Joseph’s original baptism identifies the same parents, Jacques Gendron and Geneviève Goulette. LAFRANCE indexes the mother’s burial at St-Hugues on 31 August 1837 and the father’s remarriage there on 10 January 1843. Those original acts remain to be inspected; follow both sons in the St-Hugues household.
