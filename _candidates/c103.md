## Biography

- François Hyppolite Gendron was baptized on 1 September 1822 at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier), the son of Joseph Gendron and Magdeleine Lehoulié. [Baptism](#document-act-2369181)

- He married Catherine Pagé at Sorel on 1 April 1845; the marriage act names his parents and their Batiscan parish, though it gives his name as Hypolite, without the François. [Marriage](#document-act-13400938)

- The 27 April 1846 baptism of his daughter Marie Aimée, born that day, gives the father's full name as François Hyppolite and the same wife, and the family is documented at Sorel again in November 1849 and August 1851. [Marie Aimée baptism](#document-act-4349875) [Joseph Charles baptism](#document-act-4351067) [Georges baptism](#document-act-6022564)

- His occupation is recorded as navigateur in the acts of 1845, 1846 and 1849, and as charpentier in 1851. [Marriage](#document-act-13400938) [Marie Aimée baptism](#document-act-4349875) [Joseph Charles baptism](#document-act-4351067) [Georges baptism](#document-act-6022564)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Baptism, 1 September 1822 — LAFRANCE act 2369181](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2369181)** — His baptism at Batiscan, under the name François Hyppolite. The later records that carry his life — the Sorel marriage of 1 April 1845 (act 13400938), which names his parents Joseph Gendron and the deceased Magdeleine Lehoulié, and the baptisms of his children at Sorel on 27 April 1846, in November 1849 and in August 1851 (acts 4349875, 4351067 and 6022564) — are documented in the research notes.
