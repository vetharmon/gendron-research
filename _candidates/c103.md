## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2369181
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
François Hyppolite baptized 1 September 1822 at Batiscan is correlated with Hypolite Gendron who married Catherine Pagé at Sorel on 1 April 1845. The marriage explicitly identifies the same parents, Joseph Gendron and deceased Magdeleine Lehoulié, and their Batiscan parish. The 27 April 1846 baptism independently gives the father’s compound name François Hyppolite and the same wife. Their continuing legitimate family is documented at Sorel in November 1849 and August 1851. Taken together, these originals support excluding C103 from the Missouri Franklin, who already had a different wife and infant Isaac in Missouri in 1850.
This is a combined identity assessment, not a claim that one isolated baptism alone proves impossibility. The given name François is omitted in the 1845 marriage and later child records; its presence in the 1846 original supplies the bridge. Hypolite/Hyppolite/Hippolyte are spelling variants. The father’s occupation changes from navigateur (1845/1846/1849) to charpentier (1851); this is disclosed and does not outweigh the repeated spousal identification. No exact age is asserted for the groom beyond the act’s majeur. No burial or census is claimed for this candidate. This exclusion does not independently prove Franklin’s proposed Châteauguay identity.
