## Biography

François Hyppolite Gendron was baptized on 1 September 1822 at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier), the son of Joseph Gendron and Magdeleine Lehoulié. He married Catherine Pagé at Sorel on 1 April 1845; the marriage act names his parents and their Batiscan parish, though it gives his name as Hypolite, without the François. The 27 April 1846 baptism of his daughter Marie Aimée, born that day, gives the father's full name as François Hyppolite and the same wife, and the family is documented at Sorel again in November 1849 and August 1851. His occupation is recorded as navigateur in the acts of 1845, 1846 and 1849, and as charpentier in 1851.
