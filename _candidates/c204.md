## Biography

Narcisse Thomas Gendron was born on 28 July 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Constant (La Prairie), the son of Antoine Gendron and Louise Roy. He married Euphrosine Isabelle on 24 September 1850 at St-Édouard; the act names him as the unmarried adult son of Antoine Gendron and Marie Louise Roy of St-Constant, and Euphrosine as the daughter of Joseph Isabelle and Marie Anne Riel, with his father Antoine among the witnesses. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 24 September 1850.
