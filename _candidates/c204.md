## Biography

- Narcisse Thomas Gendron was born on 28 July 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Constant (La Prairie), the son of Antoine Gendron and Louise Roy. [Baptism](#document-act-3068357)

- He married Euphrosine Isabelle on 24 September 1850 at St-Édouard; the act names him as the unmarried adult son of Antoine Gendron and Marie Louise Roy of St-Constant, and Euphrosine as the daughter of Joseph Isabelle and Marie Anne Riel, with his father Antoine among the witnesses. [Marriage](#document-act-13401037)
