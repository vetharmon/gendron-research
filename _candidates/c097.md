## Biography

Benoni Gendron was baptized on 5 March 1822 at Châteauguay, the son of Nicolas Gendron and Susanne Augé. A burial at Châteauguay on 2 August 1841 for a man of his name names different parents — Pierre Michel Gendron and Marie Susanne Poineau — so it cannot be attached to him until that conflict is resolved. No other later record has been securely connected to him.
