## Biography

Benoni Gendron was baptized on 5 March 1822 at Châteauguay, the son of Nicolas Gendron and Susanne Augé. No later record has been identified. A Châteauguay burial of 2 August 1841 for a Benoni Gendron, aged 20 years 4 months, names his parents as Pierre Michel Gendron and Marie Susanne Poineau — a different family, recorded here so the burial is not attached to him again. A Benoni Gendron who appears at McNider (Rimouski) in the 1851 census, aged 28, and at Rimouski East in 1871, aged 50, with a wife Adélaide and four children, is also a different man: he was baptized at Rimouski on 29 October 1821, the son of Prudans Gendron and Henriette Chamard, and married Adélaide Morin at Ste-Luce on 22 January 1844.

## Joachim and Félicité family follow-up

Read original Châteauguay baptism B24, 13 February 1832, image 120/535: Benjamin, born that day, legitimate son of Joachim Gendron, cultivateur, and Félicité Hébert; godparents Joachim Gendron and Catherine Hébert. This is a distinct child from C097 (1822, Nicolas Gendron/Susanne Augé). NosOrigines 1860319 and FamilySearch tree LK3X-KK2 call him Benjamin-Benoni. That family is a plausible identification for Bénony21 beside Joachim/Félicité in the 1851 census, but the census age differs by about two years and the baptism itself says Benjamin, not Benoni. Do not upgrade the census association to definitive. Inspected original Fitchburg death certificate268, page463, 23May1918, image464/497, film005084505: Benoni Gendron, born13February1832, aged86y3m10d, retired carpenter, widowed; father Benjamin Gendron, mother unknown, informant Joseph Gendron. Exact birth date agrees with Benjamin's baptism, but father differs; the tree's Fitchburg connection remains unproved. The 1868 marriage index NWRP-ZJG also gives father Benann Gendson, not Joachim. No new C097 elimination; remains needs work.

## Reconstructing Nicolas and Suzanne’s family — 21 November 2026

The [typed baptism transcription](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2892/325), volume 6, page 2138, calls the child **Bélonie**, born and baptized **5 March 1822**. It names **Nicolas Gendron**, *cultivateur du lieu*, and **Suzanne Augé**, with sponsors **Gaspard Augé** and **Catherine Gendron**. No relationship to either sponsor is stated. The handwritten baptism should still be compared with this typed copy.

The [21 July 1817 parental-couple marriage transcription](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2892/17), volume 5, page 1833, supplies the preceding generation:

| Person | Parents and status |
| --- | --- |
| **Nicolas Gendron** | Adult son of **Jean-Baptiste Gendron and Josephte Roussel**; *cultivateur*, domiciled in this parish. |
| **Suzanne Auger** | Minor daughter of **Gaspard Auger and Suzanne Poineau**; also domiciled in this parish. |

The groom-side witnesses are **Jacques Lepage and Antoine Poineau**; the bride-side witnesses are **François Gendron and Joseph Dugas**. The entry states no witness kinship and marks none of the parents deceased. Two readers independently checked both typed pages. They are transcriptions, not newly read handwritten originals. Gaspard’s recurrence is a useful comparison, but the baptism does not explicitly call its sponsor a grandfather.

These parents distinguish the parental Nicolas from the **Joseph Gendron/Agathe Faubert** family under investigation for [C057](../c057/); a shared given name does not join those men.

## The 4 August 1845 family marriage

The [typed Châteauguay register copy](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2893/378), **volume 10, page 3564**, records **Magdeleine Gendron’s marriage to Pierre Rufiange on 4 August 1845**. It calls her an adult daughter of **Nicolas Gendron, voyageur, and Suzanne Augé**, of the parish. The groom is an adult son of **Pierre Rufiange, voyageur, and Charlotte Réaume**, also of the parish; the occupation belongs to his father.

The named witnesses are **Charles Duquet and Louis Lepage**, with no stated kinship. Other relatives and friends are unnamed. **Bélonie/Benoni is not named**; this does not establish whether he attended, was alive or lived elsewhere.

The compiled retrieval lead calls the bride **Marie-Anne**. Keep that discrepancy visible: this typed copy does not establish an alias or prove that the compiled profile represents the same woman. Compare the handwritten act and another identifying family record before combining the names. The matching parental couple supports the family comparison, but supplies no later event for C097.

## The 1849 child and the archival copy

The [30 May 1849 baptism transcription](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2893/502), volume 10, page 3687, names **Adéline**, born the previous day, as the legitimate daughter of **Pierre Rufiange, voyageur, and Marie Anne Gendron**, of the parish. Sponsors **André Hébert and Sophie Champeau dite Laneuville** have no stated kinship. This supplies a dated family comparison, but gives neither the mother’s parents nor the couple’s marriage date: it does not establish that Marie Anne is the bride called Magdeleine in 1845. Adéline has also not been identified as the compiled profile’s Mélina. Bélonie/Benoni is not named, and the record supplies no later event for C097.

The [volume-end statement](https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_c2893/520) identifies Thomas Raymond, archivist, as the copyist at St. André d’Argenteuil and is dated **17 March 1926**, with a signed collation statement. Both the 1845 marriage and 1849 baptism are read here through this typed archival copy; their handwritten originals remain uninspected.
