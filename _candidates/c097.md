## Biography

Benoni Gendron was baptized on 5 March 1822 at Châteauguay, the son of Nicolas Gendron and Susanne Augé. No later record has been identified. A Châteauguay burial of 2 August 1841 for a Benoni Gendron, aged 20 years 4 months, names his parents as Pierre Michel Gendron and Marie Susanne Poineau — a different family, recorded here so the burial is not attached to him again.
