## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendreau was born and baptized on 10 March 1816 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Laurent Gendreau and Marie Louise Allard. He married Sophie Daudelin (spelled Dodelin in the marriage act) on 9 February 1847 at St-Pie; the act names the same parents for him and Jean Baptiste Dodelin and Sophie Tétreau for her. He appears again as the father of Edmond, born 2 August and baptized 6 August 1860 at Roxton Falls, with Sophie Daudelin as the mother; the father was absent at that baptism. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 6 August 1860.
