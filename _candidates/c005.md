Original baptism 10 March 1816 St-Hyacinthe, B90 lower right, names Jean Baptiste, born that day, son of Laurent Jandreault and Marie Louise Allard. Original marriage 9 February 1847 St-Pie, M7 bottom right with continuation on following page, names the same parents and bride Sophie Dodelin, daughter of Jean Baptiste Dodelin and Sophie Tétreau. Original baptism 6 August 1860 Roxton Falls, B74 lower right, names Edmond, born 2 August, son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Sophie Daudelin; father absent. PRDH family 244226 corroborates the continuing household. The linked separate family supports exclusion; no claim is made that the father attended the 1860 baptism.
