## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Jean Baptiste Gendreau was born and baptized on 10 March 1816 at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Laurent Gendreau and Marie Louise Allard. [Baptism](#document-act-2475905)
- **Marriage:** He married Sophie Daudelin (spelled Dodelin in the marriage act) on 9 February 1847 at St-Pie; the act names the same parents for him and Jean Baptiste Dodelin and Sophie Tétreau for her. [Marriage](#document-act-13400846)
- **Later family:** He is named as the father of Edmond, born 2 August and baptized 6 August 1860 at Roxton Falls, with Sophie Daudelin as the mother; the father was absent at that baptism. [Edmond's baptism](#document-act-5536060)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Baptism of Edmond, 6 August 1860 — act 5536060](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5536060)** — Edmond was born 2 August to Jean Baptiste Gendreault and Sophie Daudelin. The father was absent; this is a named-parent record, not evidence of his presence at the baptism.

- **[Marriage, 9 February 1847 — LAFRANCE act 13400846](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400846)** — His marriage to Sophie Daudelin at St-Pie (entry M7, bottom right, continuing on the following page). The act names his parents, Laurent Gendreau and Marie Louise Allard, tying the groom to the 1816 baptism. Also at [PRDH-IGD](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Acte/13400846).
- **[Baptism, 10 March 1816 — LAFRANCE act 2475905](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2475905)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe (entry B90, lower right): born the same day, son of Laurent Jandreault and Marie Louise Allard. Also at [PRDH-IGD](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Acte/2475905).
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 244226](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/244226)** — PRDH's compiled family for Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Sophie Daudelin, corroborating the continuing household, including the 1860 baptism of their son Edmond at Roxton Falls.
