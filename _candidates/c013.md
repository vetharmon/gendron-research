## Biography

Vital Gendron was baptized on 12 August 1816 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), in a joint baptism with his twin brother Jacques, the sons of Joseph Gendron and Magdeleine Minville. He died on 23 September 1816, aged about six weeks, and was buried at Montmagny the next day; the burial names the same parents.
