Original baptism 10 March 1824 Contrecoeur, act 2736894, d1p_1145b0641.jpg top left: Antoine, born previous day, son of Antoine Gendron and Euphrosine. Original marriage 27 January 1846 Contrecoeur, act 13400863, M4 bottom right, continuing on d1p_1145b1179.jpg upper left: Antoine Gendron, menuisier, adult son of Antoine Gendron and Euphrosine Audet dite Lapointe, marries Basilisse Leboeuf, daughter of Ignace Leboeuf and Josephte Gareau dite St-Onge. Original 27 November 1860 baptism at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu, act 5442591, B73 right page: Hippolyte Almer born that day, legitimate son of Antoine Gendron, menuisier, and Basilisse Leboeuf. The father is included among those who could not sign. Matching parentage, name and place link the baptism to the marriage; the continuing couple and repeated occupation support a separate Quebec family identity into 1860, incompatible with Franklin's documented Missouri family. The 1824 entry abbreviates the mother's identity to Euphrosine; her surname is supplied by the marriage, not claimed as independently written in that baptism. The preceding baptism spread is retained only as context.
