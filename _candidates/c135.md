## Biography

Antoine Gendron was born on 9 March 1824 and baptized the next day at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of Antoine Gendron and Euphrosine Audet dite Lapointe. He married Basilisse Leboeuf on 27 January 1846 at Contrecoeur; the act names him a menuisier (joiner), an adult, and gives his mother's full name. Their son Hippolyte Almer was born and baptized on 27 November 1860 at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 27 November 1860.
