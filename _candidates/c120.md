## Biography

Louis Gendreau was born on 28 May 1823 and baptized the next day at Berthierville, the son of François Gendreau and Geneviève Pagé. No later record has been connected to him.

## Assessment and research

A FamilySearch tree calls him Louis Jerdo and places him in the family of François Gendreau and Geneviève Bocage. The Pagé/Bocage difference has not been bridged by a record. The proposed father’s attached 1850 Plattsburgh census lists Francis, Samuel, Matilda and Mary Louisa Gendron; Louis is not in that household. The tree supplies a search lead, not an identification.

## Documents

- **[FamilySearch tree — unverified Louis Jerdo connection](https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/details/M3VS-YYN)** — Tree inspected 15 November 2026. Links Louis to Geneviève Bocage rather than the Pagé of his baptism; no later record attached.

## Public family checks — 16 November 2026

The [public MesAieux index](https://www.mesaieux.com/genealogy/genevieve_bocage) lists François Gendreau and Geneviève Bocage marrying at Berthierville in 1816. It also lists a Geneviève Bocage marrying Pierre Durand at Sainte-Élisabeth in 1832. These public index summaries alone do not establish the proposed family connection, identify the tree’s proposed father in the 1850 Plattsburgh household, or resolve Pagé versus Bocage.

[PB031, PDF page 26 (printed XXVI)](https://www.vtgenlib.org/vermont/PDF/PB031.pdf), repeats the known Cyrille Gendreau and Marie Anne Viens family information, naming François Gendreau and Geneviève Bocage as Cyrille’s parents. It does not name Louis. This is another location of the compiled family account already used for C086, not a new original or an independent identification of C120.

Public searches under Louis/Lewis Jerdo, Gerdo and Jardo found no adult record identifying this candidate. **Needs work remains unchanged.**

## Shared census lead reconciled — 21 November 2026

The Louis Gendron and Adélaïde household at St-Pierre-les-Becquets should be compared with **[C201, Louis Gendron](../c201/)**, whose identity is already documented in this register. His **11 August 1851 Deschaillons marriage to Adélaïde Auger**, [act 13401009](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401009), names **Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon** as his parents. The existing C199 census comparison identifies that same Louis/Adélaïde family in 1871. It is a different family from François Gendreau and Geneviève Pagé's son.

This reconciles evidence already recorded elsewhere in the project; no new original image was inspected for this comparison. Do not reopen the Becquets Louis/Adélaïde marriage as an unidentified lead for C120. No later record of C120 is established, and **Needs work remains unchanged**.
