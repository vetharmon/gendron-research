## Biography

Joseph Gendron was baptized on 11 December 1829 at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of François Gendron and Marianne Dupont — a younger son of that name after Joseph, baptized in 1828 (C237). The proposed infant burial of C237 is a likely identification with a maternal-name conflict: Isabelle Dussault at burial, Marianne Dupont at baptism. No later record identified; he is not in the 1851 census index.
