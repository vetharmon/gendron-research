## Sainte-Brigide census household read — 28 November 2026

The [original 1871 census](#document-census-1871-4395448-00122) supplies a concrete household for the existing age-40 Iberville lead. The heading reads **Sainte-Brigide**, district 124, division 2. On page 51, family 197, lines 15–17, Joseph Gendron is 40, a day labourer, and marked as a widower. Césarie Gendron, 7, and Odéna Gendron, 4, both female, are enumerated with him. The schedule has no relationship column, so their parentage is not established by this entry.

Immediately before them, family 196 consists of Joseph Gendron, 65, a married farmer, and Marie Gendron, 64, married. They form a separate numbered family; their proximity does not establish that they are the younger Joseph’s parents. The household remains a comparison for the qualified [C275](../c275/) family, whose proposed 1861 marriage still has an unresolved maternal surname. Césarie’s or Odéna’s parent-naming baptism or marriage, or Joseph’s original widowhood/remarriage record, is needed to distinguish that family from François Gendron and Marianne / Marie Anne Élisabeth Dupont. C266’s later identity and **Needs work** status remain unresolved.
