## Biography

Joseph Gendron was baptized on 11 December 1829 at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of François Gendron and Marianne Dupont — a younger son of that name after Joseph, baptized in 1828 (C237). The proposed infant burial of C237 is a likely identification with a maternal-name conflict: Isabelle Dussault at burial, Marianne Dupont at baptism. No later record identified; he is not in the 1851 census index.

## Research update — 15 November 2026

The public-tree lead for Jean Baptiste Gendron, husband of Marie Louise Chagnon, belongs to C152, born in 1824. His original marriage is dated 11 June 1850, despite the February date in the index. It is not evidence for C266 Joseph. No later Joseph record was established in the parental-couple search.

## Family search variants

That record supplies the fuller maternal name Marie Anne Élisabeth Dupont. Searches for Joseph should include Élisabeth Dupont as well as Marianne and Marie Anne Dupont, and use this documented family for witness and sponsor comparisons.
