## Biography

Joseph Gendron was baptized on 11 December 1829 at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité). His file records nothing more of his life: a search behind this register established no qualifying burial for him before 1850, which is not evidence either of survival or of emigration, and later marriage, census and burial follow-up remains to be done.
