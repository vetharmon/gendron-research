## Biography

Joseph Gendron was baptized on 11 December 1829 at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of François Gendron and Marianne Dupont — a second son of that name, after a brother Joseph who was buried in infancy in 1828. No later record identified; he is not in the 1851 census index.
