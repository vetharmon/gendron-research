### Basis and limits of the assessment

Antoine’s [1826 baptism](#document-act-4594379) names **François Geandron and Marie Anne Dupont**. His [1851 marriage](#document-act-13400860) names father **François Gendron**, deceased mother **Marie-[Anne?] Élisabeth Dupont**, and **François and Joseph Gendron, brothers of the groom**. The compressed middle given name is uncertain in this image; the clear Élisabeth Dupont and the independently documented family are retained without silently certifying that reading.

The family has two known Joseph baptisms: **C237, 12 September 1828**, and **C266, 11 December 1829**. The one-month-old Joseph buried on **13 October 1828** probably is C237, but his mother is written **Isabelle Dussault**, rather than **Marianne Dupont**. The age, father, parish and timing favour that burial identification; they do not erase the discrepancy. This makes the 1829 Joseph the probable 1851 brother-witness, rather than an individually certain match. The later family has not been exhaustively reconstructed. [Older Joseph’s baptism](#document-act-4594528) · [Proposed infant burial](#document-act-4596550)

This positive appearance in his Québec family after Franklin was established in Missouri supports a **likely elimination**. The marriage gives Joseph **no age, spouse or separate residence**, and attendance at one wedding does not establish continuous Canadian residence or rule out travel. It therefore does not support a solid elimination on its own.

The earlier 1851 census non-hit carries **no evidence of absence**: LAC lists both **Contrecoeur (546)** and **St. Antoine (549)** in Verchères as **missing**. This corrects the earlier explanation of simple under-indexing. [Official coverage](#document-findingaid-census1851-vercheres-missing)
