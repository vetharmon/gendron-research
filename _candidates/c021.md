## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Xavier Gendron was born and baptized on 11 July 1817 at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu (St-Antoine-de-Padoue), the son of Francois Gendron and Marguerite Renault. [Baptism](#document-act-2853622)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 1 August 1817, aged 21 days, and was buried in the same parish the next day; the burial names the same parents. [Burial](#document-act-2855015)
