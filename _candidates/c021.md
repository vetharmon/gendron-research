## Biography

Xavier Gendron was born and baptized on 11 July 1817 at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu (St-Antoine-de-Padoue), the son of Francois Gendron and Marguerite Renault. He died on 1 August 1817, aged 21 days, and was buried in the same parish the next day; the burial names the same parents.
