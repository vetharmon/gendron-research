## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4077549
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 7 January 1830 St-Laurent Île d'Orléans, act 4077549, image d1p_16810874.jpg, B1 middle/lower right, names Jean Charles Alexandre, born that day, son of Julien Gendreau and Marie Pouliot. Original marriage 12 October 1864 Lotbinière, act 13581589, M19, begins bottom right of image d1p_1623a0345.jpg and continues upper left of d1p_1623a0346.jpg. It identifies Alexandre Gendreau, navigating/residing in St-Laurent Île d'Orléans, adult son of Julien Gendreau and Marie Pouliot of St-Laurent, marrying Delphine Hamel, adult daughter of Urbain Hamel and deceased Esther Houde. The groom uses the son/parentage form without a previous-spouse designation; PRDH profile 4574249 classifies this as first marriage. Both original parents and origin match, establishing a separate 1864 marital identity inconsistent with Franklin's documented Missouri family. Jean Charles is omitted in the adult act; exclusion is based on linked records, not the given name or geography alone.
