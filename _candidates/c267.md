## Biography

- Jean Charles Alexandre Gendreau was born on 7 January 1830 and baptized the same day at St-Laurent (Île d'Orléans), the son of Julien Gendreau and Marie Pouliot. [Baptism](#document-act-4077549)
- He appears as Alexandre Gendreau, a navigator residing in St-Laurent, when he married Delphine Hamel on 12 October 1864 at Lotbinière; the marriage act names him as the adult son of Julien Gendreau and Marie Pouliot of St-Laurent, and his bride as the adult daughter of Urbain Hamel and the deceased Esther Houde. [Marriage, 12 October 1864](#document-act-13581589)
