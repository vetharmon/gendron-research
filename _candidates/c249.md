## Biography

Pierre Noel Gendron was born on 9 February 1829 and baptized the next day at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud, the son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire. On 20 November 1854 he married Julie Proulx at Massueville, the act naming him as a marchand domiciled at Saint-Marcel and the adult son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire of Saint-Hugues.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 20 November 1854 — LAFRANCE act 13401071](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401071)** — His marriage to Julie Proulx at Massueville (both pages of the act are saved). It names his parents, Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire, tying the groom to the 1829 baptism. The groom resides at Saint-Marcel and his parents at Saint-Hugues.
- **[Baptism, 10 February 1829 — LAFRANCE act 4184998](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4184998)** — His baptism at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud: born the previous day, son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire.

## Original marriage re-read — 21 November 2026

Both pages of **M26, folios 39 recto–verso**, were independently re-read. Pierre Noël is described as a **marchand domiciled at Saint-Marcel**. His father, **Joseph Gendron**, is a **bourgeois**, and both parents are described as **of Saint-Hugues**. This corrects the earlier claim that the marriage gave Saint-François as their home parish; Saint-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud remains Pierre Noël’s baptismal place.

The witness sentence names **Jean-Baptiste Proulx and Joseph Gendron**, without stating their relationships in that sentence. The signatures include **Pierre Noël Gendron and Hilaire Gendron**. Raymond is not named in the complete act. The parent names continue to support the established identification and elimination of Pierre Noël.
