## Biography

Pierre Noel Gendron was born on 9 February 1829 and baptized the next day at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud, the son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire. On 20 November 1854 he married Julie Proulx at Massueville, the act naming him as the adult son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Allaire of St-François. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 20 November 1854.
