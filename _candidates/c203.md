## Biography

Francois Gendron was born on 11 July 1827 at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), the son of Pierre Gendron and Louise Choinière. In the 1851 census he appears in his parents' household, unmarried and blind. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 1851.
