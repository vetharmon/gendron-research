## Biography

- François Gendron was born on 11 July 1827 and baptized that day at St-Damase, son of Pierre Gendron, a farmer of St-Césaire, and Louise Choinier, as the inspected baptism names his mother. [Baptism](#document-act-4371381)

- The 1851 census records “Frs Gendron,” 25 at his next birthday, born at St-Damase, on page 43, line 46, following Pierre Gendron, 70, and Marie Louise Choinière, 63, on lines 44–45. All three ages use the next-birthday heading. The consecutive names, birthplace and age support the identification with the baptismal family; the census does not state parent-child relationships. On the continuation, François is marked unmarried and a male resident member of the family; a further mark records him under blind males. [1851 census](#document-source-003) [Baptism](#document-act-4371381)
