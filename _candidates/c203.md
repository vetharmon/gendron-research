## Biography

- François Gendron was born on 11 July 1827 at St-Damase, son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Choinière Sabourin. [Baptism](#document-act-4371381)

- The 1851 census records François, 25, born at St-Damase, on page 43, line 46, following Pierre Gendron, 70, and Marie Louise Choinière, 63, on lines 44–45. The household names, birthplace and age connect him to the baptismal family. [1851 census](#document-source-003) [Baptism](#document-act-4371381)
