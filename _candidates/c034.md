## Biography

Amand Gendron was baptized on 2 February 1818 at Île-Perrot (Ste-Jeanne-de-Chantal), the son of Francois Gendron and Marguerite Lamagdeleine. No later record has been securely connected to him. Earlier searches reported no matching entry in the 1851, 1861 or 1871 census indexes; those search results do not establish his absence from Canada.

## Documents

- **[Baptism, 2 February 1818 — LAFRANCE act 2873851](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2873851)** — His baptism at Île-Perrot (Ste-Jeanne-de-Chantal). It is the only record so far connected to him.

## Winchester family reconstructed — 21 November 2026

The previously inspected **3 April 1888 Winchester marriage of Marie A. Gendron and Francis S. Richardson** names her father only as **A. E. Gendron** and her mother as **Marie A.** The tree's identification of that father with Amand/Almont remains unproved. New public originals now provide a much more specific family to investigate.

The **1932 Winchester directory, printed page 267**, lists **Francis S. Richardson, wife M. Alvine, at 3 Cutting Street**. The **7 October 1932 Winchester Star obituary** identifies **Marie Alvina Richardson, née Gendron**, wife of Francis S., at that address. It reports her death the previous afternoon, giving **6 October 1932** as the death date. It names two surviving brothers: **Joseph E. Gendron of Winchester** and **Charles Gendron of Olean, New York**. Its printed age, **82**, conflicts with the ages in the previously recorded marriage and census evidence; the obituary does not settle her birth year.

The **1 April 1932 Winchester Star** adds a sister, **Mrs. Joseph Moffett**, who had died in Olean the previous week. It explicitly names Joseph E. Gendron, Mrs. Frank Richardson and Charles Gendron as her siblings. Her own given name and parents are not supplied.

An original **17 June 1880 Winchester census**, enumeration district **385**, sheet **45** (stamped **77A**), dwelling **431**, family **436**, lines **11–17**, records this distinctive household:

| Person as recorded | Age | Relationship to household head |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Antoine Gendron | 63 | Head; carpenter |
| Marie A. | 59 | Wife |
| Charles E. | 32 | Son; marked as a twin with Marie E. |
| Marie E. | 32 | Daughter; marked as a twin with Charles E. |
| Marie Elvina | 28 | Daughter |
| Joseph Eleusippe | 17 | Son |
| Victor Le Conte | 23 | Nephew; exact connection not established |

The census reports Canadian birth for all seven people. The combination of Marie Elvina, Charles and Joseph Eleusippe strongly correlates this household with the later newspaper sibling group. It records the father's name as **Antoine**; it does not establish that he is Amand, nor expand the initials in the 1888 marriage by itself. The census also gives Marie Elvina an age different from her later marriage and census reports. **Marie E. has not been identified as Mrs. Moffett**, and Victor's parents remain to be identified.

The first follow-up targets were **Marie Alvina Richardson's 6 October 1932 Winchester death registration** and the siblings' parent-naming vital records. A cross-candidate comparison now supplies a specific alternative family to test before treating this household as a lead for Amand.

### Strong alternative: C017 Antoine Élisée Gendron

**[C017 Antoine Élisée Gendron]({{ '/candidates/c017/' | relative_url }})**, born **3 February 1817 at Neuville**, already has a documented son **Joseph Éleusippe**, born and baptized **9 August 1861 at Québec (St-Roch)**. The saved original, **B694, LAFRANCE act 5676294**, was re-read in this follow-up. It names the child's father as **Antoine Élisée Gendron, meublier (furniture maker)**, his mother as **Marie Anne Vézina**, and bears the father's signature, **Élisée Gendron**. C017's original 1838 marriage to Julie Clermont and 1843 remarriage as her widower connect this Québec family to his established identity.

This is a much more specific match to the 1880 household than a similar-sounding forename: **Antoine**, an appropriate paternal age and carpentry occupation, **Marie A.**, and the unusual **Joseph Eleusippe** occur together. A public **compiled genealogy** for C017 adds **Charles-Élisée and Eugénie, both born 9 September 1846**, and **Malvina, born 21 July 1850**. Those derivative entries suggest precise original baptisms to compare with the census twins and Marie Elvina. The individual child entries give Cap-Santé locators **[B93, Charles-Élisée](https://www.nosorigines.qc.ca/Genealogie_Canada_Children.aspx?genealogie=Charles-Elisee&pid=1131371)** and **[B94, Eugénie](https://www.nosorigines.qc.ca/Genealogie_Canada_Children.aspx?genealogie=Eugenie&pid=1131372)** in 1846, and **[B62, Malvina](https://www.nosorigines.qc.ca/Genealogie_Canada_Children.aspx?genealogie=Malvina&pid=1133645)** in 1850; the images have not been inspected in this follow-up.

The original **[6 November 1942 Winchester Star obituary, page 1,column 5](https://archive.org/details/WinStar_100242_122542/page/n42/mode/1up)** now provides exact American targets. **Joseph E. Gendron died 3 November 1942, aged 81**, at 22 Calumet Road. It reports **Québec City birth**, a move to Winchester in **1866 at age 5**, and his **1890 marriage to Florence Avis Holbrook**; son **Courtenay H.** is named. Those details fit the 1861 Québec baptism closely, but the obituary does not name his parents or exact birth date.

**The Winchester household is now a strong comparison with C017, and remains unassigned to C034.** A Massachusetts record naming the mother's maiden name, Vézina, would be particularly valuable. The strongest direct checks are now **Joseph's 1890 marriage**, his **3 November 1942 death registration**, and **Marie Alvina Richardson's 6 October 1932 death registration**. Ages differ across the records; neither Marie E.'s identification as Mrs. Moffett nor Victor Le Conte's parentage is established. The 1888 initials **A. E.** are compatible with Antoine Élisée, but compatibility alone does not expand them conclusively.

Even if this Winchester family is confirmed as C017's, that would close a misleading lead for C034; it would not supply Amand's own later history. His parents remain **François Gendron and Marguerite Lamagdeleine**. **Needs work remains unchanged.**
