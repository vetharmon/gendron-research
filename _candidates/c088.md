## Biography

Charles Ephrem Gendreau was baptized on 19 August 1821 at Cap-St-Ignace (St-Ignace-de-Loyola), the son of Charles Gendreau and Constance Fournier. He died aged one year and was buried at Cap-St-Ignace on 30 August 1822, the burial naming the same parents.
