## Biography

- Norbert Gendron was born on 5 October 1823 and baptized the same day at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Michel Gendron and Claire Apolline Labossiere. [Baptism](#document-act-2632108)
- He died on 28 August 1826 and was buried the next day at St-Ours; the burial states his age as two years and nine months, about a month short of the interval since his baptism. [Burial, 29 August 1826](#document-act-4656709)
