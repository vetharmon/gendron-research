## Biography

Charles Gendron was born on 7 June 1824 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Ignace Gendron, journalier, and Josephte Gendron. He died on 9 June 1824 and was buried the next day at Beauharnois; the burial names his mother as Marie Gendron and states his age as four days, though two days had elapsed since his birth. His parents, Ignace and Josephe Gendron, had married at Châteauguay on 8 July 1811.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 10 June 1824 — LAFRANCE act 2370455](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2370455)** — His burial at Beauharnois (entry S12, middle right): Charles, died the day before, son of Ignace Gendron, journalier, and Marie Gendron. The stated age of four days is not exact — he was born on 7 June and died on 9 June — but the name, father, occupation, parish and the mother's reconciled name (below) match the baptism.
- **[Baptism, 8 June 1824 — LAFRANCE act 2370102](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2370102)** — His baptism at Beauharnois (entry B40, lower left), born the previous day, son of Ignace Gendron, journalier, and Josephte Gendron. The baptism and burial appear on the same register spread.
- **His parents' records** (no links recorded) — His mother's baptism at Châteauguay on 4 December 1787 (act 644324) names her Marie Josephe, daughter of Thomas Gendron and Marie Josephe Gendron, and his parents' marriage at Châteauguay on 8 July 1811 (act 5696118) names Ignace Jeandron and Josephe Jeandron. Together they account for his mother appearing as Josephte in his baptism and as Marie in his burial.
