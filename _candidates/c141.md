## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2370102
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original Beauharnois spread: baptism B40 lower left, 8 June 1824, Charles born the previous day, son of Ignace Gendron, journalier, and Josephte Gendron. Burial S12 middle right, 10 June 1824, Charles died yesterday, son of Ignace Gendron, journalier, and Marie Gendron, aged four days. New original maternal baptism, 4 December 1787 Chateauguay, right page below the marriage continuation, names Marie Josephe, baptized conditionally, daughter of Thomas Gendron and Marie Josephe Gendron. New original parental marriage M8, 8 July 1811 Chateauguay, upper left, names Ignace Jeandron, adult son of Jean Baptiste Jeandron and Francoise Lafontaine, marrying Josephe Jeandron, adult daughter of Thomas Jeandron and deceased Josephe Jeandron. The original compound maternal name supports the Marie/Josephte usages. Charles's same name, father and occupation, reconciled mother, parish and close event sequence support identifying the infant burial with this baptism. Counted as a reasoned original-record exclusion. The burial's four-day age is NOT exact: born7June and died9June gives two elapsed days. That residual age discrepancy is disclosed, not claimed corrected. The two historical baptism/burial JPEG files show the same register spread and must not be counted as two distinct image pages.
