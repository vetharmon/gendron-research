## Biography

Antoine Gendron was born and baptized on 14 September 1826 at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of Francois Gendron and Marie Anne Dupont. A cultivateur, he married Hermine Cormier on 30 September 1851 at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu; the act names him as the adult son of Francois Gendron and the deceased Marie Anne Elisabeth Dupont, and Hermine as the adult daughter of Edouard Cormier and Rose Taille. He died on 14 August 1853 and was buried two days later at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu, aged about twenty-seven, the burial naming him as the husband of Hermine Cormier.
