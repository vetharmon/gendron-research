## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4594379
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 14 September 1826 Contrecoeur, act 4594379, d1p_1145b0688.jpg middle left: Antoine born that day, son of Francois Gendron and Marie Anne Dupont. Original marriage 30 September 1851 St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu, act 13400860, d1p_1143b0277.jpg right page: Antoine Gendron, cultivateur, adult son of Francois Gendron and deceased Marie Anne Elisabeth Dupont, marries Hermine Cormier, adult daughter of Edouard Cormier and Rose Taille. The bride's maternal surname is written Taille in this original, unlike PRDH's Tanguay reconstruction; this is disclosed and is not needed to identify Antoine. The original compound maternal name directly includes the Marie Anne used at baptism. Original burial 16 August 1853 St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu, act 5442731, d1p_1143b0334.jpg bottom right: Antoine Gendron, cultivateur, husband of Hermine Cormier, aged about twenty-seven, died two days earlier. The dated age is twenty-six years eleven months, compatible with the stated approximate twenty-seven. The original spouse bridge identifies the burial with the 1851 groom, and the parental names bridge that groom to the 1826 child. This separate marriage and 1853 death exclude him from Franklin's documented Missouri identity and survival until 1886. The burial is after 1850; exclusion does not depend on a pre 1850 death. The original 1819 parental marriage and PRDH family 135307 additionally corroborate the household. Antoine's original marriage supplies a new maternal given-name bridge also used for C152.
