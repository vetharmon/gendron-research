## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Antoine Gendron was born and baptized on 14 September 1826 at Contrecoeur (Ste-Trinité), the son of Francois Gendron and Marie Anne Dupont. [Baptism](#document-act-4594379)
- **Marriage:** A cultivateur, he married Hermine Cormier on 30 September 1851 at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu; the act names him as the adult son of Francois Gendron and the deceased Marie Anne Elisabeth Dupont, and Hermine as the adult daughter of Edouard Cormier and Rose Taille. [Marriage](#document-act-13400860)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 14 August 1853 and was buried two days later at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu, aged about twenty-seven; the burial names him as the husband of Hermine Cormier. [Burial](#document-act-5442731)
