Original baptism B22 lower left, 15 March 1821, d1p_10161139.jpg, names Octave Paschal, born previous day, son of Pierre Gendreau, journalier, and Marie Catherine Gamache. Original marriage M2, 7 January 1845 Sainte-Luce, Frenchville, opening bottom right on d1p_33461272.jpg and continuation top left on d1p_33461273.jpg, names Pascal Gendreau, cultivateur, adult son of deceased Pierre Gendreau and deceased Marie Gamache, formerly of Sainte-Anne-de-la-Pocatière, marrying Nathalie Michaud, adult daughter of Germain Michaud and Luce Couturier. Catherine is omitted in the marriage; Octave is not used in the adult name. These omissions are retained. Original 21 July 1861 Saint-François-Xavier, Madawaska baptism, d1p_31390331.jpg, fourth entry on right, names Docite born 10 June, legitimate son of Pascal Gendreau and Athalie Michaud. Nathalie/Athalie is disclosed as a variation. Combined name, parentage, chronology, regional origin and continuing spouse-linked family support a reasoned identity correlation and exclusion from Franklin's Missouri identity. No assertion of the father's attendance at the child baptism is made. FamilySearch KH5D-FW5 supplied the adult lead, but its 15 May 1821 Cap-Saint-Ignace birth claim conflicts with the original and is not adopted. Marriage is browsed original evidence, with no LAFRANCE marriage act number identified.
