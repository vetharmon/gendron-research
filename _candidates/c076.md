## Biography

- Octave Pascal Gendreau was born on 14 March 1821 and baptized the next day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Pierre Gendreau, a journalier, and Marie Catherine Gamache. [Baptism](#document-act-2870719)

- The existing research notes identify him as Pascal Gendreau in the 7 January 1845 marriage to Nathalie Michaud at Sainte-Luce, Frenchville, Maine. They describe the groom as a farmer, adult son of the late Pierre Gendreau and late Marie Gamache, formerly of Sainte-Anne-de-la-Pocatière, and the bride as the adult daughter of Germain Michaud and Luce Couturier; Octave is not used in the adult records described there. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

- He appears again as Pascal, father of Docite, born on 10 June 1861 and baptized on 21 July 1861 at Saint-François-Xavier, Madawaska. The act names the mother Athalie Michaud. The existing research identifies Athalie with the Nathalie of 1845; that equivalence is an inference from the records. [Baptism of his son](#document-act-8007851) [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
