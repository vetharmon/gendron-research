## Biography

Octave Pascal Gendreau was born on 14 March 1821 and baptized the next day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Pierre Gendreau, a journalier, and Marie Catherine Gamache. As Pascal Gendreau — the name Octave is not used in his adult records — he married Nathalie Michaud on 7 January 1845 at Sainte-Luce, Frenchville, Maine; the act names him as a cultivateur, the adult son of the late Pierre Gendreau and the late Marie Gamache, formerly of Sainte-Anne-de-la-Pocatière, and her as the adult daughter of Germain Michaud and Luce Couturier. He appears again as the father of Docite, born on 10 June 1861 and baptized on 21 July 1861 at Saint-François-Xavier, Madawaska, his wife named there as Athalie Michaud — the same woman as Nathalie, her given name varying between the records.
