## Biography

- Charles Eugene Gendreau was born on 20 July 1823 and baptized the next day at Cap-St-Ignace, the son of Charles Gendreau and Constance Cecile Fournier. [Baptism](#document-act-2878095)
- On 22 February 1848, as Eugene Gendreau, he married Cesarie Landry at Cap-St-Ignace, the daughter of Joseph Landry and Solange Richard. [Marriage, 22 February 1848](#document-act-13400802)
- The marriage act names his mother as the deceased Cecile Constance Fournier; his father is also named as deceased, but the father's given name in the act is hard to read — his parents' own marriage act, of 6 November 1820 at Cap-St-Ignace, gives that couple in its margin as Francois Charles Gendreau and Cecile Constance Fournier. [Marriage](#document-act-13400802) · [Parents’ marriage](#document-act-5716116)
- His daughter Marie Cesarie was born on 4 January 1849 and baptized the next day at Cap-St-Ignace, the child of Eugene Gendreau, a journalier, and Cesarie Landry; the father was absent. The register context is January 1849, although the written year formula omits quarante and literally reads 1809. [Daughter’s baptism](#document-act-3236118)
- A compiled-tree lead suggesting a later identity in Michigan has not been verified against any original record. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
