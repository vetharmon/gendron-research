## Biography

Charles Eugene Gendreau was born on 20 July 1823 and baptized the next day at Cap-St-Ignace, the son of Charles Gendreau and Constance Cecile Fournier. On 22 February 1848, as Eugene Gendreau, he married Cesarie Landry at Cap-St-Ignace, the daughter of Joseph Landry and Solange Richard. The marriage act names his mother as the deceased Cecile Constance Fournier; his father is also named as deceased, but the father's given name in the act is hard to read — his parents' own marriage act, of 6 November 1820 at Cap-St-Ignace, gives that couple in its margin as Francois Charles Gendreau and Cecile Constance Fournier. His daughter Marie Cesarie was born on 4 January 1849 and baptized the next day at Cap-St-Ignace, the child of Eugene Gendreau, a journalier, and Cesarie Landry; the father was absent at the baptism. A compiled-tree lead suggesting a later identity in Michigan has not been verified against any original record.
