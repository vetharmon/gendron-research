## Biography

Joachim Gendron was born and baptized on 9 May 1821 at Châteauguay, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. He married Zoé Primeau, daughter of Hyacinthe Primeau and Elisabeth Gendron, on 5 October 1846 at Châteauguay; the act names him as the adult son of the same parents. He appears again, as a cultivateur, with Zoé as the father of Philomène, born and baptized on 12 June 1850 at Châteauguay. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 12 June 1850.
