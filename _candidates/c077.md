## Biography

- Joachim Gendron was born and baptized on 9 May 1821 at Châteauguay, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. [Baptism](#document-act-2627655)

- He married Zoé Primeau, daughter of Hyacinthe Primeau and Elisabeth Gendron, on 5 October 1846 at Châteauguay; the act names him as the adult son of the same parents. [Marriage](#document-act-13555430)

- He appears again, as a cultivateur, with Zoé as the father of Philomène, born and baptized on 12 June 1850 at Châteauguay. [Baptism of his daughter](#document-act-5506473)
