## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2627655
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 9 May 1821 Châteauguay, act 2627655, image d1p_01760595.jpg, B88 middle left: Joachim, born that day, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. Original marriage 5 October 1846 Châteauguay, act 13555430, image d1p_01770370.jpg upper right, identifies Joachim Gendron, adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron, marrying Zoé Primeau, daughter of Hyacinthe Primeau and Elisabeth Gendron. This marriage image has difficult overlapping diagonal ink/bleed; the identifying names and parentage were read at magnification, with PRDH family 241820 corroborating the reconstruction. Original baptism of Philomène 12 June 1850 Châteauguay, act 5506473, image d1p_01770439.jpg, B37 bottom right, names Joachim Gendron, cultivateur, and Zoé Primeau as parents; born that day. The matching birth parents and continuing separate marital family support exclusion from Franklin's Missouri identity. The child's baptism is family evidence, without an assertion that Joachim attended. A clearer marriage copy remains desirable for presentation.
