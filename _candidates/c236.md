## Biography

- Ferdinand Gendron was born on 10 September 1828 and baptized the next day at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Francois Gendron, a farmer, and Roger Despres. [Baptism](#document-act-4080278)

- He died on 26 September 1828 and was buried at Montmagny the next day, aged fifteen days. [Burial](#document-act-4083930)
