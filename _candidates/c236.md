## Biography

Ferdinand Gendron was born and baptized on 11 September 1828 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Francois Gendron, a farmer, and Roger Despres. He died on 26 September 1828 and was buried at Montmagny the next day, aged fifteen days.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 27 September 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4083930](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4083930)** — His burial at Montmagny (upper left, entry S71): he died the previous day, fifteen days old, son of Francois Gendron and Roger Despres, of this parish. The age agrees exactly with the baptism. Register image at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4083930).
- **[Baptism, 11 September 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4080278](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4080278)** — His baptism at Montmagny (lower right, entry B89): born the same day, son of Francois Gendron, farmer, and Roger Despres. An enlarged re-reading corrected the mother's given name from an earlier reading of Roze to Roger, matching the burial. Register image at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4080278).
- **[Parents' marriage, 8 October 1811 — Généalogie Québec act 5715647](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5715647)** — His parents' marriage, on the same register spread, names the bride Marie Roger Couillard Despre, independently supporting the mother's unusual given name, Roger.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 2752311](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/2752311)** — PRDH's compiled record for the same child, consistent with the two register acts.
