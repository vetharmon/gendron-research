## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4080278
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism B89 lower right, 11 September 1828: Ferdinand born the same day, son of Francois Gendron, cultivateur, and Roger Despres. Enlarged reinspection 10 November corrects the earlier Roze reading to Roger, consistent with the burial; the previous qualified note is superseded. Original burial S71 upper left, 27 September 1828: Ferdinand died the previous day, fifteen days old, son of Francois Gendron and Roger Despres, of this parish. Elapsed age agrees exactly. Original parental marriage 8 October 1811, lower left continuing upper right on the same spread, names Marie Roger Couillard Despre, independently supporting the unusual given name. Matching child, both parents, parish and exact infant chronology support exclusion. No unsupported Rose/Roger alias is asserted.
