## Biography

Francois Gendron was baptized on 5 August 1821 at St-Henri-de-Lauzon (St-Henri), the son of Pierre Gendron and Catherine Mercier. The 11 January 1841 marriage to Marie Sevigny names the same parents, but explicitly calls François fils majeur. The assigned 1821 baptism implies an age of nineteen years and five months; this age-status tension remains a qualification to the identification. His son Pierre Alfred Edouard was baptized on 7 March 1861, that act repeating Francois and Marie Sevigny as the parents.
