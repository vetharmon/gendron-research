## Biography

Francois Gendron was baptized on 5 August 1821 at St-Henri-de-Lauzon (St-Henri), the son of Pierre Gendron and Catherine Mercier. He married Marie Sevigny on 11 January 1841, the marriage naming the same parents. His son Pierre Alfred Edouard was baptized on 7 March 1861, that act repeating Francois and Marie Sevigny as the parents. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 7 March 1861.
