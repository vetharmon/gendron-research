## Biography

Edouard Gendron was born on 20 September 1824 and conditionally baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Nicolas Gendron and Susanne Auger. He died on 26 September 1844, aged twenty years, and was buried on 28 September 1844 at Châteauguay. The burial act names his mother as Susanne Poineau rather than Susanne Auger; his parents' marriage act, recorded at Châteauguay on 21 July 1817, names the bride as Susanne Auger, daughter of Gaspard Auger and Susanne Poineau, so the burial appears to have substituted the maternal grandmother's surname for the mother's.
