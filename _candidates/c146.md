## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2370127
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism B65 lower right, 21 September 1824 Beauharnois, names Edouard born 20 September, Nicolas Gendron and Susanne Auge. Original burial S23 lower left, 28 September 1844 Chateauguay, names Edouard, died 26 September aged twenty years, Nicolas Gendron and Susanne Poineau. Newly inspected original parental marriage M13, 21 July 1817 Chateauguay, names Nicolas Gendron and Susanne Auger, daughter of Gaspard Auger and Susanne Poineau. This supplies an independent family explanation for the burial surname: it appears to substitute the maternal grandmother’s surname for the mother’s. That is an inference, not an explicit alias or correction in the burial. Exact child name, matching father and maternal given name, age within six days of the twentieth birthday, family locality, and the original grandmother bridge jointly support the identification. PRDH family 130986 lists one Edouard, born 20 September 1824, between Benoni (1822) and Honore (1827), and links the 1844 death. That reconstruction corroborates rather than independently proves the identity or completeness. Counted exclusion with the original maternal-surname difference retained. C097 is not upgraded: its separate paternal-name and age conflicts remain.
