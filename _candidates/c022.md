## Biography


- **Baptism:** Ambroise Gendreau was baptized on 18 July 1817 at Varennes (Ste-Anne), the son of Joseph Gendreau and Pauline Provost. [Baptism](#document-act-2604718)
- **Proposed Minnesota household:** A compiled tree identifies him with Ambrose Jondro of Minnesota. The existing census-reading notes record the adult household at Maple Grove, Hennepin County, in 1860 (Ambrose Jondro, 43, born Canada, with wife Eliza and four children), then at Marysville, Wright County, in 1870 and 1880, with the children's ages tracking across the censuses. [Census-reading notes](#evidence-notes)

## Research status

No later Québec record was identified in the searches recorded below. The 1860 age is broadly compatible with this baptism. One daughter is named Ouesem, echoing the tree's name for his wife, Onesime Elizabeth Vincelette. The original of the tree's claimed 1845 Worcester marriage remains unread; a newly found compiled entry records Andrew Embois and Eliza Vaslet on the claimed date, but their identification with Ambrose and his wife is unproved. The claimed 1898 death at Marysville has no record yet, and no record names Ambrose's parents. ## Worcester follow-up — 25 November 2026 {#worcester-follow-up-2026-11-25}

Full-page verification of the known [1850 directory lead](#document-worcester-directory-1850-ambrose-jandro) confirms **Ambrose Jandro**, with an oyster cellar at Washington Square and a residence on French Row. The newly read [1852 directory](#document-worcester-directory-1852-ambrose-jandro) lists him as a grocer at 23 School. These originals provide a Worcester address trail, but no age, wife, birthplace or parents to connect the man to this baptism or the Minnesota household.

The [1894 compiled marriage register](#document-worcester-marriage-1845-05-03-embois-vaslet), pages 335 and 435, records **Andrew Embois and Eliza Vaslet, 3 May 1845**. A separate transcription of state returns gives And. Embois and Eliza Voalet. The exact date and place match the existing tree claim; neither that coincidence nor a possible name reversal establishes Ambroise Jandro or Elizabeth Vincelette. The reciprocal printed entries describe one event, and the tree may derive its date from the same record.

The same compilation, page 12, lists [Emily Andrews, born 26 April 1846](#document-worcester-birth-1846-04-26-emily-andrews), and [Mary-Elizabeth Andrews, born 4 March 1848](#document-worcester-birth-1848-03-04-mary-elizabeth-andrews), daughters of Ambrose and Eliza. **Embois–Andrews–Jandro remains an unproved connection.**

Next read the original **3 May 1845 Worcester marriage entry or Massachusetts return**, searching Andrew/And. Embois and Eliza Vaslet/Voalet; its original volume and page are not yet known. Then inspect the **26 April 1846 Emily Andrews** and **4 March 1848 Mary-Elizabeth Andrews** birth originals for the mother's maiden name, the father's occupation and other identifying details. A family connection to Joseph Gendreau and Pauline Provost is still required. Assessment remains **Likely eliminated**.
