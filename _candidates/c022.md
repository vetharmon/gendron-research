## Biography

Ambroise Gendreau was baptized on 18 July 1817 at Varennes (Ste-Anne), the son of Joseph Gendreau and Pauline Provost. He has no Québec record after his baptism and is in none of the Canadian census indexes. A compiled tree identifies him with Ambrose Jondro of Minnesota, and that trail verifies in the censuses: at Maple Grove, Hennepin County, in 1860 (Ambrose Jondro, 43, born Canada — the exact age for this baptism — with his wife Eliza and four children), then at Marysville, Wright County, in 1870 and 1880, the children's ages tracking across all three censuses. One daughter is named Ouesem, echoing the tree's name for his wife, Onesime Elizabeth Vincelette. The tree's claimed 1845 marriage at Worcester, Massachusetts, has not been located, its claimed 1898 death at Marysville has no record yet, and no record names Ambrose's parents.
