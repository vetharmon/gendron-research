## Biography

Ambroise Gendreau was baptized on 18 July 1817 at Varennes (Ste-Anne), the son of Joseph Gendreau and Pauline Provost. No record after his baptism has been connected to him: the search established no qualifying burial before 1850, and a search of his family's later records turned up marriages for Gendreau daughters of the family — Marguerite, Elmire, Marie Louise and Domitille — but no adult record for Ambroise himself.
