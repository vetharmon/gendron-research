## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was baptized on 22 June 1821 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Toussaint Gendron and Claire Rolin. No later record identified. In the 1851 census his name appears at St-Clément only as an agricultural-schedule occupant, and no marriage records him as Toussaint and Claire's son.
