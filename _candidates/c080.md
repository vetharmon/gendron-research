## Biography

- Jean Baptiste Gendron was baptized on 22 June 1821 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Toussaint Gendron and Claire Rolin. [Baptism](#document-act-2369738)

## Research status

No later record identified. Same-name agricultural-schedule entries at St-Clément remain unassigned. The recorded marriage searches have not established a later record of Toussaint and Claire's son.

## A named brother in the stepfamily — 28 November 2026

The later typed Saint-Clément copy records **Lucie**, born **18 December 1842** and baptized **19 December**, as the daughter of **Jean Baptiste Gendron, day labourer, and Claire Rollin**. It names **Jean Baptiste Gendron, frère**, as godfather and **Lucie Gendron** as godmother. The word *frère* explicitly describes the godfather as a brother; no relationship is given for the godmother. Two readers independently checked the complete selected entry. [Typed baptism](#document-c2890-60-lucie-1842)

This is a concrete lead for the maternal stepfamily already located in the research log: Claire Rollin’s **2 June 1840 remarriage** to Jean Baptiste Gendron. The godfather could be C080, but this baptism does **not** name his parents or distinguish a full brother from a half brother. A child of either parent’s earlier union could qualify. The handwritten baptism and a reconstruction of both parents’ prior children are needed before attaching the godfather to Toussaint and Claire’s son.

A separate typed entry records **Lucie**, born and baptized **13 June 1842**, daughter of **Louis Roy and Claire Rollin**, with **Joseph Gendron and Lucie Gendron** as sponsors. Its different father and date must be preserved. It is not a duplicate of the December baptism, and the identity of the two women named Claire Rollin is not established by these copies. [June comparison](#document-c2890-18-lucie-roy-1842)

The new entry supplies an explicit relationship to test, but **no later record is yet securely attached to C080**. **Needs work remains unchanged.**

The **2 June 1840 remarriage** explicitly calls **Jean Baptiste Gendron** the widower of **Marguerite Gervais**, and **Claire Rollin** the widow of **Toussaint Gendron**. **Charles Gendron and Jean Baptiste Gendron** appear among the witnesses, with no stated relationship, parents or age. Their names are plausible comparisons for the two sons of Toussaint and Claire, but the act does not itself establish those identities. [Remarriage](#document-c2889-973-marriage-1840)

The stepfather's **25 August 1817 Saint-Joachim marriage** names **Jean-Baptiste Gendron and Jossephte Roussel** as his parents, and **the late Louis Gervais and the late Louise Deniau** as Marguerite's parents. The **11 October 1790 baptism**, with the year supplied from the surrounding page, records a child of **J. Bte. Jeandron and Josette Roussel**. These typed-copy readings support the parental comparison; they are not readings of the contemporary manuscripts. [1817 marriage](#document-c2892-24-marriage-1817) · [1790 baptism](#document-c2891-383-baptism-1790)

One possible same-name child is now excluded from the 1842 godfather comparison: **Jean-Baptiste**, a son of **Jean-Baptiste Gendron and Marguerite Gervais**, died **17 April 1833**, recorded as aged **two**, and was buried **19 April**. The same-named child of this parent pair, **born 16 June and baptized 17 June 1831**, is strongly correlated with that burial; his calculated age was about **22 months**, so the recorded age is retained rather than treated as exact. The deceased child could not be Lucie's godfather in December 1842.
