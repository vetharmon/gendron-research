## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was baptized on 22 June 1821 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Toussaint Gendron and Claire Rolin. No later record identified. Same-name agricultural-schedule entries at St-Clément remain unassigned. The recorded marriage searches have not established a later record of Toussaint and Claire's son.
