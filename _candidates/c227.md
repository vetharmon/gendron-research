## Biography

- Louis de Gonzague Gendron was born on 30 March 1828 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Joachim Gendron and Marie Spenard. [Baptism](#document-act-3367482)

- He married Lucie Giroux on 22 October 1850 at Trois-Rivières, where the act names him as the minor son (*fils mineur*, as written) of Joachim Gendron and the deceased Marie Espenard, corresponding to Marie Spenard in the baptism. [Marriage](#document-act-13401022)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 22 October 1850 — LAFRANCE act 13401022](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401022)** — His marriage to Lucie Giroux at Trois-Rivières. The act names his parents, Joachim Gendron and the deceased Marie Spenard, tying the groom to the 1828 baptism.
- **[Baptism, 31 March 1828 — LAFRANCE act 3367482](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3367482)** — His baptism at St-Pierre-les-Becquets: born the previous day and given the full name Louis de Gonzague, son of Joachim Gendron and Marie Spenard.
