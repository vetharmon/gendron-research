## Biography

Louis de Gonzague Gendron was born on 30 March 1828 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Joachim Gendron and Marie Spenard. He married Lucie Giroux on 22 October 1850 at Trois-Rivières, where the act names him as the adult son of Joachim Gendron and the deceased Marie Spenard. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 22 October 1850.
