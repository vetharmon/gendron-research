## Biography

François Séverin Gendreau was baptized on 8 February 1828 at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies (L'Islet). He may be the young child buried on 30 January 1829 at Deschaillons — but that burial names a François Séraphin, aged two years, while the child of the baptism, named François Séverin, was not quite one year old. Both originals have been checked, and the differences of name and age remain, so it is not certain the buried child was this one.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 30 January 1829 — LAFRANCE act 4517806](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4517806)** — The proposed later record: a burial at Deschaillons naming François Séraphin, aged two years. The given name (Séraphin, not Séverin) and the age (two years, against not quite one for the baptized child) both differ from the baptism, which is why the identification is left open.
