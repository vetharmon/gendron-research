## Biography

François Séverin Gendreau was baptized on 8 February 1828 at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies (L'Islet), a twin with his sister Marie Zoé, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau, a shoemaker, and Modeste Ouellet, who had married in that parish on 31 January 1826. His older brother, Félix Lazare, had been baptized there on 28 November 1826 and lived to marry at Rivière-Ouelle in 1848.

Séverin died a small child and was buried on 30 January 1829 at Deschaillons. The burial names him François Séraphin and gives his age as about two years; he was not quite one. The identification rests on his family: Jean Baptiste and Modeste had no other son — no baptism exists for any François Séraphin, or for any son of this couple besides Félix Lazare and Séverin — so the child buried at Deschaillons is Séverin, despite the name the priest wrote and the age he estimated. The family reconstruction behind this is LAFRANCE's, checked on 7 November 2026, over the two register originals in the archive.

The act names the child François Séraphin, aged about two years; both the given name and the age differ from the baptism, and are answered by the family reconstruction below rather than by this act alone.
- **[Baptism, 8 February 1828 — LAFRANCE act 3382641](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3382641)** — His baptism at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies (L'Islet), naming him François Séverin. He was baptized with his twin sister, [Marie Zoé (act 3382640)](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3382640).
- **[Parents' marriage, 31 January 1826 — LAFRANCE act 13400815](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400815)** — Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Modeste Ouellet's marriage at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies. It opens the couple's reconstructed family: married January 1826, a son that November, the twins in February 1828 — no room for another son born about 1826–27.
- **[Baptism of his brother Félix Lazare, 28 November 1826 — LAFRANCE act 3382505](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3382505)** — The couple's first child, baptized at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies. He is the only other son the reconstruction gives this couple.
- **[Marriage of his brother Félix Lazare, 11 January 1848 — LAFRANCE act 13400804](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400804)** — Félix Lazare's marriage at Rivière-Ouelle, nineteen years after the Deschaillons burial. Because the older brother was alive in 1848, he cannot be the boy buried in 1829 — which leaves Séverin.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 2761229](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/2761229)** — PRDH's compiled record for François Séverin Gendreau.
