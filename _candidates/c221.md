## Biography

- François Séverin Gendreau was baptized on 8 February 1828 at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies (L'Islet), a twin with his sister Marie Zoé, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendreau, a shoemaker, and Modeste Ouellet, who had married in that parish on 31 January 1826. [Baptism](#document-act-3382641) [Twin Marie Zoé baptism](#document-act-3382640) [Parents' marriage](#document-act-13400815)

- His older brother, Félix Lazare, had been baptized there on 28 November 1826 and lived to marry at Rivière-Ouelle in 1848. [Baptism of his brother Félix Lazare](#document-act-3382505) [Marriage of his brother Félix Lazare](#document-act-13400804)

- Séverin died a small child and was buried on 30 January 1829 at Deschaillons. The burial names him François Séraphin and gives his age as about two years; he was not quite one. [Burial](#document-act-4517806) [Baptism](#document-act-3382641)

## Research status

The identification rests on the matching parental pair and the family sequence. The original-source review of 23 November 2026 covers the parents’ marriage, Félix Lazare’s baptism and later marriage, both twins’ baptisms, and the disputed burial. Félix’s 1848 marriage independently excludes the older brother as the child buried in 1829. Earlier LAFRANCE reconstruction and targeted searches, recorded on 7 November 2026, reported no additional son François Séraphin; those searches were not rerun in this pass. The name Séverin/Séraphin and approximate-age discrepancy remain visible, and the identification is a family-based conclusion rather than a literal name-and-age match. - **[Parents' marriage, 31 January 1826 — LAFRANCE act 13400815](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400815)** — Jean Baptiste Gendreau and Modeste Ouellet's marriage at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies. It opens the couple's reconstructed family: married January 1826, a son that November, the twins in February 1828 — a tight sequence that leaves the reconstruction showing no other son.
- **[Baptism of his brother Félix Lazare, 28 November 1826 — LAFRANCE act 3382505](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3382505)** — The couple's first child, baptized at St-Roch-des-Aulnaies. He is the only other son the reconstruction shows for this couple.
- **[Marriage of his brother Félix Lazare, 11 January 1848 — LAFRANCE act 13400804](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400804)** — Félix Lazare's marriage at Rivière-Ouelle, nineteen years after the Deschaillons burial. Because the older brother was alive in 1848, he cannot be the boy buried in 1829 — which leaves Séverin.
