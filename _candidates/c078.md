## Biography

Laurent Gendron was born and baptized on 26 May 1821 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Paul Gendron, a cultivateur, and Catherine Lebeuf. He married Elmire Tessier on 18 February 1851 at Beauharnois; the act names him as the adult son of Paul Gendron and the late Catherine Lebeuf, and her as the minor daughter of Antoine Tessier Lavigne and Marie Anne Branchaud. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 18 February 1851.
