## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2369729
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 26 May 1821 Beauharnois, act 2369729, image d1p_01700108.jpg, B62 lower left, identifies Laurent, born that day, son of Paul Gendron, cultivateur, and Catherine Lebeuf. Original marriage 18 February 1851 Beauharnois, act 13401006, image d1p_01701219.jpg, M8 left middle/lower, identifies Laurent Gendron, adult son of Paul Gendron and deceased Catherine Lebeuf, marrying Elmire Tessier, minor daughter of Antoine Tessier Lavigne and Marie Anne Branchaud. It uses the son/parentage form without a previous-spouse designation; PRDH profile 928542 and family 272131 classify this as his first marriage. The original acts match both parents and establish a separate 1851 marital identity, inconsistent with Franklin already heading his Missouri family in 1850. Lebeuf/Leboeuf is an orthographic variation; no exclusion is based on Laurent's given name alone.
