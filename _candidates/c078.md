## Biography

Laurent Gendron was born and baptized on 26 May 1821 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Paul Gendron, a cultivateur, and Catherine Lebeuf. He married Elmire Tessier on 18 February 1851 at Beauharnois; the act names him as the adult son of Paul Gendron and Catherine Lebeuf, and her as the minor daughter of Antoine Tessier, maçon and Marie Anne Branchaud.
