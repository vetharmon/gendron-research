## Biography

Theodore Gendron was baptized on 23 August 1816 at Sorel (St-Pierre). A burial at Sorel on 1 July 1820, of a child of the same parents and about the right age, may be his — but that act names the child Héliodore, not Theodore. Both register originals have been checked and the name discrepancy is real; the same parents and approximate age alone do not resolve it, so it is not certain that the buried child was this boy.
