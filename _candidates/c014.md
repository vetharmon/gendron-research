## Biography


- **Baptism:** Theodore Gendron was baptized on 23 August 1816 at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Joseph Gendron and Louise Mathieu. [Baptism](#document-act-2523435)
- **Burial:** The child linked to him was buried at Sorel on 1 July 1820 under the name Héliodore. The burial records his age as four; comparison with Theodore's baptism implies three years ten months. [Burial](#document-act-2525594) · [Baptism](#document-act-2523435)

## Research status

PRDH's reconstitution links the two acts to the same child (individual 2512063, family 104020), and no second baptism exists for a second son of this couple. This existing compiled association is retained as a research note; it is not a new primary-record citation.
