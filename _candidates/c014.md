## Biography

Theodore Gendron was baptized on 23 August 1816 at Sorel (St-Pierre), the son of Joseph Gendron and Louise Mathieu. He died as a small child and was buried at Sorel on 1 July 1820. The burial records his age as four; the birth and death dates imply three years ten months. The burial names him Héliodore; the baptism names him Theodore. PRDH's reconstitution links the two acts to the same child (individual 2512063, family 104020), and no second baptism exists for a second son of this couple.
