The marriage explicitly identifies **Joseph Gendron and Nicolas Gendron as the groom's brothers**, **Guillaume Halaine as the bride's father**, and **Joseph Lepage as her uncle**. Those are specific relatives to trace. Guillaume's recurrence in the baptism is useful correlation, but the baptism does not itself call its godfather the child's grandfather.

The transcription supports a **17 November 1819 birth** at its own evidence level. It gives the correct parish context of Châteauguay; the earlier tree preview's Saint-Joachim-de-Montmorency location remains unsupported. **Hélaine, Halaine and Alaire are the spellings in these copies**. They do not establish that Allen, Hallaire and Allaire in the online trees are interchangeable with the canonical Halaire index entry. The handwritten acts should be compared before normalizing the name.
