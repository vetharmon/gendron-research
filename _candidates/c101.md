## Biography

Augustin Onesime Gendron was born on 8 July 1822 and baptized the same day at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville (St-Jean-Baptiste), the son of Pierre Gendron and Louise Sabourin. He married Celeste Racine on 19 September 1848 at St-Damase; the act names him as the adult son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Choiniere. His son Augustin Stanislas, born on 17 May 1849, was baptized at St-Damase on 13 July 1849, that act describing Augustin, a day labourer (journalier), and Celeste Racine as of Charles Creek, Vermont, with Pierre Gendron as the child's godfather. His daughter Marie Alphonsine, born on 19 May 1851, was baptized at St-Damase the next day, the parents then described as of that parish and the father recorded as absent. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 20 May 1851.

## Documents

- **[Marriage, 19 September 1848 — LAFRANCE act 13554369](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13554369)** — His marriage to Celeste Racine at St-Damase (entry M16), naming him as the adult son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Choiniere — his mother appears as Louise Sabourin in his baptism, and PRDH reconstructs her as Marie Louise Choiniere Sabourin. The bride was a minor daughter of Charles Racine and Celeste Brunelle. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13554369).
- **[Baptism of his son, 13 July 1849 — LAFRANCE act 4409588](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4409588)** — Baptism of Augustin Stanislas at St-Damase (entry B75): the child was born on 17 May 1849 and baptized on 13 July, the legitimate son of Augustin Gendron, a day labourer, and Celeste Racine, described as of Charles Creek, Vermont; the child's godfather was Pierre Gendron.
- **[Baptism of his daughter, 20 May 1851 — LAFRANCE act 5412580](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5412580)** — Baptism of Marie Alphonsine at St-Damase (entry B54): born the previous day, daughter of Augustin Gendron and Celeste Racine, then described as of that parish; the act notes the father was absent.
- **[Baptism, 20 March 1853 — LAFRANCE act 5412708](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5412708)** — A further St-Damase baptism (entry B32) for Marie Louise, born the previous day to Celeste Racine — but the father's name appears to read Eugene Gendron, so the record is kept as a qualified lead and is not treated as an exact match for Augustin.
- **[Baptism, 8 July 1822 — LAFRANCE act 2761113](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2761113)** — His baptism at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville (entry B121, lower left): born that day, son of Pierre Gendron and Louise Sabourin. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/2761113).
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 860103](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/860103)** — PRDH's compiled record for Augustin, which reconstructs his mother's double surname (Choiniere Sabourin).
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 251969](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/251969)** — PRDH's compiled family record, which links the 1853 St-Damase record to this family despite the father's given name appearing there as Eugene.
