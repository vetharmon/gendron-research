## Biography

Augustin Onesime Gendron was born on 8 July 1822 and baptized the same day at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville (St-Jean-Baptiste), the son of Pierre Gendron and Louise Sabourin. He married Celeste Racine on 19 September 1848 at St-Damase; the act names him as the adult son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Choiniere. His son Augustin Stanislas, born on 17 May 1849, was baptized at St-Damase on 13 July 1849, that act describing Augustin, a day labourer (journalier), and Celeste Racine as of Charles Creek, Vermont, with Pierre Gendron as the child's godfather. His daughter Marie Alphonsine, born on 19 May 1851, was baptized at St-Damase the next day, the parents then described as of that parish and the father recorded as absent.

- **[Baptism of his daughter, 20 May 1851 — LAFRANCE act 5412580](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5412580)** — Baptism of Marie Alphonsine at St-Damase (entry B54): born the previous day, daughter of Augustin Gendron and Celeste Racine, then described as of that parish; the act notes the father was absent.
