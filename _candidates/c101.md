## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2761113
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Five original scans downloaded and inspected 9 November. Baptism B121, 8 July 1822, lower left, names Augustin Onesime, born that day, son of Pierre Gendron and Louise Sabourin. Marriage M16, 19 September 1848 St-Damase, upper left, names Augustin Gendron, adult son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Choiniere, marrying Celeste Racine, minor daughter of Charles Racine and Celeste Brunelle. The maternal Sabourin/Choiniere variation is disclosed; PRDH profile 860103 and parental family 112534 reconstruct her as Marie Louise Choiniere Sabourin. The 1849 son baptism additionally supplies a paternal-family bridge: 13 July 1849 St-Damase, B75 left middle, Augustin Stanislas born the previous 17 May, legitimate son of Augustin Gendron, journalier, and Celeste Racine, of Charles Creek, Vermont; godfather Pierre Gendron. Do not confuse the May birth date with the July baptism, or claim the parents resided in St-Damase in 1849. Original baptism B54, 20 May 1851 St-Damase, upper/middle left, names Marie Alphonsine, born the previous day, legitimate daughter of Augustin Gendron, journalier, and Celeste Racine, of this parish. It explicitly says the father was absent; no attendance is claimed. This marriage followed by the same distinct marital family in 1849 and 1851, linked to the baptism by paternal identity, compatible chronology and the reconstructed maternal double surname, supports exclusion from Franklin's Missouri family. The maternal surnames are not transcribed as identical; the database reconstruction is corroboration, not an original parental marriage verified in this pass.
Additional qualified record, not needed for the exclusion: 20 March 1853 St-Damase, B32 right middle, Marie Louise born previous day, mother Celeste Racine, but father appears to read Eugene Gendron. PRDH family 251969 links this record to Augustin; the original given-name conflict is retained and not normalized. This record is saved but is not relied upon as an exact Augustin identity match.
