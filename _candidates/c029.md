## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Édouard Gendron was born on 6 October 1817 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. [Baptism](#document-act-2626898)
- **Marriage:** He married Henriette Dugas, daughter of Joseph Dugas and Hypolitte Audet, as named in the marriage, on 21 October 1844 at Châteauguay. [Marriage](#document-act-13555407)
- **Later family:** He appears at St-Louis-de-Gonzague as the father of Noé, born on 26 September 1861 and baptized the next day; the act describes Édouard as a journalier (day labourer). [Noé's baptism](#document-act-6083667)
