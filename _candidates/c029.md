## Biography

Édouard Gendron was born on 6 October 1817 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Catherine Gendron. He married Henriette Dugas, daughter of Joseph Dugas Labreche and Apolline Audet Lapointe, on 21 October 1844 at Châteauguay. He appears at St-Louis-de-Gonzague as the father of Noé, born on 26 September 1861 and baptized the next day; the act describes Édouard as a journalier (day labourer). He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 27 September 1861.
