## Biography

- Michel Gendron was born on 12 May 1820 and baptized the same day at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville (St-Jean-Baptiste), the son of Francois Gendron and Archange Chagnon. [Baptism](#document-act-2760732)

- He died on 29 January 1832 and was buried at St-Damase on 31 January 1832; the burial gives his age as twelve — he was about eleven years and eight months — and names the same parents. [Burial](#document-act-4374182) [Baptism](#document-act-2760732)

- **[Burial, 31 January 1832 — LAFRANCE act 4374182](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4374182)** — His burial at St-Damase: he died two days earlier, and the act names the same parents as his baptism and gives his age as twelve.
