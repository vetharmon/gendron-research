## Biography

Michel Gendron was born on 12 May 1820 and baptized the same day at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville (St-Jean-Baptiste), the son of Francois Gendron and Archange Chagnon. He died on 29 January 1832 and was buried at St-Damase on 31 January 1832; the burial gives his age as twelve — he was about eleven years and eight months — and names the same parents.
