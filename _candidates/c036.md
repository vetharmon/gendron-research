## Biography

François Gendreau was baptized on 18 February 1818 at Pointe-du-Lac (La-Visitation), the son of François Gendreau and Josephine Fafare. He died as a small child and was buried on 22 June 1822 at Pointe-du-Lac, aged four years and four months; the burial names the same parents.
