## Biography

Édouard Cyrille Gendron was born on 12 October 1824 and baptized the next day at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier), the son of Joseph Gendron, a navigateur, and Magdeleine Lehoulier. On 10 January 1860 he married Sarah Marchand at Batiscan; that act, which uses only the name Édouard, calls him a cultivateur of the parish, aged thirty-five, and the adult son of Joseph Gendron and the late Magdeleine Lehoulier. The matching parents and age are what tie the groom to the 1824 baptism despite the dropped second name. PRDH (profile 1042629, family 334849) treats the 1860 marriage as his first; a second marriage listed by PRDH for 1865 was not inspected for this file. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 10 January 1860.
