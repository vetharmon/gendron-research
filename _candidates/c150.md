## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2369228
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 13 October 1824 Batiscan, act 2369228, image d1p_25010480.jpg upper right, names Édouard Cyrille, born previous day, son of Joseph Gendron, navigateur, and Magdeleine Lehoulier. Original marriage 10 January 1860 Batiscan, act 13400892, image d1p_25010949.jpg upper right, identifies Édouard Gendron, cultivateur of that parish, aged thirty-five, adult son of Joseph Gendron and deceased Magdeleine Lehoulier, marrying Sarah Marchand, minor daughter of Théodore Marchand and Zoé Brunel. Both parents and age agree with the 1824 baptism. The act uses the son/parentage form without a prior-spouse designation; PRDH profile 1042629 and family 334849 classify this as his first marriage. The linked original acts establish a separate 1860 marital identity, inconsistent with Franklin's documented Missouri family. Cyrille is omitted in the marriage; the identification is based on parentage, compatible age and place, not the given name alone. A second marriage listed in PRDH for 1865 was not needed or independently inspected for this exclusion.
