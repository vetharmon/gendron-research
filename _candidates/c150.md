## Biography

- Édouard Cyrille Gendron was born on 12 October 1824 and baptized the next day at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier), the son of Joseph Gendron, a navigateur, and Magdeleine Lehoulier. [Baptism](#document-act-2369228)
- On 10 January 1860 he married Sarah Marchand at Batiscan; that act, which uses only the name Édouard, calls him a cultivateur of the parish and the adult son of Joseph Gendron and the late Magdeleine Lehoulier. [Marriage, 10 January 1860](#document-act-13400892)
- The matching parents and locality tie the groom to the 1824 baptism despite the dropped second name. [Baptism](#document-act-2369228) · [Marriage](#document-act-13400892)

## Research status

PRDH (profile 1042629, family 334849) treats the 1860 marriage as his first; a second marriage listed by PRDH for 1865 was not inspected for this file. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

- **[Baptism, 13 October 1824 — LAFRANCE act 2369228](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2369228)** — His baptism at Batiscan (upper right of the image): Édouard Cyrille, born the previous day, son of Joseph Gendron, navigateur, and Magdeleine Lehoulier. Also at [Généalogie Québec — record image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/2369228).

The marriage gives majority, not a numerical age. He would have been thirty-five if identified with the 1824 baptismal child; that calculated age is not independent evidence supplied by the marriage.
