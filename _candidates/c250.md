## Biography

- Eusebe Gendreau was born on 10 March 1829 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. [Baptism](#document-act-2996271)
- By the spring of 1858 he was living at St-Valérien-de-Milton, where his daughter Délima was born on 22 April 1858 and baptized on 17 May. [Baptism of his daughter, 17 May 1858](#document-act-6070311)
- He died at St-Valérien on 27 July 1859 and was buried two days later, named in the burial as Éliza Labrie's husband; the act gives his age as 30, exactly the time elapsed since his baptism, and names no parents. [Burial, 29 July 1859](#document-act-6070474)
- The only other Eusèbe of his generation, the Batiscan husband of Sophie Vermette, is a documented different man. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Research status

He married Éliza Labrie — PRDH's compiled family places the marriage in the United States before 1856, and no marriage act for the couple appears in the Québec index; the marriage record itself has not been recovered. PRDH's reconstitution of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput's family (family 153389) lists Eusebe among their children with his 10 March 1829 birth at St-Hyacinthe, the marriage before 1856 in the United States, and his 27 July 1859 death at St-Valérien to a Labrie spouse — tying the baptism to the St-Valérien burial on exact dates at both ends.
