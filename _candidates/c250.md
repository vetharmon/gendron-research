## Biography

Eusebe Gendreau was born on 10 March 1829 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. He may be the Eusebe Gendreau who married Éliza Labrie — PRDH's compiled family links him to a marriage in the United States before 1856 — and who died on 27 July 1859 and was buried on 29 July 1859 at St-Valérien-de-Milton, aged 32, named in the burial as the husband of Éliza Labrie. But that burial does not name his parents, and its age of 32 differs from the 30 years elapsed since this baptism, so it is not yet certain the buried man and the baptized child are the same person.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 29 July 1859 — LAFRANCE act 6070474](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/6070474)** — The proposed later-life record: Eusebe Gendreau, husband of Éliza Labrie, died two days earlier aged 32 and buried at St-Valérien-de-Milton. It names no parents, and the age does not match the 1829 baptism exactly, which is what leaves the identification open.
- **[Baptism, 11 March 1829 — LAFRANCE act 2996271](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2996271)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe: born the previous day, son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput.
- **[PRDH-IGD family 153389](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/153389)** — PRDH's compiled family, which links this baptism to the 1859 burial and to a marriage in the United States before 1856. It is a compiled linkage, not an original bridge between the child and the husband of Éliza Labrie.
