## Biography

Eusebe Gendreau was born on 10 March 1829 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. He married Éliza Labrie — PRDH's compiled family places a marriage in the United States before 1856, and no marriage act for the couple appears in the Québec index. By the spring of 1858 he was living at St-Valérien-de-Milton, where his daughter Délima was born on 22 April 1858 and baptized on 17 May. He died at St-Valérien on 27 July 1859 and was buried two days later, named in the burial as Éliza Labrie's husband. The burial gives his age as 32, two years more than the 30 elapsed since his baptism, and names no parents. The identification rests on the name, the spouse and the parish, taken with his daughter's baptism in the same parish fourteen months before his burial — and on the only other Eusèbe of his generation, the Batiscan husband of Sophie Vermette, being a documented different man.
