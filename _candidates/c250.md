## Biography

- Eusebe Gendreau was born on 10 March 1829 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. [Baptism](#document-act-2996271)
- The prior index reading places the proposed adult match at St-Valérien-de-Milton, where his daughter Délima was reported born on 22 April 1858 and baptized on 17 May. This original was unavailable for reinspection in the present audit. [Baptism of his daughter, 17 May 1858](#document-act-6070311)
- The proposed adult match died at St-Valérien on 27 July 1859 and was buried two days later, named in the burial as Éliza Labrie's husband; the original gives his age as 32 and names no parents. That age differs by about two years from the 1829 baptism. [Burial, 29 July 1859](#document-act-6070474)
- The only other Eusèbe of his generation, the Batiscan husband of Sophie Vermette, is a documented different man. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Research status

The likely identification is the husband of Éliza Labrie. PRDH's compiled family places the marriage in the United States before 1856, and no marriage act for the couple appears in the Québec index; the marriage record itself has not been recovered. PRDH's reconstitution of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput's family (family 153389) lists Eusebe among their children with his 10 March 1829 birth at St-Hyacinthe, the marriage before 1856 in the United States, and his 27 July 1859 death at St-Valérien to a Labrie spouse — supporting the proposed link with exact dates at both ends. The original burial instead gives age 32, about two years older than the baptized child, and does not name parents. The identification remains likely until the United States marriage or another adult original independently links the spouse to Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput. [Search log](#search-log)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Baptism of his daughter, 17 May 1858 — LAFRANCE act 6070311](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/6070311)** — The prior index reading describes Delina (Délima) Gendreau, born 22 April 1858 and baptized at St-Valérien-de-Milton, daughter of Eusebe Gendreau and Elisa Labrie. The original image was unavailable for reinspection on 23 November 2026. It places Eusebe and Éliza as a couple at St-Valérien the year before his burial there — a household link between the adult couple and the burial; it does not name Eusèbe’s parents.
- **[Burial, 29 July 1859 — LAFRANCE act 6070474](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/6070474)** — Eusebe Gendreau, husband of Éliza Labrie, died two days earlier and was buried at St-Valérien-de-Milton. It names no parents and gives his age as 32. The original was read again on 23 November 2026 and clearly says “trente Deux ans”; the earlier reading of 30 was incorrect.
- **[Baptism, 11 March 1829 — LAFRANCE act 2996271](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2996271)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe: born the previous day, son of Joseph Gendreault and Louise Chaput.
