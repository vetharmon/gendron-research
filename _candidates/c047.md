## Biography

Godefroy Gendreau was born on 9 March 1819 and baptized the same day at St-Luc (St-Jean), the son of Louis Gendreau and Marie Anne Girard. He married Flavie Morin on 18 August 1846 at L'Acadie; the act names her as the daughter of Paul Morin and Marguerite Gendreau. He appears as the father of David Arthur, born and baptized on 20 January 1859 at St-Luc. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 20 January 1859.
