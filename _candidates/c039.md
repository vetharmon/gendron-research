## Biography

Michel Archange Gendron was born on 3 April 1818 and baptized the next day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (Ste-Anne), the son of Louis Gendron and Marguerite Gervais. No qualifying burial before 1850 has been established for him. A follow-up search on 10 November 2026 over the 25 events recorded for his exact parents between 1800 and 1918 opened an 1849 marriage naming Hubert Gendron and Marguerite Perrault, an 1854 marriage naming Theotiste Gendron and Alexis Vallée, and an 1856 burial of a Henriette aged 51 — none of them identifies Michel Archange — and a compiled NosOrigines family likewise leaves him without an endpoint. What became of him is not known.
