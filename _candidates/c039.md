## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Michel Archange Gendron was born on 3 April 1818 and baptized the next day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (Ste-Anne), the son of Louis Gendron and Marguerite Gervais. [Baptism](#document-act-2936743)
- **Ontario identification lead:** A compiled Ancestry profile for Cornelius George Gendron names his father as Michel Archange Gendron (1818–1874), with Amanda Vanda Judson as the mother. [Compiled profile](#document-source-004)
- **Ontario family evidence:** A municipal cemetery transcription records Delilah, daughter of Mitchell and Amanda Gendron, who died on 10 December 1864, aged six years eleven months. Neither this source nor the compiled profile identifies the father as the son of Louis Gendron and Marguerite Gervais. [Cemetery transcription abstract](#document-source-005) · [Compiled profile](#document-source-004)

No later record has been securely connected to his baptism. C039 remains possible. Neither Michel nor Alexandre was found in their mother's 1851 household or their brother Hubert's 1861 household. These are search results, not proof of death or emigration. ## Ontario identification conflict

A. C. Osborne’s *Old Penetanguishene*, in *Pioneer Papers*, No. 6 (1917), [printed pages 143–144](https://electriccanadian.com/history/ontario/pioneerpapers.pdf#page=351), gives the tanner’s birthplace as **St-Martin, Québec**. It connects him to Smith’s Falls and Lyn near Brockville, to a wife named Miss Judson, and to sons C. G., Sidney, Edmond, Michel, Vincent and Anthony. It records his death in September 1874 and his wife’s in 1896. The preceding page names Charles Gendron, father of Carolus, as Michel’s half-brother.

The St-Martin birthplace conflicts with the 1818 Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade baptism. This is a concrete reason to question the compiled-tree identification. Osborne does not name Michel’s parents, and his account was published decades after Michel’s death. The reported move to Penetanguishene about 1835 also needs checking against the family chronology. The Ontario tanner cannot yet be used to eliminate C039.

## Later Ontario evidence

The original 1871 census of Tiny records Michael Gendron, 50, born in Québec, with Amanda, 39, and their children. John William Gendron’s 1909 death certificate names his parents as Michel Gendron and Amanda Judson and gives Michel’s birthplace as “Ste. Ann’s, Que.” This supports the Ontario lead, but does not identify Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade uniquely or name Michel’s parents. The reported St-Martin origin and conflicting 1871/1874 death dates remain unresolved. **Possible elimination** is the current assessment.

## Marriage and witness lead — 21 November 2026

The Lennox and Addington County Museum and Archives publishes a [modern typed transcription of Reverend Charles Manson’s marriages](https://archive.org/details/reverend-charles-manson-baptisms-1848-1888). [Page 1 of the marriage PDF](https://archive.org/download/reverend-charles-manson-baptisms-1848-1888/Reverend%20Charles%20%20Manson%20Marriages%201848-1888.pdf#page=1) records **Michael Gendron and Vanda Judson**, dated **22 January 1851**, with **Anson J Brown and Alex. Gendron** as witnesses. The residence column gives **Bastard** for Michael and “do” for Vanda; it does not establish the ceremony location.

This page was visually inspected, but **the underlying manuscript has not been read**. Preserve **Vanda** as transcribed; its relationship to the Amanda Judson named in later Ontario records remains to be verified. The transcription gives neither parents nor a relationship between Michael and Alex. It therefore does not identify the groom as C039 or the witness as his brother [Alexandre, C065](../c065/).

Retrieve the underlying Manson entry to check the names, residence and any signatures or additional details, then compare the groom and witness with independently identified family records. This adds a precise marriage and witness lead while leaving **Possible elimination** unchanged.

The combination **Michel / Vanda / Bastard** strongly correlates with the 1851 marriage transcription for **Michael Gendron / Vanda Judson**, while Alexander provides a specific person to compare with its witness **Alex. Gendron**. Neither comparison is a completed identity proof. The census ages are younger than expected from the 1818 and 1820 Québec baptisms, and the original does not connect either man to **Louis Gendron and Marguerite Gervais**. Continue with the underlying Manson marriage and independently parent-naming family records; **C039 remains a Possible elimination and C065 remains Needs work**.
