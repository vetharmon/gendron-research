## Biography

Michel Archange Gendron was born on 3 April 1818 and baptized the next day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (Ste-Anne), the son of Louis Gendron and Marguerite Gervais. No later record has been connected to him. A compiled Ancestry profile for Cornelius George Gendron names his father as Michel Archange Gendron (1818–1874), with Amanda Vanda Judson as the mother. This is an Ontario identification lead. A municipal cemetery transcription also records Delilah, daughter of Mitchell and Amanda Gendron, who died on 10 December 1864, aged six years eleven months. Neither source identifies the father as the son of Louis Gendron and Marguerite Gervais. C039 remains possible.

The Québec side was worked systematically on 14 November 2026: his parents' family was reconstituted (twelve children), his mother's 1851 household and his brother Hubert's 1861 household were read, and he appears in no census — Canadian or US — and in no marriage, burial, or godparent record. The Ontario identification above is the one open lead.

## Ontario identification conflict

A. C. Osborne’s *Old Penetanguishene*, in *Pioneer Papers*, No. 6 (1917), [printed pages 143–144](https://electriccanadian.com/history/ontario/pioneerpapers.pdf#page=351), gives the tanner’s birthplace as **St-Martin, Québec**. It connects him to Smith’s Falls and Lyn near Brockville, to a wife named Miss Judson, and to sons C. G., Sidney, Edmond, Michel, Vincent and Anthony. It records his death in September 1874 and his wife’s in 1896. The preceding page names Charles Gendron, father of Carolus, as Michel’s half-brother.

The St-Martin birthplace conflicts with the 1818 Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade baptism. This is a concrete reason to question the compiled-tree identification. Osborne does not name Michel’s parents, and his account was published decades after Michel’s death. The reported move to Penetanguishene about 1835 also needs checking against the family chronology. The Ontario tanner cannot yet be used to eliminate C039.
