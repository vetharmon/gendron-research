## Why he is excluded

The baptism transcript identifies Joseph, born **9 August 1828** and baptized the following day at Châteauguay, as the son of **Joachim Gendron and Félicité Hébert**. The 1851 census places **Jos. Gendron, 23, journalier**, immediately after **Joachim Gendron, 66**, and **Félicité Hébert, 60**, with Benony Gendron, 21, following him (page 27, lines 41–44). His name, age and both parents connect the census man to this baptism.

The reverse page records Joseph as a **resident male family member**, with no absent-member mark. The census does not explicitly label him “son”; that relationship is established by matching the baptismal parents and the household. This family-linked Canadian adult record, after Franklin was already living with his wife and child in Missouri in 1850, supports his exclusion under the project's continuing-family criterion.

## The 1848 burial belongs elsewhere

The Châteauguay burial of 10 May 1848 (act 4594153) names **Joseph Octave**, aged 21. It must not be attached to this Joseph, who would have been 19 and is identified alive in the later census. That burial is considered separately on [C176's page](../c176/).

- **Baptism:** the publicly accessible typed archival transcript, C-2892, image 797, page 2601; the handwritten baptism was not inspected in this follow-up. The indexed act is 4588784.
- **Census:** original images e002302792 and e002302793, Châteauguay, pages 27–28. Joseph is line 43 on both sides.
