## Biography

- Joseph Gendron was born on 9 August 1828 and baptized the following day at Châteauguay, son of Joachim Gendron and Félicité Hébert. [Baptism](#document-act-4588784)

- The 1851 census lists Jos. Gendron, 23, journalier, with Joachim Gendron, 66, Félicité Hébert, 60, and Benony Gendron, 21 (page 27, lines 41–44). The reverse page records Joseph as a resident male family member. His name, age, and household connect him to the baptismal family. [Census — Joseph Gendron household](#document-source-003) [Baptism](#document-act-4588784)

## Research status

The Châteauguay burial of 10 May 1848 (act 4594153) names Joseph Octave, aged 21. It is considered on [C176's page](../c176/).
