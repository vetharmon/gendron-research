## Biography

- Joseph Gendron was born on 9 August 1828 and baptized the following day at Châteauguay, son of Joachim Gendron and Félicité Hébert. [Baptism](#document-act-4588784)

- The 1851 census lists Jos. Gendron, 23, journalier, following Joachim Gendron père, 66, and Félicité Hébert, 60, expressly called Joachim’s wife; Bénony Gendron, 21, follows Joseph (page 27, lines 41–44). All ages are at the next birthday. The reverse page marks Joseph as a resident male family member and leaves the absent-member columns blank. His name, age and family group support the identification with the baptismal family, although the census does not explicitly call him their son. [Census — Joseph Gendron household](#document-source-003) [Baptism](#document-act-4588784)

## Research status

The Châteauguay burial of 10 May 1848 (act 4594153) names Joseph Octave, aged 21. It is considered on [C176's page](../c176/).
