## Biography

Joseph Octave Gendron was born on 19 September 1824 and baptized on 3 October 1824 at Rimouski (St-Germain), the son of Prudent Gendron and Christine Chamard. He may be the Octave Gendron who married Vitaline Bossé on 30 October 1848 at Ste-Luce — that act calls the groom the adult son of Prudent Gendron and **Henriette** Chamard, of Métis — and who appears with Vitaline as the father of Victoria, baptized on 21 November 1861 at Baie-des-Sables. If the groom was this child, his adult life was lived in the Bas-Saint-Laurent, and he has been connected to Canadian records until at least 21 November 1861. But the mother's given name differs between the baptism (Christine) and the marriage (Henriette), and no original record has been found that bridges the two names, so it is not certain the groom and the child are the same man.

The mother's name in this act is one side of the conflict described below.
- **[Marriage, 30 October 1848 — LAFRANCE act 13401042](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401042)** ([register image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13401042)) — The proposed later-life record: Octave Gendron, adult son of Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard of Métis, marries Vitaline Bossé at Ste-Luce (left page, entry M40). Same father, same parish region, right age — but the mother is Henriette, not Christine, which leaves the identification uncertain.
- **[Parents' marriage, 23 January 1821 — LAFRANCE act 5729014](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5729014)** ([register image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5729014)) — Prudent Gendron's marriage at Rimouski names the bride as Marie Henriette Chamard, with no Christine alias — it supports Henriette and gives no support to Christine. PRDH's compiled family (no. 140854) reconstructs the mother as "Marie Henriette Christine Chamard," but that is a compiled identity, not an original record.
- **[Baptism of his daughter, 21 November 1861 — LAFRANCE act 5496956](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5496956)** ([register image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5496956)) — Victoria, born 18 November, daughter of Octave Gendron and Vitaline Bossé, baptized at Baie-des-Sables (top right, entry B71). It confirms the married couple of the 1848 act were still together in 1861; it does not by itself prove the groom was the child baptized in 1824.
