## Biography

Joseph Octave Gendron was born on 19 September 1824 and baptized on 3 October 1824 at Rimouski (St-Germain), the son of Prudent Gendron and Christine Chamard. A probable match is Octave Gendron, who married Vitaline Bossé at Ste-Luce on 30 October 1848. The marriage names Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard of Métis as his parents. Octave and Vitaline's daughter Victoria was baptized at Baie-des-Sables on 21 November 1861. The mother's given name differs between the baptism and marriage: Christine/Henriette.

## Documents

- **[Baptism, 3 October 1824 — LAFRANCE act 2671257](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2671257)** ([register image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/2671257)) — His baptism at Rimouski (right page, entry B68): born 19 September, son of Prudence (Prudent) Gendron and Christine Chamard. The mother's name in this act is one side of the conflict described below.
- **[Marriage, 30 October 1848 — LAFRANCE act 13401042](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401042)** ([register image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/13401042)) — A possible match: Octave Gendron, adult son of Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard of Métis, marries Vitaline Bossé at Ste-Luce (left page, entry M40). Same father, same parish region, right age — but the mother is Henriette, not Christine, which leaves the identification uncertain.
- **[Parents' marriage, 23 January 1821 — LAFRANCE act 5729014](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5729014)** ([register image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5729014)) — Prudent Gendron's marriage at Rimouski names the bride as Marie Henriette Chamard, with no Christine alias — it supports Henriette and gives no support to Christine. PRDH's compiled family (no. 140854) reconstructs the mother as "Marie Henriette Christine Chamard," but that is a compiled identity, not an original record.
- **[Baptism of his daughter, 21 November 1861 — LAFRANCE act 5496956](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5496956)** ([register image](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/5496956)) — Victoria, born 18 November, daughter of Octave Gendron and Vitaline Bossé, baptized at Baie-des-Sables (top right, entry B70). It confirms the married couple of the 1848 act were still together in 1861.
