## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2671257
CURRENT STATUS: Qualified later-life lead — not counted
Original baptism 3 October 1824 Rimouski, act 2671257, B68 right page: Joseph Octave born 19 September, son of Prudence Gendron and Christine Chamard. The proposed marriage 30 October 1848 Ste-Luce, act 13401042, M40 left page, calls Octave the adult son of Prudent Gendron and Henriette Chamard of Metis, marrying Vitaline Bosse. The original parental marriage 23 January 1821 Rimouski, act 5729014, right page, names Prudent Gendron and Marie Henriette Chamard but does not give Christine as an alias. PRDH family 140854 reconstructs Marie Henriette Christine Chamard; that compiled identity is a lead rather than an independent original bridge. The 21 November 1861 Baie-des-Sables baptism, act 5496956, B71 top right, records Victoria born 18 November, daughter of Octave Gendron and Vitaline Bosse, confirming that couple but not independently identifying Octave with the 1824 child. Four originals are saved. The Christine/Henriette maternal given-name difference remains unresolved, so C148 is outside the excluded count.
