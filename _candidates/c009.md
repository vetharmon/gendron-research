## Biography

Jean Baptiste Ulderic Gendron was born on 22 June 1816 and baptized on 24 June at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon. On 23 August 1842, in the same parish, he married Josephine Dupuis; the act names him Ulderique Gendron and gives the same parents. He appears again as the father of Joseph, born 14 February and baptized 15 February 1850 at St-Pierre-les-Becquets, with Josephine Dupuis as the mother; that act describes him as a journalier (day labourer) of the parish.
