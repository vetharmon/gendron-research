## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2913164
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 24 June 1816 St-Pierre-les-Becquets, right page middle: Jean Baptiste Ulderic, born yesterday, son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon. Original marriage 23 August 1842 same parish, upper right: Ulderique Gendron, son of Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon, marrying Josephine Dupuis. The earlier reading Marie Roy was erroneous and is superseded: enlarged direct reading shows Gagnon; comparison with the same priest's writing of Marie Gagnon in Adele Gendron's 29 January 1839 marriage supports this reading. Adele's act explicitly names Charles Gendron and Marie Gagnon, and Charles as her father among the witnesses. No Roy alias or stepmother is needed or claimed. Original child baptism 15 February 1850, B13 lower right continuing at top left of the next page, names Joseph, born yesterday, son of Ulderique Gendron, journalier, and Josephine Dupuis of this parish. Matching unusual given name, both parents, parish and subsequent same-spouse family support the identity chain. Counted as excluded by the continuing Quebec family in February 1850, when Franklin had a different family in Missouri. This is a combined-record identity assessment, not a PRDH linkage alone. Baptism gives birth23June1816; derivative22June date is not adopted.
