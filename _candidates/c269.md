## Biography

Joseph Gendron was born on 6 May 1830 and baptized the next day at Yamachiche (Ste-Anne), the son of Francois Gendron, a cultivateur, and Marie Pellerin, of St-Barnabe. He died on 5 July 1830, aged two months, and was buried at Yamachiche on 7 July 1830; the burial act names his mother as Judith Pellerin. His mother's own baptism at Yamachiche on 23 November 1787 names her Marie Judith, daughter of Jean Pellerin and Marie Landry, and his parents' marriage at Yamachiche on 21 July 1807 names the bride as Marie Pellerin — Marie and Judith were usages of her compound name.
