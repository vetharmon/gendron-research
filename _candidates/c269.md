## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 3351520
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original infant baptism, 7 May 1830 Yamachiche, lower left: Joseph born the previous day, Francois Gendron, cultivateur, and Marie Pellerin of St-Barnabe. Original burial, 7 July 1830 same parish, upper right: Joseph died two days earlier, aged two months, son of Francois Gendron and Judith Pellerin. New original maternal baptism, 23 November 1787 Yamachiche, second act on left page, explicitly names Marie Judith, daughter of Jean Pellerin and Marie Landry/Lendry. New original parental marriage, 21 July 1807 Yamachiche, upper left, names Francois Gendron, adult son of deceased Hyacinthe Gendron and deceased Angelique Labossiere, marrying Marie Pellerin, minor daughter of Jean Pellerin and Marie Landry. This original parentage bridge supports Marie/Judith as usages of the mother's compound name; it supersedes the earlier claim that no independent Judith evidence had been established. Child name, father, maternal surname, parish and two-month age fit. Counted as excluded by infant death, on the combined original records, not on a database merge alone.
