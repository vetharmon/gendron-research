## Biography

Jacques Gendron was born and baptized on 15 January 1821 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of François Gendron and Marie Roger Després. He married Mathilde Bérubé on 8 February 1848 at Rivière-du-Loup; the act names him as the adult son of François Gendron and the late Marie Roger Couillard Després. He appears again — and signs the act — as the father of Louis Wilbrod, born on 20 December 1860 and baptized two days later at St-Antonin, the child's mother being Mathilde Bérubé.
