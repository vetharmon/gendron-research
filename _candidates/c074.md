## Biography

- Jacques Gendron was born and baptized on 15 January 1821 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of François Gendron and Marie Roze Després. [Baptism](#document-act-2870705)

- He married Mathilde Bérubé on 8 February 1848 at Rivière-du-Loup; the act names him as the adult son of François Gendron and the late Marie Roze Couillard Després. [Marriage](#document-act-13400946)

- According to the existing research notes, he was the father of Louis Wilbrod, born on 20 December 1860 and baptized two days later at St-Antonin, the child’s mother being Mathilde Bérubé. Those notes record the father in the nonsigning clause and identify the visible lay signature as the godfather’s. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
