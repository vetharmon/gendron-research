## Biography

- Narcisse Gendron was born on the morning of 24 March 1823 and baptized the same day at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Joseph Gendron and Susanne Laurence. [Baptism](#document-act-2632036)
- He died on 18 August 1823, aged five months, and was buried at St-Ours the next day. [Burial, 19 August 1823](#document-act-2633459)
