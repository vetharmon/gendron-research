## Biography

Narcisse Gendron was born on the morning of 24 March 1823 and baptized the same day at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Joseph Gendron and Genevieve Laurence. He died on 18 August 1823, aged five months, and was buried at St-Ours the next day.
