## Biography

Antoine Gendron was baptized on 9 October 1821 at St-Constant (La Prairie), the son of Antoine Gendron and Marie Louise Roy. No later record identified. An 1851 St-Constant household headed by an older Antoine Gendron includes a son Antoine of his age, but the household cannot be anchored to his family — no marriage of an Antoine Gendron to the wife named there, Julienne, exists in the indexes.
