## Biography

Antoine Gendron was baptized on 9 October 1821 at St-Constant (La Prairie), the son of Antoine Gendron and Marie Louise Roy. He has no later record in Québec: no marriage or burial, and he is not in the Canadian censuses. An 1851 St-Constant household headed by an older Antoine Gendron includes a son Antoine of his age, but that household is not his — the wife named there is Julienne, and no marriage of an Antoine Gendron to a Julienne exists in the indexes.

He is likely the Antoine Jandro who settled at Plattsburgh, New York. In 1860 he is there, aged 38, working as a teamster, in the household of Antoine Jandro, 70, and his wife Louise, 70 — an older couple carrying his parents' names — with Peter, 19, and Anna, 27, all born in Canada. The elder Antoine died at Plattsburgh on 3 January 1865, aged about 78, and was buried in the Old Roman Catholic Cemetery. In 1870 the younger Antoine, 48, a labourer, heads his own Plattsburgh household; with him are Mary Le Roi — the surname is Roy — and a Jandrau woman of 37 whose first name is read as Eneresie or Enemie.

The identification is likely, not proven. Neither census states how the household members were related. Mary Le Roi's age is read as 60 on the schedule and 66 in the index; at 60 she would be too young to be the mother of a child baptized in 1821, so whether she is his mother, a stepmother, or another relative is not settled by the census. No wife is named for him in any record, and no New York marriage or death record for him has been found.
