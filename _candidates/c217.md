## Biography

- François Adolphe Gendron was born and baptized on 13 December 1827 at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier), the son of Joseph Gendron and Suzanne Lepelle dite Lahaie. [Baptism](#document-act-4549327)

- On 20 June 1853 he married Éléonore Massicot, daughter of François Massicot and Marguerite Gervais, at Ste-Geneviève-de-Batiscan; the marriage act names his parents as Joseph Gendron and Susanne Lahaie. [Marriage](#document-act-13400851)

- Marie Éléonore was born and baptized at Batiscan on 8 July 1861, daughter of Adolphe Gendron, farmer, and Éléonore Massicot. The original includes the father in its statement that the sponsors and father could not sign. [Daughter's baptism](#document-act-6004019)

- Earlier research notes also record Marie Éléonore at Ste-Geneviève in 1854, Alfred at Batiscan in 1855, and Hubert there in 1858; those three originals were not newly inspected in this pass. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
