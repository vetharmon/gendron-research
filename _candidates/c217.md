## Biography

- François Adolphe Gendron was baptized on 13 December 1827 at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier), the son of Joseph Gendron and Suzanne Lepelle dite Lahaie. [Baptism](#document-act-4549327)

- On 20 June 1853 he married Éléonore Massicot, daughter of François Massicot and Marguerite Gervais, at Ste-Geneviève-de-Batiscan; the marriage act names his parents as Joseph Gendron and Susanne Lahaie. [Marriage](#document-act-13400851)

- The existing research notes record four children baptized to the couple: Marie Éléonore at Ste-Geneviève in 1854, then Alfred (1855), Hubert (1858) and Marie Éléonore (1861) at Batiscan. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
