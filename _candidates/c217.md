## Biography

François Adolphe Gendron was baptized on 13 December 1827 at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier). The October 2026 packet names his parents as Joseph Gendron and Suzanne Lepelle dite Lahaie and identifies PRDH individual 956895. It reports a marriage on 20 June 1853 and children through 1861, but the spouse, marriage place and act number have not been recovered here.
