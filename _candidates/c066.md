## Biography

- Jean Baptiste Gendreau was born on 19 July 1820 and baptized the next day at St-Denis-sur-Richelieu (St-Denis), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Courtemanche. [Baptism](#document-act-2692541)

- He died on 9 August 1820, aged three weeks, and was buried at Boucherville on 11 August 1820; the burial names the same parents. [Burial](#document-act-2704071)

- **[Burial, 11 August 1820 — LAFRANCE act 2704071](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2704071)** — His burial at Boucherville (entry S81): he died two days earlier, aged three weeks, and the act names the same parents as his baptism.
