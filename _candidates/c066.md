## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendreau was born on 19 July 1820 and baptized the next day at St-Denis-sur-Richelieu (St-Denis), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Courtemanche. He died on 9 August 1820, aged three weeks, and was buried at Boucherville on 11 August 1820; the burial names the same parents.
