## Biography

Isaie Gendron was born on 27 August 1821 and baptized the same day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (Ste-Anne), the son of Francois Gendron and Marie Julie Vachon. He died on 27 January 1822, aged about five months, and was buried at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade on 29 January 1822, the burial naming the same parents.
