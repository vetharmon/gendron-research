## Biography

- Isaie Gendron was born on 27 August 1821 and baptized the same day at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (Ste-Anne), the son of Francois Gendron and Marie Julie Vachon. [Baptism](#document-act-2937049)

- He died on 27 January 1822, aged about five months, and was buried at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade on 29 January 1822, the burial naming the same parents. [Burial](#document-act-2938179)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 29 January 1822 — LAFRANCE act 2938179](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2938179)** — His burial at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (entry S4, lower left): he died two days earlier, aged about five months, and the act names his parents, his mother there as Marie Vachon.
