## Biography

Pierre Zephirin Gendron was born and baptized on 18 August 1829 at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, the son of Pierre Gendron and Angele Langlois. On 11 February 1850 he married Josephte Douville at St-Casimir, the act naming the groom Zephirin Gendron, minor son of Pierre Gendron and Angele Langlois. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 11 February 1850.
