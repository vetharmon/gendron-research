## Biography

- Pierre Zephirin Gendron was born and baptized on 18 August 1829 at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade, the son of Pierre Gendron and Angele Langlois. [Baptism](#document-act-4566625)
- On 11 February 1850 he married Josephte Douville at St-Casimir, the act naming the groom Zephirin Gendron, adult son of Pierre Gendron and Angele Langlois. [Marriage, 11 February 1850](#document-act-13401079)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 11 February 1850 — LAFRANCE act 13401079](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401079)** — His marriage to Josephte Douville at St-Casimir, under his second given name, Zephirin. It names his parents, Pierre Gendron and Angele Langlois, tying the groom to the 1829 baptism.
