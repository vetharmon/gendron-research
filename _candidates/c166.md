## Biography

Francois Ferdinand Gendreau was baptized on 2 July 1825 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Abraham Gendreau and Marie Pellerin. He died on 22 November 1825, aged five months, and was buried at Montmagny on 25 November 1825.
