## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Francois Ferdinand Gendreau was born on 1 July 1825 and baptized on 2 July 1825 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Abraham Gendreau and Marie Pellerin. [Baptism](#document-act-4079847)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 22 November 1825, aged five months, and was buried at Montmagny on 25 November 1825. [Burial](#document-act-4083727)
