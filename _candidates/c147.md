## Biography

François Gendron was born on 28 September 1824 and baptized at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Joseph Gendron and Marie Vien. He married Esther Lefebvre on 17 November 1846 at Sainte-Rosalie; the marriage act names him as the adult son of the late Joseph Gendron and Thérèse Vien, and it legitimized the couple's son François, born on 23 July 1844. The two forms of his mother's name meet in the baptism of an older sibling on 4 April 1802, which names her Marie Thérèse Vien, and in his parents' marriage on 3 August 1801, which names Joseph Gendron and Thérèse Vien. Three more children of François and Esther were baptized in the following years: Joseph on 23 July 1848 at Sainte-Rosalie, Marie Élise on 27 September 1850 at Saint-Simon, and Jean Baptiste, born 11 October, on 12 October 1852 at Sainte-Rosalie.

## Documents

- **Marriage, 17 November 1846 — LAFRANCE act 13497821** — His marriage to Esther Lefebvre at Sainte-Rosalie (entry M24), naming him as the adult son of the late Joseph Gendron and Thérèse Vien, and reciting the legitimization of their son François, born 23 July 1844.
- **Baptisms of his children, 1848–1852 — LAFRANCE acts 4200193, 5778691, 5357413** — Joseph, baptized 23 July 1848 at Sainte-Rosalie (entry B53); Marie Élise, baptized 27 September 1850 at Saint-Simon (entry B69); and Jean Baptiste, born 11 October and baptized 12 October 1852 at Sainte-Rosalie (entry B36). All three name François Gendron and Esther Lefebvre as the parents.
- **[Baptism, 28 September 1824 — LAFRANCE act 2479588](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2479588)** — His baptism at St-Hyacinthe (entry B314): François, born that day, son of Joseph Gendron and Marie Vien.
- **Older sibling's baptism, 4 April 1802 — LAFRANCE act 2471803, and parents' marriage, 3 August 1801 — LAFRANCE act 5731453** — The sibling's baptism names the mother Marie Thérèse Vien in full, and the parents' marriage names Joseph Gendron and Thérèse Vien; together they account for the mother appearing as Marie Vien in 1824 and as Thérèse Vien in 1846.
