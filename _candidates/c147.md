## Biography

François Gendron was born on 28 September 1824 and baptized at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Joseph Gendron and Marie Vien. He married Esther Lefebvre on 17 November 1846 at Sainte-Rosalie; the marriage act names him as the adult son of the late Joseph Gendron and Thérèse Vien, and it legitimized the couple's son François, born on 23 July 1844. The two forms of his mother's name meet in the baptism of an older sibling on 4 April 1802, which names her Marie Thérèse Vien, and in his parents' marriage on 3 August 1801, which names Joseph Gendron and Thérèse Vien. Three more children of François and Esther were baptized in the following years: Joseph on 23 July 1848 at Sainte-Rosalie, Marie Élise on 27 September 1850 at Saint-Simon, and Jean Baptiste, born 11 October, on 12 October 1852 at Sainte-Rosalie. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 12 October 1852.
