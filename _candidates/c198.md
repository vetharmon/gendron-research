## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Antoine Gendron was born and baptized on 14 April 1827 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Charles Gendron and Angelique Bourbonnais. [Baptism](#document-act-4253712)
- **Linked burial:** A burial in the same parish on 11 September 1828 records Antoine, legitimate son of Charles Gendron and Marguerite Angelique Bourbonnais, who had died two days earlier; the act gives no age. [Burial](#document-act-4257554)

## Research status

The existing file identifies this burial using PRDH's reconstruction of the family, which lists Antoine as the couple's only child, born 14 April 1827 and linked to a death on 9 September 1828. That compiled basis is retained here as a research note, rather than presented as wording from the original burial.

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 11 September 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4257554](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4257554)** — The burial at Beauharnois (entry S26, upper right): Antoine, legitimate son of Charles Gendron and Marguerite Angelique Bourbonnais, died two days earlier; no age is stated. The mother's added given name, Marguerite, is the one difference from the baptism. Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4257554).
- **[Baptism, 14 April 1827 — LAFRANCE act 4253712](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4253712)** — His baptism at Beauharnois (entry B27, lower left): Antoine, born the same day, son of Charles Gendron and Angelique Bourbonnais (an earlier maternal given name in the act is struck through). Also at [Généalogie Québec](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/img/acte/4253712).
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 159519](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/159519)** — PRDH's compiled family record: it places his parents' marriage at Beauharnois on 9 January 1827 and lists a single child, Antoine, born 14 April 1827 and linked to the 9 September 1828 death. The parents' marriage act itself (indexed act 13400876) is not among the linked sources.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 2752321](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/2752321)** — PRDH's compiled record for Antoine.
