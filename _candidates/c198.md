## Biography

Antoine Gendron was born and baptized on 14 April 1827 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Charles Gendron and Angelique Bourbonnais. A burial in the same parish on 11 September 1828 records an Antoine, legitimate son of Charles Gendron and Marguerite Angelique Bourbonnais, who had died two days earlier; the act gives no age. PRDH's reconstruction of the family lists Antoine as the couple's only child, born 14 April 1827 and linked to a death on 9 September 1828, and the burial is identified as his on that basis.
