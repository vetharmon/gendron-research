## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 4253712
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism B27 lower left, 14 April 1827: Antoine, born the same day, son of Charles Gendron and Angelique Bourbonnais (a preceding maternal given name is struck through). Original burial S26 upper right, 11 September 1828: Antoine died two days earlier, legitimate son of Charles Gendron and Marguerite Angelique Bourbonnais. No age appears in the burial passage. Both full original JPEGs were re-opened and read on 9 November. Additional parental-family check: PRDH family 159519 places the parents' marriage at Beauharnois on 9 January 1827 (indexed act 13400876) and lists a single child, Antoine born 14 April 1827, linked to death on 9 September 1828. No competing Antoine is shown in that reconstructed family. Matching child and father, the shared maternal name Angelique Bourbonnais, the same parish and the corroborating child sequence support the burial link and exclusion before Franklin's Missouri life. This is a reasoned identification of the two originals, supported by a database reconstruction, not a claim that the unaged burial itself states infancy or that the indexed family is guaranteed complete. The burial's additional maternal given name Marguerite is retained as a discrepancy. PRDH's displayed age of one year is calculated from the linkage and is not independent age evidence. The parents' marriage original was not inspected or newly downloaded in this pass.
