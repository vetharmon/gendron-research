## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 15 August 1819 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Louis Gendron and Marie Gaudet. He died on 30 August 1819, aged fifteen days, and was buried at St-Hyacinthe the following day; the burial act gives his mother's surname in the variant form Godette.
