## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born on 20 August 1829 and baptized the next day at Marieville (St-Nom-de-Marie), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Felicite Bachand dite Vertefeuille. On 3 November 1852 he married Lucie Laroque at Marieville, the act naming him as the adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Philie Bachand — Philie being the form of his mother's given name used there, the variation linked in PRDH's profile of her (949342). He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 3 November 1852.
