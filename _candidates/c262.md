## Biography

- Pierre Gendron was born on 20 August 1829 and baptized the next day at Marieville, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Felicite Bachand dite Vertefeuille. [Baptism](#document-act-4510747)
- On 3 November 1852 he married Lucie Laroque at Marieville. The act calls him the adult son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Philie Bachand, retaining the different form of his mother's given name. [Marriage](#document-act-13401058)

## Research context

The earlier research linked the Philie/Felicite name forms through the compiled profile of his mother (PRDH 949342). [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 3 November 1852 — LAFRANCE act 13401058](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13401058)** — His marriage to Lucie Laroque at Marieville. It names his father, Jean Baptiste Gendron, and his mother as Philie Bachand; the father's name, the maternal surname, and the parish agree with the baptism, and PRDH links the Felicite/Philie given-name variation.
- **[Baptism, 21 August 1829 — LAFRANCE act 4510747](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4510747)** — His baptism at Marieville: the original act says he was born the previous day, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Felicite Bachand dite Vertefeuille. PRDH gives his birth as 21 August instead; the original act's "previous day" is followed here.
