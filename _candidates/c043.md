## Biography

François Gendron was born on 24 May 1818 and baptized the next day at St-Antoine-sur-Richelieu (St-Antoine-de-Padoue), the son of François Gendron and Marie Archange Chagnon Larose Chaillon. He married Françoise Vasseur on 25 February 1840 at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville (St-Jean-Baptiste). He appears as the father of Pie Philomène, baptized on 5 May 1859 at St-Pie.
