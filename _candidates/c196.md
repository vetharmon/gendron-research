## Biography

Jean Theophile Gendron was born on 3 April 1827 and baptized the next day at St-Jean-Port-Joli (L'Islet), the son of Abraham Gendron and Madeleine Beaulieu. He died on 13 June 1830, aged three, and was buried the next day at L'Islet; the burial names his parents as Pierre Abraham Gendron and Marie Magdeleine Baulieu, the fuller forms of the names in his baptism.
