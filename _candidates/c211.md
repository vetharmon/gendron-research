## Biography

- Pierre Gendron was born and baptized on 1 October 1827 at St-Hyacinthe, the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Dussault. LAFRANCE spells his mother’s name Dusseault. [Baptism](#document-act-2995673)

## Research status

No later record has been connected to him.

## Assessment and research

Three St-Pie burial entries were checked: Élophe, age five, in 1837; Pierre Zéphyrin, age seven and a half, in 1838; and Ambroise, age six, in 1846. None establishes C211’s death. The Pierre Zéphyrin question was settled by the original records read on 16 November 2026: his separate baptism on 2 February 1830 names Pierre Gendrault and Marie Dusseau, and his burial on 16 January 1838 names the mother Dussault. This is [C268](../c268/), the younger brother. The separate baptism, rather than the burial age alone, establishes the distinction. C211’s later life remains to be traced through this St-Damase and St-Pie family.
