## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born and baptized on 1 October 1827 at St-Hyacinthe, the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Dussault. LAFRANCE spells his mother’s name Dusseault. No later record has been connected to him.

## Assessment and research

Three St-Pie burial indexes were checked: Élophe, age five, in 1837; Pierre Zéphyrin, age seven and a half, in 1838; and Ambroise, age six, in 1846. None currently establishes C211’s death. The Pierre Zéphyrin entry needs a specific follow-up: inspect the original and locate the supposed younger child’s baptism before treating him as definitively distinct from C211. C211 would have been ten years and three months old, about two years and nine months older than the indexed burial age. The family’s St-Damase and St-Pie records provide the next places to search.
