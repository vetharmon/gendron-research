## Biography

- Pierre Gendron was born and baptized on 1 October 1827 at St-Hyacinthe, the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Dussault. The inspected register writes his mother’s name Dusseault and records his father as a farmer who was absent. [Baptism](#document-act-2995673)

## Research status

No later record has been connected to him.

## Assessment and research

Three St-Pie burial entries were checked: Élophe, age five, in 1837; Pierre Zéphyrin, age seven and a half, in 1838; and Ambroise, age six, in 1846. None establishes C211’s death. The Pierre Zéphyrin question was settled by the separate original records, re-read for C268 on 23 November 2026 and reproduced here: his separate baptism on 2 February 1830 names Pierre Gendrault and Marie Dusseau, and his burial on 16 January 1838 names the mother with a difficult spelling retained as [Dussault?]. This is [C268](../c268/), the younger brother. The separate baptism, rather than the burial age alone, establishes the distinction. C211’s later life remains to be traced through this St-Damase and St-Pie family.

## Closed Sainte-Rosalie namesake lead — 28 November 2026

The **1871 Pierre 42 / Louise 44 household** at Sainte-Rosalie, previously unassigned under FamilySearch **1:1:M4K1-9HX**, belongs to **C233 Pierre Samuel Gendron**.
