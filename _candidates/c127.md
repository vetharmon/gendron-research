## Research assessment

**The record.** François Gendron was born and baptized on 6 September 1823 at Châteauguay (Saint-Joachim), act 2628150. The register spells the family name **Jandron**: the child appears as "Francois JANDRON," his father as "Pierre JANDRON," a day labourer, and his mother as "Marie PARE" — standardized by PRDH as François Gendron, Pierre Gendron, and Marie Madeleine Paré. His parents married on 16 November 1818 at Caughnawaga (Sault-St-Louis). PRDH records three children for the couple — Louis (1819, died in infancy), an unnamed infant (1821), and François — and no later Québec marriage or burial for François himself.

**Why this candidate is retained.** Franklin Jondro appears in Missouri by 1850 (St. Clair County), again in 1860 (Polk Township), where his birthplace is given as **"Montreal Canada"** — Châteauguay sits in Montréal's immediate orbit — and in 1880 (Dallas Township). His lifetime ages, taken together across the censuses and his 1863 draft registration, fit an 1823 birth. He died in Hickory County, Missouri, on 13 April 1886. Every other same-name candidate in this register's 1816–1830 window has been excluded on primary records or remains only as a qualified lead; this is the one baptism whose subject disappears from Québec records in a way consistent with emigration, at the right age, from the right place. An older Châteauguay namesake — François Xavier Gendron, born 1815, just outside this register's window — cannot be eliminated on Québec records either, but identifying him with Franklin would require seven or more years of consistent age misstatement across Franklin's lifetime records.

**The limits of the case.** No single document names the Missouri man as the Châteauguay child. The identification rests on convergence: the age fit, the Montréal-orbit birthplace, the disappearance from Québec, and the systematic exclusion of the alternatives. Franklin's gravestone gives his birth as 22 July 1820, three years before this baptism; that conflict is answered by the pattern of his lifetime ages rather than by any one record, and it is disclosed here rather than smoothed over. Treat this page as the statement of a well-supported hypothesis, not a proof.
