## Biography

François Gendron was born and baptized on 6 September 1823 at Châteauguay (Saint-Joachim), the son of Pierre Gendron, a day labourer, and Marie Madeleine Paré, who had married at Caughnawaga (Sault-St-Louis) on 16 November 1818. The register spells the family name **Jandron** — the child appears as "Francois JANDRON," his father as "Pierre JANDRON," his mother as "Marie PARE." He was the couple's third recorded child, after Louis (1819, died in infancy) and an unnamed infant (1821). No marriage or burial for him has been found in Québec.

He is next recorded in Missouri, as Franklin Jondro: in St. Clair County in 1850, in Polk Township in 1860 — where his birthplace is given as "Montreal Canada" — on a draft registration in 1863, and in Dallas Township in 1880. His ages across those records, taken together, fit an 1823 birth. He died in Hickory County, Missouri, on 13 April 1886; his gravestone gives his birth as 22 July 1820. His daughter Nancy Ann's death certificate names him as her father.

No single document names the Missouri man as the Châteauguay child. The identification rests on the age fit, the Montréal-orbit birthplace, the disappearance from Québec records, and the exclusion of the other candidates in this register — and on this page it is stated as what it is: the proposed identity, not a proven one. An older Châteauguay namesake, François Xavier Gendron, born in 1815 and just outside this register's window, has no Québec marriage or burial either; identifying him with Franklin would require seven or more years of consistent age misstatement across Franklin's lifetime records.
