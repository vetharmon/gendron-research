## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2485476
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 15 December 1819 Neuville, lower left, names Joseph Prudent born that morning, son of Pierre Gendron and Christine Anger. Original marriage 17 February 1852 Québec Notre-Dame, right lower entry, names Prudent Gendron, of Toronto, son of Pierre Gendron and deceased Justine Anger, marrying Antoinette Morphise Vallerand. Original remarriage 8 February 1858 Québec Notre-Dame, M11 upper left, names Joseph Prudent Gendron, mécanicien, widower of Antoinette Morphise Vallerand, marrying Mathilde Catherine Miville dite Deschênes. The Christine/Justine maternal given-name variation is disclosed; it also occurs in brother C017’s records and PRDH reconstructs her as Christine Justine Lefebvre Angers. The matching paternal identity and independent spouse bridge into 1858 support exclusion. LAFRANCE’s remarriage dropdown says Gagnon; the original is read as Gendron and is retained unchanged.
