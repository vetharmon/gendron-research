## Biography

- Joseph Prudent Gendron was born on 15 December 1819 and baptized the same day at Neuville (St-François-de-Sales), the son of Pierre Gendron and Christine Anger. [Baptism](#document-act-2485476)

- He married Antoinette Morphise Vallerand on 17 February 1852 at Québec (Notre-Dame); the act names his mother as the late Christine Anger and places both spouses at Québec; Toronto concerns the dispensation and the bride’s former residence. [Marriage](#document-act-13400943)

- After Antoinette's death he married again on 8 February 1858 at Québec (Notre-Dame), where the act calls him an orfèvre (goldsmith), widower of Antoinette Morphise Vallerand, and his bride Mathilde Catherine Miville dite Deschênes. [Remarriage](#document-act-13397198)

- The inspected baptism and 1852 marriage both support Christine as his mother’s given name. [Baptism](#document-act-2485476) [Marriage](#document-act-13400943)
