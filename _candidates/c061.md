## Biography

Joseph Prudent Gendron was born on 15 December 1819 and baptized the same day at Neuville (St-François-de-Sales), the son of Pierre Gendron and Christine Anger. He married Antoinette Morphise Vallerand on 17 February 1852 at Québec (Notre-Dame); the act describes him as of Toronto and names his mother as the late Justine Anger. After Antoinette's death he married again on 8 February 1858 at Québec (Notre-Dame), where the act calls him a mécanicien (mechanic), widower of Antoinette Morphise Vallerand, and his bride Mathilde Catherine Miville dite Deschênes. His mother's given name appears as Christine in his baptism and Justine in his marriage; PRDH reconstructs her as Christine Justine Angers. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 8 February 1858.
