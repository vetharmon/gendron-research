## Biography

Charles Gendreau was born on 25 October 1827 and baptized the same day at Montmagny, the son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne. He married Marie Parent on 7 January 1851 at Rimouski, the act naming him as the adult son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne of St-Thomas. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 7 January 1851.
