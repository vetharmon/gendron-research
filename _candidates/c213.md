## Biography

- Charles Gendreau was born on 25 October 1827 and baptized the same day at Montmagny, the son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne. [Baptism](#document-act-4080162)

- He married Marie Parent on 7 January 1851 at Rimouski, the act naming him as the adult son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne of St-Thomas. [Marriage](#document-act-13400795)
