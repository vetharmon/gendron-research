## Biography

- Charles Gendreau was born on 25 October 1827 and baptized the same day at Montmagny, the son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne. [Baptism](#document-act-4080162)

- He married Marie Parent on 7 January 1851 at Rimouski, the act naming him as the adult son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne of St-Fabien. [Marriage](#document-act-13400795)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 7 January 1851 — LAFRANCE act 13400795](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400795)** — His marriage to Marie Parent at Rimouski. It names his parents, Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne of St-Fabien, tying the groom to the 1827 baptism.
- **[Baptism, 25 October 1827 — LAFRANCE act 4080162](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4080162)** — His baptism at Montmagny: born the same day, son of Augustin Gendreau and Julie Gagne; the naming of the parents continues at the top of the right-hand page.
