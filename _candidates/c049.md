## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born on 31 May 1819 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Joseph Gendron and Marie Anne Guy. He married Angélique Boyer on 26 November 1847 at Beauharnois; the act names her as the daughter of Faustin Boyer and Angélique Tessier, and his own mother as deceased. He appears as the father of Félix, born on 19 November 1859 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois, where Pierre is described as a carrossier (carriage maker). He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 20 November 1859.
