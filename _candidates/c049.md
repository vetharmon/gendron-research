## Biography

Pierre Gendron was born on 31 May 1819 and baptized the next day at Châteauguay, the son of Joseph Gendron and Marie Anne Guy. He married Angélique Boyer on 16 November 1847 at Beauharnois; the act names her as the daughter of Faustin Boyer and Angélique Tessier, and his own mother as deceased. He appears as the father of Félix, born and baptized on 20 November 1859 at Beauharnois, where Pierre is described as a carrossier (carriage maker).

## Original-reading corrections — 21 November 2026

Three readers independently re-examined the saved marriage and child-baptism images. **M30 is dated 16 November 1847**, correcting the earlier 26 November date. The archived marriage filename still contains `1847-11-26`; it is retained as a locator, not as the event date. Pierre is a **forgeron**, adult son of Joseph Gendron and the deceased Marie Anne Guy.

The witnesses are **Joseph Gendron, father of the groom**; **Barthélemi Sénécal**, whose relationship is not stated; and two men named **Faustin Boyer**, expressly described as the bride’s father and brother.

**Félix’s B181 baptism is dated 20 November 1859 and says he was born that day**, correcting the earlier 19 November birth. It names Pierre Gendron, carrossier, and Angélique Boyer as parents. The sponsors are Antoine Boyer and Angèle, whose surname remains uncertain in this reading. No relationship is stated for the sponsors.

Neither entry names Pierre’s brother [George, C220](../c220/). These are useful records of George’s family, but they do not establish his whereabouts or identify the Montréal namesake. Pierre’s established identity and **solid elimination remain unchanged**.
