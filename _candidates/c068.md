## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 27 August 1820 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Francois Gendron and Marguerite Lunel. He died the following day, 29 August 1820, aged two days, and was buried at Beauharnois on 30 August 1820; the burial names the same parents, his mother styled Marguerite Lunel Lachapelle.
