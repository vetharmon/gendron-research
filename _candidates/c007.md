## Biography


- **Baptism:** Ferdinand Gendron was baptized on 13 May 1816 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), the son of Joseph Gendron and Françoise Proux. [Baptism](#document-act-2870119)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 7 November 1816, aged six months, and was buried at Montmagny the next day. The burial transcription also preserves an anomalous July phrase in its date line. [Burial](#document-act-2872186)
