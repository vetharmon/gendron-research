## Biography

Joseph Octave Gendron was born and baptized on 14 February 1826 at Beauharnois, son of Joachim Gendron, cultivateur, and Angélique Hébert, from Châteauguay.

A probable burial at Châteauguay on 10 May 1848 names Joseph Octave Gendron, who died on 8 May, aged 21, son of Joachim Gendron, cultivateur, and Félicité Hébert. Witnesses were Pierre Boursier and Michel Allard. The compound name, father, maternal surname, and parish agree. The mother's given name differs, and the recorded age is one year below his baptismal age.

The younger Joseph, [C232](../c232/), is recorded alive with his parents in the 1851 census.
