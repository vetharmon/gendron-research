## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Joseph Octave Gendron was born and baptized on 14 February 1826 at Beauharnois, son of Joachim Gendron, cultivateur, and Angélique Hébert, from Châteauguay. [Baptism and archival transcript](#document-act-4253586)
- **Probable burial:** A burial at Châteauguay on 10 May 1848 names Joseph Octave Gendron, who died on 8 May, aged 21, son of Joachim Gendron, cultivateur, and Félicité Hébert. Witnesses were Pierre Boursier and Michel Allard. The compound name, father, maternal surname and parish agree; the mother's given name differs, and the recorded age is one year below his baptismal age. [Burial](#document-act-4594153) · [Baptism](#document-act-4253586)

## Research status

The younger Joseph, [C232](../c232/), is recorded alive with his parents in the 1851 census.

## Further record checks

The handwritten baptism (B.16, d1p_01700214.jpg), inspected on 16 November 2026, confirms Angélique Hébert; the maternal given name is not an error introduced by the typed transcript. A Châteauguay marriage on 23 January 1809 names Joachim Jeandron and Angélique Hébert, daughter of Adrien Hébert and Josephte Leduc. This provides a parentage lead for identifying the mother, but does not yet establish that she is the Félicité Hébert named in the 1848 burial. The assessment remains likely.
