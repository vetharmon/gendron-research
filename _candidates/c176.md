## Probable burial — not yet a firm exclusion

The baptism transcript records **Joseph Octave**, born and baptized **14 February 1826** at Beauharnois, son of **Joachim Gendron, cultivateur, and Angélique Hébert**, whose parents were from Châteauguay.

The original Châteauguay burial, **10 May 1848**, names **Joseph Octave Gendron**, who died **“avant-hier” (8 May)**, aged **21**, son of **Joachim Gendron, cultivateur, and Félicité Hébert**, of this parish. Witnesses were Pierre Boursier and Michel Allard, who did not sign.

The compound name, father, maternal surname and Châteauguay connection strongly favor this candidate. Two discrepancies remain: **Angélique versus Félicité** for the mother, and a stated age of 21 when this child would have been 22. An independently documented connection between the mother's given names would strengthen the identification. Until then, this is a **qualified lead and is not counted as an exclusion**.

The burial is not C232's: that Joseph was born in August 1828 and is identified with Joachim and Félicité in the later census. See [C232](../c232/).

- Burial: original register image supplied by the user, Châteauguay 1848, left page S.13; LAFRANCE act 4594153. The mother's name in this image is Félicité.
