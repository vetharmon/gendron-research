## Biography

Jacques Gendron was baptized on 12 August 1816 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), in a joint baptism with his twin brother Vital, the sons of Joseph Gendron and Magdeleine Minville. He died on 2 October 1816, aged about six weeks, and was buried at Montmagny the next day; the burial names the same parents.
