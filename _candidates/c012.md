## Biography


- **Baptism:** Jacques Gendron was baptized on 12 August 1816 at Montmagny (St-Thomas), in a joint baptism with his twin brother Vital, the sons of Joseph Gendron and Magdeleine Minville. [Joint baptism](#document-act-2870161)
- **Death and burial:** He died on 2 October 1816, with a recorded age of one and a half months (51 days elapsed from birth to death), and was buried at Montmagny the next day; the burial names the same parents. [Burial](#document-act-2872179) · [Birth date](#document-act-2870161)
