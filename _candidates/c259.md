## Biography

- Julien Gendreau was born on 30 July 1829 and baptized the next day at Pointe-du-Lac (La-Visitation), the son of Julien Gendreau and Sophie Miville. [Baptism](#document-act-4542389)
- On 18 August 1851 he married Emilie Lefebvre Descoteaux at Trois-Rivières, the act naming him as the unmarried adult son of Julien Gendreau and Sophie Miville. [Marriage, 18 August 1851](#document-act-13400820)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 18 August 1851 — LAFRANCE act 13400820](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400820)** — His marriage to Emilie Lefebvre Descoteaux at Trois-Rivières (both pages of the act are saved). It names his parents, Julien Gendreau and Sophie Miville, and describes him as an unmarried adult, tying the groom to the 1829 baptism.
- **[Baptism, 31 July 1829 — LAFRANCE act 4542389](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4542389)** — His baptism at Pointe-du-Lac: born the previous day, son of Julien Gendrau and Sophie Miville.
