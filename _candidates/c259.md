## Biography

Julien Gendreau was born on 30 July 1829 and baptized the next day at Pointe-du-Lac (La-Visitation), the son of Julien Gendreau and Sophie Miville. On 18 August 1851 he married Emilie Lefebvre Descoteaux at Trois-Rivières, the act naming him as the unmarried adult son of Julien Gendreau and Sophie Miville. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 18 August 1851.
