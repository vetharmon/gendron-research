## Biography

Alexandre Gendron was baptized on 22 June 1820 at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (Ste-Anne), the son of Louis Gendron and Marguerite Gervais, one of twelve children of that couple baptized there. No later record identified: no marriage, burial, or godparent appearance, no entry in the Canadian censuses of 1851 and 1861 or the US censuses of 1850 and 1860, and he is not in his widowed mother's 1851 household at Ste-Anne or his brother Hubert's 1861 household at St-Casimir.
