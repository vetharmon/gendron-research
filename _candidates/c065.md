## Biography

Alexandre Gendron was baptized on 22 June 1820 at Ste-Anne-de-la-Pérade (Ste-Anne). No later record of Alexandre himself has been connected to him: no burial before 1850 was established, and follow-up work on the related Louis Gendron and Marguerite Gervais family — an 1849 marriage for Hubert, an 1854 marriage for Theotiste and an 1856 burial for Henriette — did not establish what became of him.
