## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2382598
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 29 July 1824 St-Damase, act 2382598, top left: Abdon born that day, son of Francois Gendron and Archange Chayon. Original marriage 21 May 1849 St-Dominique, act 13497816, M3 upper right: Abdon Gendron, adult son of Francois Gendron and Archange Chagnon, marries Priscille Desmarais, daughter of Jean Baptiste Desmarais and Madeleine Lacasse. Original 4 June 1861 St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville baptism, act 4750838, B31 middle right: Michel born previous day, legitimate son of Abdon Gendron, journalier, and Priscille Desmarais. Chayon/Chagnon is a disclosed maternal surname spelling variation; the uncommon Abdon, matching paternal name and maternal given name, local baptism/marriage context and subsequent spouse-linked family support the identity assessment. These acts establish a separate Quebec family continuing in 1861, incompatible with Franklin's documented Missouri family. The exclusion rests on the linked family, not solely on a May 1849 marriage.
