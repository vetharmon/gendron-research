## Biography

Abdon Gendron was born on 29 July 1824 and baptized the same day at St-Damase (St-Hyacinthe), the son of François Gendron and Archange Chayon. He married Priscille Desmarais on 21 May 1849 at St-Dominique; the act names him an adult and gives his mother's surname as Chagnon. Their son Michel was born on 3 June 1861 and baptized the next day at St-Jean-Baptiste-de-Rouville. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 4 June 1861.
