## Biography

Jean Gendron was born on the evening of 27 December 1823 and baptized the next day at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier), the son of Alexis Gendron, cultivateur of that parish, and Julie Adam. He died on 25 January 1826 and was buried at Batiscan on 11 February 1826; the burial gives his age as about three years, though he was two years and one month old. His parents had married at Batiscan on 11 January 1819; an older child of the couple was baptized at Champlain on 24 February 1821, and his sister Marie Delphine was baptized at Batiscan on 27 September 1826, after his death.
