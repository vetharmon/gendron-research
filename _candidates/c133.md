Follow-up 9 November 2026: Original baptism at bottom left reads 23 December 1823, born the previous day, Jean, son of Alexis Gendron, cultivateur of this parish, and Julie Adam. Original burial at left middle reads 11 February 1826, Jean, son of Alexis Gendron, cultivateur of this parish, and Julie Adam, died 25 January, aged about three years. Calculated age is two years one month three days: the burial age is approximate and discrepant, not exact agreement. Additional original family evidence: parents' marriage 11 January 1819 at Batiscan, act 5694026, begins bottom left and continues upper right on the same spread; 24 February 1821 Champlain baptism, act 2419136, identifies the same parental couple as residents of St-Francois-Xavier de Batiscan; 27 September 1826 Batiscan baptism of Marie Delphine, act 4549298, repeats both parents. The parent-linked GQ search returned one indexed Jean baptism for this couple, but is not proof that every birth is captured. Matching child's name, both parents, occupation, parish and the independently corroborated family support the reasoned identification of this burial with C133 despite the approximate-age discrepancy. This is a cumulative assessment, not a claim that the new family acts correct the burial's stated age. Earlier audit raised a year conflict; reinspection resolved the year as 1826. The baptism index and older filename wrongly used 28 December; the manuscript reads 23 December.
