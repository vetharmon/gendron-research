## Biography

- Jean Gendron was born on the evening of 27 December 1823 and baptized the next day at Batiscan (St-François-Xavier), the son of Alexis Gendron, cultivateur of that parish, and Julie Adam. [Baptism](#document-act-2369212)
- He died on 25 January 1826 and was buried at Batiscan on 11 February 1826; the burial gives his age as about three years, though he was two years and one month old. [Burial, 11 February 1826](#document-act-4550106)
- His parents had married at Batiscan on 11 January 1819; an older child of the couple was baptized at Champlain on 24 February 1821, and his sister Marie Delphine was baptized at Batiscan on 27 September 1826, after his death. [Parents’ marriage](#document-act-5694026) · [Older child’s baptism](#document-act-2419136) · [Marie Delphine’s baptism](#document-act-4549298)

- **[Baptism, 28 December 1823 — LAFRANCE act 2369212](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2369212)** — His baptism at Batiscan (bottom left of the page), born the previous day, son of Alexis Gendron and Julie Adam. The inspected greffe manuscript reads 28 December and says né hier soir, placing the birth on the evening of 27 December. The exact alternate parish copy remains unverified.
- **Parents' marriage, 11 January 1819, and siblings' baptisms, 1821 and 1826** ([marriage 5694026](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/5694026), [1821 baptism 2419136](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2419136), [1826 baptism 4549298](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4549298)) — His parents' marriage at Batiscan, a sibling's baptism at Champlain on 24 February 1821 naming the same couple as residents of Batiscan, and his sister Marie Delphine's baptism at Batiscan on 27 September 1826, again naming Alexis Gendron and Julie Adam. Together they confirm the family the baptism and burial belong to.
