## Biography

François Xavier Gendron was born and baptized on 25 January 1817 at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud (St-François-de-Sales), the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Anne Ruel. He became a physician. On 22 April 1844 he married Marie Edna Elisabeth Dominy, daughter of Robert Dominy and Jemina Carter, in the same parish, and later Québec records show his family there. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 22 April 1844.
