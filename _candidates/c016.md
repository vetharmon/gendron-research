## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** François Xavier Gendron was born and baptized on 25 January 1817 at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud (St-François-de-Sales), the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Anne Ruel. [Baptism](#document-act-2587260)
- **Physician and first marriage:** On 22 April 1844 he married Mary Anna Elisabeth Dominy, daughter of Robert Dominy and Jemima Carter, in the same parish. The original names his parents and identifies him as a physician. [Marriage](#document-act-13400920)
- **Remarriage:** On 12 October 1877 he married Émilie Girard at Notre-Dame de Québec. This later act identifies him as a physician residing at St-Thomas-de-Montmagny and widower of Mary Anna Elisabeth Dominy; he signed Fr. X. Gendron, MD. The named former wife and profession link the two adult records and carry his documented Québec life to 1877. [Remarriage](#document-act-13682418) · [First marriage](#document-act-13400920)
