## Biography

François Xavier Gendron was born and baptized on 25 January 1817 at St-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud (St-François-de-Sales), the son of Jacques Gendron and Marie Anne Ruel. He became a physician. On 22 April 1844 he married Marie Edna Elisabeth Dominy, daughter of Robert Dominy and Jemina Carter, in the same parish, and later Québec records show his family there.
