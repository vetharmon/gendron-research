## Research assessment

Baptism/index act: 2718653
CURRENT STATUS: Eliminated — original images verified
Original baptism 3 April 1824 Marieville, act 2718653, B59 left page: Jean Baptiste, born previous day, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Felicite Bachand dite Vertefeuille. Original marriage 15 February 1847 Mont-St-Gregoire, act 13400955, M3 right page: Jean Baptiste Gendron, cultivateur, aged twenty-two, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Felicite Bachand, marries Euphrasie Besset, aged seventeen, daughter of Pierre Besset and Rosalie Bedard. Original 22 December 1861 baptism there, act 4745667, B118 upper left: Adeline born the same day, legitimate daughter of Jean Baptiste Gendron, cultivateur, and Euphrasie Besset; the father could not sign. The matching parents and compatible age bridge the candidate to a distinct marriage and continuing Quebec family in 1861. This supports exclusion from Franklin's documented Missouri identity; it is not inferred from the name alone. The baptism margin is B59, correcting an intermediate B39 reading.
