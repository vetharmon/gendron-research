## Biography

Jean Baptiste Gendron was born on 2 April 1824 and baptized the next day at Marieville (St-Nom-de-Marie), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Felicite Bachand dite Vertefeuille. He married Euphrasie Besset on 15 February 1847 at Mont-St-Grégoire; the act names him a cultivateur aged twenty-two and again names his parents. Their daughter Adeline was born and baptized on 22 December 1861 at Mont-St-Grégoire.
