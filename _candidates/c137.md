Original marriage 15 February 1847 Mont-St-Gregoire, act 13400955, M3 right page: Jean Baptiste Gendron, cultivateur, aged twenty-two, son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Felicite Bachand, marries Euphrasie Besset, aged seventeen, daughter of Pierre Besset and Rosalie Bedard. Original 22 December 1861 baptism there, act 4745667, B118 upper left: Adeline born the same day, legitimate daughter of Jean Baptiste Gendron, cultivateur, and Euphrasie Besset; the father could not sign. The matching parents and compatible age bridge the candidate to a distinct marriage and continuing Quebec family in 1861.
