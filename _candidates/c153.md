## Biography

Jacques Gendreau was conditionally baptized on 14 November 1824, aged ten days, implying birth about 4 November 1824 at Iberville (St-Athanase-de-Bleury), the son of Jacques Gendreau, a cultivateur, and Geneviève St Yves. He died on 13 August 1834 and was buried on 15 August 1834 at St-Jean-sur-Richelieu; the burial names the same parents, his father there described as a journalier. The burial states his age as about eleven years, while the baptism puts him at nine years and nine months — a discrepancy the underlying research leaves standing. An earlier compiled uniqueness claim for this parental pair was not checked or used in this audit.
