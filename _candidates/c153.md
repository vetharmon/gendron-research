## Biography

- Jacques Gendreau was conditionally baptized on 14 November 1824, aged ten days, implying birth about 4 November 1824 at Iberville (St-Athanase-de-Bleury), the son of Jacques Gendreau, a cultivateur, and Geneviève St Yves. [Baptism](#document-act-2379951)
- He died on 13 August 1834 and was buried on 15 August 1834 at St-Jean-sur-Richelieu; the burial names the same parents, his father there described as a journalier. [Burial, 15 August 1834](#document-act-3111770) · [Baptism, 14 November 1824](#document-act-2379951)
- The burial states his age as about eleven years, while the baptism puts him at nine years and nine months — a discrepancy the underlying research leaves standing. [Baptism](#document-act-2379951) · [Burial](#document-act-3111770)
- An earlier compiled uniqueness claim for this parental pair was not checked or used in this audit. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Burial, 15 August 1834 — LAFRANCE act 3111770](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3111770)** — His burial at St-Jean-sur-Richelieu (upper right, entry S73): Jacques Gendreau, died two days earlier, son of Jacques Gendreau and Geneviève St Yves, with his age stated as about eleven years.
- **[Baptism, 14 November 1824 — LAFRANCE act 2379951](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2379951)** — His conditional baptism at Iberville (lower left, entry B131): Jacques, aged ten days, implying birth about 4 November, son of Jacques Gendreau, cultivateur, and Geneviève St Yves. Counted from this date he was nine, not eleven, at his death.
