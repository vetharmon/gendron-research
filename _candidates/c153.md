Original baptism B131, 14 November 1824 at Iberville, d1p_02250637.jpg, lower left: Jacques, born on the fourth day of the month, son of Jacques Gendreau, cultivateur, and Genevieve St Yves. Original burial S73, 15 August 1834 at St-Jean-sur-Richelieu, d1p_02540694.jpg, upper right: Jacques Gendreau, died two days earlier, son of Jacques Gendreau, journalier, and Genevieve St Yves. The original age is eleven years; the baptism yields nine years nine months. This discrepancy and the father's cultivateur/journalier descriptions remain explicit. New family check 9 November: family 101118 lists one Jacques, between Florence born 10 October 1820 and Theophile born 18 May 1826; no competing indexed Jacques is shown. The original matching parental pair, given name and nearby localities, corroborated by the sibling sequence, support identifying the childhood burial with C153. Counted as a supported exclusion with the age conflict retained, rather than silently replacing it with PRDH's calculated age of nine.
