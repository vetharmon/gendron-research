## Biography

Louis Gendron was born on 7 March 1823 and baptized the next day at St-Hyacinthe (Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire), the son of Pierre Gendron and Marie Louise Daniel. He died on 31 July 1823 and was buried at St-Hyacinthe on 2 August 1823.
