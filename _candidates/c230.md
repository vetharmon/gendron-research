## Biography

- François Xavier Gendron was born on 15 July 1828 and baptized on 18 July 1828 at Châteauguay, the son of Francois Xavier Gendron and Susanne Hallen. [Baptism](#document-act-4588765)

- He died on 26 April 1829 and was buried at Châteauguay on 28 April 1829, aged nine months. [Burial](#document-act-4592644)
