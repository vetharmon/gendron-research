## Biography

- Michel Gendron was born and baptized on 12 November 1820 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Poirier. [Baptism](#document-act-2369656)

- He married Adélaïde Daoust, daughter of Augustin Daoust and Félicité Delisle, on 17 November 1846 at St-Timothée; the act names his parents and his home parish of St-Clément. [Marriage](#document-act-13401026)

- The existing research notes record him and Adélaïde as the parents of Eulalie, born on 2 January 1861 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)
