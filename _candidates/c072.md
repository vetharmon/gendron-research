## Biography

Michel Gendron was born and baptized on 12 November 1820 at Beauharnois (St-Clément), the son of Jean Baptiste Gendron and Marie Poirier. He married Adélaïde Daoust, daughter of Augustin Daoust and Félicité Delisle, on 17 November 1846 at St-Timothée; the act names his parents and his home parish of St-Clément. He appears again with Adélaïde as the father of Eulalie, born on 2 January 1861 and baptized the next day at Beauharnois.
