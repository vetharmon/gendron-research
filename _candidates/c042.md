## Biography


- **Birth and baptism:** Paul Gendron was born and baptized on 25 May 1818 at Châteauguay, the son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Daigneau. [Baptism](#document-act-2627039)

## Research status

No later record identified. Same-name 1851 agricultural-schedule entries remain unassigned, and no 1861 census match has been established. [Search log](#search-log)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Baptism, 25 May 1818 — LAFRANCE act 2627039](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2627039)** — His baptism at Châteauguay. It is the only record so far connected to him.

## Original baptism

The [original register entry, B95](https://www.ancestry.com/imageviewer/collections/1091/images/d13p_01760472?pid=5293422&usePUB=true&usePUBJs=true), records Paul’s birth and baptism on **25 May 1818** at Châteauguay (St-Joachim). His father was Joseph Gendron, a cultivator, and his mother Marguerite Daigneau. Marguerite Daigneau is named as godmother. The godfather’s given name remains uncertain: the older summary reads Louis, while the newer texts read Paul. The original must be rechecked before choosing either reading.

Ancestry’s index calls his mother Marguerite Rouillard. The handwritten act reads Daigneau and confirms the parentage already recorded here. This baptism does not identify the Paul who married Monique Huyet dite Champagne in Michigan in 1844.

## Michigan marriage lead

A [Monroe County marriage index](https://migenweb.org/archive_ftp/monroe/vitals/marriages/dbn/dbn-nov2012-g-h.txt) records **Paul Gendron and Monica Huyet dit Champagne on 6 May 1844**. A [compiled family tree](https://gw.geneanet.org/monartque?lang=en&n=gendron&oc=2&p=paul) gives the groom a birth year of 1818 but no parents. This marriage has not been connected to the Châteauguay baptism.

The original marriage is the next record to inspect. The [Burton Historical Collection guide, page 26](https://d2qp1eesgvzzix.cloudfront.net/uploads/files/Catholic-Church-Records.docx.pdf) lists St Mary, Monroe, marriages for 1794–1930 on **BHC1286, reel41**, and deaths for 1795–1930 on reel41A. The guide notes a baptism gap from February1830 through July1882 in this microfilm collection; that is not evidence of the family’s absence or of loss of the original registers.

These marriage and repository leads were already recorded on 14 November. The follow-up consolidated them here; it did not establish a new identification.

## Additional baptism witness — 28 November 2026

The complete Public Archives typed copy, C-2892 image 73, printed page 1888, was read independently by two readers. It names Paul as born and baptized on 25 May 1818, with father Joseph Gendron, farmer **of Beauharnois**, and mother Marguerite Daigneau. The copied godfather is explicitly **Paul Lebboeuf** (typed across a line as Leb / boeuf), with Marguerite Daigneau as godmother. The typed witness and its full English translation are retained as another copy of the baptism; the previously selected manuscript remains selected. This is not a new direct reading of the manuscript or a resolution by itself of its older Louis/Paul reading discrepancy. [Grouped baptism sources](#document-act-2627039)

The joint family search also reopened a childhood-burial lead for [Étienne, C181](../c181/): a 26 January 1828 copied burial names the same parents but calls the infant Joseph. It does not identify Paul or settle the Michigan groom/soldier question. **Needs work remains unchanged.**

## Parental-family records — 28 November 2026

A newly read later typed archival copy records an **unnamed child of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Daigneau**, privately baptized at home and buried at St-Clément on **7 July 1824**, aged **half an hour**. The child had died “avant-hier,” giving **5 July 1824** as the death date. The act does not explicitly state the child's sex. Antoine and François Héneault were present and could not sign. This infant was already dead before Étienne's birth in April 1826 and cannot be the Joseph buried in January 1828. [Complete copied burial and English translation](#document-burial-unnamed-gendron-1824-07-07)

The complete typed baptism of the already known **Joseph, born 3 May and baptized 4 May 1806** at Châteauguay, is now supplied in French and English. It names **Joseph Jeandron and Marguerite Deniau** as parents and **Benjamin Cousino and Marie Huot** as sponsors. This Joseph remains a **probable older sibling**: the parental-name variants are consistent with the family, but a later explicit relationship has not been established. The expanded text adds no secure later-life record for him. [Complete copied baptism and English translation](#document-baptism-joseph-jeandron-1806-05-04)

These family records were checked jointly with [Étienne, C181](../c181/). They establish no later survival for either candidate and supply no identifying connection to the Michigan groom or soldier. **Needs work remains unchanged.**
