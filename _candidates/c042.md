## Biography

Paul Gendron was born and baptized on 25 May 1818 at Châteauguay, the son of Joseph Gendron and Marguerite Daigneau. No later record identified. Same-name 1851 agricultural-schedule entries remain unassigned, and no 1861 census match has been established.

## Documents

- **[Baptism, 25 May 1818 — LAFRANCE act 2627039](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/2627039)** — His baptism at Châteauguay. It is the only record so far connected to him.

## Original baptism

The [original register entry, B95](https://www.ancestry.com/imageviewer/collections/1091/images/d13p_01760472?pid=5293422&usePUB=true&usePUBJs=true), records Paul’s birth and baptism on **25 May 1818** at Châteauguay (St-Joachim). His father was Joseph Gendron, a cultivator, and his mother Marguerite Daigneau. The godparents were Louis Leboeuf and Marguerite Daigneau; they did not sign.

Ancestry’s index calls his mother Marguerite Rouillard. The handwritten act reads Daigneau and confirms the parentage already recorded here. This baptism does not identify the Paul who married Monique Huyet dite Champagne in Michigan in 1844.

## Michigan marriage lead

A [Monroe County marriage index](https://migenweb.org/archive_ftp/monroe/vitals/marriages/dbn/dbn-nov2012-g-h.txt) records **Paul Gendron and Monica Huyet dit Champagne on 6 May 1844**. A [compiled family tree](https://gw.geneanet.org/monartque?lang=en&n=gendron&oc=2&p=paul) gives the groom a birth year of 1818 but no parents. This marriage has not been connected to the Châteauguay baptism.

The original marriage is the next record to inspect. The [Burton Historical Collection guide, page 26](https://d2qp1eesgvzzix.cloudfront.net/uploads/files/Catholic-Church-Records.docx.pdf) lists St Mary, Monroe, marriages for 1794–1930 on **BHC1286, reel41**, and deaths for 1795–1930 on reel41A. The guide notes a baptism gap from February1830 through July1882 in this microfilm collection; that is not evidence of the family’s absence or of loss of the original registers.

These marriage and repository leads were already recorded on 14 November. The follow-up consolidated them here; it did not establish a new identification.

## Land-record leads — 16 November 2026

Normandeau’s 2024 land history, PDF p.834 (lot 214, printed p.12), cites Paul Gendron leasing to Charles Goyette on 25 October 1858 (J.-G. Longpre, minute 1927) and selling to Louis Faubert on 9 October 1866 (Lemoyne de Martigny, minute 5453). No parents or spouse are given in the summary; these remain unassigned leads. A separate Paul, son of Paul and husband of Francoise Tessier, appears on PDF p.914 and is not C042. The published pages were visually checked; original deeds remain unread. Needs work unchanged.
