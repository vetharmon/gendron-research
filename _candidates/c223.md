## Biography

- François Xavier Gendron was baptized on 16 February 1828 at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Joseph Gendron and Zoé Leboeuf. [Baptism](#document-act-4727392)

- According to the existing research notes, he married Lucie Bourassa on 1 October 1849; the marriage repeats his parents’ names. [Research notes](#evidence-notes)

- Their son François Xavier was baptized on 22 December 1861 at Saint-Jean, the act again naming François Xavier Gendron and Lucie Bourassa as the parents. [Baptism of his son François Xavier](#document-act-6041766)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Baptism, 16 February 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4727392](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4727392)** — His baptism at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), naming his parents, Joseph Gendron and Zoé Leboeuf.
- **[Marriage, 1 October 1849 — LAFRANCE act 13400905](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400905)** — His marriage to Lucie Bourassa at St-Ours. The act names his parents as Joseph Gendron and the late Zoé Leboeuf, and the bride as the daughter of the late Ignace Bourassa and Marie Leboeuf.
- **[Baptism of his son François Xavier, 22 December 1861 — LAFRANCE act 6041766](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/6041766)** — At St-Jean-sur-Richelieu (Cathédrale); the register names the parents as François Gendron and Lucie Bourassa. (An earlier research packet transcribed the mother as Lucie Bissonnette; the register reads Bourassa.)
