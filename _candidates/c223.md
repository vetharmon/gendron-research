## Biography

- François Xavier Gendron was born and baptized on 16 February 1828 at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Joseph Gendron, a farmer of the parish, and Zoé Leboeuf. [Baptism](#document-act-4727392)
- He married Lucie Bourassa at St-Ours on 1 October 1849. The marriage names him as the adult son of Joseph Gendron and the late Zoé Lebeuf, and Lucie as the adult daughter of the late Ignace Bourassa and Marie Lebeuf. [Marriage](#document-act-13400905)
- Their son François Xavier was born and baptized on 22 December 1861 at Saint-Jean. The act names the parents as François Gendron and Lucie Bourassa and says that the godparents and father could not sign. [Baptism of his son](#document-act-6041766)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Birth and baptism, 16 February 1828](#document-act-4727392)** — The parish copy, B28, says “né aujourd’hui” and names Joseph Gendron and Zoé Leboeuf. The surname of godmother Apolline remains uncertain in the saved image.
- **[Marriage, 1 October 1849](#document-act-13400905)** — Both pages of M33 were inspected together. The continuation repeats the baptismal parent pair, with Zoé deceased, and names Lucie Bourassa. The dispensation interlineation and several witness names remain partly unread; the source panel therefore labels the reading as partial. The act records the couple’s mutual consent.
- **[Baptism of his son, 22 December 1861](#document-act-6041766)** — The civil copy, B188, names François Gendron and Lucie Bourassa. It supports the same couple at Saint-Jean through 1861. The father’s given name in this act is François; François Xavier is the child. The earlier Bissonnette reading of the mother is not supported by the inspected image.
- The matching parental pair in the 1828 and 1849 acts, followed by the same married couple in 1861, supports the separate adult identity. The assessment remains eliminated.
