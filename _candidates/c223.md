## Biography

François Xavier Gendron was baptized on 16 February 1828 at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Joseph Gendron and Zoé Leboeuf. He married Lucie Bourassa on 1 October 1849, the marriage repeating his parents' names. Their son François Xavier was baptized on 22 December 1861 at Saint-Jean, the act again naming François Xavier Gendron and Lucie Bourassa as the parents. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 22 December 1861.
