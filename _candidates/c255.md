## Biography

- Ephrem Gendron was born on 23 May 1829 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Damase Gendron and Sophie Baril. [Baptism](#document-act-3367579)
- On 20 July 1852 he married Ursule Hebert Mehot at Deschaillons, the act naming him as the adult son of Damase Gendron and the deceased Sophie Baril. [Marriage, 20 July 1852](#document-act-13400896)

## Evidence notes {#evidence-notes}

- **[Marriage, 20 July 1852 — LAFRANCE act 13400896](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/13400896)** — His marriage to Ursule Hebert Mehot at Deschaillons. It names his parents, Damase Gendron and the deceased Sophie Baril, tying the groom to the 1829 baptism.
- **[Baptism, 24 May 1829 — LAFRANCE act 3367579](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/3367579)** — His baptism at St-Pierre-les-Becquets: born the previous day, son of Damase Gendron and Sophie Baril.
