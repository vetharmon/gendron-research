## Biography

Ephrem Gendron was born on 23 May 1829 and baptized the next day at St-Pierre-les-Becquets (St-Pierre-Apôtre), the son of Damase Gendron and Sophie Baril. On 20 July 1852 he married Ursule Hebert Mehot at Deschaillons, the act naming him as the adult son of Damase Gendron and the deceased Sophie Baril. He has been connected to Canadian records until at least 20 July 1852.
