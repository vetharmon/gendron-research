## Biography

Athanase Gendron was born on 6 September 1828 and baptized the next day at St-Ours (Immaculée-Conception), the son of Michel Gendron and Claire Apolline Labossière. He died on 15 March 1829 and was buried at St-Ours on 17 March 1829, aged about six months — the burial itself says five months.

## Documents

- **[Burial, 17 March 1829 — LAFRANCE act 4656879](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4656879)** — His burial at St-Ours: he died two days earlier, with the same parents as the baptism. The burial states his age as five months, against six months elapsed since his birth; the small discrepancy is left as recorded.
- **[Baptism, 7 September 1828 — LAFRANCE act 4653284](https://www.genealogiequebec.com/Membership/LAFRANCE/acte/4653284)** — His baptism at St-Ours: born the previous day, son of Michel Gendron and Claire Apolline Labossière.
- **[PRDH-IGD individual record 2752049](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Individu/2752049)** — PRDH's compiled record for the same child, consistent with the two register acts.
- **[PRDH-IGD family record 108237](https://www.prdh-igd.com/Membership/fr/PRDH/Famille/108237)** — PRDH's compiled family places this Athanase as the couple's only indexed child of that name, between Charles (1827) and Aglaé (1829) — corroboration for matching the burial to the baptism, though an index is not proof of completeness.
