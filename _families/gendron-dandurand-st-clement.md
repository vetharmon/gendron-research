## What is actually observed

The 1861 census of St-Clément-de-Beauharnois (p. 101, lines 45–47) lists François Gendron, 40, his wife Angèle Dandurand, 38, and their son Joseph, 4. The husband, born about 1821, is older than candidate C226 (François Régis Gendron, baptized 1828), who would have been 33.

## What the indexes do not contain

Controlled searches on 11 November 2026 found **no** Gendron × Dandurand marriage in PRDH or in LAFRANCE — couple searches across 1800–1910, bride-side searches under Angélique / Angèle / Amable Dandurand, and the St-Clément marriage lists for 1852, 1853, and 1854 read in full (no Gendron groom in any of the three years). PRDH family 124668, briefly attached to this couple in an early pass, is in fact the family of François Blache and Luce Therrien (m. 1814, Montréal) and has nothing to do with them.

## Status

The couple existed — the census entry is real — but their marriage is not in the indexes: an unindexed or damaged register act, a marriage in another parish, or a differently recorded bride's name are all possible. The St-Clément register itself has not been read page-by-page for 1853. Until a marriage or baptism names this François's parents, he stays an unidentified older namesake: **not** C226, and not evidence about C226 either way.
